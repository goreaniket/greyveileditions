A Note from the Founder

Understanding Life.
One Page at a Time.

Lalit Rakesh Mishra
Founder, Greyveil Editions

Not everyone has someone they can talk to.

Some have people around them but cannot find the words to explain what they feel. Some struggle to understand themselves. Some spend so much time trying to understand everything and everyone that they eventually become exhausted. And some have simply grown tired of explaining.

I believe this is a part of being human. And perhaps, we understand it far less than we should.

The Way I See Life

I have always been interested in understanding people — their thoughts, behaviour, emotions, decisions and the things that quietly shape their lives.

My belief is simple: the more clearly we understand ourselves, the simpler life begins to feel.

Not necessarily easier, but simpler. We begin to recognise what matters, what does not, what we can change and what we need to stop carrying.

The world will always have its expectations, opinions and contradictions. We cannot understand everything around us, nor do we need to. Sometimes, understanding our own place in it is a much better beginning.

I am not interested in making simple things complicated just to sound intellectual. I would rather understand something deeply enough to explain it simply.

Why Greyveil Exists

Greyveil Editions was built around this very intention: to explore the human experience and make its complex dimensions easier to understand.

Through literature, psychology, observation and independent thought, I want to explore the things people experience every day but often struggle to put into words — identity, relationships, emotions, perception, awareness and the contradictions of everyday life.

I want our books to be accessible without being shallow, thoughtful without being unnecessarily complicated, and honest without pretending to have every answer.

More Than Books

I have no particular fascination with simply writing books, putting a price on them and watching them sell.

Of course, publishing requires work and a sustainable business. But sales alone have never been the purpose behind this vision.

What matters to me is what happens when someone reads.

Perhaps they finally understand a feeling they could never explain. Perhaps they recognise a pattern before repeating it. Perhaps they see a situation differently, make a better decision, or begin understanding themselves a little earlier.

I don't claim that a book can heal everyone. Healing is personal, and no book can replace the support a person may need. But the right words, encountered at the right time, can make understanding easier to reach.

I consider that a responsibility.

I want to publish books that reach people, not just markets. Books that make difficult ideas easier to approach and give readers something meaningful to take into their own lives.

Because sometimes, understanding something at the right time can make more difference than we realise.

I don't want Greyveil to simply add more books to the world. I want it to add something meaningful to the people reading them.

That is the direction. That is the standard I want to pursue.

Lalit Rakesh Mishra
Founder, Greyveil Editions
