01 / A NOTE FROM LALIT

Understanding life.
One page at a time.

I have always been interested in understanding people — their thoughts, behaviour, emotions, decisions and the things that quietly shape their lives.

Not everyone has someone they can talk to. Some have people around them but cannot find the words to explain what they feel. Some struggle to understand themselves. Some spend so much time trying to understand everything and everyone that they eventually become exhausted.

I believe this is part of being human, and perhaps we understand it far less than we should.

My belief is simple: the more clearly we understand ourselves, the simpler life begins to feel. Not necessarily easier, but simpler. We begin to recognise what matters, what does not, what we can change and what we need to stop carrying.

Why Greyveil exists

Greyveil Editions was built to explore the human experience through literature, psychology, observation and independent thought. The catalogue looks at identity, relationships, emotions, perception, awareness and the contradictions of everyday life.

I want the work to be accessible without being shallow, thoughtful without being unnecessarily complicated, and honest without pretending to have every answer.

More than books

I don't claim that a book can heal everyone. Healing is personal, and no book can replace the support a person may need. But the right words, encountered at the right time, can make understanding easier to reach.

I want to publish books that reach people, not just markets — books that make difficult ideas easier to approach and give readers something meaningful to take into their own lives.

“I don't want Greyveil to simply add more books to the world. I want it to add something meaningful to the people reading them.”

Lalit Rakesh Mishra
Founder, Greyveil Editions
