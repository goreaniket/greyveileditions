About the author

Lalit Rakesh Mishra is an Indian writer and independent publisher whose work explores the human mind and the layered nature of human experience. His writing examines psychology, attachment, identity, perception, relationships and the questions people carry but do not always express.

At 18, he began building Greyveil Editions as an independent publishing project alongside his studies. Rather than positioning the work as a conventional self-help programme, his literary approach is reflective and exploratory: it looks at emotional and psychological experiences through writing, observation and ideas.

He is also the creator of The Human Collections, a planned 24-book literary catalogue organised across two volumes and six series. The project brings together themes ranging from the human mind and human paradoxes to fiction and the wider human experience.
