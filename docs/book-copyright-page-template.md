## Pre-Publication Checklist

- Confirm the copyright owner separately from the author name.
- Confirm whether Greyveil Editions is publisher only, rights holder, licensee, or another role.
- Confirm the edition year and publication year.
- Confirm whether cover art, portraits, fonts, illustrations, or design assets require attribution.
- Confirm whether any third-party quotations, references, lyrics, images, datasets, or trademarks appear in the book.
- Confirm whether permissions should be handled only by Greyveil or also by another rights holder.
- Confirm that the wording matches the book's actual distribution format and access level.
