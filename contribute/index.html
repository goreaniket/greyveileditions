WAYS TO CONTRIBUTE

There is more than one way to help.

  • Welcome and support community activities.
  • Help with events, reading circles and workshops.
  • Share skills in design, writing, technology or operations.
  • Contribute ideas to practical community projects.
  • Support research, partnerships and accessibility.
What happens next?

This is an expression of interest, not an automatic volunteer appointment. Greyveil reviews applications, discusses role expectations and checks suitability before assigning responsibilities. Some roles may require references, training or safeguarding checks.
