A CLEAR ACCOUNT OF WHAT STAYS PRIVATE

Privacy, with
room to choose.

This page describes the information used by Greyveil Editions’ existing account, checkout and protected-reader services, and the optional Human Response experience.

Account, purchases and reading access

When you sign in, Greyveil’s Supabase-backed account and access systems use your account information to authenticate you, show your library and protect eligible reader content. Purchases are handled through the existing Greyveil checkout and Razorpay integration. Payment and account records are subject to the relevant service providers’ policies.

Stories and personal responses

If you choose to send a story, it is linked to your signed-in account so you can review or delete it and receive any personal editor response in My Space. An “anonymous” choice controls the public byline only; it does not make the private submission anonymous to Greyveil’s authorised editors.

Stories stay private unless you separately grant public-sharing permission and an authorised editor approves publication. Publicly displayed stories are removed from the public collection when permission is withdrawn. Personal responses and internal editorial notes are not public. A response is not guaranteed, and no response is promised by email.

Story submission is available only when the Greyveil story service is configured. If the form reports that the service is unavailable, the story was not submitted.

Writing and saves on this device

Feel Heard drafts, bookmarks, reading history, private letters and saved lines are stored in this browser, not synced to your Greyveil account. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to see that local data. Use “Clear this device’s Greyveil data” in My Space to remove it from this browser.

Your choices and questions

You can manage story-sharing permission and delete your submissions from My Space. For account, privacy or publishing questions, contact greyveileditions@gmail.com. Links to external services are governed by those services’ own privacy terms.
