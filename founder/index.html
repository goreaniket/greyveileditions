Founder & CEO of Greyveil Editions

Lalit Rakesh Mishra

He is building a body of work around one conviction: the human experience is too layered to be reduced to simple answers. Through Greyveil Editions, Lalit Rakesh Mishra brings that conviction into books, collections, and a publishing vision of his own.

Lalit Rakesh Mishra is a writer, publisher, and the Founder & CEO of Greyveil Editions. He is the creator of The Human Collections, an original 24-book literary project shaped around the mind, contradiction, fiction, and lived human experience.

His work moves between authorship and institution-building. He develops the ideas behind the books, builds their larger collection architecture, shapes Greyveil’s creative identity, oversees publication, and directs the digital platform through which the work reaches readers.

From the first concept to the finished reading experience, he works across the connected disciplines that give an independent publishing house its voice, structure, and direction.

Lalit Rakesh Mishra is the creator and author behind The Human Collections — Greyveil Editions' central literary project. He developed its two-volume structure: Volume I, The Human Collections, and Volume II, The Human Experience. Across six connected series and 24 books, the project explores human thought, identity, perception, relationships, contradiction, and lived experience.

Behind Greyveil

Building more than a catalogue.

Building a publishing house means dealing with things that readers rarely see.

An idea has to become a concept. A concept has to become a manuscript. A manuscript has to be developed, reviewed, prepared, designed, and published. Beyond the book itself, there is an identity to build, a platform to maintain, and an experience to create for the person who eventually finds it.

This is where Lalit's work takes shape.

He works across writing, research, editorial development, publishing operations, creative direction, book production, digital infrastructure, website development, and the commercial side of publishing.

He also works on the systems behind the reader experience — from how books are organised and discovered to how readers access and engage with them.

The objective is not simply to put books online. It is to build a publishing environment where the work, the presentation, and the reader experience belong to the same vision.

The Collections

The Human Collections — created by Lalit Rakesh Mishra.

The Human Collections is an original publishing project conceived and developed by Lalit Rakesh Mishra for Greyveil Editions. It is organised into two volumes, six connected series, and 24 books exploring different dimensions of the human experience.

The Human Collections

The Human Mind The Human Paradox The Human Fiction The Inner Experience The Lived Experience The Expanded Experience

Each series approaches its subject differently. Together, they form a larger exploration of what it means to think, feel, exist, connect, and understand.

The Human Paradox Collection

Volume I — The Human Collections.

What Comes Next

Greyveil Editions is still developing.

There are more ideas to examine, more collections to build, more books to prepare, and more ways to make the work accessible to readers. Its direction is not limited to what has already been published.

For Lalit, the work is not finished when a book is written or published. It continues in how the publishing house grows, how its ideas develop, and how its work reaches people.

The ambition is to build something that can stand on its own — not just as a collection of books, but as a publishing identity with a clear point of view.
