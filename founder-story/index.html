THE FOUNDER STORY

Built from questions that never stayed quiet.

Greyveil did not begin as a plan to simply publish books. It began with a recurring need to understand people: what they carry, what they hide, how they interpret the world, and why the same experience can mean something entirely different to two human beings.

For a long time, writing and reflection were ways to make sense of things that did not arrive with easy explanations. Loss, change, memory, identity and the distance between what we feel and what we manage to say became questions worth staying with.

That curiosity gradually became a larger responsibility. Instead of keeping the work as disconnected notes and ideas, Lalit began shaping it into an independent publishing house: a place where thought could become literature, and literature could make difficult parts of the human experience easier to approach.

“I don't want Greyveil to simply add more books to the world. I want it to add something meaningful to the people reading them.”

As Founder and CEO, Lalit leads the creative direction, collection architecture, authorship, editorial development and digital experience of Greyveil Editions. The work is not limited to writing individual titles; it is about building a connected body of literature with a clear point of view and a lasting structure.

The Human Collections brings together 24 books across two volumes and six series. Across psychology, philosophy, identity, perception, relationships and lived experience, the collection follows one central intention: to look beneath the surface without pretending that every human question has a simple answer.

Greyveil is still being built. Its direction remains independent, thoughtful and human-centred: serious about its work, careful with the people who enter it, and open to the possibility that understanding can begin in more than one way.
