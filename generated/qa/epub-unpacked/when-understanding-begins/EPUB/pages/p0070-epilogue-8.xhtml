Not because it has ended.

But because now the real reading begins.

Not of these pages.

Of yourself.

Of every reaction.

Every fear.

Every attachment.

Every certainty you once considered unquestionable.

Do not believe this book.

Do not reject it either.

Observe.

Silently.

Honestly.

Relentlessly.

Because the greatest fiction was never written inside these pages.

It was written every time you said,

"This is who I am."

And perhaps...
