But have you ever wondered why millions of people are equally convinced of completely opposite truths?

How can certainty exist on both sides?

Because certainty does not prove truth.

It proves attachment.

The mind does not seek reality.

It seeks stability.

If reality threatens stability...

the mind rewrites reality.

That is why two people can experience the same moment...

and remember entirely different stories.

Not because memory records life.

Because memory edits it.

Perhaps the greatest illusion is not that we lie to others.

It is that we mistake our explanations for reality.

Every time life hurts us...

the mind rushes to create a story.

"They never loved me."
