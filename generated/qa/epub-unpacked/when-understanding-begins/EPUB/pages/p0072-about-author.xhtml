ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Lalit R Mishra is an 18-year-old student who observes life more than he speaks

about it.

He is someone who tries to understand human behavior, emotions, and the

silent problems that people often ignore or fail to express clearly.

Through writing, he attempts to reflect the thoughts and struggles that exist

within relationships, especially those that are misunderstood or left unspoken.

He does not write as an expert or as someone who has all the answers. Instead,

he writes as someone who is still learning — from life, from people, and from

his own experiences.
