This is achievement.

This is respect.

This is God.

This is purpose.

This is love.

And because you were too young to question them...

you accepted them.

Not because they were true.

Because they arrived before doubt did.

Years passed.

Those borrowed ideas became your beliefs.

Those beliefs became your decisions.

Those decisions became your habits.

Those habits became your identity.

And one day...

you looked into a mirror...

and called all of it

"Me."
