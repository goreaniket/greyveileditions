"Can it teach me something I don't already know?"

Notice something strange.

You haven't even entered the book...

yet your mind has already created an opinion.

That is what the human mind does.

It predicts before it observes.

It concludes before it understands.

It protects before it questions.

And after enough years...

we stop calling this conditioning.

We call it our personality.

Every human being believes they are becoming someone.

A better version.

A stronger version.

A wiser version.

A happier version.

We spend our entire lives chasing a future version of ourselves...
