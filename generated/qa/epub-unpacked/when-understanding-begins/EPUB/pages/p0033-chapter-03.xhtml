HOW MISUNDERSTANDING STARTS EARLY

shuruaat yaha se hoti hai…

misunderstanding kabhi ekdum se nahi hoti

ye dheere dheere build hoti hai

ek choti si baat se

jo hum ignore kar dete hai

ek “no”

ek daant

ek chhoti si baat

ek ignored feeling

aur hum sochte hai:

“chhoti baat hai”

par andar kuch aur hota rehta hai
