The most dangerous prison has no walls.

It has certainty.

Once you become certain about who you are...

growth quietly dies.

Not because truth disappeared...

but because questions did.

Human beings rarely fear death.

We fear discovering that the person we spent our entire lives protecting...

never truly existed.

Imagine spending decades defending an identity...

only to realize it was built from fear...

comparison...

approval...

memory...

and survival.

Would you call that freedom?

Or would you call it a beautifully decorated cage?

Your memories feel real.

Your emotions feel undeniable.

Your opinions feel original.
