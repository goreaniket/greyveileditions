NOTE FROM THE AUTHOR

This book is not written to point out who is right or wrong.

It is simply a reflection of what I have observed, experienced, and thought

about while trying to understand relationships more deeply.

I am not speaking against parents, nor against children. In my understanding,

both sides are right in their own way. Both are carrying their own emotions,

fears, responsibilities, and perspectives.

Sometimes what I have written may feel true, and sometimes it may feel

incomplete — because every reader has lived a different version of the same

reality.

This book is not a final truth. It is just one way of seeing things.

My intention is not to judge anyone, but to observe how misunderstandings are

created even when love exists on both sides.

If this book helps someone pause, think, or understand a situation a little

differently, then its purpose is fulfilled.
