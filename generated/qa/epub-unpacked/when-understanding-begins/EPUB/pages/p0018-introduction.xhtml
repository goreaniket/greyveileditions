INTRODUCTION

Hum sab ek aise relation mein paida hote hain jise hum choose nahi karte, par usi ke andar hum

apni poori life define karne lagte hain.

Parent aur child ka relation duniya ka sabse natural bhi hai aur sabse complex bhi — kyunki

yahan sirf love nahi hota, yahan fear, responsibility, expectation, ego aur silence sab ek saath

rehte hain.

Aur sabse badi problem ye nahi hoti ki hum ek dusre se pyaar nahi karte…

problem ye hoti hai ki hum ek dusre ko samajhne ki koshish hi nahi karte.

Hum roles ko dekhte hain “parent” aur “child”

Insaan ko nahi dekhte.

Aur jab insaan invisible ho jata hai, tab emotions confuse ho jaate hain.
