aur fir dheere dheere…

har choti baat ek thought ban jati hai

har thought ek perception ban jata hai

aur har perception ek belief ban jata hai

aur woh belief hota hai:

“mujhe samjha nahi jata”

but ab ek aur important baat samajhni hai…

misunderstanding se aaj har relation mai hum problem dekh rahe hai

kahi baap bete mai

kahi maa baap aur bachho mai

kahi do bhai mai

kahi bhai behan mai

aur etc

par jo misunderstanding ko samajh gaya na is point ko

wo har situation handle kar sakta hai

mai ab bhi jo likh raha hu yaha se bhi na jaane kitne log misunderstanding

create karenge

aur kitne log isse padh ke heal hoke samjhenge feel karke aage badhenge
