unko ek challenege banao

samajh lena easy hai

par change karna mushkil hota hai

aur yahi gap decide karta hai

ki insaan grow karega ya sirf sochta rahega

jab tum ye sab padh lo…

toh ek question bachta hai:

“ab main karunga kya?”

sirf realize kar lena life change nahi karta

samajhna easy hai

react na karna hard hai

forgive karna harder hai

aur change rehna sabse hard hai

agar tum chahte ho relation better ho:

reply dene se pehle 3 sec rukna

gusse mein answer na dena

“kyun” puchhne se pehle “kaise feel ho raha hoga” sochna

kabhi kabhi bas itna karna hota hai:

ek din bina argument ke sun lena

ek baar khud se baat start karna
