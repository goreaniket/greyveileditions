Yet hidden beneath those words...

is often a lifetime of unquestioned conditioning pretending to be identity.

The prison rarely locks its doors.

It simply convinces the prisoner...

that leaving would mean losing themselves.

If this book has made you uncomfortable...

do not rush to resolve that discomfort.

Stay with it.

The human mind is addicted to conclusions.

It wants certainty because certainty feels safe.

But growth has never happened inside certainty.

Growth begins the moment certainty becomes unbearable.

The moment you stop defending yourself...

and start observing yourself.

Without excuses.

Without blame.

Without performance.

Without the desperate need to remain the same person you were yesterday.

One day...
