"Now you know who you are."

But if this book has done its job...

those sentences should feel impossible.

Because understanding is not a destination.

It is the permanent destruction of certainty.

Perhaps nothing inside these pages was new.

Perhaps you had heard fragments of these ideas before.

The tragedy of humanity has never been a lack of information.

It has always been the illusion that information transforms people.

It doesn't.

People read thousands of books.

Attend countless seminars.

Watch endless videos.

Collect philosophies like souvenirs.

Yet remain imprisoned by the same mind that collected them.

Knowledge decorates the prison.

Awareness questions why the prison exists.
