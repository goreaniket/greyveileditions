phir tumhe lagega:

“us time dimaag khula kyun nahi”

aaj hum daat pe jawab de dete hai

par kabhi socha hai

unhe kaisa feel ho raha hota hai?

jab hum chhote the

school se aake sab share karte the

“aaj ye padha, ye hua, ye kiya”

par jab real time aata hai share karne ka

tab hum baat bhi nahi karna chahte

haan idhar parents ko bhi thoda samajhna chahiye

kyunki bachche bhi pressure mein rehte hai

kabhi kabhi suicidal thoughts tak chale jate hai

ye common ho gaya hai

tab parents ko bhi regret hota hai:

“mujhe strict nahi hona chahiye tha”

“mujhe samajhna chahiye tha”

is point pe dono side ko samajhna chahiye

aur har relation ka base hona chahiye:

kyunki agar dost jaisa bond ho

toh bina dar ke baat ho sakti hai
