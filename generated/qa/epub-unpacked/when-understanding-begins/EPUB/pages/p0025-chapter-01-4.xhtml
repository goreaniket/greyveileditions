kabhi kabhi dimag itna disturb hota hai ki vo imaginary world mai vo sab

easily bana leta hai hume lagta hai vo sab dekhne ke baad ki life akele best hai

peace hai healing hai

reality hit karegi hard ik

but still mera kaam hai kehna

real life mai bina parents ke hum kar to lenge

but na jaane kitne laat kha ke kitne pain face karke pressure overthinking no

track of life kya karna hai aage kuch bhi sab khokhla ho jayega

aur jab ye sab hoga na

tab samajh aayega ki jo aaj hume bojh lagta hai

vo kal absence ban jata hai

aur absence sabse bada bojh hota hai

atleast abhi ek path hai hum sabke samne

haan hume bas khud ko aur thoda sa parents ka nature change karne ki try

karni hai

aur kuch nahi
