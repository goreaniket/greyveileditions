“galat decision uski life change kar sakta hai”

isliye woh kabhi kabhi soft nahi lagte

par andar se woh fragile hote hai

woh kabhi nahi bolte:

“mujhe dar lagta hai”

“main thak gaya hu”

“mujhe bhi samajh nahi aa raha”

kyunki unko lagta hai

parent ko strong rehna hi hota hai

hum sochte hai parents powerful hai

par sach ye hai…

wo bhi confused hote hai

bas dikhate nahi hai

hum love ko softness se measure karte hai

par parents ka love kabhi kabhi:

strictness ban jata hai

silence ban jata hai

rules ban jata hai

par intention wahi hota hai:

“bachcha safe rahe”

problem love ki nahi hoti
