ENDING

FINAL REFLECTION

At some point in life, every child will feel misunderstood.

And every parent will feel unappreciated.

But most people never realize — both are happening at the same time, inside

the same home.

We keep waiting for the other side to understand first…

and in that waiting, years quietly pass.

Maybe understanding was never about agreeing.

Maybe it was always about seeing the other person without reducing them to

their reaction.

And if you ever look back and feel “they never understood me” or “I was never

understood” —

pause and ask one thing:

Did I ever try to understand before expecting to be understood?
