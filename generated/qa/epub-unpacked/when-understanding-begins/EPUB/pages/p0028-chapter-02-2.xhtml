jo hum nahi dekhte

unke dimaag mein chalta hai:

agar mera bachcha galat raah pe chala gaya toh?

agar uska future kharab ho gaya toh?

agar main usko protect nahi kar paya toh?

hum sochte hai woh hume control kar rahe hai

par unko lagta hai woh hume bachaa rahe hai

kabhi kabhi “no” jo hota hai na

woh rejection nahi hota

woh protection hota hai

par problem yahi hoti hai…

hum intention nahi dekhte

hum reaction dekhte hai

aur jab reaction pe focus hota hai

toh intention blur ho jata hai

hum kehte hai: “mujhe samjha nahi jata”

par parents bhi sochte hai: “main sahi kar raha hu ya nahi?”

ye dono side ka confusion hai

par dono ko lagta hai woh akela hai is feeling mein

kabhi kabhi woh strict isliye hote hai
