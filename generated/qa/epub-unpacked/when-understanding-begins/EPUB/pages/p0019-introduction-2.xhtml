Bachcha apne perspective se dekhta hai aur sochta hai — “mujhe samjha nahi ja raha.”

Parent apne pressure se dekhta hai aur sochta hai — “mujhe follow nahi kiya ja raha.”

Dono apni jagah sahi hote hain, par dono ek hi reality mein nahi hote.

Kyuki reality common nahi hoti… reality interpreted hoti hai.

Har parent apne past ka result hota hai, aur har child apne present ka pressure.

Aur jab past aur present ek hi ghar mein collide karte hain, tab misunderstanding normal ban jati

hai.

Ghar mein aksar shanti hoti hai, par connection nahi hota.

Baat hoti hai, par understanding nahi.

Presence hoti hai, par awareness nahi.

Aur hum is gap ko normal samajh lete hain — jabki yahi gap sab kuch todta hai.

Ye book kisi ko defend karne ke liye nahi hai, aur kisi ko blame karne ke liye bhi nahi.

Ye ek attempt hai us invisible truth ko observe karne ka jo hum daily ignore kar dete hain —
