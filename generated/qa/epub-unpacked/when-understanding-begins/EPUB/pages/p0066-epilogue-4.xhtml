The strangest thing about identity...

is that it survives without ever proving its existence.

Remove your name.

Are you still you?

Remove your memories.

Who remains?

Remove your achievements.

Your failures.

Your beliefs.

Your opinions.

Your political views.

Your religion.

Your ambitions.

Your fears.

What is left?

Most people never ask these questions.
