par society ne ek thought bana diya hai:

“parents hai na, unko sab pata hota hai”

par reality ye hai…

unke paas bhi feelings hoti hai

unke paas bhi fear hota hai

unke paas bhi confusion hota hai

aur agar hum unhe thoda samajhne lage…

toh relation sirf survive nahi karega

woh grow karega

kyunki jab dono side judge kam karti hai

aur samajhne zyada lagti hai

toh waha bond strong ho jata hai

“shayad problem generation gap nahi hai… shayad problem ek dusre ko

human na samajhna hai”
