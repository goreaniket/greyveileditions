hum jo “freedom” maangte hai, woh pehle “stability” soch rahe hote hai

unki duniya mein “future” humara present se zyada important hota hai

mene jo galti ki

main nahi chahta vo ab aur koi kare

hume judge nahi karna chahiye

hume unhe safe feel karvana chahiye

society ka pressure hota hai

“log kya kahenge”

“society kya bolegi”

unke bhi sapne hote hai

par woh unko side rakh dete hai

kyunki pehle hum hote hai

kabhi socha hai…

wo jo thak ke bhi kaam karte rehte hai

wo jo apne sapne chhod dete hai

wo jo apni khushi postpone karte rehte hai

sirf isliye kyunki hum safe rahe…

agar hum unko hi na samjhe

toh phir samjhega kaun?
