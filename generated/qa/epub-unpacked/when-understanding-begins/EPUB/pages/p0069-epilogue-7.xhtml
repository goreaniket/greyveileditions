perhaps years from now...

you may forget every sentence written in this book.

That is perfectly fine.

Words were never the point.

If one question survives...

that is enough.

Not...

"Who am I?"

That question still assumes someone permanent exists to be found.

Instead...

ask something quieter.

What inside me is afraid of disappearing?

Sit with that question long enough...

and you may discover that most suffering does not come from life itself.

It comes from the endless effort required to protect an image that was never stable to begin with.

So close this book.
