It is realizing that what you have always called yourself...

Truth has never been responsible for comfort.

Only illusion has.

So before you turn the page...

leave one thing behind.

The person you are absolutely certain you are.

Because if you cannot question your identity...

you will only use this book to strengthen it.

And that would be the greatest misunderstanding of all.

The pages ahead are not trying to change your life.

They are trying to introduce you...

to the possibility...

that your life has been narrated by a voice you never chose.

Read slowly.

Question relentlessly.

Believe nothing too quickly.
