LAYER 1 - THE HUMAN MASK

WHY PARENTS FEEL STRICT TO US

shuruaat yaha se hoti hai…

hum jab parents ko dekhte hai na

toh hume lagta hai ki woh humesha strict hai

har choti chiz pe rokna

har choti galti pe tokna

har decision pe question uthana

aur hum sochte hai:

“kyun hamesha mujhe hi control kiya jata hai?”

par kabhi ruk ke socha hai…

ki jo strictness hume pressure lagti hai

shayad woh unka dar hota hai

parents ke andar bhi ek mind hota hai
