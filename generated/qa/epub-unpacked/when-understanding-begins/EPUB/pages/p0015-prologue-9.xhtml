Belief is easy.

Questioning is rare.

Instead...

I invite you into something far more uncomfortable.

Observe your own mind while reading.

Notice the sentences that make you defensive.

Notice the paragraphs you immediately reject.

Notice the ideas you desperately want to argue against.

Those reactions matter more than my words.

Because books never reveal who the author is.

They reveal who the reader is.

Every page becomes a mirror.

Some people will only see ink.

Some will see themselves.

Do not read this book looking for motivation.

Motivation fades.

Do not read it looking for answers.

Answers become new beliefs.

Read it as someone willing to lose certainty.

Because sometimes...

the greatest act of intelligence...
