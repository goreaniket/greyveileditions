tumhara purana belief slow dissolve hoga

Na koi guidance milegi.

Na koi comfort structure banega.

Sirf ek gradual exposure hoga:

ki jo “center” tum apne andar feel karte the…

woh actually stable tha hi nahi.

Aur yeh realization loud nahi hota.

Yeh shock nahi hota.

Yeh breakdown nahi hota.

Yeh ek silent system error jaisa hota hai…

jo tumhe pehli baar dikhaata hai:

experience ho raha hai

lekin us experience ka permanent owner unclear hai

Aur jaise jaise tum aage badhte ho…

tumhe yeh feel hone lagta hai ki:

cheezein change nahi ho rahi…

bas unka “meaning layer” weak ho raha hai.

Aur jab meaning layer weak hoti hai…

toh poora internal structure thoda unstable ho jaata hai:

thought less authoritative

identity less fixed
