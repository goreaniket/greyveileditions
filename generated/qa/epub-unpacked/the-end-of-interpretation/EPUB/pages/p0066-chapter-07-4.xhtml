Kyuki yahan kuch new add nahi ho raha.

Sirf unnecessary cheezein drop ho rahi hain.

Na tum effort kar rahe ho.

Na tum practice kar rahe ho.

Bas ek natural reduction ho raha hai:

interpretation ka pressure kam ho raha hai.

Aur jab interpretation kam hota hai…

toh ek cheez bachi rehti hai:

direct experience.

Jo na explain hota hai…

na retain hota hai…

bas happen hota hai.

Aur tum pehli baar notice karte ho:

tum life ko “samajh” nahi rahe ho

tum life ko “see” kar rahe ho

Aur yeh seeing koi special state nahi hoti.

Na peace announce hoti hai.

Na enlightenment feel hota hai.

Bas ek simplicity hoti hai.

Aur is simplicity mein ek aur strange realization hota hai:
