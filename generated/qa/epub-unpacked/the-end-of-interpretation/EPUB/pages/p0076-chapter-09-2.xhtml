Lekin ab ek strange shift hota hai.

Situation same hoti hai.

Life same chal rahi hoti hai.

Log same rehte hain.

Lekin andar ek cheez change ho jaati hai:

har cheez ko solve karne ki urgency kam ho jaati hai

Pehle: “Iska matlab kya hai?”

Ab: “Shayad koi matlab fix karna zaroori hi nahi.”

Pehle: “Main theek hoon ya nahi?”

Ab: “Yeh question hi important nahi lagta.”

Aur yeh laziness nahi hoti.

Yeh avoidance nahi hota.

Yeh ek natural collapse hota hai:

constant solving mechanism ka.

Jaise dimaag ko hamesha har cheez ko fix karne ki aadat thi…

aur ab woh aadat weak pad rahi ho.

Kabhi tum kisi situation mein hote ho…

aur pehli baar tumhe yeh urge nahi hota:

73
