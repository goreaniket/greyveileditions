AFTER THE SYSTEM STOPS INSISTING

If you have reached here…

you might feel like something has finally been understood.

But the truth is more uncomfortable than understanding.

Everything that happened until now…

was not a journey toward answers.

It was a system slowly observing itself.

And the moment a system starts observing itself…

a strange instability appears:

the observer is also changing while observing

You saw it happen step by step:

meaning collapses

thought becomes noise

narration breaks

identity loosens

reaction loses ownership
