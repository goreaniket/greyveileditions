Kabhi kabhi tum react karte ho aur turant baad ek thought aata hai:

“main aisa kyun feel kar raha tha?”

Aur us question ka answer kisi explanation se nahi aata.

Kyuki explanation ab utna satisfying nahi lagta.

Aur yeh bhi ek strange cheez hai:

tum reaction ko control nahi kar rahe…

tum reaction se identify nahi kar rahe…

tum reaction ko stop bhi nahi kar rahe…

Bas tum usko “dekh” rahe ho.

Aur jab dekhna start hota hai…

toh ek cheez naturally weak ho jaati hai:

reaction ka ownership illusion.

Aur yeh weakening loud nahi hoti.

Kisi breakthrough jaisi nahi.

Kisi realization explosion jaisi nahi.

Bas ek silence hoti hai reaction ke andar.
