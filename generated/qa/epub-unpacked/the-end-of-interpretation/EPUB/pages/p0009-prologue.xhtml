BEFORE THE SYSTEM BREAKS

Yeh kahani shuru nahi ho rahi.

Yeh kahani us jagah se aa rahi hai jahan kahaniyan ban’na start hoti hain.

Kuch cheezein life mein aisi hoti hain jo loud nahi hoti.

Na wo entrance leti hain.

Na wo announcement karti hain.

Bas dheere dheere sab kuch change kar deti hain.

Shuru mein insaan apni reality ko simple samajhta hai.

Main hoon.

Duniya hai.

Aur beech mein experiences hain.

Bas itna hi.

Lekin dheere dheere yeh simplicity ek complex system mein badal jaati hai.
