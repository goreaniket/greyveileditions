Lekin tum us chain ke andar fully trapped nahi hote.

Jaise ek radio chal raha ho background mein…

lekin tum uski frequency pe poori tarah tuned nahi ho.

Aur dheere dheere ek aur change hota hai.

Internal narration slow nahi hoti.

Woh sirf “necessary” nahi lagti.

Jaise pehle har feeling ko sentence banana zaroori lagta tha…

ab woh feeling bas reh jaati hai.

Without translation.

Without explanation.

Kabhi tum kisi situation mein hote ho…

aur pehli baar yeh nahi hota:

“Main kya soch raha hoon?”

Bas yeh hota hai:

kuch ho raha hai.
