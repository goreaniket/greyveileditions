This book is not here to replace that story with a better one.

It is here to show you that the story was never solid to begin with.

So as you read, something might feel uncomfortable.

Not because something is wrong…

but because something familiar is becoming less convincing.

Let it be.

Don’t rush to fix it.

Don’t rush to understand it.

Even understanding is part of the same movement.

There is no final point here.

No hidden conclusion.

No correct interpretation.

Just this simple possibility:

what you call “my thoughts”

might just be thoughts happening

what you call “my reactions”
