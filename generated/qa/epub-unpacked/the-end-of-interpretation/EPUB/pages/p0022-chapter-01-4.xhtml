“Kyun hua?”
“Iska matlab kya hai?”
“Main kahin galat toh nahi?”

Lekin ab reaction full speed mein nahi aata.

Beech mein ek pause aa jaata hai.

Aur us pause mein tum pehli baar notice karte ho:

Shayad jo tum “meaning” bana rahe ho…

woh situation ka truth nahi hai.

Woh sirf tumhari mind ki commentary hai.

Jaise life ek simple movement ho…

aur tum us movement par continuously ek layer of narration add kar rahe ho.
