ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Lalit R. Mishra

There are people who write books to tell stories.

And then there are people who write because silence becomes too structured inside them.

Lalit R. Mishra belongs to neither category completely.

He does not write to explain life.

He writes to observe how explanation itself builds a false sense of life.

At first glance, his writing may look like philosophy.

But underneath, it is something more uncomfortable:

a systematic breakdown of how the mind constructs “reality” moment by moment.

His work does not offer motivation.

It does not offer comfort.

It does not offer conclusions.
