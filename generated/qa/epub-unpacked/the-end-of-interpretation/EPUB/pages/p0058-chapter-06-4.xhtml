“Iska hidden meaning kya hai?”

Aur tum itne familiar ho is process se…

ki tum kabhi question hi nahi karte ki yeh zaroori bhi hai ya nahi.

Lekin dheere dheere ek subtle fatigue aata hai.

Na emotional.

Na dramatic.

Bas ek internal overload jaisa feel.

Jaise har cheez ko explain karte karte…

explanation hi heavy lagne lagta hai.

Aur yahin pe ek chhota sa crack open hota hai.

Ek moment hota hai jab tum kuch dekh rahe hote ho…

aur dimaag abhi tak uska meaning bana raha hota hai…

lekin tum us meaning ke pehle hi aware ho jaate ho.

Aur yeh strange lagta hai.

Kyuki pehli baar tumhe dikh raha hota hai:

thought aa raha hai

thought ban raha hai
