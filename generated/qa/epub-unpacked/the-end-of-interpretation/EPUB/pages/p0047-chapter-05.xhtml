THE END OF INTERPRETATION

THE SILENCE INSIDE REACTION

Kuch moments aise hote hain jahan life mein kuch bhi extraordinary nahi hota…

sab kuch bilkul normal chal raha hota hai.

Log baat kar rahe hote hain.

Situation same hoti hai.

Environment same hota hai.

Lekin andar kuch ek cheez change ho jaati hai —

jo shayad kisi ko externally dikhai bhi nahi deti.

Pehle reaction ek straight line hota tha.

Kuch hota → tum react karte → us reaction ko tum “main” bana lete.

Simple.
