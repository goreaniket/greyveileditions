Har action ka intention.

Har silence ka matlab.

Har failure ka hidden lesson.

Har rejection ka hidden purpose.

Jaise dimaag ko lagta hai ki agar woh sab kuch decode kar lega…

toh shayad life thodi manageable ho jayegi.

Lekin real life aise kaam nahi karti.

Real life kisi explanation ka wait nahi karti.

Woh bas happen hoti rehti hai.

Aur tum beech mein khade hote ho…

usko samajhne ki koshish karte hue.

Pehle yeh normal lagta hai.

“Main soch raha hoon” — yahi tumhara identity ban jaata hai.

Tum har cheez ko analyse karte ho.

Ek chhoti si baat bhi andar poora debate bana deti hai.

Kisi ne kuch keh diya…

tum uska matlab dhoondhte ho.

Kuch nahi kaha gaya…
