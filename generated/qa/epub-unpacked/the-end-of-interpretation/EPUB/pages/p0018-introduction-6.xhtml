narration less dominant

reaction less owned

Aur tum beech mein khade ho…

na pehle jaise sure…

na completely lost…

bas ek unfamiliar clarity mein.

Aur yeh book tumhe kisi conclusion tak nahi le jaayegi.

Yeh tumhe conclusion ki necessity hi doubt karna sikha degi.

Aur shayad yahi sabse strong shift hai:

jab tumhe answers nahi milte…

tumhe questions ki urgency hi kam lagne lagti hai

Aur tab yeh book start nahi hoti…

yeh slowly unfold hoti hai…

jaise koi system apni layers ko ek ek karke loose kar raha ho…

without noise.

without drama.

just silence breaking structure.
