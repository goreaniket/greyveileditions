Na dramatic.

Na emotional breakdown jaisa.

Bas ek instability.

Kabhi tum kisi cheez ko observe kar rahe hote ho…

aur ek second ke liye yeh clarity aati hai:

experience ho raha hai

lekin “kaun” experience kar raha hai… woh pakka nahi lagta

Aur yeh confusion nahi hota.

Yeh direct noticing hoti hai.

Thoughts aa rahe hote hain.

Feelings chal rahi hoti hain.

Sensations ho rahe hote hain.

Memories arise ho rahi hoti hain.

Lekin har cheez ke saath ek aur layer feel hoti hai…

koi fixed owner nahi

koi stable center nahi

koi permanent reference point nahi

Jaise sab kuch happening hai…

lekin kisi ek jagah pe “belong” nahi kar raha.

Aur pehle yeh idea completely unacceptable hota.
