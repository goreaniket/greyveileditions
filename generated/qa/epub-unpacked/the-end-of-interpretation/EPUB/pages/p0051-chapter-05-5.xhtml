“reaction ho raha hai”

lekin saath mein

“main reaction nahi hoon”

Aur yeh dono cheezein ek saath chal rahi hoti hain.

Confusing nahi hota.

Bas unfamiliar hota hai.

Reaction phir bhi hota hai.

Gussa bhi aata hai.

Sadness bhi aati hai.

Discomfort bhi hota hai.

Lekin ek difference hai:

pehle reaction ke saath ek story chipak jaati thi.

ab reaction sirf ek movement lagta hai.

Jaise hawa chal rahi ho.

Tum dekh rahe ho.
