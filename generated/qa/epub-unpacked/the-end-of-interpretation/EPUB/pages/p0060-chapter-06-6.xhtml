Aur yeh shift bahut small lagta hai…

lekin system-level change hota hai.

Kyuki jab internal dialogue weak hota hai…

toh identity ko continuously define karne ka mechanism bhi weak ho jaata hai.

Aur jab identity ko bar-bar define nahi karte…

toh ek strange emptiness nahi…

ek neutral clarity aane lagti hai.

Na woh positive hoti hai.

Na negative.

Bas less constructed.

Aur yeh sab kisi practice se nahi hota.

Koi effort nahi.

Koi technique nahi.

Bas ek natural reduction jaisa feel hota hai.

Jaise dimaag ko apni hi awaaz se thoda break mil raha ho.

Aur jab tum peeche dekhte ho…

toh realize hota hai:

tum life ko live nahi kar rahe the
