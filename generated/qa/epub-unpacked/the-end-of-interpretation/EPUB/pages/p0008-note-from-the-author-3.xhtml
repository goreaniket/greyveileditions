what you call “my self”

might just be a process unfolding

And if that feels too simple to be true…

notice that too.

Because this book is not trying to change your mind.

It is quietly pointing out that the mind is already changing on its own.

So don’t try to hold onto anything from here.

Not ideas.

Not insights.

Not states.

Not clarity.

Even this note is not something to carry.

It is something to pass through.

And if something remains after all of this…

it will not be an answer.

It will be the absence of urgency to find one.
