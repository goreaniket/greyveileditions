Kyuki dissatisfaction aksar solution ke expectation se aati hai.

Aur jab expectation weak hoti hai…

toh experience neutral ho jaata hai.

Na better.

Na worse.

Bas present.

Kabhi kabhi tum kisi cheez ko feel karte ho…

aur turant usko change karne ki need nahi hoti.

Bas usko allow kiya jaata hai.

As it is.

Aur yeh “allowing” koi practice nahi hoti.

Koi technique nahi hoti.

Yeh bas ek fatigue hota hai:

constant interference ka fatigue.

Aur jab tum peeche dekhte ho…

toh ek cheez clear hoti hai:
