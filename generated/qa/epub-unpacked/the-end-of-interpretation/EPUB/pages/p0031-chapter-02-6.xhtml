But clarity jitni zyada chase hoti hai…

utni hi distant lagne lagti hai.

Aur dheere dheere ek realization form hota hai:

thoughts problem nahi hain

thoughts ka constant interpretation problem hai

Tum notice karte ho:

jab tum soch rahe ho…

tum present experience ko directly nahi jee rahe hote.

Tum us experience ka “version” jee rahe hote ho.

Aur woh version kabhi complete nahi hota.

Woh update hota rehta hai.

Har second.

Har feeling.

Har assumption.

Aur isi loop mein ek insaan poori zindagi spend kar sakta hai…

without realizing ki woh life ko jee nahi raha tha…

woh life ko interpret kar raha tha.

Aur phir ek chhota sa gap aata hai.

Thought ke beech ka gap.
