Kyuki poori life ek cheez sikhi gayi thi:

har experience ka “main” hota hai

Lekin ab woh “main” thoda strange lagne lagta hai.

Jab tum usko pakadne jaate ho…

woh khud ek thought ban jaata hai.

Kabhi kabhi tum kisi moment mein hote ho…

aur suddenly ek question aata hai:

“yeh dekh kaun raha hai?”

Aur yeh question pehle philosophical lagta tha…

ab yeh ek trap jaisa lagta hai.

Kyuki har answer ek naya object ban jaata hai.

Agar tum kehte ho “main dekh raha hoon”…

toh “main” ek aur thought ban jaata hai jo dekha ja sakta hai.

Agar tum kehte ho “awareness dekh rahi hai”…

toh “awareness” bhi ek concept ban jaata hai.

Aur yeh loop band nahi hota:

observer dhoondo
