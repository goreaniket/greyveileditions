“isko samajhna hai.”

Bas cheez ho rahi hoti hai.

Without extra layer.

Aur interesting baat yeh hai:

problem disappear nahi hoti…

sirf “problem” ka label lose ho jaata hai.

Ek situation hoti hai:

kuch bhi — argument, silence, memory, delay.

Pehle instant movement hota:

analyse

interpret

assign meaning

find cause

predict outcome

Ab woh chain start hoti hai…

lekin complete nahi hoti.

Beech mein ek stop jaisa feel hota hai.

Not forced.
