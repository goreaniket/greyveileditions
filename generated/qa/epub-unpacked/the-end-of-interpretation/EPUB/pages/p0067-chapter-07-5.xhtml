jo cheez samajhne ki koshish thi… woh zaroori hi nahi thi

Aur jab yeh clear hota hai…

toh mind ka ek aur function loose ho jaata hai:

continuous interpretation.

Aur ab cheezein appear hoti hain…

aur disappear bhi hoti hain…

without being fully converted into stories.

Aur yahi pe Book 3 ek subtle turn leta hai:

understanding replace nahi hoti

understanding unnecessary ho jaati hai
