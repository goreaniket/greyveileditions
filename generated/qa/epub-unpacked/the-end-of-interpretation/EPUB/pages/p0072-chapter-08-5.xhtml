observer bhi observe ho jaata hai

phir naye observer ki search

Aur dheere dheere yeh search tiring nahi lagti…

yeh irrelevant lagne lagti hai.

Jaise system ko samajh aa gaya ho:

jisko dhoondh rahe ho, woh object ban nahi sakta

Aur yahi pe ek quiet collapse hota hai.

Observer disappear nahi hota.

Bas uski solidity toot jaati hai.

Na tum completely vanish hote ho.

Na tum ek fixed identity ban ke rehte ho.

Bas experience hota hai…

without a permanent owner.

Kabhi tum kisi cheez ko dekh rahe hote ho…

aur yeh automatic label nahi aata:

“main dekh raha hoon”

Bas seeing hoti hai.

Aur yeh seeing koi special state nahi hoti.
