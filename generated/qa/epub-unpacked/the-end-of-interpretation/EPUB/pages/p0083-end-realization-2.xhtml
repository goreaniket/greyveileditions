internal dialogue fades

seeing becomes direct

the observer loses its center

solving stops feeling necessary

And when all layers fall away…

one expectation naturally arises:

“the final truth will remain”

But nothing final appears.

What remains is even more unsettling:

nothing feels final anymore

Not confusion.

Not clarity.

Not silence.

None of them settle as an endpoint.

Everything feels like an ongoing movement…

that never turns into a conclusion.

And this is the uncomfortable part.

Because the mind always wants an end.
