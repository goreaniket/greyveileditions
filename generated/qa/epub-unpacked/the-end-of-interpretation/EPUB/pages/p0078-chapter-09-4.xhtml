Not intentional.

Bas natural pause.

Aur us pause mein ek cheez clear hoti hai:

cheez ko solve karne se zyada, cheez ko dekhna possible hai

Aur yeh “dekhna” bhi effort nahi maangta.

Jaise pehli baar tum realize karte ho:

har cheez ek puzzle nahi hai.

Kuch cheezein bas happening hain.

Aur jab tum unhe solve nahi karte…

toh woh incomplete nahi lagti.

Woh just “as it is” lagti hain.

Aur yeh shift bahut subtle hai.

Kyuki kuch dramatic change nahi hota.

Na life rukti hai.

Na thoughts completely disappear hote hain.

Bas ek internal pressure kam ho jaata hai:

constant solving ka pressure

Aur jab solving kam hoti hai…

toh ek aur layer dissolve hoti hai:

continuous dissatisfaction
