Tum kisi insaan ko dekhte ho.

Pehle mind instantly story banata tha:

yeh aisa hai

iska intention yeh hai

iski history yeh hogi

Lekin ab…

tum dekhte ho aur wahi ruk jaate ho.

Without adding extra layer.

Without final judgment.

Without conclusion.

Aur yeh “ruk jaana” effort nahi hota.

Yeh natural lagta hai.

Jaise mind thoda thak gaya ho interpretation se…

aur ab sirf seeing allowed reh gaya ho.

Kabhi tum kisi sound ko sunte ho…

aur turant uska meaning nahi nikalte.

Sirf sound hota hai.

Kabhi tum kisi face ko dekhte ho…

aur turant identity assign nahi karte.

Sirf presence hoti hai.

Aur yeh phase strange hai.
