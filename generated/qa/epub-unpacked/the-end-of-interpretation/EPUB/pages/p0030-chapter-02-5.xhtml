thoughts aa rahe hain

thoughts ja rahe hain

thoughts contradict ho rahe hain

thoughts justify ho rahe hain

Aur koi central authority nahi hai jo decide kare:

“kaunsa thought real hai?”

Tab pehli baar tumhe doubt nahi hota duniya par…

tumhe doubt hota hai:

“jo main soch raha hoon… kya woh sach hai ya sirf movement?”

Aur yeh doubt dangerous hai.

Kyuki is doubt ka answer aur thoughts se nahi milta.

Is point pe mind ek aur trick karta hai:

Woh aur zyada sochna start kar deta hai…

“clarity” ke naam par.
