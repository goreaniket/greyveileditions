ab har cheez ko explain karna compulsory nahi lagta

Aur jab explanation compulsory nahi hota…

toh experience apne aap thoda sa clean ho jaata hai.

Na tum kuch pakad rahe hote ho.

Na tum kuch chhod rahe hote ho.

Bas cheezein ho rahi hoti hain.

Aur shayad yahi sabse strange truth hai:

jo tum dhoondh rahe the… woh kabhi missing tha hi nahi

Lekin usko “dhoondhne wala” hi hamesha noise create karta raha.

Aur jab woh noise thoda kam hota hai…

toh reality change nahi hoti.

Bas usko dekhne ka pressure kam ho jaata hai.

Aur tab ek simple sa feel bachta hai:

kuch bhi solve nahi hua…

lekin kuch bhi urgent bhi nahi raha.

Aur yeh hi Book 3 ka end nahi hai.

Yeh sirf ek pause hai…

jahan system apni hi speed se thoda alag ho jaata hai.
