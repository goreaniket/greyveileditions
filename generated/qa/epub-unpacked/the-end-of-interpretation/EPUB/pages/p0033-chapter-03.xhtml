THE END OF INTERPRETATION

THE FAILURE OF INTERNAL NARRATION

Kuch moments hote hain jab tumhe lagta hai ki tum life jee rahe ho…

lekin actually tum sirf uski explanation sun rahe hote ho.

Har cheez pe ek commentary chal rahi hoti hai.

Har moment ke upar ek invisible voice.

Har experience ke saath ek running interpretation.

Jaise tum kisi cheez ko directly touch nahi kar rahe…

bas uske baare mein soch rahe ho.

Aur interesting baat yeh hai:

yeh voice kabhi rukti nahi.

Na permission leti hai.
