And when that pressure drops…

an uncomfortable honesty remains:

you were never directly experiencing reality

you were continuously interpreting it

And interpretation quietly became your entire world.

Now as interpretation weakens…

the world does not disappear.

It simply loses its weight.

Not meaningful.

Not meaningless.

Just uncompressed.

A raw presence remains…

moving without explanation.

And perhaps the most disturbing realization is this:

life is not meant to be fully understood

life cannot be fully controlled

life does not need to be concluded

And when all three drop…

what is left?

Just this:

something is happening…
