Aur dimaag us line ke upar:

intention

hidden meaning

past connection

future threat

ego impact

sab kuch bana deta hai.

Aur asli moment?

woh kahin peeche chala jaata hai.

Phir tum ek memory ko dobara dekhte ho.

Lekin tum us moment ko nahi dekhte…

tum us moment ki edited version ko dekhte ho.

Aur har baar jab tum us memory ko revisit karte ho…

woh thodi si change ho jaati hai.

Not reality.

But interpretation.

Aur yeh cycle itni normal ho jaati hai ki tum usse life samajhne lagte ho.

Phir ek point aata hai.

Thoda sa heavy feel.

Thoda sa internal silence.
