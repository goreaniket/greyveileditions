THE END OF INTERPRETATION

THE POINT WHERE THOUGHT BECOMES NOISE

Kabhi kabhi problem ye nahi hoti ki tum bahut zyada soch rahe ho.

Problem ye hoti hai ki tum soch ko “truth” samajh chuke hote ho.

Shuru mein thoughts helpful lagte hain.

Decision lene ke liye.

Situation samajhne ke liye.

Logon ko decode karne ke liye.

Khud ko define karne ke liye.

Lekin dheere dheere ek subtle shift hota hai.

Tum thoughts ko use nahi karte…

tum thoughts ke andar rehne lagte ho.
