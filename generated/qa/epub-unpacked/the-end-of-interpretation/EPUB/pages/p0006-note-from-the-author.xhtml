INTRODUCTION

NOTE FROM THE AUTHOR

Lalit R. Mishra

If you are reading this…

you are already inside something you usually avoid noticing.

Not the book.

Not the words.

But the act of being aware right now.

This book was never written to give you answers.

Because answers are where most journeys quietly stop.

It was written to disturb something very normal inside you:

the automatic need to explain everything you experience.

You don’t notice it usually.

But your mind is constantly doing something in the background:

turning moments into meaning,

feelings into labels,

thoughts into identity,
