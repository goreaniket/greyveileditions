EPILOGUE-

AFTER EVERYTHING IS SEEN

Agar tum yahan tak aaye ho…

toh tumne kuch “seekha” nahi hai.

Tumne sirf ek cheez notice ki hai:

jo seekh raha tha… woh bhi change ho raha tha.

Kuch moments ke baad yeh realization bhi important nahi lagta.

Na yeh special lagta hai.

Na yeh final lagta hai.

Sab kuch normal hi lagta hai.

Bas thoda less heavy.

Thoda less necessary.

Thoda less urgent.

Thoughts ab bhi aate hain.

Feelings ab bhi hoti hain.

Life ab bhi chalti hai.

Lekin ek cheez change ho jaati hai:
