Aur yahi pe ek friction create hota hai: experience vs identity

Experience flexible hota hai.

Identity rigid hoti hai.

Aur jab dono takraate hain…

toh tum experience ko ignore kar dete ho…

sirf apni identity ko protect karne ke liye.

Lekin kabhi kabhi ek crack aata hai.

Ek moment jab tum react karte ho…

aur turant baad ek thought aata hai:

“yeh main kyu kar raha tha?”

Aur us question mein pehli baar gap aata hai…

between action and identity.

Aur us gap mein ek uncomfortable realization hota hai

shayad “main” itna fixed nahi hoon jitna main sochta tha

Na woh totally stable hai.

Na woh fully defined hai.
