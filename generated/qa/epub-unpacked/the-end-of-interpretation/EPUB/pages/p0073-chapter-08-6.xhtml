Na achievement.

Na enlightenment feeling.

Bas ek reduction hota hai:

unnecessary center ka fall.

Aur jab yeh center loose hota hai…

toh perception spread ho jaata hai.

Wide.

Open.

Unfixed.

Jaise sab kuch happening ho…

lekin kisi ek point par lock na ho.

Thoughts aate hain…

lekin woh kisi fixed identity ke through pass nahi hote.

Feelings aati hain…

lekin woh kisi permanent owner ko define nahi karti.

Aur dheere dheere ek strange openness develop hoti hai…

jo kisi structure pe based nahi hoti.

Na control zyada hota hai.

Na confusion zyada hota hai.
