WHEN THE SYSTEM STARTS TO FAIL QUIETLY

Yeh koi motivational book nahi hai.

Aur na hi yeh kisi “better life” ke direction mein jaane wali journey hai.

Yeh us phase ke baare mein hai jahan insaan improve nahi hota…

insaan ka internal system hi apni authority loose karne lagta hai.

Shuru mein har cheez simple lagti hai.

Life = problem + solution.

Emotion = reason + response.

Thought = analysis + conclusion.

Identity = label + behaviour.

Aur lagta hai yahi reality hai.

Poora din ek invisible rule follow hota hai:

har cheez ko explain karo

har cheez ko define karo
