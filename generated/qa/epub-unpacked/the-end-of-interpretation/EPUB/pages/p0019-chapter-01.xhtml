THE END OF INTERPRETATION

WHEN MEANING STOPS SURVIVING REALITY

Kabhi kabhi life koi dramatic moment se change nahi hoti.

Na koi big breakdown.

Na koi life-altering event.

Bas ek normal sa din hota hai.

Same routine.

Same people.

Same noise.

Lekin andar kuch subtle sa shift ho jaata hai.

Tum notice karte ho ki tum har cheez ko samajhne ki aadat mein jee rahe ho.

Har baat ka reason.
