Because according to him, conclusions are where perception stops evolving.

Instead, his writing moves through layers:

meaning → thought → narration → identity → reaction → dialogue → observation → silence

Not as theory…

but as lived internal dissection.

He is not presenting himself as a teacher.

In fact, his central argument quietly dissolves the need for one:

if experience is continuously changing, no fixed authority can define it

The tone of his work is intentionally unsettling.

Not to disturb the reader…

but to expose how much of life is already constructed disturbance masked as normal thinking.

He does not ask the reader to believe anything.

He only points toward one uncomfortable possibility:

most of what we call “me” is a process, not a person

And if that feels strange, unclear, or even uncomfortable…

that reaction itself is part of the observation.

Because in his framework, nothing is outside the system.
