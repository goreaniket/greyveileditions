Lekin tum hawa nahi ban jaate.

Aur yahi pe ek deep realization nahi hota…

ek silent noticing hoti hai.

Tum dekhte ho ki:

reaction ko rokne ki zarurat nahi hai

reaction ko samajhne ki urgency nahi hai

reaction ko justify karne ka pressure nahi hai

Aur phir bhi reaction hota rehta hai.

Bas uska weight change ho gaya hai.

Jaise pehle reaction ek identity statement hota tha:

“main aisa hoon”

Ab woh ek passing phenomenon lagta hai:

“yeh ho raha hai”

Aur is shift mein sab kuch change nahi hota…

lekin kuch bhi same bhi nahi rehta.
