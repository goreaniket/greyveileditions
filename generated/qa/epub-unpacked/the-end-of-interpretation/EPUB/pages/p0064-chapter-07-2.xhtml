Log bol rahe hote hain.

Cheezein ho rahi hoti hain.

Life apni speed mein chal rahi hoti hai.

Aur pehle jo automatic system tha:

analyze

interpret

judge

predict

woh sab chalna start karta hai…

Lekin ab uske beech mein ek gap aa jaata hai.

Tum dekhte ho:

cheez ho rahi hai

aur

cheez ka meaning ban raha hai

Aur yeh dono cheezein separate lagne lagti hain.

Jaise pehli baar tum observe karte ho ki:

experience ek cheez hai

explanation uske upar chipki hui ek layer hai

Aur interesting baat yeh hai:

experience change nahi hota.

Bas explanation optional lagne lagta hai.
