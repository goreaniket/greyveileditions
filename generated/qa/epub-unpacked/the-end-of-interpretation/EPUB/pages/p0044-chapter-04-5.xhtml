“Main aisa hi hoon.”

Aur yahi pe ek silent trap ban jaata hai.

Kyuki ek baar identity fix ho jaaye…

toh har experience usko prove karne lagta hai.

Agar tum gussa ho jaate ho…

toh tum kehte ho: “main gusse wala hoon.”

Agar tum chup ho jaate ho…

toh tum kehte ho: “main introvert hoon.”

Agar tum confuse ho…

toh tum kehte ho: “main overthinker hoon.”

Aur dheere dheere tum experience nahi jee rahe hote…

tum apni identity ko repeat kar rahe hote ho.

Ek din tum notice karte ho…

ki kuch situations mein tum differently react kar rahe ho.

Lekin tum usko accept nahi kar paate.

Kyuki woh tumhari “story” ke against ja raha hota hai.
