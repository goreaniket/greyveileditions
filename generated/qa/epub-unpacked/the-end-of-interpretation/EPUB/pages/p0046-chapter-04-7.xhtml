Woh sirf ek moving pattern hai…

jo situations ke saath shape change karta rehta hai.

Aur jab yeh thoda clear hota hai…

toh ek cheez dheere dheere weak hone lagti hai:

certainty about self.

Na tum khud ko completely samajh paate ho…

na completely define kar paate ho.

Aur yeh confusion nahi hota.

Yeh collapse hota hai.

Soft.

Silent.

Continuous.

Aur yahi pe Book 3 ek aur layer break karta hai:

jo “I am this” lagta tha… woh ab sirf “this is happening” lagne lagta hai
