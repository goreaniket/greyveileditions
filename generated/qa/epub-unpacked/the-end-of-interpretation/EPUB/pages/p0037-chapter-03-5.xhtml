Narration chal rahi hoti hai…

lekin tum usko “truth” nahi maan rahe hote.

Aur yahi sabse dangerous shift hota hai:

tum reality nahi jee rahe the

tum reality ka running explanation jee rahe the

Aur jab yeh thoda sa clear hota hai…

toh ek aur layer collapse hoti hai.

Tum dekhte ho:

thoughts still aate hain

interpretations still happen

stories still form hoti hain

Lekin unka hold kam ho jaata hai.

Woh authority lose kar dete hain.

Jaise pehle woh decision lete the…

ab woh sirf suggestions lagte hain.

Aur yeh shift loud nahi hota.

No dramatic realization.

No sudden clarity explosion.

Bas ek internal silence jaisa gap.

Jisme cheezein pehli baar “final truth” nahi lagti.
