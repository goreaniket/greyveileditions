A solution.

A closure.

A final explanation.

But here, even closure becomes just another thought.

You cannot fully “understand” anything…

because the one trying to understand…

is also part of the same shifting process.

And this is the deepest break:

The knower is not stable

The known is not stable

even knowing is continuously moving

The world is still there.

People are still there.

Situations still continue.

But something subtle has changed:

nothing exists to be constantly explained anymore

things simply happen

And this “just happening” does not feel comforting.

It does not feel like clarity either.

It only removes the pressure…

that was constantly being placed on reality.
