Aur interesting baat yeh hai:

woh narration kabhi rukta nahi tha…

bas tum pehli baar usse dekh rahe ho.

Kisi real-life situation mein:

log kuch bolte hain

tum uska matlab bana lete ho

kuch chhoot jaata hai

tum usko rejection bana lete ho

kuch delay hota hai

tum usko destiny ya failure bana lete ho

Aur har baar actual event same hota hai…

lekin tumhara experience change ho jaata hai.

Tab ek strange realization aata hai:

shayad pain life mein nahi hai

pain interpretation mein hai

Aur yeh realization loud nahi hota.

Koi transformation music nahi bajta.

Koi “awakening moment” feel nahi hota.

Bas ek silence sa aa jaata hai…

jaise tumhara mind pehli baar thoda slow ho gaya ho.

Aur tum dekhte ho:
