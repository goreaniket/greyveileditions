Reaction ke beech ka gap.

Meaning ke beech ka gap.

Aur us gap mein…

kuch loud nahi hota.

Kuch dramatic nahi hota.

Bas yeh dikhne lagta hai:

thought tum nahi ho

thought sirf ek ongoing process hai

Aur jab yeh thoda sa clear hota hai…

toh thought ki authority pehli baar weak hoti hai.

Na woh gayab hota hai.

Na woh stop hota hai.

Bas itna hota hai:

woh “absolute truth” nahi lagta
