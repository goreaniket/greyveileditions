THE END OF INTERPRETATION

THE OBSERVER WITHOUT A CENTER

Kabhi kabhi ek phase aata hai jahan tumhe lagta hai ki tum sab kuch samajh rahe ho…

lekin dheere dheere ek aur strange realization hota hai:

shayad jo “samajhne wala” tha, woh itna solid kabhi tha hi nahi.

Shuru mein har experience ka ek fixed center hota hai.

Ek point jahan se sab kuch measure hota hai.
