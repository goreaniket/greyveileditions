Almost unnoticeable.

Situation hoti hai.

Same trigger.

Same type of words.

Same type of silence.

Reaction bhi aata hai.

But not in a clean straight line.

Beech mein ek interruption hota hai.

Ek micro gap.

Ek half-second ka pause.

Aur us pause mein kuch ajeeb sa hota hai.

Reaction start hota hai…

lekin usko “main” instantly hold nahi karta.

Jaise body respond kar rahi ho…

lekin identity us response ko turant own nahi kar rahi ho.

Pehele yeh impossible lagta tha.

Kyuki tumhe lagta tha:

reaction = identity

emotion = truth

thought = reality
