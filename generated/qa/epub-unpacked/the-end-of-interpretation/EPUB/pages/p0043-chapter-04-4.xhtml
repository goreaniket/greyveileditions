Aur tum us output ko identity bana lete ho.

Kabhi tum gussa karte ho.

Kabhi tum overthink karte ho.

Kabhi tum withdraw ho jaate ho.

Kabhi tum over-explain karte ho.

Aur har baar tum kehte ho:

“yeh main hoon.”

Lekin ek strange observation aati hai…

kya yeh sab tum choose kar rahe ho?

Ya yeh sab automatically ho raha hai?

Kyuki jab tum observe karte ho…

toh pata chalta hai:

reaction pehle aata hai

explanation baad mein aata hai

identity label sabse last mein aata hai

Aur phir bhi tum us last label ko truth bana dete ho.
