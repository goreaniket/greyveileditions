jab kuch hota hai…

tum automatically usko story banane ke liye utne compulsive nahi ho.

Tum dekhte ho…

ki tum reaction ho rahe ho…

lekin tum reaction ban nahi rahe ho.

Aur yahin se ek aur layer break hoti hai:

tum life ko samajhne ki jagah

life ko “as it is” dekhne lagte ho

Aur jab tum dekhte ho…

ki tumne poori zindagi cheezein explain karke jeene ki koshish ki…

tab ek aur cheez halki ho jaati hai:

“Iska matlab kya hai?”

Aur us jagah…

kuch nahi hota.

Na answer.

Na question.
