THE END OF INTERPRETATION

WHEN SEEING BECOMES ENOUGH

Kabhi kabhi ek phase aata hai jahan tum life ko samajhne ki koshish kam nahi karte…

tum bas samajhne ki need hi lose kar dete ho.

Shuru mein har cheez ko meaning chahiye hota hai.

Har cheez ko label.

Har moment ko explanation.

Har feeling ko translation.

Lekin ab ek strange simplicity aane lagti hai.

Tum kisi cheez ko dekhte ho…

aur turant usko convert karne ki urgency nahi hoti.

Ek situation hoti hai.

Normal si.
