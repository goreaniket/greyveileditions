“yeh mere against hai”
“yeh destiny hai”

Aur tum notice bhi nahi karte ki:

event already khatam ho chuka hota hai…

lekin narration abhi bhi chal rahi hoti hai.

Jaise life ek movie ho…

aur tum screen nahi dekh rahe…

tum uska continuous explanation sun rahe ho.

Aur dheere dheere ek strange distortion create hota hai.

Reality aur narration merge ho jaate hain.

Aur tum bhool jaate ho:

kaunsa real tha… aur kaunsa interpretation.

Kisi insaan ne kuch normal sa bol diya.

Bas ek line.
