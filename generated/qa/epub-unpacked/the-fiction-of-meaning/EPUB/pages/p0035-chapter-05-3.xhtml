toh usko “turning point” bana deta hai.

Aur isi tarah life ek screenplay ban jati hai.

Lekin problem ye hai:

screenplay reality nahi hoti.

Wo editing hoti hai.

Aur editing ke through hum reality ko readable bana lete hain.

Lekin readable hona ≠ true hona.

Story ka sabse powerful effect ye hai:

wo experience ko stable identity mein convert kar deta hai.

Aur fir hum apne aap ko ek character ki tarah dekhne lagte hain:

main yeh hoon

main yeh tha

main yeh ban raha hoon

Lekin ye “main” story ke andar exist karta hai…

experience ke andar nahi.

Agar story ko side mein rakha jaye…

toh sirf events bachte hain.

Aur events ke beech koi forced connection nahi hota.
