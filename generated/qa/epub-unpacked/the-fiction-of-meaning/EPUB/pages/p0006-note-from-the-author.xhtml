INTRODUCTION

NOTE FROM THE AUTHOR

The Human Fiction is not written to convince, convert, or correct.

It is written to observe.

What it observes is not the world in an external sense…

but the way experience is internally structured through thought, memory, and interpretation.

Most of what we call “understanding” is actually narration happening in real time.

A continuous attempt to make experience coherent.

This book does not oppose meaning.

It only points to how meaning is constantly being generated.

Silently.

Automatically.

Without permission.
