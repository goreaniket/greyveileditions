CHAPTER 4

The Search for Hidden Significance

Hum aksar ye believe karte hain ki har cheez ke peeche kuch hidden hota hai.

Ek deeper meaning.

Ek secret pattern.

Ek unseen design.

Aur isi belief ke saath hum life ko dekhte hain.

Jaise har moment ek puzzle ho…

jo solve hona chahiye.

Lekin interesting baat ye hai:

jo cheez “hidden” maani ja rahi hoti hai…

wo aksar already visible hoti hai.

Bas hum usko accept nahi karte.
