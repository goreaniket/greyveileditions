CHAPTER 5

The Story-Making Mind

Hum jo bhi experience karte hain…

wo raw nahi rehta.

Wo turant ek story ban jata hai.

Mind ka sabse automatic function ye hai:

experience ko narrative mein convert karna

Ek moment hota hai…

aur us moment ke saath ek frame attach ho jata hai:

ye kya hua

ye kyu hua

iska matlab kya hai

aur aage kya hoga

Lekin moment khud kabhi ye questions nahi laata.
