shayad reality meaningful nahi hai…

sirf mind usko meaningful bana raha hai.

Book II isi invisible habit ko explore karti hai:

kaise hum experience ko story bana dete hain,

events ko lesson bana dete hain,

randomness ko design bana dete hain,

aur silence ko message bana dete hain.

Na ki meaning ko destroy karne ke liye…

but uske origin ko clearly dekhne ke liye.

Kyuki jab meaning ka source clearly dikh jata hai…

toh wo apni authority lose karne lagta hai.

Aur tab ek strange shift aata hai:

experience ko explain karne ki urgency kam ho jati hai.

Aur jab urgency kam hoti hai…

toh jo bachta hai wo confusion nahi hota.

Wo hota hai directness.

Raw presence.

Unfiltered reality.

Ye book tumhe koi new belief dene ke liye nahi hai.

Ye sirf ye dikhane ke liye hai:

ki understanding khud ek continuous creation hai.
