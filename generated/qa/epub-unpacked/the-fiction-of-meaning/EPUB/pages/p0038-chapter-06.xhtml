CHAPTER 6

The Collapse of Meaning Systems

Har meaning system stable lagta hai…

jab tak usko question nahi kiya jata.

Hum apni life ko multiple meaning layers ke through live karte hain:

ye good hai ya bad hai

ye success hai ya failure hai

ye right hai ya wrong hai

ye loss hai ya lesson hai

Aur ye categories hume reality ko organize karne mein help karti hain.

Lekin agar in sab ko closely dekha jaye…

toh ye sab fixed reality nahi hoti.
