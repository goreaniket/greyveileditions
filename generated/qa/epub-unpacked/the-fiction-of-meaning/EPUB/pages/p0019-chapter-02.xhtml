CHAPTER 2

The Fiction of Interpretation

Hum jo bhi experience karte hain…

wo direct nahi hota.

Wo hamesha interpret hota hai.

Ek cheez hoti hai…

aur uske baad turant mind uska meaning generate karta hai.

Reality pehle aati hai.

Interpretation baad mein.

Lekin awareness mein dono itne close hote hain…

ki gap feel hi nahi hota.

Aur isi gap-less illusion ko hum “understanding” samajh lete hain.
