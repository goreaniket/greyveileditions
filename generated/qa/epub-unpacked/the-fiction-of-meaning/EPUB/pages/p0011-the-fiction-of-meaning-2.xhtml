Ek silence hota hai.

Lekin almost instantly…

mind kuch bhi raw nahi rehne deta.

Wo translate karta hai.

Label deta hai.

Explain karta hai.

Conclusion bana deta hai.

Aur is instant translation mein…

raw experience ek structured reality ban jata hai.

Ye book ye nahi kehti ki meaning galat hai.

Ye ek deeper cheez dikhati hai:

ki meaning milta nahi…

banaya jata hai.

Continuously.

Unconsciously.

Automatically.

Aur kyuki ye process itni fast hoti hai…

hume lagta hai ki meaning pehle se exist karta hai.

Jaise reality already meaningful ho.

Lekin agar thoda aur clearly dekha jaye…

toh ek strange possibility dikhti hai:
