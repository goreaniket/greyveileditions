Ye shifting interpretations hoti hain.

Ek hi event…

alag states mein completely different meaning le leta hai.

Iska matlab:

meaning system stable nahi hai.

Wo context dependent hai.

Lekin mind usko stable maan leta hai.

Kyuki stability ek psychological need hai.

Aur jab kisi system ko stable maan liya jata hai…

toh usko defend bhi kiya jata hai.

Isliye beliefs, opinions, ideologies…

sirf thoughts nahi hote.

Wo identity structures ban jate hain.

Aur jab identity involved ho jati hai…

toh meaning emotional ho jata hai.

Aur emotional meaning…

neutral observation ko replace kar deta hai.

Isliye hum reality ko dekhte kam hain…

interpret zyada karte hain.

Lekin jab observation deep hota hai…
