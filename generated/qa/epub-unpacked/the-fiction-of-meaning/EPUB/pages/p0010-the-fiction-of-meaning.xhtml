INTRODUCTION

THE FICTION OF MEANING

Ek moment har insaan ki life mein aata hai…

jab sirf experience kaafi nahi lagta.

Kuch andar se push karta hai:

“Iska matlab kya hai?”

Aur isi ek question ke saath…

reality ka ek second layer create ho jata hai.

Na actual world…

but world ka interpretation.

The Human Fiction — Book II yahin se start hoti hai:

na ki meaning already present hai…

balke is urge se ki meaning create karna zaroori lagta hai.

Meaning se pehle sirf occurrence hota hai.

Ek thought aata hai.

Ek feeling pass hoti hai.

Ek event unfold hota hai.
