CHAPTER 7

When Nothing Means Anything

Aisa lagta hai jaise hum hamesha meaning ke andar jee rahe hain.

Har cheez ko explain karne ki koshish…

har moment ko justify karne ki aadat…

har experience ko “kuch banane” ki urgency.

Lekin jab saari meaning systems dheere-dheere collapse hone lagti hain…

toh ek strange state bachta hai.

Na meaning.

Na explanation.

Na hidden purpose.
