END REALIZATION

Agar tum yahan tak pahunche ho…

toh ek subtle shift already ho chuka hota hai.

Cheezein waise hi chal rahi hoti hain.

Thoughts, feelings, events — sab same.

Lekin ek cheez quietly change ho jati hai:

unko explain karne ki urgency.

Pehele har cheez “kuch” banani padti thi:

lesson

sign

reason

meaning

hidden purpose

Lekin ab wo compulsion weak ho jati hai.

Aur jab compulsion weak hoti hai…

toh experience pehli baar bina filter ke appear hota hai.
