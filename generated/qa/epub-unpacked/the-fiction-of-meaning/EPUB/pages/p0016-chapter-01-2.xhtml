Ek situation hoti hai…

aur turant mind usko frame karta hai:

ye kyu hua

isse kya seekhna hai

iska deeper meaning kya hai

Lekin situation khud kabhi ye questions carry nahi karti.

Wo neutral hoti hai.

Raw hoti hai.

Uninterpreted hoti hai.

Purpose ek story layer hai jo chaos ko structure deta hai.

Aur structured chaos humare liye bearable ho jata hai.

Isliye mind quickly ek assumption create karta hai:

“Har cheez kisi reason se ho rahi hai.”

Lekin ye assumption kabhi verify nahi hota.

Ye sirf comfort generate karta hai.

Agar kisi moment ko bina meaning ke dekha jaye…

toh wo uncomfortable lag sakta hai.

Kyuki mind ko anchor chahiye hota hai.
