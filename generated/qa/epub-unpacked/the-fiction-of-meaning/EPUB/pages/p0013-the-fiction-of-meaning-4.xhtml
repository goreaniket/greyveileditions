Aur jab ye mechanism clearly dikhta hai…

toh life same rehti hai…

but usko dekhne ka tarika change ho jata hai.

Na meaning disappear hota hai…

na experience change hota hai.

Sirf meaning ki grip soft ho jati hai.

Aur jo bachta hai…

wo ek simple realization hota hai:

life kabhi explain hone ke liye bani hi nahi thi…

sirf jee jaane ke liye thi.

Aur explanation hamesha baad mein add kiya gaya tha.
