Kyuki question hi dissolve ho jata hai.

Aur jo bachta hai…

wo silence nahi hota as an object…

wo silence as absence of need hota hai.

Need for meaning.

Need for explanation.

Need for conclusion.

Aur jab ye need nahi hoti…

toh experience apne aap sufficient lagta hai.

Not because it is understood…

but because it is no longer being forced into understanding.

Aur yahin pe Book II khatam nahi hoti…

ye bas apni necessity kho deti hai.
