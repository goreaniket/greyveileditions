CHAPTER 1

The Illusion of Purpose

Hum har cheez ko ek purpose ke lens se dekhte hain.

Har event ke peeche ek “reason” dhundte hain.

Har pain ke peeche ek “lesson” assign karte hain.

Aur har loss ke peeche ek “hidden meaning” create kar lete hain.

Lekin agar direct experience ko dekha jaye…

toh cheezein sirf ho rahi hoti hain.

Without explanation.

Without justification.

Without narrative intent.

Purpose baad mein attach hota hai.

Mind ke through.
