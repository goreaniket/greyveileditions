reality same hai…

sirf interpretation weight change kar raha hai.

Lekin mind is weight ko reality samajh leta hai.

Aur jab meaning strong ho jata hai…

toh usko defend karna padta hai.

Isliye arguments, conflicts, misunderstandings…

zyadatar reality ke against nahi…

meaning ke against hote hain.

Loop ka core structure ye hai:

meaning creates identity response

identity response strengthens meaning

Aur phir dono ek dusre ko feed karte rehte hain.

Is loop ka sabse deep effect ye hai:

hum experience ko live nahi karte…

hum usko continuously interpret karte rehte hain.

Aur interpretation kabhi stop nahi hoti…

kyuki silence ko mind unsafe feel karta hai.

Isliye meaning ek protection ban jata hai.

Ek shield.

Lekin wahi shield…

hume direct experience se door bhi rakhta hai.
