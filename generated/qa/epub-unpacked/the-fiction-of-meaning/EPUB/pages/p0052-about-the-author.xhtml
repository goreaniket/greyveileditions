ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Lalit R. Mishra is the creator of The Human Fiction series.

His work does not come from academic philosophy or structured theory…

but from observation of everyday experience — thought, identity, interpretation, and the quiet mechanisms behind them.

He is interested in one recurring question:

why does life feel like it is happening to a “someone” inside experience?

Rather than offering answers, his writing focuses on exposing patterns:

how the mind constructs continuity,

how meaning attaches itself to events,

how identity forms through memory and narration,

and how a stable sense of “I” emerges without ever being directly found.
