EPILOGUE

AFTER MEANING DISAPPEARS

Agar tum yahan tak pahunche ho…

toh ek cheez already change ho chuki hoti hai.

Reality change nahi hui hoti…

sirf usko explain karne ki urgency kam ho chuki hoti hai.

Jo pehle “meaningful” lagta tha…

wo ab sirf “happening” lagne lagta hai.

Na usme koi hidden message hota hai…

na koi final explanation.

Sirf unfolding hota hai.

Thoughts aate hain…

par unko justify karne ki zarurat kam ho jati hai.

Feelings hoti hain…

par unko label karna heavy lagta hai.

Events hote hain…

par unko story banane ka pressure gir jata hai.
