Aur jab ye visible hota hai…

toh ek strange realization aata hai:

koi bhi cheez inherently heavy nahi hoti…

hum usko heavy banate hain.

Aur jab attachment loosen hota hai…

toh meaning khatam nahi hota…

bas uski urgency gir jati hai.

Aur urgency ke girne ke saath…

ek gap appear hota hai:

jahan experience sirf experience hota hai…

without being processed into meaning.
