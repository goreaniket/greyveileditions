Wo sirf present hota hai.

Story baad mein create hoti hai.

Aur itni fast create hoti hai…

ki lagta hai wo pehle se exist kar rahi thi.

Aur yahin pe illusion strong hota hai:

hum experience ko live nahi karte…

hum experience ko narrate karte rehte hain.

Mind har cheez ko sequence mein convert karta hai:

beginning → middle → end

Lekin reality mein aksar koi clear beginning ya end nahi hota.

Sirf unfolding hota hai.

Story ka structure hamesha meaning add karta hai.

Kyuki bina meaning ke story stable nahi reh sakti.

Isliye mind randomness ko tolerate nahi karta.

Wo har cheez ko connect karta hai.

Agar kuch unclear hota hai…

toh mind usko “setup” bana deta hai.

Agar kuch painful hota hai…

toh usko “lesson” bana deta hai.

Agar kuch unexpected hota hai…
