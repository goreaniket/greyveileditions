Aur meaning wahi anchor ban jata hai.

Lekin truth ye hai:

experience ko meaning ki zarurat nahi hoti.

Sirf mind ko hoti hai.

Purpose ka illusion yahan se start hota hai:

jab mind randomness ko accept nahi kar pata.

Aur randomness ko accept na karne ka solution hai:

“hidden design” imagine karna.

Isliye har cheez ek “lesson” ban jati hai.

Har pain ek “growth opportunity” ban jata hai.

Har loss ek “path correction” ban jata hai.

Lekin reality mein…

cheezein na lesson hoti hain…

na punishment…

na reward.

Wo sirf events hote hain.

Aur jab ye clearly dikhta hai…

toh ek strange shift aata hai:

meaning collapse nahi hota…

bas unnecessary lagne lagta hai.

Aur jab meaning unnecessary lagta hai…
