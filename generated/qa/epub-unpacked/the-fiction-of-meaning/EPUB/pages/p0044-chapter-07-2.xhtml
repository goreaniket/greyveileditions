shayad kuch bhi explain karna zaroori hi nahi tha.

Lekin yahan ek misunderstanding bhi ho sakti hai.

Ye state “empty” nahi hoti…

ye sirf “uninterpreted” hoti hai.

Cheezein ab bhi ho rahi hoti hain.

Thoughts ab bhi aate hain.

Feelings ab bhi arise hoti hain.

Life ab bhi chal rahi hoti hai.

Sirf ek cheez missing hoti hai:

un par forced meaning.

Aur jab meaning drop hota hai…

toh reality disappear nahi hoti.

Reality zyada simple lagne lagti hai.

Ek moment hota hai…

aur wo kisi “sign” mein convert nahi hota.

Ek feeling hoti hai…

aur wo kisi “message” mein convert nahi hoti.

Ek thought hota hai…

aur wo kisi “deeper truth” mein convert nahi hota.

Sirf occurrence hoti hai.

Pure occurrence.
