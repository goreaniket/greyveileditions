Aur is simplicity ko mind initially resist karta hai.

Kyuki mind ka kaam hi meaning banana hai.

Lekin jab meaning banana band ho jata hai…

toh kuch dangerous nahi hota.

Bas unnecessary complexity gir jati hai.

Aur jab complexity girti hai…

toh ek quiet clarity reh jati hai:

jo bhi ho raha hai…

wo already ho raha hai.

Without explanation.

Without justification.

Without symbolic weight.

Aur yahin pe ek final realization quietly emerge hota hai:

shayad reality kabhi complex thi hi nahi…

sirf interpretation ne usko complex bana diya tha.

Aur jab interpretation ka pressure hat jata hai…

toh cheezein waise hi reh jati hain jaise wo pehle se thi:

simple, direct, and happening.

Is point pe koi final answer nahi hota.
