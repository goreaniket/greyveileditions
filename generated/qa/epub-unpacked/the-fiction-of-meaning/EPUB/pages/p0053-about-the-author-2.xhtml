His approach is not to convince or conclude…

but to observe carefully enough that assumptions begin to lose their automatic strength.

In his view, clarity is not something added to life.

It is what remains when unnecessary interpretation slows down.

The Human Fiction series reflects this inquiry:

not as a belief system,

not as a philosophy to follow,

but as a structured exploration of how human experience becomes personal through narration.

He does not claim authority over truth.

He only points toward the structure through which truth is usually interpreted.

And within that structure, one question remains central:

if experience is happening, where exactly is the “one” it is happening to?

This question is not meant to be answered.

It is meant to be seen clearly enough that its urgency dissolves on its own.
