Connection hum create karte hain.

Kyuki disconnection mind ko unsafe lagta hai.

Isliye story ek safety system ban jati hai.

Lekin wahi safety system…

hume direct experience se door bhi le jata hai.

Aur jab ye clear hota hai…

toh ek strange gap appear hota hai:

jo cheezein “meaningful story” lagti thi…

wo sirf interpreted sequence lagne lagti hain.

Aur us sequence ke peeche koi hidden author nahi milta.

Sirf narration hoti hai…

jo continuously chal rahi hoti hai.

Aur jab ye narration clearly dikhti hai…

toh ek silence bachta hai:

jahan kuch explain karne ki zarurat nahi hoti.

Sirf unfolding hota hai…

without story.
