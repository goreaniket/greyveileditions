Mind simple experience ko insufficient maan leta hai.

Usko lagta hai:

“Itni simple reality ka matlab nahi ho sakta.”

Aur yahin se search start hoti hai.

Har event ko hum scan karte hain:

iska deeper reason kya hai

ye mujhe kya signal de raha hai

iske peeche kya intention hai

Lekin reality apni taraf se kuch bhi hide nahi karti.

Wo sirf unfold hoti hai.

Hidden significance ek projection hai.

Jo simplicity ko tolerate nahi kar pata.

Isliye mind complexity create karta hai…

taaki experience meaningful lage.

Aur jab meaning mil jata hai…

toh ek temporary satisfaction hota hai:

“ab samajh aaya”

Lekin wo understanding sirf search ko pause karti hai…

end nahi karti.

Kyuki agle moment mein…
