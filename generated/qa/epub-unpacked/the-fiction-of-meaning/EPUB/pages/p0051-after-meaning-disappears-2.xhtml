Aur jab story banane ka pressure girta hai…

toh kuch khaas nahi bachta.

Bas directness bachta hai.

Ye directness koi special state nahi hai.

Ye sirf interpretation ka absence hai.

Aur interpretation ke absence mein…

experience apne aap sufficient lagne lagta hai.

Na “kya matlab hai” ki urgency hoti hai…

na “kyu ho raha hai” ka burden.

Sirf ye hota hai:

jo ho raha hai… wo ho raha hai.

Aur yahin pe Book II khatam nahi hoti…

wo sirf apni zarurat kho deti hai.

Kyuki jahan meaning nahi chahiye…

wahan explanation bhi unnecessary ho jata hai.
