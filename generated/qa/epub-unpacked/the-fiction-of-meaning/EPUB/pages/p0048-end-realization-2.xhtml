Na usko defend karne ki zarurat.

Na usko reject karne ki zarurat.

Sirf ye recognition:

jo bhi ho raha hai… wo already ho raha hai.

Aur ye koi mystical state nahi hai.

Na enlightenment.

Na achievement.

Ye sirf ek simple clarity hai:

ki reality ko explain karne ki zarurat kabhi thi hi nahi…

wo sirf mind ka habit tha.

Aur jab habit dikhaai de jati hai…

toh wo automatically powerful nahi rehti.

Meaning yahan disappear nahi hota…

wo bas background noise ban jata hai.

Aur us silence mein…

kuch naya nahi milta.

Sirf ek cheez absent hoti hai:

“explanation ki necessity”.

Aur jab necessity nahi hoti…

toh experience apne aap sufficient ho jata hai.

Not because it makes sense…
