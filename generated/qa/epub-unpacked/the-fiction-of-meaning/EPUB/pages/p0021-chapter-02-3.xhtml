bina experience change hue.

meaning fixed nahi hai.

Lekin mind us fluidity ko ignore karta hai…

aur ek stable version choose kar leta hai:

“ye iska meaning hai”

wo experience ko story bana deta hai.

Aur story banne ke baad…

hum experience ko nahi dekhte…

hum story ko dekhte hain.

Aur story ko defend karte hain.

Isliye do log same reality mein bhi…

completely different “truth” experience karte hain.
