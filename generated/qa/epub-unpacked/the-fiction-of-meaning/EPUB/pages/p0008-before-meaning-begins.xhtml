PROLOGUE

BEFORE MEANING BEGINS

Har insaan ke experience ke peeche ek silent habit hoti hai…

jo kabhi question nahi ki jaati:

“Iska matlab kya hai?”

Ye question itna fast aata hai…

ki lagta hai wo natural hai.

Lekin ye natural nahi hai…

ye learned reflex hai.

Experience pehle hota hai.

Meaning baad mein attach hota hai.

Lekin dono itne close ho jaate hain…

ki gap invisible ho jata hai.

Ek thought aata hai…

aur turant uska interpretation ban jata hai.

Ek feeling aati hai…

aur turant uska reason create ho jata hai.
