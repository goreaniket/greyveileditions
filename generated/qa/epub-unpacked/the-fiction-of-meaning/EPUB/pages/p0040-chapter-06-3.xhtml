toh ek strange instability visible hoti hai:

jo cheez “truth” lagti thi…

wo sirf perspective lagne lagti hai.

Aur perspective infinite hote hain.

Isliye final truth hold nahi hota.

Meaning systems collapse ka matlab ye nahi hota ki kuch toot jata hai physically.

Matlab hota hai:

certainty dissolve ho jati hai.

Aur jab certainty dissolve hoti hai…

toh ek uncomfortable openness bachi rehti hai.

Jahan kuch bhi fixed nahi hota.

Na label.

Na conclusion.

Na final answer.

Aur yeh state initially unsettling lag sakti hai…

kyuki mind closure dhoondhta hai.

Lekin closure available nahi hota.

Aur jab closure nahi milta…

toh search weak ho jati hai.

Aur search weak hone ke saath…
