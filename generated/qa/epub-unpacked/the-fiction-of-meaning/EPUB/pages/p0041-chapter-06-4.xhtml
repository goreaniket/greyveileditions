ek quiet clarity emerge hoti hai:

cheezein explain nahi ho rahi hoti…

sirf unfold ho rahi hoti hain.

Aur jab meaning systems collapse hote hain…

toh reality khatam nahi hoti…

sirf interpretation ka weight kam ho jata hai.

Aur us reduced weight ke saath…

experience thoda zyada direct lagne lagta hai.

Without forcing meaning.

Without demanding explanation.
