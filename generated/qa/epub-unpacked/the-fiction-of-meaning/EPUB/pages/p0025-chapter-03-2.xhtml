Aur ye loop itna fast hota hai…

ki lagta hai sab kuch natural ho raha hai.

Lekin agar slow observation ki jaye…

toh dikhta hai ki kuch bhi linear nahi hai.

Mind har event ko instantly label karta hai:

ye acha hai

ye bura hai

ye important hai

ye meaningless hai

Aur jo label attach hota hai…

wo emotional weight create karta hai.

Aur emotional weight hi attachment ban jata hai.

Isliye hum cheezon se nahi…

meaning se attach hote hain.

Ek simple situation bhi…

agar “important” label le le…

toh wo heavy ho jati hai.

Aur wahi situation…

agar “normal” label le le…

toh wo light ho jati hai.
