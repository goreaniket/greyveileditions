Aur interpretation always subjective hoti hai.

Jab ye clearly dikhta hai…

toh ek strange thing happen hoti hai:

events same rehte hain…

but unka “weight” change ho jata hai.

Kyuki weight meaning se aata hai…

event se nahi.

Aur jab meaning ka illusion visible ho jata hai…

toh ek gap open hota hai:

between what happened…

and what we think it means.

Aur us gap mein…

quietness appear hoti hai.

Jo na explanation maangti hai…

na rejection.

Sirf ek observation reh jata hai:

interpretation is happening… not truth is being revealed.
