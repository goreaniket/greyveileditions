Ek situation hai.

Aur usi instant pe multiple interpretations generate ho jati hain:

ye galat hai

ye sahi hai

ye kismat hai

ye lesson hai

ye sign hai

Lekin situation khud kuch bhi declare nahi karti.

Wo silent hoti hai.

Neutral hoti hai.

Interpretation ek filter hai.

Jo raw experience ko readable banata hai.

Aur jo cheez readable ban jaye…

wo controllable lagne lagti hai.

Isliye mind interpretation ko hold karta hai.

Kyuki interpretation uncertainty ko reduce karti hai.

Lekin problem ye hai:

interpretation reality nahi hoti.

Wo sirf framing hoti hai.

Aur framing change ho sakti hai…
