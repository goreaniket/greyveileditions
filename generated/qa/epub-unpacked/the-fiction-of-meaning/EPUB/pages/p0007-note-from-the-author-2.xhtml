that what feels like a fixed “self” is actually a moving interpretation…

held together by memory, language, and expectation.

Nothing in these pages demands belief.

And nothing requires rejection.

Because belief and rejection both belong to the same system — interpretation.

This work is not about reaching a conclusion.

It is about noticing the process through which conclusions are formed.

And once that process is seen clearly…

its authority begins to soften.

Not through effort.

But through understanding itself.

The Human Fiction does not end with an answer.

It ends with a shift in seeing:

that experience does not need to be explained in order to be real.

It only needs to be seen.
