CHAPTER 3

The Meaning Attachment Loop

Hum sirf meaning create nahi karte…

hum meaning se attach bhi ho jate hain.

Aur yahi attachment ek loop bana deta hai.

Ek experience hota hai…

aur turant usko meaning diya jata hai.

Lekin meaning yahin stop nahi hota.

Wo ek aur cheez generate karta hai:

reaction.

Aur reaction phir ek naya meaning demand karta hai.

Is tarah ek loop start hota hai:

experience → meaning → reaction → new meaning → deeper reaction
