phir ek aur layer dhoondhi jati hai.

Is tarah ek endless loop ban jata hai:

simple → unsatisfying → search → explanation → temporary relief → again search

Aur ye loop hi “spiritual” ya “deep thinking” jaisa feel hota hai.

Lekin core mechanism same rehta hai:

inability to accept simplicity.

Agar directly dekha jaye…

toh cheezein already complete hoti hain.

Unko complete banane ki zarurat nahi hoti.

Lekin mind completion ko accept nahi karta…

wo “extra meaning” add karta rehta hai.

Aur jab ye process clearly dikhta hai…

toh ek strange clarity aati hai:

kuch bhi hidden nahi hai…

sirf interpretation overlay hai.

Aur jo hum “depth” samajhte hain…

wo aksar over-layering hoti hai.

Is realization ke baad search khatam nahi hoti…

bas uski urgency kam ho jati hai.
