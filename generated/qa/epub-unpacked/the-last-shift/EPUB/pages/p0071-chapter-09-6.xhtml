Aur jab future present ban jaata hai…

toh hum already kisi naye future mein hote hain.

Aur yeh cycle sirf repeat nahi hota…

yeh aadat ban jaata hai.

Phir ek din regret aata hai…

par us waqt sirf samajh hoti hai, sudharne ka time nahi.

Aur wahi se acceptance start hota hai.

Acceptance loud nahi hota.

Woh bas ek inner shift hota hai—

ki har cheez ko hold karna zaroori nahi hai.

Har cheez forever nahi hoti.

Aur har cheez rokne layak bhi nahi hoti.

Log aate hain.

Phase complete karte hain.

Lesson dete hain.

Aur quietly chale jaate hain.

Aur jo reh jaata hai…

woh sirf memory nahi hota.

Woh ek transformed version hota hai tumhara.

Kabhi kabhi lagta hai:
