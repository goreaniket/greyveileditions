uska mind continuously questions generate karta rehta hai.

“Kya main overthink kar raha hoon?”

“Kya yeh sirf meri side hai?”

“Kya main zyada feel kar raha hoon?”

Aur jab questions answers se zyada ho jaate hain…

tab frustration quietly enter karti hai.

Isi liye kuch log gradually distant ho jaate hain.

Kuch quiet ho jaate hain.

Kuch emotionally cold lagne lagte hain.

Not because feelings khatam ho gayi hoti hain.

But because continuously feel karna exhausting ho jaata hai.

Silent attachment ka end kabhi loud nahi hota.

Woh breakup jaisa nahi hota.

Woh closure jaisa bhi nahi hota.

Woh bas ek slow realization hota hai—

ki feelings grow kar rahi hain…

par clarity grow nahi kar rahi.

Aur jab clarity nahi hoti…

toh emotions apna weight khud carry karne lagte hain.
