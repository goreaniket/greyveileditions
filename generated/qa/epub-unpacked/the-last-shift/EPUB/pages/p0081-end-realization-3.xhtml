Aur woh silence empty nahi hoti… woh stable hoti hai.

Sabse bada shift yeh hota hai ki hum life ko interpret karna kam kar dete hain.

Har cheez ka meaning dhoondhna band ho jata hai.

Aur us jagah pe sirf experience reh jata hai — raw, simple, real.

Tab samajh aata hai ki shayad hum kabhi kisi ko “lose” nahi karte…

hum sirf unhe ek certain distance se dekhna band kar dete hain.

Aur shayad hum khud ko bhi wahi kahin chhod dete hain — apne overthinking ke beech.

End pe koi dramatic realization nahi hota.

Na koi perfect closure.

Bas ek quiet acceptance hoti hai —

ki sab kuch hold karna zaroori nahi tha.

Aur jo cheez tumhe tod rahi thi…

wo kabhi tumhe complete kar rahi thi, tumhe pata hi nahi tha.

Aur shayad yahi “Last Shift” hai —

jab tum zindagi ko samajhne ki koshish chhod dete ho,

aur usse jeena shuru kar dete ho… jaise woh already enough ho.
