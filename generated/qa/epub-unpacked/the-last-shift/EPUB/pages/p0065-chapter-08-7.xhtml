Aur jab patterns dikhne lagte hain…

toh blame automatically fade ho jaata hai.

Phir ek aur stage aata hai.

Acceptance ka nahi…

observation ka.

Tum cheezein fix karna band kar dete ho.

Tum sirf notice karte ho.

Aur yeh shift bahut quiet hota hai.

No celebration.

No relief.

Bas ek simple change:

Reaction slow ho jaati hai.

Pause grow ho jaata hai.

Aur shayad isi pause mein clarity grow karti hai.

Aur phir ek final realization aata hai:

Main kisi aur ko lose nahi kar raha tha…

main khud ko misread kar raha tha.

Aur jab yeh samajh aata hai…

tab pehli baar story end nahi hoti.

Bas uska perspective change hota hai.
