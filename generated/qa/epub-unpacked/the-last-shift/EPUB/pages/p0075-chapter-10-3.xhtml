shayad problem duniya nahi thi…

problem tumhara resistance tha.

Kuch cheezein fix karne ke liye hoti hi nahi…

sirf feel karne ke liye hoti hain.

Par hum unhe solve karne lagte hain…

aur wahi se sab aur heavy ho jaata hai.

Aur jab tum unhe bas dekhna shuru karte ho…

toh woh dheere dheere apni jagah par settle hone lagti hain.

Aur us settling ke andar ek ajeeb si peace hoti hai…

jo shor mein kabhi notice hi nahi hui thi.

Phir samajh aata hai…

peace koi achievement nahi hai.

woh control chhodne ka byproduct hai.

Par control chhodna easy nahi hota.

Kyuki control ek illusion deta hai…

ki tum safe ho.

Aur insaan us illusion ko hi safety samajh leta hai.

Par life dheere dheere us illusion ko todti hai…

situations se.

people se.
