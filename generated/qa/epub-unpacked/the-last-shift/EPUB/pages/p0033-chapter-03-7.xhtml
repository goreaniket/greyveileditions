Woh exhaustion jahan tum kisi ko hold bhi nahi kar paate…

aur chhod bhi nahi paate.

Aur tab samajh aata hai ki kuch connections force se nahi chal sakte.

Jo naturally grow karte hain unhe prove nahi karna padta.

Aur jo constantly proof maangte hain… woh sabse pehle thak jaate hain.

Shayad yahi difference hota hai fear aur awareness mein.

Fear hold karna chahta hai.

Awareness release karna sikha deti hai.

Fear control dhoondhta hai.

Awareness acceptance.

Aur shayad healing wahi se start hoti hai—

jab tum kisi aur ko lose karne se pehle…

khud ke paas wapas aanas start kar dete ho.
