Not giving up…

but stopping unnecessary holding.

Kam holding.

Kam forcing.

Kam explaining.

Kam proving.

Aur achanak life thodi jagah de deti hai saans lene ke liye.

Ek chai jo sirf chai lagti hai, ritual nahi.

Ek walk jisme destination nahi hota.

Ek conversation jisme silence awkward nahi hota.

Aur yeh sab pehli baar “small” nahi lagta…

yeh sab enough lagta hai.

Kyuki tab samajh aata hai ki richness kabhi loud nahi thi…

woh quiet thi.

Aur humne quiet cheezon ko hamesha ignore kiya.

Phir ek aur realization aata hai:

hum future ko handle karne mein itne busy ho jaate hain…

ki present humse bina permission ke nikal jaata hai.
