Par interesting baat yeh hoti hai — kuch bhi suddenly missing nahi hota.

Bas response kam ho jaata hai.

Kabhi kabhi tum phone uthate ho.

Naam scroll hota hai.

Ek second rukte ho.

Pehle yahan se ek feeling start hoti thi —

ab sirf ek pause hota hai.

Phir tum normal ho jaate ho.

Jaise kuch hua hi nahi.

Aur yahi wo jagah hoti hai jahan tum pehli baar notice karte ho:

Tum pehle jaise react nahi kar rahe.

Lekin tumhe abhi bhi lagta hai ki tum same ho.

Messages aate hain.

Tum dekhte ho.

Reply bhi kar dete ho.

Par andar kuch nahi uthta.

Na curiosity.

Na urgency.

Na attachment ka pull.
