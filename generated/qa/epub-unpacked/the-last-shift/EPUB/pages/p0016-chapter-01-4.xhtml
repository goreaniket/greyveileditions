Unplanned check-ins.

Aur woh messages jo shayad kisi aur ke liye kuch bhi nahi hote… lekin aapke liye thode zyada ho jaate hain.

Aur dheere dheere “ordinary” ka meaning change hone lagta hai.

Woh cheezein jo pehle neutral thi… ab emotional weight carry karne lagti hain.

Aur insaan ko pata bhi nahi chalta ki kab woh transition ho gaya.

Kyuki outwardly kuch badalta nahi.

Sab kuch same rehta hai.

Bas andar ek subtle imbalance create ho jaata hai.

Phir ek din kuch chhota sa trigger hota hai.

Ek dry reply.

Ek late response.

Ek unread message.

Ya bas ek silence jo pehle exist karta tha par notice nahi hota tha.
