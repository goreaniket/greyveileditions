Introduction

Strange how humans work, na…

Puri life connection dhundte rehte hain—someone who finally feels like home.

Aisa koi jiske saath silence bhi heavy na lage.

Jiske saath existence explain karni na pade.

Bas feel ho ki “haan, yahin theek hai.”

But the moment we actually get attached to someone, something changes quietly.

Not outside. Inside.

Fear enters… silently.

Fear of losing them.

Fear of being replaced.

Fear of caring more than the other person does.

Fear of being the only one who feels deeply.
