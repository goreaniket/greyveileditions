Ek din tum kisi baat par unka opinion lena chahte ho… phir khud hi rok lete ho.

Ek din tum realize karte ho ki jo cheezein pehle automatically share hoti thi… ab unke liye sochna pad raha hai.

Aur yahin se silent shift start hota hai.

Kyuki uske baad tum conversations ko sirf experience nahi karte…

tum unhe analyze karne lagte ho.

Tone kaisi thi.

Interest same hai ya nahi.

Distance real hai ya imagination.

Aur dheere dheere insaan relationship mein kam… aur interpretation mein zyada jeene lagta hai.

Reality wahi rehti hai.

Lekin meaning grow karta rehta hai.

Phir tum khud ko samjhate ho:

“Main overthinking kar raha hoon.”

Aur agle hi pal dimaag kehta hai:

“Ya main sirf woh notice kar raha hoon jo change ho raha hai.”
