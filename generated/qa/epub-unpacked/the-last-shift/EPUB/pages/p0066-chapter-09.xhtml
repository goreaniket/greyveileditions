PHASE VI - THE LAST SHIFT

The Things We Realize Too Late

Kabhi kabhi insaan poori life cheezein collect karta rehta hai.

People. Moments. Words. Promises. Memories. Expectations. Dreams.

Aur usse lagta hai ki woh sab kuch sambhaal raha hai…

jaise kuch bhi kho nahi sakta.

Par dheere dheere life ek simple sa truth sikhati hai:

Kuch cheezein sambhaalne ke liye hoti hi nahi hain.

Bachpan mein sab permanent lagta hai.

School. Friends. Home. People. Routine.

Sab kuch ek fixed structure jaisa lagta hai.
