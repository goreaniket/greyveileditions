PHASE V - AWARENESS

The Mind Questions Itself

Kabhi kabhi insaan ek din mein nahi badalta.

Woh na hi ek moment mein toot-ta hai.

Woh dheere dheere apne hi andar jaagta hai.

Aur yeh awakening peaceful nahi hoti.

Yeh confusing hoti hai.

Pehle sab kuch simple lagta hai.

Koi pasand aata hai → attention jaati hai

Koi baat hoti hai → feel hota hai

Koi silence hota hai → thoda disturb hota hai

Life ek straight line jaisi lagti hai.

Phir dheere dheere layers add hoti jaati hain.

Attachment.
