PHASE IV - VOID

Emotional Shutdown

Emotional shutdown kabhi ek din mein nahi hota.

Na hi ek dramatic moment hota hai jahan insaan decide kare ki ab woh feel nahi karega.

Yeh ek slow collapse hota hai.

Itna slow ki tumhe pehle lagta hai tum bas “thak” rahe ho.

Phir lagta hai tum “cope” kar rahe ho.

Phir lagta hai tum “normal” ho rahe ho.

Par asal mein tum dheere dheere kam feel karna start kar dete ho.

Pehle har cheez andar tak jaati thi.

Ek message.
