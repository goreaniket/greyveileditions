“Main change ho gaya hoon?”

“Ya bas thak gaya hoon feel karte karte?”

Par koi answer nahi aata.

Sirf silence.

Detachment ka ye phase bahar se invisible hota hai.

Log kehte hain tum normal ho.

Tum baat karte ho.

Smile kar lete ho.

Function kar rahe hote ho.

Par andar ek cheez slowly shift ho chuki hoti hai:

Tum cheezon ke around ghoomna band kar dete ho.

Aur jab mind ghoomna band karta hai…

toh emotions apni jagah se hil jaate hain.

Kabhi kabhi tum kisi ka naam dekhte ho.

Aur pehle jaisa “pull” nahi hota.

Bas ek recognition hota hai.

Jaise kisi purani cheez ko dekh rahe ho jiska effect ab tum par nahi hai.

Aur yeh absence tumhe pehle notice hoti hai.

Phir normal lagne lagti hai.

Phir routine ban jaati hai.
