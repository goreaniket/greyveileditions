Par dheere dheere ek cheez change hoti hai:

Tum apne emotions ko name dena band kar dete ho.

Pehle tum clearly jaante the:

“Main sad hoon.”

“Main angry hoon.”

“Main excited hoon.”

Phir ek point aata hai jahan bas ek word reh jaata hai:

“Okay nahi lag raha.”

Bas itna.

Aur jab emotions unclear ho jaate hain,

toh insaan khud ke liye bhi unclear ho jaata hai.

Kabhi kisi ne pucha hai:

“Tu theek hai?”

Aur tum pause kar dete ho.

Answer dhoondhne ke liye nahi…

feel dhoondhne ke liye.

Par andar kuch milta hi nahi jo clearly bol sake.

Yahi pehli real fracture hoti hai.

Jab insaan apne hi andar se feedback lena band kar deta hai.
