“Main lazy hoon kya?”

“Ya main change ho gaya hoon?”

Par dono answer ek saath true lagte hain.

Kabhi kabhi tum mirror ke saamne khade hote ho.

Apne aap ko dekhte ho.

Aur ek second ke liye lagta hai:

“Ye main hi hoon na?”

Par recognition delay ho jaata hai.

Self-distance ka sabse silent part yahi hai:

Tum apne hi reflection ko familiar feel nahi karte.

Aur jab familiarity kam hoti hai,

toh comfort bhi kam ho jaata hai.

Aur jab comfort kam hota hai,

toh connection khud se break hone lagta hai.

Phir tum distractions ki taraf jaate ho.

Phone. Scroll. Noise. People. Anything.

Bas ek pause chahiye hota hai apne andar se.

Kyuki andar jab silence loud ho jaati hai,

toh bahar ka noise zaroori lagne lagta hai.

Par problem yeh hoti hai:

Noise sirf temporarily distract karti hai.
