PHASE II - DISTANCE

Fear of Losing

Fear of losing ek sudden feeling nahi hota.

Yeh dheere dheere build hota hai.

Yeh tab start hota hai jab tum kisi ko “important” bolne se pehle hi unko apni routine ka part bana chuke hote ho—bina is realization ke ki ab tumhara normal unki presence se quietly depend karne laga hai.

Phir ek point aata hai jahan tumhe khud bhi notice nahi hota, aur tum kisi ke replies, tone, aur presence ke hisaab se apna mood adjust karne lagte ho.

Aur shuruaat mein yeh dependency normal lagti hai.

Kyuki kuch bhi officially defined nahi hota.

Lekin problem yahi hoti hai—
