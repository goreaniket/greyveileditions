Woh ek slow accumulation hoti hai—experiences ka, losses ka, realizations ka.

Pehle hum cheezein feel karte hain bina soche.

Phir hum har cheez ko samajhne lagte hain.

Aur ek point ke baad hum sirf observe karte hain… bina react kiye.

Aur yahi woh jagah hoti hai jahan insaan khud ko pehli baar clearly dekh pata hai.

Ki kis cheez ne usse change kiya.

Kaun si baatein uske andar reh gayi.

Kaun se moments uske andar quietly settle ho gaye.

Phir ek din insaan rukta hai.

Kisi reason se nahi.

Kisi event ki wajah se nahi.

Bas ek internal fatigue ki wajah se.

Woh fatigue jo dikhai nahi deta…

par har thought ke neeche chhupa hota hai.

Aur us silence mein pehli baar clarity nahi aati…

pehli baar acceptance aati hai.

Ki har cheez ko hold karna zaroori nahi tha.

Ki har connection permanent nahi hota.

Ki har feeling ka last hona bhi normal hai.
