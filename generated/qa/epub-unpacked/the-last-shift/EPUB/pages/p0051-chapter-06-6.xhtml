Aur isi beech ek aur change hota hai:

Tum logon se kam expect karte ho.

Kam explain karte ho.

Kam react karte ho.

Not because kuch problem hai…

but kyuki energy nahi bachti.

Log kehte hain tum distant ho gaye ho.

Par andar sach simple hota hai:

Tum emotionally overdrawn ho chuke ho.

Aur yeh overdrawn state visible nahi hoti.

Isliye explain bhi nahi hoti.

Kabhi kabhi tum sochte ho:

“Main jee raha hoon ya sirf function kar raha hoon?”

Par question bhi dull lagta hai.

Aur yeh dullness hi emotional shutdown ka peak hai.

Jahan pain bhi sharp nahi rehta…

sirf background noise ban jaata hai.

Aur phir ek din kuch subtle change hota hai:

Tum silence se fight karna band kar dete ho.

Na tum usse fill karte ho.

Na tum usse run karte ho.
