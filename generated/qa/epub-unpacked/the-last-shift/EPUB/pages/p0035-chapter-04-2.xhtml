phir ruk jaate ho.

Sochte ho — “bhejun ya nahi?”

Aur phir delete kar dete ho.

Kisi ka naam screen pe aata hai…

aur tum us moment par kuch feel nahi karte…

bas ek automatic pause hota hai.

Kabhi kabhi gaana chal raha hota hai…

jo pehle tumhe instantly hit karta tha…

aur ab woh bas background noise ban jaata hai.

Aur tum samajh nahi paate ki change hua kya hai.

Kyuki kuch “toota” nahi hota.

Bas kuch “respond” karna dheere dheere kam kar deta hai.

Shuruaat mein tum wait karte ho.

Reply ka.

Attention ka.

Clarity ka.

Phir tum overthink karte ho.

Har chhoti silence ka meaning nikalne lagta hai.

Har late reply ek hidden signal lagta hai.

Aur phir tum explain karne lagte ho
