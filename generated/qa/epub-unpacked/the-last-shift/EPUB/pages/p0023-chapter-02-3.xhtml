“It’s just friendship.”

Aur shayad dono log is baat ko sach bhi maante hain.

Lekin andar kuch cheezein normal nahi rehti.

Online aane ka wait notice hone lagta hai.

Chat khol ke bina message ke bhi dekhna habit ban jaata hai.

Ek dry reply ko baar baar padhna aur usme hidden meaning dhoondhna.

Aur har baar ek chhota sa question repeat hota hai

“Sab theek hai na?”

Woh cheezein jo pehle casual thi… ab emotional weight lene lagti hain.

Aur yahi silent attachment ka contradiction hai—

bahar se sab same, andar se sab change.

Ek dusre ki mood shifts notice hone lagti hain.

Silence notice hone lagti hai.

Energy notice hone lagti hai.

Aur phir bhi pretend kiya jaata hai ki kuch affect nahi ho raha.

Kyuki kuch bhi officially exist nahi karta.

Aur jab kuch officially exist nahi karta…
