toh usse justify karna bhi possible nahi hota.

Is phase mein insaan ek ajeeb emotional state mein hota hai

Jealousy hoti hai, lekin right nahi hota.

Expectations hoti hain, lekin agreement nahi hota.

Pain hota hai, lekin explanation nahi hota.

Aur shayad sabse confusing part yahi hai—

kabhi kabhi dono log same cheezein feel kar rahe hote hain…

par dono hi chup rehte hain.

Ek isliye ki connection na toot jaye.

Aur dusra isliye ki rejection ka risk na ho.

Aur isi silence ke beech confusion grow karta rehta hai.

Pehle confusion manageable lagti hai.

Phir woh routine ban jaati hai.

Aur phir dheere dheere woh mentally exhausting lagne lagti hai.

Jis person ko clarity mil jaati hai… woh stable ho jaata hai.

Lekin jiske paas sirf mixed signals hote hain…
