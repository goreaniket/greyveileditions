“Main itna thak kyun gaya hoon?”

Aur is baar jawab kisi aur ke baare mein nahi hota.

Kabhi kabhi awareness ka sabse bada shift yahi hota hai:

Tum questions poochna band nahi karte…

tum direction change kar dete ho.

Pehle sawaal yeh hota tha:

“Unhone aisa kyun kiya?”

Ab sawaal yeh hota hai:

“Maine isse itna deeply kyun le liya?”

Aur yeh shift subtle hota hai.

Par powerful hota hai.

Kyuki pehli baar focus bahar se andar shift hota hai.

Aur jab focus andar aata hai…

toh story change ho jaati hai.

Phir tum realize karte ho:

Main sirf unhe samajhne ki koshish nahi kar raha tha…

main apne emotions ko bhi samajhne se bach raha tha.

Aur shayad yahi awareness ka uncomfortable truth hai:

Hum dusron ko tab tak blame karte hain

jab tak hum apne patterns ko face nahi kar lete.
