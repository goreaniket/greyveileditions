Woh bas thoda sa interesting lagta hai.

Woh bas ek aisi presence hoti hai jisme kuch bhi threatening nahi lagta.

Jaise koi aadat jo apne aap daily routine mein ghul jaati hai… aur aapko pata bhi nahi chalta ki woh aadat ban chuki hai.

Aap notice nahi karte ki kab aapka mind kisi ek insaan ki availability ko assume karne laga hai.

Kab aapka mood unke response ke around subtly shape hone laga hai.

Kab aap unki presence ko “good part of the day” samajhne lage ho—without realizing it.

Aur kab unki absence…

thodi zyada noticeable ho gayi hai.

Even when you act like it doesn’t matter.

Strange thing yeh hai ki attachment kabhi ek din mein strong nahi hoti.

Woh ek accumulation hota hai.

Chhoti chhoti baatein.

Ordinary conversations.

Random jokes.
