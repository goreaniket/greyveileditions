Situations ko bhi.

Even yourself ko bhi.

Aur yeh silence clarity nahi hoti pehle.

Yeh confusion hoti hai without direction.

Par dheere dheere isi confusion ke andar ek pattern dikhne lagta hai.

Tum notice karte ho:

Main hamesha kisi na kisi cheez ko hold karne ki koshish karta hoon.

Main hamesha kisi na kisi outcome ko control karna chahta hoon.

Main hamesha uncertainty se bhaagta hoon.

Aur yeh realization uncomfortable hota hai.

Kyuki problem bahar nahi hoti…

andar hoti hai.

Phir kabhi kabhi ek moment aata hai.

Bilkul normal sa moment.

Tum khidki ke bahar dekh rahe hote ho.

Ya ek conversation khatam hoti hai.

Ya raat bilkul silent hoti hai.
