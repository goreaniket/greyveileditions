Tab samajh aata hai ki attachment sirf love nahi hota.

Kabhi kabhi yeh emotional dependency hoti hai

jo comfort ke naam pe quietly grow karti rehti hai.

Hum sab kehte hain — “Don’t get too attached.”

Par sach yeh hai ki insaan completely detached rehne ke liye bana hi nahi hai.

Hum connect karte hain.

Hum feel karte hain.

Hum hold karte hain.

Kyuki deep down, everyone is just looking for someone

jo temporary na lage…

jo moment na lage…

jo safe lage.

Someone who feels like peace…

not like a phase.
