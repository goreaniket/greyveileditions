Author's Note

This book is not about one person.

It is not about one relationship.

It is not about one ending.

It is about the silent psychological shifts that happen

inside people when attachment, distance, expectations,

loneliness, and self-awareness slowly reshape the way they

experience life.

Some chapters may feel familiar.

Some may feel uncomfortable.

Some may feel like they were written from your own

thoughts.

If that happens, remember:

You are not reading this book to find answers.

You are reading it to observe the questions that were

already there.

Welcome to The Last Shift.
