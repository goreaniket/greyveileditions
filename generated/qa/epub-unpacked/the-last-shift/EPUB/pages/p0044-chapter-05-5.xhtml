Detachment ek loud feeling nahi hai.

Woh ek slow reduction hai.

Reaction ka.

Expectation ka.

Interpretation ka.

Emotional dependency ka.

Aur isi reduction ke beech tum apne aap ko ek aur angle se dekhte ho:

Tum kam nahi ho rahe…

tum heavy cheezein carry karna band kar rahe ho.

Par us time par ye relief nahi lagta.

Ye bas empty lagta hai.

Kabhi kabhi tum sochte ho:

“Main free hoon ya bas kuch feel nahi kar raha?”

Aur dono answers ek jaise lagte hain.

Pehle silence uncomfortable hoti thi.

Ab silence normal lagti hai.

Aur kabhi kabhi… silence thodi peaceful bhi lagne lagti hai.

Aur yeh wahi point hota hai jahan detachment apna real shape leta hai:
