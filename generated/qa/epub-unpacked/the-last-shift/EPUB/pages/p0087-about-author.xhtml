About the Author

Lalit R. Mishra is a writer focused on the psychology of

human behavior, attachment, self-awareness, emotional

patterns, and the silent transformations that often go

unnoticed within everyday life.

His work explores the space between thought and feeling—

the questions people carry quietly, the connections they

struggle to understand, and the internal shifts that shape

who they eventually become.

Rather than providing conclusions, his writing invites

readers to observe their own experiences through a deeper

psychological lens, uncovering the patterns hidden beneath

attachment, distance, loss, growth, and self-discovery.

The Last Shift is his debut work and the first installment in

an ongoing exploration of the human mind and its many

layers.

Because sometimes the longest journey is not towards

someone else

but back to yourself.

— Lalit R. Mishra
