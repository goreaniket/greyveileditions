Ek delay.

Ek silence.

Ek tone.

Sab kuch trigger karta tha kuch na kuch.

Phir ek phase aata hai jahan trigger hona hi kam ho jaata hai.

Message aata hai… tum dekhte ho…

aur response mechanical ho jaata hai.

Na excitement.

Na irritation.

Na curiosity.

Bas read.

Bas exist.

Aur yahi pehli confusion hoti hai.

“Main theek hoon?”

“Ya main bas feel karna band kar raha hoon?”

Par dono ka difference samajh nahi aata kyuki symptoms same lagte hain.

Dheere dheere tum notice karte ho:

Wohi gaane jo pehle repeat pe chalte the… ab complete ho jaate hain bina kuch feel karaye.
