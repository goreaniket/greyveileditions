Part II - Second Layer (Teaser)

Agar tumhe lagta hai yeh journey khatam ho gayi…

toh tum galat ho.

Kyuki abhi ek layer baaki hai.

Woh jo attachment se bhi zyada deep hai…

aur awareness se bhi zyada uncomfortable.

Jahan sawal logon ke baare mein nahi…

khud reality ke baare mein hota hai.

Aur jab tum wahan pahunchte ho…

toh tumhe jawab nahi milte.

Sirf ek aur question milta hai:

“Agar yeh sab main nahi hoon… toh phir main hoon kya?” ye end of part -1 ke baad lagana
