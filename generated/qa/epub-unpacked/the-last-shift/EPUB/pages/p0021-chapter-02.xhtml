PHASE I - ATTACHMENT

Silent Attachment

Silent attachment ka start itna normal hota hai ki insaan ko pata hi nahi chalta ki kuch change ho raha hai.

Koi announcement nahi hoti.

Koi label nahi hota.

Koi clear moment nahi hota jahan aap bol sako ki “ab yeh kuch aur hai.”

Bas do log baat karte rehte hain…

aur dheere dheere baat karna hi ek habit ban jaata hai.

Pehle conversations light hoti hain.

Random. Casual. Easy.
