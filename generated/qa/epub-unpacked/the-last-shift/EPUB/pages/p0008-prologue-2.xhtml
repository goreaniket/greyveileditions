Aur bina realize kiye mind ek continuous internal activity mein chala jaata hai.

Shuruaat mein yeh sab normal lagta hai.

Lagta hai jaise bas thoda zyada aware ho gaya hoon, that’s it.

Par dheere dheere yeh awareness background noise ban jaati hai.

Soch rukti nahi.

Even jab kuch happening nahi hota.

Same moments baar baar mind mein replay hote hain.

Same conversations different angles se analyze hoti rehti hain.

Aur yahan se ek invisible fatigue start hota hai…

jo dikhai nahi deta, par feel zaroor hota hai.

Neend hoti hai, par rest nahi hota.

Din hota hai, par presence nahi hoti.

Aur sabse strange shift yeh hota hai…

insaan duniya ko samajhne se pehle khud ko observe karna start karta hai.

Woh apne thoughts ko dekh raha hota hai…

apni feelings ko decode kar raha hota hai…

aur kabhi kabhi apni hi silence ko question kar raha hota hai.

“Main aisa kyun feel kar raha hoon?”
