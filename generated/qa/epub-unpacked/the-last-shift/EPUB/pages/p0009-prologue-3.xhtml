“Main kuch zyada soch raha hoon ya kuch sach mein change ho raha hai?”

Aur jab yeh layer activate hoti hai…

toh change bahar visible nahi hota.

Lekin andar ek quiet shift start ho chuka hota hai.

Slow.

Invisible.

But irreversible.

Aur yahin se asli journey shuru hoti hai.

Not outside.

Inside.
