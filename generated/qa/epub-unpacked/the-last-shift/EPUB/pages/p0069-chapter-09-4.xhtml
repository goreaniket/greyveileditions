toh lagta hai zindagi tez nikal gayi.

Par shayad zindagi tez nahi thi…

hum usme poori tarah the hi nahi.

Hum ya toh plan kar rahe the… ya yaad kar rahe the.

Beech ka jo “abhi” tha… woh quietly slip ho gaya.

Phir ek shift aata hai.

Attention. Validation. Outcomes. Control. Certainty.

Shuru mein yeh sab zaroori lagta hai.

Jaise yahi life ka centre hai.

Par dheere dheere ek strange fatigue aata hai…

ki jitna control karne ki koshish karo, utna hi life aur unpredictable ho jaati hai.

Aur ek din realization simple ho jaata hai:

shor kam hone lagta hai, par andar ka pressure badh jata hai.

Aur us pressure ke neeche ek cheez hamesha hoti hai—thakan.

Phir wahi thakan clarity ban jaati hai.

Aur insaan dheere dheere samajhta hai ki jeetna zaroori nahi tha…

sirf chhodna seekhna zaroori tha.
