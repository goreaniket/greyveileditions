par ab woh loud nahi hota.

Woh bas ek distant echo hota hai…

“kaash main pehle samajh jaata.”

Par ab tum usse ladte nahi…

tum usse dekhte ho.

Kyuki ab tumhe change nahi chahiye…

tumhe understanding chahiye.

Aur understanding hi healing ban jaati hai.

Aur dheere dheere ek last shift hota hai…

Tumhe sab answers nahi milte…

par tumhe sab questions ki urgency khatam ho jaati hai.

Aur yeh clarity loud nahi hoti…

yeh calm hoti hai.

Yahan kuch prove karna nahi rehta.

Kuch chase karna nahi rehta.

Kuch fix karna nahi rehta.

Bas ek simple realization bachta hai:

life ko samajhna kabhi goal tha hi nahi…

usko jeena hi asli baat thi.

Aur shayad isi ko kehte hain…
