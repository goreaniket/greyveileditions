Problem yeh thi ki humne questions ko hi apni identity bana liya.

Ek waqt ke baad insaan realize karta hai ki har cheez ko hold karna zaroori nahi hota.

Na logon ko. Na moments ko. Na un versions ko jo hum the ya banne ki koshish kar rahe the.

Kuch log aate hain life mein sirf ek phase complete karne ke liye.

Woh hume kuch feel karwate hain jo hum khud se kabhi nahi mil paate.

Aur phir quietly chale jaate hain — bina explanation ke, bina closure ke.

Pehle yeh incompleteness bahut heavy lagti hai.

Lagta hai kuch galat ho gaya, kuch adhura reh gaya.

Par dheere dheere samajh aata hai ki har cheez ka complete hona zaroori nahi hota.

Kuch cheezein sirf shape dene ke liye hoti hain, permanently rehne ke liye nahi.

Aur jab hum control chhodna seekhte hain, tab ek strange si lightness aati hai.

Jaise pehli baar hum apni hi zindagi ko breathe karne dete hain.

Na sab kuch perfect hota hai, na sab kuch clear hota hai.

Par phir bhi ek silence hoti hai — jo pehle kabhi nahi thi.
