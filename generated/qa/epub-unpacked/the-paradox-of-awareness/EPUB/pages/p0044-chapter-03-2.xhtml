PART I — THE SPLIT

Pehle insaan ek hota hai.

Sochta hai → karta hai → jeeta hai.

Phir awareness aati hai…

Aur insaan do ho jaata hai:

Ek jo jee raha hota hai.

Aur ek jo usse dekh raha hota hai.

Example:

Tum kuch galat bol dete ho…

aur turant sochte ho: “Maine aisa kyun bola?”

Tum hass rahe hote ho…

aur ek part kehta hai: “Main sach mein khush hoon ya bas react kar raha hoon?”

Aur dheere dheere simple cheezein complex ho jaati hain.

PART II — THE LOSS OF NATURAL SELF

Ek bachcha girta hai → rota hai → uth jaata hai.

Koi analysis nahi.

Ek adult girta hai → sochta hai → judge karta hai → repeat karta hai → freeze ho jaata hai.

Difference skill ka nahi hai.
