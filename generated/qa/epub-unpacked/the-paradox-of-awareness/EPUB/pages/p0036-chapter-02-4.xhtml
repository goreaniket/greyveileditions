Aur yahin ek invisible death hoti hai.

Not physical.

Simple living ki death.

Aur hum isse “maturity” bol dete hain.

PART III — THE CURSE OF UNDERSTANDING PEOPLE

Pehle lagta tha — “Woh accha hai ya bura hai.”

Ab lagta hai — “Woh aisa kyun hai?”

Aur “kyun” kabhi end nahi hota.

Ek real life moment:

Tum kisi insaan se hurt hote ho.

Pehle tum usse hate karte ho.

Phir tum samajhne lagte ho ki usne aisa kyun kiya.

Phir tum confuse ho jaate ho — blame karein ya understand?

Aur yahi phase insaan ko tod deta hai.

Mahabharat mein bhi yahi conflict tha —

Arjun ko problem war se nahi thi…

problem samajh se thi.

Usko sab dikhta tha — dharm bhi, adharm bhi, apne bhi, saamne wale bhi.

Aur jab sab dikhta hai… decision heavy ho jaata hai.
