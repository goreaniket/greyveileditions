jab sab cheezein samajh aa chuki hoti hain.

Log. Patterns. Emotions. Reactions.

Sab explainable.

Sab readable.

Sab known.

Aur us moment mein…

kuch bhi naya nahi bachta.

Na confusion.

Na clarity ka excitement.

Bas ek raw emptiness hoti hai…

jo kisi thought mein fit nahi hoti.

FINAL LINE — THE SHIFT THAT DOESN’T HAVE WORDS

Aur shayad sabse ajeeb realization yeh hai:

ki jab tum sab kuch samajh lete ho…

tab tumhe pehli baar lagta hai ki tum kisi cheez ko jee hi nahi rahe the.

Tum sirf usko interpret kar rahe the.

Aur jab interpretation ruk jaata hai…

toh jo bachta hai…

woh zindagi hoti hai.

Bina explanation ke.
