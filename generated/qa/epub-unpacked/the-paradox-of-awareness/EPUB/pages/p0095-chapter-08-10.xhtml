Books padhne mein.

Podcasts sunne mein.

Opinions collect karne mein.

Lekin amateur reh jaate ho...

khud ko sunne mein.

Aur yahin paradox paida hota hai:

Jitna zyada tum guidance collect karte ho...

utna hi kam tum apni direction par trust karte ho.

ENDING — THE LONG WAY BACK HOME

Shayad self-trust ka matlab yeh nahi hai ki tum hamesha sahi decision loge.

Shayad self-trust ka matlab yeh hai...

ki galat hone ki possibility ke saath bhi tum apne saath khade rahoge.

Kyuki life ka sabse bada loss failure nahi hota.

Sabse bada loss yeh hota hai...

ki ek din tum khud ko itna doubt kar do...

ki tumhari andar ki awaaz bolna band kar de.

Aur shayad isi liye...

ghar wapas aana koi jagah nahi hoti.

Ghar wapas aana woh moment hota hai...
