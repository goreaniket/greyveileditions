PART III — THE MEMORY OF BEING WRONG

Problem ek mistake nahi hoti.

Problem us mistake ki memory hoti hai.

Ek baar trust kiya.

Hurt hue.

Ek baar risk liya.

Fail hue.

Ek baar believe kiya.

Disappoint hue.

Aur phir dimaag ek dangerous conclusion nikal leta hai:

"Dekha? Apne aap par trust karoge toh yahi hoga."
