THE PARADOX OF AWARENESS

THE THING THAT REMAINS WHEN EVERYTHING IS UNDERSTOOD

OPENING — WHEN SILENCE STARTS REPLACING QUESTIONS

Kabhi kabhi aisa hota hai…

ki tum kisi cheez ka jawab dhoondh rahe hote ho.

Aur dhoondhte dhoondhte…

tumhe yaad bhi nahi rehta ki sawaal kya tha.

Aur phir ek din…

tumhe lagta hai jawab mil gaya.

Par kuch badalta nahi.

Na andar.

Na bahar.

Bas ek strange stillness aa jaati hai…

jaise mind ne apna kaam band kar diya ho.
