“Main control mein hoon.”

Reality:

Thoughts bina permission aate hain.

Emotions bina reason trigger hote hain.

Memory random time pe attack karti hai.

Aur jab nahi hota…

“Toh main weak hoon.”

Lekin shayad weakness nahi hai.

Shayad problem yeh hai ki hum ocean ko haath mein hold karna chahte hain.

Mind ko control karna…

jaise weather ko command karna.
