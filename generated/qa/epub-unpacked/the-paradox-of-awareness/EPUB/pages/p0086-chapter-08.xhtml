THE PARADOX OF AWARENESS

THE MOMENT YOU STOP TRUSTING YOURSELF

OPENING — THE BETRAYAL NOBODY TALKS ABOUT

Kabhi socha hai?

Life mein sabse painful betrayal kaunsa hota hai?

Kisi dost ka?

Kisi partner ka?

Kisi apne ka?

Shayad nahi.

Shayad sabse dangerous betrayal woh hota hai...

jab tum dheere dheere khud ko chhodna shuru kar dete ho.

Aur ajeeb baat yeh hai:

Yeh ek din mein nahi hota.
