PART V :

MAHABHARAT — WHEN SEEING EVERYTHING DESTROYS ACTION

Arjun ka sabse dangerous moment war nahi tha.

Woh moment tha jab usne sab kuch clearly dekh liya tha.

apne bhi

paraye bhi

dharm bhi

adharm bhii

Aur jab sab ek saath valid lagne lage…

toh action impossible ho jaata hai.

Kyuki ignorance tumhe chalne deti hai…

lekin clarity tumhe rok deti hai.

Krishna ne usse answer nahi diya.

usne usse reality ka weight diya.

“Samajhna enough nahi hota. Karna hi truth hota hai.”
