Jo hota tha, woh bas hota tha.

Aur jo feel hota tha, usko explain karne ki zarurat nahi padti thi.

Dukh aata tha — dukh hota tha.

Khushi aati thi — khushi hoti thi.

Aur life kisi meaning ke peeche nahi bhaagti thi.

Phir insaan ne ek kaam kiya.

Usne jeena nahi chhoda…

bas jeete hue sochna shuru kar diya.

Aur yahi woh invisible fracture tha jahan reality do hisso mein toot gayi:

Ek taraf jo ho raha tha

Dusri taraf jo dimaag uske baare mein bana raha tha

Aur problem yeh hai…

dimaag ka version hamesha zyada loud hota hai.

Awareness ko hum gift kehte hain.

Aur woh hai bhi.

Par koi yeh nahi batata ki awareness sirf roshni nahi badhati…

woh contrast bhi badha deti hai.

Aur jahan contrast badh jaata hai…

wahan simple cheezein complex lagne lagti hain.
