lekin tum us room mein nahi ho.

Tum kisi conversation mein ho…

lekin tum reply mein nahi ho.

Tum present mein ho…

par mentally tum future ke courtroom mein khade ho.

Apne hi case ki hearing chal rahi hai.

Aur judge bhi tum ho.

Aur accused bhi tum.

PART V — THE TRAP OF “IF I KNOW, I’LL BE SAFE”

Insaan ka sabse bada illusion:

“Agar mujhe sab samajh aa gaya… toh main safe ho jaunga.”

Par truth ulta hai.

Jitna zyada tum samajhte ho…

utna zyada tum possibilities dekhte ho.

Aur jitni zyada possibilities…

utna zyada fear.

Awareness tumhe safe nahi banati.
