Ambition wala “main”

Aur interesting baat?

Har layer dusri ko contradict karti hai.

Tum kisi ko hurt nahi karna chahte…

par kabhi kabhi tum harsh ho jaate ho.

Tum disciplined banna chahte ho…

par tum procrastinate karte ho.

Aur har baar tum kehte ho: “Main aisa hi hoon.”

Lekin kaunsa wala?

PART III — THE SOCIAL EDITING OF SELF

Dheere dheere insaan ek skill seekh leta hai:

editing himself for survival.

Har situation mein tum apne aap ko thoda adjust karte ho.

Yahan soft

Wahan strong

Yahan funny

Wahan serious

Aur ek din tumhe yaad hi nahi rehta ki original version kaisa tha.

Tum jeete nahi…
