Lekin agar dhyaan se dekho...

Toh poori human history isi pal se nikli hai.

Science.

Philosophy.

Religion.

Art.

Poetry.

Civilizations.

Sab ek hi cheez ki santaan hain.

Awareness.

Lekin yahan ek ajeeb baat hai.

Hum awareness ko hamesha gift ke roop mein dekhte hain.

Aur shayad sahi bhi hai.

Awareness ke bina hum seekh nahi sakte.

Badal nahi sakte.

Grow nahi kar sakte.

Lekin ek sawaal hai jo kam log poochte hain.

Agar awareness itni badi gift hai...

Toh phir duniya ke sabse zyada aware log aksar sabse zyada pareshan kyu dikhte hain?
