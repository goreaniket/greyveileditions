Pehle life surprise thi.

Ab life predictable lagti hai.

Aur jab surprise khatam hota hai:

excitement slow ho jaati hai

reactions muted ho jaate hain

moments same lagne lagte hain

Aur tum sochte ho:

“main mature ho raha hoon”

Par kabhi kabhi sach yeh hota hai:

 tum mature nahi ho rahe… tum numb ho rahe ho

Shayad sabse uncomfortable truth yeh hai:

Jitna zyada tum samajhte ho…

utna zyada tum kam jeete ho.

Aur jitna zyada tum dekhte ho…

utna zyada tum door ho jaate ho.

Aur yeh paradox simple nahi hai:

 awareness tumhe truth deti hai

 par truth tumse innocence le leta hai.
