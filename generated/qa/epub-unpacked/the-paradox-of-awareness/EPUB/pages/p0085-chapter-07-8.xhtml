Itni possibilities...

Ki certainty mar gayi.

Trust mar gaya.

Presence mar gayi.

Aur shayad isi liye...

jitna zyada tum samajhte gaye...

utna zyada tum feel karna bhoolte gaye.

Aur wahi se ek naya paradox paida hua:

Kabhi kabhi clarity andhera nahi mitaati.

Kabhi kabhi woh bas usse naam de deti hai.
