jo silence hai… woh bhi tum ho

Toh problem kabhi yeh nahi thi ki kya ho raha hai…

problem yeh thi ki tum usko alag-alag cheez samajh rahe the.

Aur ab sab kuch simple ho jaata hai…

Sab kuch ho raha hai.

Bas ho raha hai.

Na kisi ke liye.

Na kisi against.

Na kisi meaning ke upar.

Sirf flow.

Aur flow ko samajhne ki zarurat hi nahi hoti…

kyunki flow ko rokna possible hi nahi hota.

Aur yeh realization deep isliye nahi hai kyunki yeh powerful hai…

yeh deep isliye hai kyunki yeh kuch naya nahi deta.

Na hope.

Na fear.

Na escape.

Na conclusion.

Bas ek stillness…

jo pehle se thi…

par tumne notice nahi ki thi.
