THE PARADOX OF AWARENESS

THE PARADOX OF IDENTITY

OPENING — WHEN YOU REALIZE YOU ARE NOT ONE PERSON

Kabhi socha hai?

Jis “main” ko tum apna sabse real version samajhte ho…

woh shayad ek fixed cheez hi nahi hai.

Kabhi tum confident hote ho.

Kabhi tum doubtful hote ho.

Kabhi tum ruthless decision le lete ho.

Aur kabhi ek chhoti si baat tumhe tod deti hai.

Phir bhi tum kehte ho: “ye main hoon.”

Lekin agar tum ek hi ho…

toh tum itne different kaise ho?
