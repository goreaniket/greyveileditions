PART IX — THE SECOND PARADOX

Agar “main” fixed nahi hai…

toh hum responsible kaise hain?

Agar har din hum thode different hain…

toh hum kaun hain?

Aur yahi pe story break hoti hai.

Kyuki answer yahan nahi milta.

Sirf question reh jaata hai.

Shayad sabse scary realization yeh nahi hai ki duniya complex hai.

Shayad yeh hai:

Ki jo “main” duniya ko samajhne ki koshish kar raha hai…

woh khud bhi fixed nahi hai.

Aur shayad yahi human experience ka sabse bada paradox hai:

“Tum khud ko dhoondh rahe ho… jabki tum badalte ja rahe ho.”
