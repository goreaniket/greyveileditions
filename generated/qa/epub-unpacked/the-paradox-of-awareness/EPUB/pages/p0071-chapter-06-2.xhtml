PART I — THE MOMENT LIFE STOPS BEING SIMPLE

Ek time hota hai jab life direct hoti hai:

koi has diya → tum has diye

koi chala gaya → tum bhool gaye

koi mila → tum jee liye

Phir ek din kuch change hota hai.

Tum dekhna start karte ho.

Aur dekhna sirf dekhna nahi rehta…

 dekhna = todna ban jaata hai

Har cheez ke peeche ek layer:

intention

psychology

past

hidden meaning

Aur jo duniya pehle “live” thi…

woh ab “decoded” ho jaati hai.

PART II — WHEN YOU START INTERRUPTING YOUR OWN LIFE

Sabse dangerous shift yeh hota hai:

Tum experience nahi karte…

tum apne experience ko observe karte ho.
