Khushi khushi thi.

Aur life… bas life thi.

Koi hidden meaning nahi.

Koi analysis nahi.

Koi second thought nahi.

Phir ek cheez hui.

Insaan ne dekhna nahi chhoda…

par usne dekhte hue sochna shuru kar diya.

Aur yahi woh invisible fracture tha jahan reality split ho gayi:

ek taraf jo ho raha tha

aur dusri taraf jo uske baare mein socha ja raha tha

Aur dheere dheere… soch, experience se zyada real lagne lagi.

Awareness ko hum gift kehte hain.

Kyuki woh hume duniya samajhne deti hai.

Logon ko read karne deti hai.

Aur khud ko dekhne ki ability deti hai.

Par koi yeh nahi batata ki awareness sirf light nahi deti…

woh shadow bhi create karti hai.

Aur jahan shadow badh jaati hai…

wahan simple cheezein simple nahi rehti.

Ek smile ka matlab nikalne lagta hai.
