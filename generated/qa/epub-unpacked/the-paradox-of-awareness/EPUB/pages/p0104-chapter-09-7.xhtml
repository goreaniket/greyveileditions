Situations repeat hoti hain.

Patterns exist karte hain.

Aur yahan ek silent shift hota hai:

Surprise kam ho jaata hai.

Reaction slow ho jaati hai.

Emotion filtered ho jaata hai.

Tum jeete nahi…

tum observe karte ho jeene ko.

Aur jo insaan apni life ko observe karta hai…

woh usse fully touch nahi kar pata.
