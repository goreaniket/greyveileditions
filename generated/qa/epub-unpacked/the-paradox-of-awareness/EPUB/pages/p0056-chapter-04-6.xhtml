PART V — THE INNER CROWD

Ek point pe tum realize karte ho:

Tumhare andar ek insaan nahi… ek crowd hai.

Ek voice bolti hai: “risk le”

Dusri bolti hai: “safe reh”

Ek kehti hai: “sab theek ho jayega”

Ek kehti hai: “sab khatam ho jayega”

Aur tum in sab ko “main” bol dete ho.

Isliye confusion itna natural hai.

Kyuki decision ek mind nahi le raha… multiple versions fight kar rahe hain.

PART VI — MAHABHARAT WITHIN

Arjun ka war sirf bahar nahi tha.

Uske andar bhi ek battlefield tha.

Duty vs Emotion

Love vs Dharma

Fear vs Action

Aur sabse heavy baat:

Krishna usse answer nahi de raha tha… usse clarity ke saath confusion de raha tha.
