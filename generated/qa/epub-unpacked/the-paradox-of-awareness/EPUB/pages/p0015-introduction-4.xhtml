“dekhna” zyada ho jaata hai.

Phir ek aur layer aati hai — pattern ki.

Log predictable lagne lagte hain.

Situations repeat hone lagti hain.

Emotions recognizable ho jaate hain.

Aur jab sab kuch recognizable ho jaata hai…

toh surprise mar jaata hai.

Aur surprise ke marne ke saath ek aur cheez bhi mar jaati hai:

rawness.

Woh rawness jahan kuch bhi explain nahi hota…

sirf feel hota hai.

Aur yeh shift loud nahi hota.

Koi alarm nahi bajta.

Koi breakdown nahi hota.

Insaan normal hi lagta hai.

Woh kaam karta hai.

Woh haste hai.

Woh relationships mein rehta hai.

Woh life “handle” karta hai.

Par andar ek invisible distance develop ho jaata hai…

Life aur mind ke beech.
