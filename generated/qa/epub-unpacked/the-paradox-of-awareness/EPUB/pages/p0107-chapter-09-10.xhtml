“Mujhe aisa lagna chahiye ki main apni life samajh raha hoon.”

Par truth yeh hai:

Tum life ko samajh nahi rahe hote…

Tum bas uske andar jeene ki koshish kar rahe hote ho.

Aur shayad sabse uncomfortable truth yeh hai:

Life tumhe hurt nahi karti.

Life sirf tumhari expected version ko gently erase karti rehti hai.

Aur jo bachta hai…

woh real hota hai.

Chahe tum ready ho ya nahi.
