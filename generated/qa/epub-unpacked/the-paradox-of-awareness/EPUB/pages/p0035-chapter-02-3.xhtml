PART II — THE DEATH OF EASY WORLD

Bachpan mein duniya straight line thi.

Galti → daant

Mehnat → result

Sach → acceptance

Phir awareness aayi.

Aur duniya ek maze ban gayi.

Ab har cheez ke multiple meanings hain.

Har smile ke peeche interpretation.

Har silence ke peeche theory.

Example:

Ek friend reply nahi karta → “busy hai” nahi… “ignore kar raha hai” bhi ho sakta hai.

Ek teacher kuch nahi kehta → “okay” nahi… “disappointed” bhi ho sakta hai.

Insaan ab jeeta nahi… decode karta hai.
