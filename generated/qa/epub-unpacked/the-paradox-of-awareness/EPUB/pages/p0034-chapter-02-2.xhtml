Ek insaan pehli baar samajhta hai ki har pyaar lifetime nahi hota…

aur woh har naye insaan ko thoda cautiously jeene lagta hai.

Ajeeb baat hai.

Truth hume liberate karti hai…

lekin saath hi hume pehle jaise rehne nahi deti.

Aur yahin se shuru hota hai yeh chapter.

PART I — THE FIRST CUT OF REALITY

Reality kabhi softly nahi aati.

Woh cut karti hai.

Aur har cut ke baad ek purana version quietly gir jaata hai.

Pehli baar jab tumhe samajh aata hai ki duniya fair nahi hai…

tum thode silent ho jaate ho.

Pehli baar jab effort ka expected result nahi milta…

tum expectations ko rewrite kar dete ho.

Pehli baar jab koi tumhe galat samajh leta hai…

tum apni honesty ko filter karna seekh lete ho.
