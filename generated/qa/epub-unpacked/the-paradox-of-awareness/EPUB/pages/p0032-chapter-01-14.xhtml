Toh sawaal yeh nahi hai ki awareness gift hai ya nahi.

Gift toh woh hai.

Sawaal yeh hai...

Kya har insaan us gift ko sambhalna jaanta hai?

Kyuki talwar bhi ek gift ho sakti hai.

Aur zakhm bhi de sakti hai.

Awareness ne hume dekhna sikhaya.

Lekin shayad ab hume yeh seekhna hoga...

Kab dekhna hai.

Aur kab dekhte rehna hi problem ban jaata hai.

Shayad insaan isliye suffer nahi karta kyunki woh kam dekh pata hai.

Shayad woh isliye suffer karta hai...

Kyunki woh itna zyada dekh leta hai.

Aur shayad...

Yahi awareness ka pehla paradox hai.
