Difference awareness overload ka hai.

Jitna tum apne actions ko dekhte ho…

utna tum naturally react karna bhool jaate ho.

Aur yahin se ek invisible loss hota hai:

spontaneity ka death.

PART III — THE SOCIAL MASK BECOMES YOU

Dheere dheere insaan ek aur cheez seekhta hai:

“Main har situation mein same nahi ho sakta.”

Office mein ek version.

Friends ke saath ek version.

Family ke saath ek version.

Aur phir ek din…

tumhe yaad nahi rehta ki asli version kaunsa tha.

Real life example:

Tum kisi group mein haste ho.

Lekin ghar aake sochte ho — “Kya woh main tha?”

Aur jawab simple nahi hota.

Kyuki ab “main” fixed nahi raha.
