Aur hum poori zindagi us script ko protect karne mein laga dete hain.

Par reality simple nahi hoti.

Reality kabhi poochti hi nahi:

“Tumhari expectation kya thi?”

Woh bas hoti hai.

Aur jab reality aur expectation mismatch hota hai…

toh insaan toot nahi jaata.

Woh redefine ho jaata hai.

Aur shayad yahi sabse bada fear hai:

Ki jab life tumhe todti nahi…

bas tumhe kisi aur shape mein bana deti hai.

Jo tumne kabhi socha hi nahi tha.

Insaan control nahi chahta.

Insaan sirf ek cheez chahta hai:
