THE PARADOX OF AWARENESS

THE PRICE OF KNOWING

OPENING — THE DAY NOTHING FEELS THE SAME AGAIN

Kabhi socha hai?

Kuch sachchaiyan sunne ke baad…

insaan pehle jaisa kyun nahi reh jaata?

Ek bachcha pehli baar samajhta hai ki Santa Claus real nahi hai…

aur uske liye duniya “galat” nahi hoti — bas thodi kam magical ho jaati hai.

Ek ladka pehli baar samajhta hai ki uske parents perfect nahi hain…

aur uske andar ek invisible gap ban jaata hai — respect kam nahi hota, par innocence shift ho jaati hai.
