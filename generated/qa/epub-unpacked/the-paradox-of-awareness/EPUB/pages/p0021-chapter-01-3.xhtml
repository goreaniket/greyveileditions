Na koi dharm.

Na koi science.

Na koi philosophy.

Usse galaxies ke baare mein kuch nahi pata.

Atoms ke baare mein kuch nahi pata.

Universe ke baare mein kuch nahi pata.

Lekin uske paas ek cheez hai.

Ek sawaal.

Woh aasmaan ki taraf dekhta hai.

Sitare chamak rahe hain.

Hawa chal rahi hai.

Sab kuch waise hi hai jaise kal tha.

Lekin aaj kuch alag hai.

Aaj usne sirf sitare nahi dekhe.

Aaj usne pehli baar khud ko dekha.

Aur shayad usi pal ek nayi cheez paida hui.

Awareness.

Khud ko dekhne ki kshamata.

Khud ke baare mein sochne ki kshamata.

Khud se sawaal karne ki kshamata.

Yeh ek chhota sa pal lagta hai.
