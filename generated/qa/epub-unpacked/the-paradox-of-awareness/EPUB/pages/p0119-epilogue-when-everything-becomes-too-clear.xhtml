EPILOGUE — WHEN EVERYTHING BECOMES TOO CLEAR

Aur phir ek din…

sab kuch samajh aane lagta hai.

Log bhi.

Patterns bhi.

Situations bhi.

Emotions bhi.

Khud ka dimaag bhi.

Sab explainable ho jaata hai.

Sab predictable lagne lagta hai.

Aur pehli baar lagta hai —

“Ab clarity aa gayi.”

Par strange baat yeh hoti hai…
