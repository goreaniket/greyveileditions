Awareness tumhe duniya nahi dikhati…

tumhe tum dikhati hai.

Aur khud ko dekhna…

kabhi kabhi sabse violent experience hota hai.

Kyuki tum sirf “who you are” nahi dekhte…

tum “who you were not” bhi dekhte ho.

Har insaan jo samajhne lagta hai…

woh thoda badal jaata hai.

Aur har badla hua insaan…

kuch peeche chhod deta hai.

Innocence.

Blind trust.

Simple peace.

Shayad isi liye knowledge free nahi hoti.

Woh milti hai…

lekin kuch le kar.

Aur shayad asli sawaal yeh nahi hai:

“Hum kya jaan rahe hain?”
