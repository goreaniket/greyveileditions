PART VII — WHEN “I” STARTS BREAKING

Ek point aata hai jab tum realize karte ho:

Tumhara “I” ek solid cheez nahi hai.

Woh change hota rehta hai:

mood ke saath

logon ke saath

situations ke saath

memories ke saath

Aur phir ek strange feeling aati hai:

“Main hoon bhi ya main ban raha hoon?”

PART VIII — THE BURDEN OF SELF-AWARENESS

Self-awareness gift hai…

lekin overuse ho jaaye toh woh burden ban jaata hai.

Kyuki:

Tum sirf galti nahi dekhte…

tum galti karne wale ko bhi dekhte ho.

Aur phir forgiveness bhi complicated ho jaati hai.
