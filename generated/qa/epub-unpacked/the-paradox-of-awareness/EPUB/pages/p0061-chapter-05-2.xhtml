PART I — THE END OF SIMPLE WORLD

Ek time hota hai jab duniya seedhi lagti hai:

kisi ne kuch bola → sach

kisi ne mana kiya → galat

result aaya → final

Phir awareness aati hai…

Aur duniya ek simple line nahi rehti.

wo ek maze ban jaati hai.

Har cheez ke peeche:

reason bhi

intention bhi

psychology bhi

past bhi

trauma bhi

Aur ab insaan jeeta nahi…

woh interpret karta hai.

Aur jab interpretation zyada ho jaati hai…
