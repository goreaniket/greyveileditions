Result kal aane wala hai.

Aaj kuch hua nahi.

Sab normal hai.

Lekin tumhara dimaag?

Usne kal ko sau baar jee liya.

Fail hua toh?

Pass hua toh?

Log kya kahenge?

Future ka kya hoga?

Reality abhi aayi bhi nahi.

Lekin suffering aa gayi.

Aur shayad isi liye insaan aksar do baar suffer karta hai.

Ek baar imagination mein.

Aur ek baar reality mein.

Kabhi kabhi toh reality aati hi nahi.

Lekin suffering aa jaati hai.

Yahi awareness ka gift hai.

Hum woh dekh sakte hain jo abhi hua nahi.

Isi wajah se hum sapne dekhte hain.

Isi wajah se hum badalte hain.
