Ek silence ek story ban jaata hai.

Ek normal moment ek psychological puzzle ban jaata hai.

Aur insaan jeena band nahi karta…

par jeete hue har cheez ko interpret karna shuru kar deta hai.

Aur interpretation ka sabse bada trap yeh hai…

ki woh kabhi khatam nahi hota.

Woh har answer ke andar ek naya sawaal chhupa deta hai.

Har clarity ke andar ek naya doubt paida kar deta hai.

Aur har moment se uski rawness chheen leta hai.

Aur yahin se ek silent transformation start hota hai.

Ab insaan pyaar nahi karta… woh pyaar ko analyze karta hai.

Ab insaan dukh nahi feel karta… woh dukh ko decode karta hai.

Ab insaan khush nahi hota… woh khushi ko verify karta hai.

Aur jo cheezein pehle seedha feel hoti thi…

ab woh filtered experience ban jaati hain.

Sabse dangerous shift yahin hota hai:

insaan apne experience ke andar nahi rehta…

woh apne experience ke bahar khada ho jaata hai.

Ek part jee raha hota hai…

aur dusra part usse continuously observe kar raha hota hai.

Aur dheere dheere “jeena” kam ho jaata hai…
