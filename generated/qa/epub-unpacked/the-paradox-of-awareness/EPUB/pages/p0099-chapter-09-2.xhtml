PART I — THE LIE WE CALL PREPARATION

Hum kehte hain hum prepare kar rahe hain.

Lekin sach kuch aur hota hai.

Hum prepare nahi kar rahe hote.

Hum future ko mentally live kar rahe hote hain.

Har possible scenario ko pehle hi jhel lena.

Har possible loss ko pehle hi suffer kar lena.

Jaise agar dard pehle ho gaya…

toh asli dard kam ho jayega.

Par reality?

Jab asli life aati hai…

woh imagination ko respect nahi karti.

Woh naya dard laati hai.
