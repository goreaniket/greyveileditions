PART VI — WHEN PEOPLE STOP FEELING REAL

Ek stage ke baad log fake nahi lagte.

Log confusing bhi nahi lagte.

Log predictable lagne lagte hain.

Aur yahi problem hai.

Kyuki surprise connection ka hissa hota hai.

Wonder connection ka hissa hota hai.

Lekin jab tum har cheez ko pattern bana dete ho...

toh insaan insaan nahi rehta.

Theory ban jaata hai.

Aur theories ko feel nahi kiya jaata.

Unhe analyse kiya jaata hai.

Shayad awareness ka sabse bada cost overthinking nahi hai.

Shayad uska sabse bada cost yeh hai...

ki woh tumhe har cheez samjha deti hai...

except how to sit with uncertainty.

Aur phir ek din tum realize karte ho:

Tum logon se door nahi hue.

Tum possibilities ke kareeb aa gaye.
