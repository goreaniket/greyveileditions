Log kehte hain insaan death se darta hai.

Shayad nahi.

Insaan death se nahi…

uncertainty se darta hai.

Death ek final certainty hai.

Par uncertainty?

Woh har din zinda rehti hai.

Aur insaan ko sabse zyada problem us cheez se hoti hai jo:

dikhti nahi

control nahi hoti

predict nahi hoti

Isliye insaan ne ek puri civilization bana di:

“unknown ko known banane ki machine”

Science. Religion. Astrology. Data. Logic. Psychology. Systems.

Sab ek hi kaam kar rahe hain:
