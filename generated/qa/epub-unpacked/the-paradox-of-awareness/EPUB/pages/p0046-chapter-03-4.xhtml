PART IV — MEMORY STARTS CONTROLLING YOU

Sabse dangerous shift yahin hota hai.

Tum past ko yaad karte ho…

aur dheere dheere past tumhe define karne lagta hai.

Ek failure → “main aisa hi hoon”

Ek rejection → “mere saath aisa hi hota hai”

Ek mistake → “main galat insaan hoon”

Aur insaan apni identity experience se bana deta hai…

na ki present se.
