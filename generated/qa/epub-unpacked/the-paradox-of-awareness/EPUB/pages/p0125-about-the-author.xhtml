ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Lalit is a student and writer who explores human consciousness not as a concept, but as a lived disturbance.

His work does not attempt to explain life.

It observes what happens when life starts getting observed too closely.

He is interested in the subtle breaking point where awareness stops being clarity and starts becoming overload where understanding no longer simplifies experience, but starts separating the experiencer from the experience itself.

Most of his writing moves through the invisible shifts inside the human mind:

when instinct turns into analysis,

when feeling turns into interpretation,

and when presence slowly gets replaced by observation.

He does not see confusion as lack of understanding.
