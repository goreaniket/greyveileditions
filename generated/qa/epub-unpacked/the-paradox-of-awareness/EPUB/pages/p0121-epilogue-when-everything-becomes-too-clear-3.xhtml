Bas explain ho jaate hain.

Aur jo cheez explain ho jaati hai…

woh dheere dheere feel hona band kar deti hai.

Aur phir ek subtle realization aata hai…

Shayad problem duniya nahi thi…

Shayad problem yeh thi ki tum duniya ko dekhte waqt

uske andar kabhi the hi nahi.

Tum hamesha thoda sa bahar khade the…

samajhne ke liye.

Aur yahi sabse hidden illusion tha:

ki tum “experience” kar rahe ho.

jabki tum sirf “observe” kar rahe the experience ko.

Aur ek point pe yeh dono cheezein mix ho jaati hain…

aur tumhe lagta hai ki tum jee rahe ho.

Par andar ek invisible distance ban chuki hoti hai…

life aur mind ke beech.

Phir ek aur layer break hoti hai…

Tumhe samajh aata hai:

jo confusion tha… woh bhi tum the

jo clarity hai… woh bhi tum ho

jo overthinking hai… woh bhi tum ho
