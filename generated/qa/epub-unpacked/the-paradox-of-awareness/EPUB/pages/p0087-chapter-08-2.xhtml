Koi dramatic moment nahi aata.

Koi warning nahi milti.

Bas dheere dheere...

tum apni hi awaaz par bharosa karna kam kar dete ho.

Pehle tumhe kuch feel hota hai.

Phir tum usse question karte ho.

Phir doubt karte ho.

Phir ignore karte ho.

Aur ek din...

tumhe yaad hi nahi rehta ki tumhari asli awaaz kaisi lagti thi.

Aur shayad human awareness ka ek aur paradox yahi hai:

Jitna zyada insaan duniya ko samajhne lagta hai...

utna hi kabhi kabhi khud ko sunna bhool jaata hai.
