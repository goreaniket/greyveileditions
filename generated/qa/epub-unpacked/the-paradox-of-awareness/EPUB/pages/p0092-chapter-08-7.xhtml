Lekin kabhi kabhi reality kuch aur hoti hai.

ki ab khud par trust karne se darta hai.

toh wisdom jaisa lagne lagta hai.

PART V — THE SECRET WAY PEOPLE ABANDON THEMSELVES

Log ekdum se khud ko nahi khote.

Woh chhote chhote compromises mein khote hain.

Jab woh "haan" bolte hain...

jabki andar se "na" hoti hai.

Jab woh rukte hain...

jabki andar se jaana chahte hain.

Jab woh haste hain...

jabki andar se toot rahe hote hain.

Har baar duniya ko comfort dene ke liye...

woh khud ko thoda sa abandon kar dete hain.
