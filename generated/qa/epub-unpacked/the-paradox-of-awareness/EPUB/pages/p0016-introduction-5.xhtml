Experience aur analysis ke beech.

Aur sabse dangerous…

self aur self ke beech.

Aur insaan us distance ko explain bhi nahi kar paata…

bas silently feel karta hai ki kuch “door” ho gaya hai.

Aur phir ek point aata hai jahan sab kuch stable hota hai…

par kuch bhi alive nahi lagta.

Na confusion bachta hai jo explore kare.

Na clarity ka thrill bachta hai jo excite kare.

Na surprise bachta hai jo shake kare.

Sirf ek silence reh jaata hai…

jo peace jaisa nahi hota.

jo emptiness jaisa nahi hota.

jo thakaan jaisa hota hai…

par naam nahi milta.

Is book mein koi comfort nahi hai.

Koi motivational escape nahi hai.

Koi final truth nahi hai jisko pakad kar insaan safe feel kare.

Yeh sirf ek exposure hai… us cheez ka jo hum roz jeete hain

par kabhi seedha dekhte nahi:

ki awareness hume duniya nahi dikhati sirf…
