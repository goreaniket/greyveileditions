PROLOGUE

PROLOGUE:

Shayad insaan ki sabse badi problem yeh nahi hai ki woh kuch nahi jaanta…

problem yeh hai ki woh thoda zyada jaanta hai.

Aur yeh “thoda zyada” hi woh jagah hai jahan se sab kuch quietly tootna shuru hota hai.

Kyuki jab insaan kam jaanta hai, woh jee leta hai.

Par jab insaan zyada samajhne lagta hai…

toh woh jeene ke saath-saath dekhne bhi lagta hai.

Aur phir ek split hota hai — bina shor ke.

Ek taraf life hoti hai.

Dusri taraf us life ko dekh raha dimaag.

Aur jo dekh raha hota hai…

woh jeene wale ko dheere dheere akela kar deta hai.

Ek waqt tha jab duniya simple thi.

Log jo kehte the, woh sach lagta tha.
