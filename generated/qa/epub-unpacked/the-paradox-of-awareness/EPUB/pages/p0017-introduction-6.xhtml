woh hume khud se thoda door bhi kar deti hai.

Aur shayad sabse uncomfortable realization yeh hai…

ki jo cheez hume sab kuch samajhne ki ability deti hai…

woh hume kabhi kabhi yeh bhi bhula deti hai ki kuch cheezein samajhne ke liye nahi hoti…

sirf jeene ke liye hoti hain.

Aur end mein sirf ek question nahi bachta…

ek silence bachta hai jo seedha dekh raha hota hai:

“Tum zindagi jee rahe ho… ya sirf usse decode kar rahe ho?”
