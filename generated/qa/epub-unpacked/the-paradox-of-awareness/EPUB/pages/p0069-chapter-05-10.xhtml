Shayad sabse strange truth yeh hai:

Jitna zyada tum dekhte ho…

utna zyada tum simple nahi rehte.

Aur jitna zyada tum samajhte ho…

utna zyada tum khud se thoda door ho jaate ho.

Aur yahin pe paradox complete hota hai:

Awareness tumhe free nahi karti…

woh tumhe itna aware kar deti hai ki tum kabhi fully free the hi nahi.

Aur shayad isi liye clarity gift bhi hai…

aur burden bhi.
