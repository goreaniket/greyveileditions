jab tum saalon baad pehli baar apni hi awaaz sunte ho...

aur usse ignore nahi karte.

Aur shayad awareness ka agla paradox bhi yahi hai:

Insaan poori duniya ko samajhne nikal padta hai...

sirf yeh realize karne ke liye...

ki sabse mushkil cheez duniya ko samajhna nahi thi.

Sabse mushkil cheez thi...

khud par dobara bharosa karna.
