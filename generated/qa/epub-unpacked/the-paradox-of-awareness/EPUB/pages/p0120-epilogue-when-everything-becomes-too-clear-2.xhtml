ki clarity ke saath peace nahi aati.

Na confusion.

Na excitement.

Na relief.

Bas ek ajeeb sa silence.

Pehle duniya alive thi.

Ab duniya readable hai.

Pehle har cheez surprise thi.

Ab har cheez repeat lagti hai.

Pehle moment feel hota tha.

Ab moment analyze hota hai.

Aur dheere dheere tum ek shift notice karte ho…

Tum life ko jee nahi rahe hote…

Tum life ko recognize kar rahe hote ho.

Aur recognition kabhi naya experience nahi deta…

sirf echo deta hai.

Jaise sab kuch pehle ho chuka hai…

aur ab bas replay chal raha hai.

Log bhi change nahi hote.

Bas patterns ban jaate hain.

Emotions bhi raw nahi rehte.
