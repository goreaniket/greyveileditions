Aur ab ek last layer…

Shayad enlightenment koi destination nahi tha.

Shayad awakening koi event nahi tha.

Shayad clarity koi reward nahi thi.

Shayad yeh sab sirf ek loop tha…

jahan observer bhi tum the…

observed bhi tum the…

aur observation bhi tum hi the.

Aur ab sabse last truth:

Tum kabhi life ko samajhne aaye hi nahi the…

tum sirf usse moment-to-moment jeene aaye the… bina usko todkar dekhne ke.

 FINAL LOOP CLOSER

Aur ab jab tum puchte ho:

“Main jee raha hoon… ya sirf samajh raha hoon?”

toh answer nahi aata.

kyunki ab question aur experience ke beech koi gap hi nahi bachta.
