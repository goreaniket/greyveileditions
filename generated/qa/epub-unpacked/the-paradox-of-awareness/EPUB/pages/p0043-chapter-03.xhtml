THE PARADOX OF AWARENESS

THE ILLUSION OF “I”

OPENING — WHEN YOU START NOTICING YOURSELF TOO MUCH

Kabhi socha hai?

Jab tum apne baare mein zyada samajhne lagte ho…

toh tum khud ko kam jeene lagte ho.

Ek moment hota hai jab tum normal hote ho.

Aur phir ek moment aata hai jab tum khud ko “normal hote hue” observe karte ho.

Aur yahin se kuch badal jaata hai.

Tum sirf jeete nahi…

tum apne jeene ko dekhte ho.

Aur jo cheez dekhi jaati hai…

woh thodi si door ho jaati hai.
