THE PARADOX OF AWARENESS

CHAPTER-6:

THE POINT WHERE UNDERSTANDING STARTS FEELING HEAVY 

OPENING — WHEN CLARITY STOPS BEING LIGHT

Kabhi notice kiya hai?

Jab tumhe duniya samajh aane lagti hai…

toh log tumhe “smart” kehte hain.

Par tum andar se kuch aur feel karte ho.

Na excitement.

Na peace.

Bas ek ajeeb sa slowdown.

Jaise kisi ne tumhare andar ki speed kam kar di ho.

Aur sabse strange baat:

 tum confused nahi hote… tum “clear” hote ho
