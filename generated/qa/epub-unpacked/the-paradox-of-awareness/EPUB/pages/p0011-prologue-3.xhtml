Ek smile ke peeche meaning.

Ek silence ke peeche story.

Ek normal din ke peeche infinite interpretation.

Aur insaan dheere dheere jeena nahi…

interpret karna shuru kar deta hai.

Aur interpretation kabhi rukta nahi.

Har answer ek naya sawaal ban jaata hai.

Har clarity ek naya doubt.

Aur phir ek point aata hai…

insaan experience nahi karta…

woh experience ko observe karta hai.

Aur observation…

jeene ke beech ek gap bana deta hai.

Yeh gap shuru mein invisible hota hai.

Par dheere dheere yahi gap…

poori zindagi ka tone badal deta hai.

Aur shayad yahi se yeh journey start hoti hai.
