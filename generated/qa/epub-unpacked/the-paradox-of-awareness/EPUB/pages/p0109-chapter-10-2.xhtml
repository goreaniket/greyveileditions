Aur ye silence shanti jaisa nahi hota.

Ye thakaan jaisa hota hai.

PART I — WHEN LIFE STOPS SURPRISING YOU

Ek waqt hota hai jab har cheez nayi lagti hai.

Log unpredictable.

Situations alive.

Feelings raw.

Phir dheere dheere sab repeat hone lagta hai.

Same reactions.

Same patterns.

Same endings.

Aur ek din tum realize karte ho…

ki tum life ko jee nahi rahe.

Tum life ko recognize kar rahe ho.

Aur recognition kabhi surprise nahi deta.

Sirf confirmation deta hai.

PART II — WHEN YOU START ARRIVING BEFORE THE MOMENT

Kabhi notice kiya hai?

Tum kisi moment mein jaate ho…

par tumhara dimaag already wahan hota hai.

Tum kisi insaan se milte ho…
