Sab log hote hain.

Conversation hoti hai.

Responses hoti hain.

Interaction hota hai.

Par andar ek thin distance hota hai…

jo kisi ko visible nahi hota.

Tum present hote ho…

par merge nahi hote.

Tum samajhte ho…

par absorb nahi karte.

Aur yeh distance kabhi explain nahi hota.

Sirf feel hota hai.

PART VII — WHEN UNDERSTANDING BECOMES A WALL

Ek dangerous phase aata hai.

Jab tumhe lagta hai tum sab samajh gaye ho.

Logon ko.

Khud ko.

Life ko.

Aur samajh ek illusion create karti hai:

ki ab kuch unpredictable nahi hoga.
