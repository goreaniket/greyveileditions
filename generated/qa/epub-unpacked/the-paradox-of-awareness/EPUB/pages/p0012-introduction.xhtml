INTRODUCTION

Shayad insaan ki sabse badi problem yeh nahi hai ki woh kuch nahi jaanta…

problem yeh hai ki woh thoda zyada jaanta hai.

Aur yeh “thoda zyada” hi woh jagah hai jahan se insaan dheere dheere normal nahi rehta…

woh aware ho jaata hai.

Aur awareness hamesha gift ki tarah feel hoti hai.

Par har gift sirf milta nahi… kuch cheezein saath le bhi jaati hain.

Ek waqt tha jab duniya simple thi.

Log jo kehte the, woh sach lagta tha.

Jo hota tha, woh bas hota tha.

Aur jo feel hota tha, usse explain karne ki zarurat nahi padti thi.

Dukh dukh tha.
