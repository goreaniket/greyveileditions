ENDING — THE THING WE NEVER SAY CLEARLY

Shayad hum future se nahi darte…

Hum us version se darte hain jo future hume bana dega.

Hum death se nahi darte.

Hum us uncertainty se darte hain jo hume apni hi story se alag kar de.

Kyuki har insaan ke andar ek invisible script chal rahi hoti hai:

main successful hoga

main respected hoga

main loved hoga

main stable hoga
