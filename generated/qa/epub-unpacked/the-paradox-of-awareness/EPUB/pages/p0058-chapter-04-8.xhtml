PART IX — THE SECOND PARADOX

Agar “main” fixed nahi hai…

toh phir responsibility kaun leta hai?

Agar har din hum different hain…

toh “real me” kaun hai?

Aur yahin story break hoti hai.

Kyuki answer conceptual nahi hai…

existential hai.

Shayad sabse uncomfortable truth yeh hai:

Tum ek nahi ho.
