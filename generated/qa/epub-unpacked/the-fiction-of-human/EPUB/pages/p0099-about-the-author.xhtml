ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Lalit R. Mishra is a writer driven by a simple but profound question:

Why do human beings think, feel, suffer, search, and transform the way they do?

Rather than approaching these questions through rigid theories or fixed conclusions, his work explores them through observation, introspection, and inquiry.

His writing focuses on the inner world of human experience, including identity, meaning, perception, overthinking, emotional pain, healing, consciousness, and the psychological structures that shape everyday life.
