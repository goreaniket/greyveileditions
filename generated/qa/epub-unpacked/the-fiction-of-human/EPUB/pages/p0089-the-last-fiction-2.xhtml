ek aur layer milti gayi.

Jaise reality koi solid object nahi...

balki ek endless unfolding ho.

Aur phir dheere dheere...

ek strange realization saamne aane laga.

Har cheez jise hum absolute samajhte the...

wo movement nikli.

Har certainty temporary nikli.

Har conclusion incomplete nikla.

Aur har story...

ek story nikli.

Lekin shayad ek fiction aur baaki thi.

Sabse subtle.

Sabse invisible.

Sabse difficult.

Wo fiction jiske bina baaki saari fictions survive nahi kar sakti thi.

Wo fiction tha:

Ki kahin na kahin...

koi final answer hai.

Ek final understanding.
