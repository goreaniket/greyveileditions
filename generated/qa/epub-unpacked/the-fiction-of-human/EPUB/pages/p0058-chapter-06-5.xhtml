ki usse notice karna mushkil tha.

Main story ko jee raha hoon.

Story mere andar chal rahi hai.

Aur ye dono statements ek jaise nahi hain.

Ek mein tum character ho.

Ye dramatic ending nahi hai.

Ye sirf ek gentle seeing hai.

Ek recognition.

Ki shayad har thought ko follow karna zaroori nahi.
