Aur phir poori life ek bridge banane ki koshish ban jati hai.

Love.

Friendship.

Achievement.

Recognition.

Sab bridge banne lagte hain.

Lekin bridge ki zarurat tabhi hoti hai...

jab pehle separation maana gaya ho.

Hum connection dhoondte hain.

Lekin jo connection dhoond raha hai...

wo pehle se separation assume kar chuka hota hai.

Aur shayad isi liye...

jitna zyada hum connection ko chase karte hain...

utna hi separation real lagne lagta hai.

Observe karo.

Kitni baar thought silently "me" ke around ghoomta hai.

Mera future.

Meri problem.

Mera pain.
