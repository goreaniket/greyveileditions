Jahan story dheemi padti hai...

wahan ek space nazar aati hai.

Ek aisi space jo kabhi gayab nahi hui thi.

Bas noise zyada tha.

Aur shayad isi space mein...

life sabse direct hoti hai.

Bina explanation ke.

Bina narration ke.

Bina ownership ke.

"After the Human" ka matlab human ko reject karna nahi.

Human ko transcend karna bhi nahi.

Human ko destroy karna bhi nahi.

Iska matlab sirf itna hai:

Human story ko story ki tarah dekh pana.

Uski beauty ko appreciate kar pana.

Uski suffering ko samajh pana.

Aur phir bhi uske andar completely kho na jana.
