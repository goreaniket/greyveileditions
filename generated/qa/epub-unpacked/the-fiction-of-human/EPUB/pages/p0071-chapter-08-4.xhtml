Lekin kya hoga agar ek moment ke liye...

tum search ko side rakh do?

Na answer dhoondo.

Na destination.

Na final version.

Bas dekho.

Kabhi kabhi jo milta hai...

wo answer nahi hota.

Relief hota hai.

Ek strange relief.

Jaise saalon tak ek heavy bag carry karne ke baad...

pata chale ki usse rakh bhi sakte ho.

Aur phir suddenly...

weight gayab nahi hota.

Lekin usse carry karne ki compulsive need gayab hone lagti hai.

Toh story ke bina kya bachta hai?

Shayad koi grand mystical answer nahi.

Shayad koi perfect state nahi.

Shayad koi superhuman transformation nahi.

Shayad bas ye.
