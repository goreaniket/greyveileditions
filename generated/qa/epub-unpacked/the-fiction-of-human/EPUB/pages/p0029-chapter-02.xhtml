CHAPTER 2

The Construction of Identity

Tumhara naam tum nahi tha.

Tumhari personality bhi tum nahi thi.

Tumhari history bhi tum nahi thi.

Lekin ek waqt aisa aaya...

jab ye sab cheezein milkar ek feeling create karne lagi:

"Ye main hoon."

Aur shayad wahi moment tha...

jahan identity ka construction shuru hua.
