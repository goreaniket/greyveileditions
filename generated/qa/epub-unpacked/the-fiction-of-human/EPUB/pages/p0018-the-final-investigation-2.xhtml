Na happiness ke question se.

Balki us question se...

jo shayad har question ke peeche chhupa hua hai.

Is series ki shuruaat human mind se hui thi.

Humne dekha thoughts kaise kaam karte hain.

Memory kaise reality ko influence karti hai.

Perception kaise experience ko shape karti hai.

Phir hum paradoxes tak gaye.

Jahan understanding aur confusion ek saath exist karte nazar aaye.

Jahan certainty aur doubt ek hi coin ke do sides lagne lage.

Phir hum fictions tak aaye.

Self.

Meaning.

Becoming.

Aur dheere dheere ek uncomfortable possibility saamne aayi.

Ho sakta hai bahut si cheezein jise hum reality samajh kar jeete hain...

stories hon.

Useful stories.

Powerful stories.

Lekin stories.
