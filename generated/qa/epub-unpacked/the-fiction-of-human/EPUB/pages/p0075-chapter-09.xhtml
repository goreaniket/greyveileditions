CHAPTER 9

The End of the Search

Har story ka ek destination hota hai.

Ek ending.

Ek point jahan character finally pahunchta hai.

Aur shayad isi liye...

human life kaafi had tak ek search ban jati hai.

Kuch paane ki search.

Kuch banne ki search.

Kuch samajhne ki search.

Kuch heal karne ki search.

Kuch complete karne ki search.

Aur dheere dheere...

search itni normal lagne lagti hai...
