Aur har chapter mein ek cheez common thi.

Kuch constantly change ho raha tha.

Aur kuch quietly present tha.

Identity badli.

Beliefs badle.

Dreams badle.

Relationships badle.

Stories badli.

Lekin experience ki basic aliveness...

kabhi gayab nahi hui.

Human story ka foundation ek feeling par tikta hai.

Ek feeling ki:

"Mere andar kuch missing hai."

Aur isi feeling se search shuru hoti hai.

Meaning ki search.

Love ki search.

Completion ki search.

Self-improvement ki search.
