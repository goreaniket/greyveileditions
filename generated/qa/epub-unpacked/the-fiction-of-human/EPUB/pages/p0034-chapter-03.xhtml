CHAPTER 3

The Narrative Animal

Kabhi socha hai...

ki insaan sirf duniya mein nahi rehta.

Insaan kahaniyon mein rehta hai.

Ek ped ko ped hone ke liye kahani ki zarurat nahi.

Ek nadi ko behne ke liye meaning ki zarurat nahi.

Ek aasman ko exist karne ke liye explanation ki zarurat nahi.

Lekin insaan...

almost har cheez ko story mein convert kar deta hai.

Ek event hota hai.

Hum uski kahani bana dete hain.

Ek emotion aata hai.
