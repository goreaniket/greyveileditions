Jab ek bachcha paida hota hai...

uske paas body hoti hai.

Sensations hoti hain.

Experience hota hai.

Lekin identity nahi hoti.

Identity dheere dheere build hoti hai.

Ek naam diya jata hai.

Phir us naam ko baar baar repeat kiya jata hai.

Aur kuch saalon baad...

wo naam sirf ek label nahi rehta.

Wo self banne lagta hai.

Human mind ki ek strange tendency hai.

Jo cheez baar baar repeat hoti hai...

wo familiar lagne lagti hai.

Aur jo familiar lagti hai...

wo true lagne lagti hai.

Isi tarah identity build hoti hai.

Not through discovery.

Through repetition.

THE COLLECTION PROCESS

Identity ek single thing nahi hoti.
