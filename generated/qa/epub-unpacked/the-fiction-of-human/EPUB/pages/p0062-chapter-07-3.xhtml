Strange baat ye hai...

ki ye wall physical nahi hoti.

Isse touch nahi kiya ja sakta.

Isse dekha nahi ja sakta.

Lekin phir bhi...

insaan poori zindagi iske andar jeeta hai.

Ek invisible boundary.

Ek feeling:

"Main yahan khatam hota hoon...

aur duniya wahan shuru hoti hai."

Aur kyuki ye feeling itni familiar hai...

isliye koi usse question nahi karta.

Loneliness aksar logon ki kami se nahi aati.

Kabhi kabhi loneliness us feeling se aati hai...

ki tum fundamentally separate ho.

Ki tum poori tarah kisi tak pahunch nahi sakte.

Ki koi poori tarah tum tak pahunch nahi sakta.
