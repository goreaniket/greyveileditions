Mera success.

Meri story.

Aur dheere dheere...

poori reality ek centre ke around organise ho jati hai.

Lekin kya wo centre actual hai?

Ya narration ka effect?

Ek moment aata hai...

jab insaan genuinely observe karta hai.

Bina conclusion ke.

Bina philosophy ke.

Bina belief ke.

Aur us moment mein ek strange cheez notice hoti hai.

Experience ho raha hai.

Lekin us experience ke centre mein...

wo solid character nazar nahi aata...

jise saalon se "main" kaha ja raha tha.

Thought hai.

Emotion hai.

Memory hai.

Sensation hai.
