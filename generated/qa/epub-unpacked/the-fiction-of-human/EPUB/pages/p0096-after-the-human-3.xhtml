Usne religions banaye.

Usne philosophies banayi.

Usne wars bhi banaye.

Usne poetry likhi.

Usne heartbreaks feel kiye.

Usne stars ko dekha.

Usne khud ko dhoonda.

Aur shayad sabse interesting baat ye thi...

ki poori journey mein...

human continuously khud ko samajhne ki koshish karta raha.

Shayad kuch bhi nahi.

Aur shayad sab kuch.

Duniya wahi hai.

Aasman wahi hai.

Hawa wahi hai.

Life wahi hai.

Lekin dekhne ka tareeka badal gaya.

Ab har thought ko believe karna zaroori nahi.

Har fear ko obey karna zaroori nahi.

Har story ko reality samajhna zaroori nahi.

Aur har version of yourself ko protect karna bhi zaroori nahi.
