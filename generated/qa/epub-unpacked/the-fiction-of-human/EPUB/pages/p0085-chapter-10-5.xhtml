Hum certainty dhoondne nikle the.

Lekin openness tak pahunch gaye.

Hum better self dhoondne nikle the.

Lekin self ko hi question kar baithe.

Aur shayad isi liye...

ye journey circle nahi thi.

Ye return thi.

Ek aisa return...

jahan tum wahi dekhte ho jo hamesha se tha.

Lekin pehli baar bina filters ke.

Ho sakta hai tumhari life perfect na ho.

Ho sakta hai tumhare paas abhi bhi questions ho.

Ho sakta hai fears bhi ho.

Dreams bhi ho.

Pain bhi ho.

Lekin shayad pehli baar...

tumhe un sab ko complete hone ke liye solve karne ki zarurat na lage.

Kyuki completion kisi future event ka naam nahi.

Completion shayad wo moment hai...
