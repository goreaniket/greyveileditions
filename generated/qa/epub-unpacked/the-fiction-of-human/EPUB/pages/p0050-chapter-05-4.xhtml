Aur psychological stories kuch aisi hi hoti hain.

Wo tumhe bandh kar deti hain...

lekin bars dikhte nahi.

Kyuki bars thoughts se bane hote hain.

Identity se.

Meaning se.

Becoming se.

Aur isi liye ye trilogy exist karti hai.

Book I ne self ko dekha.

Book II ne meaning ko dekha.

Book III ne becoming ko dekha.

Ab Book IV us poori duniya ko dekh rahi hai...

jo in teeno se milkar bani thi.

Strange baat ye hai...

ki log aksar kehte hain:

"Life bahut heavy hai."

Lekin kabhi kabhi life heavy nahi hoti.

Uske baare mein bani hui story heavy hoti hai.
