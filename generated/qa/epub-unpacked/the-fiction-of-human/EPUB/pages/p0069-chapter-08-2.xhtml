Agar story kuch der ke liye silent ho jaye...

toh kya bachega?

Zyada tar log is question se uncomfortable feel karte hain.

Kyuki mind immediately imagine karta hai:

No identity.

No meaning.

No becoming.

Aur phir ek conclusion nikal leta hai:

"Toh phir kuch bhi nahi bachega."

Lekin shayad ye conclusion bahut jaldi hai.

Kyuki jo disappear hota hai...

wo narration hoti hai.

Experience nahi.

Book I mein self ko dekha.

Book II mein meaning ko dekha.

Book III mein becoming ko dekha.

Book IV mein human story ko dekha.
