Failure ki stories.

Love ki stories.

Pain ki stories.

Hope ki stories.

Aur dheere dheere...

un stories ne milkar ek character bana diya.

Ek character jise humne naam diya:

"Main."

Lekin ab...

hum us point par khade hain jahan kahani pehli baar khud ko dekh chuki hai.

Aur ek baar kahani khud ko dekh le...

toh usse pehle jaisa maanna mushkil ho jata hai.

Interesting baat ye hai...

ki stories kabhi completely disappear nahi hoti.

Thoughts aate rehte hain.

Memories aati rehti hain.

Dreams aate rehte hain.
