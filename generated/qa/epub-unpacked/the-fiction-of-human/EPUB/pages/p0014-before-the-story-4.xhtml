Compare karta hai.

Dream karta hai.

Regret karta hai.

Aur sabse important...

khud ko continuously define karta rehta hai.

Jaise ek story writer...

jo kabhi likhna band nahi karta.

Aur dheere dheere...

story aur storyteller ek hi cheez lagne lagte hain.

Ye kitab history ke human ke baare mein nahi hai.

Na biology ke human ke baare mein.

Na society ke human ke baare mein.

Ye kitab us psychological human ke baare mein hai...

jo har reader ke andar rehta hai.

Wo jo kehta hai:

meri life

mera future

mera pain

meri journey

Aur jo shayad...

khud bhi ek story ho.
