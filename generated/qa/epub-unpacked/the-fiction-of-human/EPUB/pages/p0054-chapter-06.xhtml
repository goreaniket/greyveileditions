CHAPTER 6

When the Story Sees Itself

Har story ki ek weakness hoti hai.

Wo tab tak powerful rehti hai...

jab tak usse story na dekha jaye.

Jis moment kahani khud ko kahani ki tarah dekh leti hai...

kuch fundamental change ho jata hai.

Story turant disappear nahi hoti.

Lekin uska hypnosis tootne lagta hai.

Aur shayad human life ka sabse important moment wahi hota hai.

Jab pehli baar...

story khud ko dekhna shuru karti hai.
