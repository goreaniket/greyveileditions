INTRODUCTION

NOTE FROM THE AUTHOR

If you are reading this...

thank you.

Not just for picking up this book.

But for being willing to pause.

In a world that constantly asks us to move faster...

achieve more...

become more...

and chase the next thing...

the simple act of stopping and looking is becoming increasingly rare.

This series was born from that act.

Not from certainty.

Not from expertise.

And not from the belief that I had answers.

It was born from questions.
