Shayad tum lost bhi nahi the.

Shayad tum sirf stories ke maze mein chal rahe the.

Aur har turn par tumhe lagta tha ki exit agle corner par hai.

Jabki poora maze...

ek hi centre ke around bana hua tha.

Ek imagined centre.

Ek psychological centre.

Ek story.

Ab yahan...

is final page par...

koi grand message nahi hai.

Koi command nahi.

Koi doctrine nahi.

Koi system nahi.

Bas ek observation.

Ek quiet observation.

Ki life ko solve karne ki zarurat nahi.

Khud ko complete karne ki zarurat nahi.

Har mystery ko close karne ki zarurat nahi.

Aur har uncertainty ko certainty mein convert karne ki zarurat nahi.
