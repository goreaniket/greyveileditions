Ye kisi achievement ki tarah nahi aata.

Na meditation ke certificate ke saath.

Na enlightenment ke label ke saath.

Kabhi kabhi ye ek ordinary afternoon mein aata hai.

Kabhi ek heartbreak ke baad.

Kabhi ek loss ke baad.

Kabhi ek aisi raat mein...

jab tum thak chuke hote ho.

Aur achanak ek thought aata hai:

"Agar main itne saalon se apne baare mein jo soch raha tha...

wo poori truth na ho toh?"

Bas itna.

Aur wahi se crack shuru hota hai.

Pehle tum story ke andar the.

Ab tum story ko dekhne lagte ho.

Pehle thought reality lagta tha.

Ab thought thought lagne lagta hai.
