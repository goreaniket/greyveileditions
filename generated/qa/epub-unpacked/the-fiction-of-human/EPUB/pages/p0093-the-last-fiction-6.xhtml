Kabhi kabhi...

dekh lena hi kaafi hota hai.

Kabhi kabhi...

hona hi kaafi hota hai.

Aur kabhi kabhi...

isi pal mein...

jitna hai...

utna hi poora hota hai.

Isliye agar is poori journey ko ek sentence mein sametna pade...

toh shayad wo sentence ye hoga:

You were never searching for life.

You were searching through stories.

And the moment the stories became visible...

life was already here.

The End.

Or perhaps...

The Beginning.
