Questions that refused to leave.

Questions about thought.

Questions about identity.

Questions about suffering.

Questions about meaning.

Questions about what it truly means to be human.

Over time, those questions became pages.

Those pages became books.

And those books became a journey.

A journey through the human mind.

A journey through paradoxes.

A journey through stories.

And finally...

a journey into the structure of the human experience itself.

While writing these books, I discovered something unexpected.

The deeper I went searching for answers...

the more I found myself returning to observation.

Not conclusions.

Not beliefs.

Observation.
