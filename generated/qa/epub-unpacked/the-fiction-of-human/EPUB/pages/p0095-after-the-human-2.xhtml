ek strange cheez saamne aati gayi.

Jitna hum dekhte gaye...

utna certainty dissolve hoti gayi.

Lekin uske saath saath...

ek aur cheez emerge hoti gayi.

Lightness.

Hum sochte hain freedom answers se aati hai.

Lekin kai baar freedom...

answers ke kam hone se aati hai.

Hum sochte hain peace certainty se aati hai.

Lekin kai baar peace...

certainty ko carry na karne se aati hai.

Aur hum sochte hain completion future mein hai.

Lekin kai baar completion...

future ko chase karna band karne mein hoti hai.

Human story beautiful bhi thi.

Painful bhi.

Confusing bhi.

Meaningful bhi.

Aur contradictory bhi.

Usne civilizations banayi.
