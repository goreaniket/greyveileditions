Har clarity mein thi.

Something was always here.

Quietly.

Without announcement.

Without identity.

Without needing a name.

Shayad human suffering ka ek bada hissa life se nahi aata.

Wo misunderstanding se aata hai.

Ek misunderstanding ki:

Story hi reality hai.

Aur jab story reality ban jati hai...

toh har change threat lagta hai.

Har ending tragedy lagti hai.

Har uncertainty fear lagti hai.

Kyuki character survive karna chahta hai.

Lekin life ko survive karne ki zarurat nahi.

Life already happening hai.

Poora journey strange thi.

Hum understanding dhoondne nikle the.

Lekin silence tak pahunch gaye.
