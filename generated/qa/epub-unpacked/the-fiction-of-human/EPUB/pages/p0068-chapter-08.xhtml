CHAPTER 8

What Remains Without the Story

Poori zindagi ek strange movement chalti rehti hai.

Kuch banne ki.

Kuch samajhne ki.

Kuch paane ki.

Kuch fix karne ki.

Aur aksar hume lagta hai...

ki ye movement hi life hai.

Ki agar movement ruk gayi...

toh sab kuch ruk jayega.

Lekin ek question hai.

Ek aisa question jo shayad poori series ka sabse important question hai.
