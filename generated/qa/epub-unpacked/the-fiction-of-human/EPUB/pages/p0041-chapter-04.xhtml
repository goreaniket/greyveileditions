CHAPTER 4

The Reality-Making Machine

Hum usually sochte hain ki reality pehle aati hai...

aur interpretation baad mein.

Pehle duniya.

Phir uski understanding.

Pehle event.

Phir uska meaning.

Ye assumption itna natural lagta hai...

ki hum kabhi usse question hi nahi karte.

Lekin agar thoda closely dekha jaye...

toh ek strange possibility saamne aati hai.

Shayad hum reality ko directly nahi dekhte.

Shayad hum us version ko dekhte hain...
