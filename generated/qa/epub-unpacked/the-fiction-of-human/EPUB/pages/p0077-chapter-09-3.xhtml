Wo kaun hai jo complete hona chahta hai?

Wo kaun hai jo continuously next answer ke peeche bhaag raha hai?

Aur agar usse closely dekha jaye...

toh ek strange cheez nazar aati hai.

Seeker bhi story ka part hai.

Search ki problem ye nahi hai ki wo galat hai.

Problem ye hai ki uska horizon continuously move karta rehta hai.

Jab ek goal milta hai...

doosra khada ho jata hai.

Jab ek answer milta hai...

naya question aa jata hai.

Jab ek mountain climb hota hai...

door ek aur mountain dikhne lagta hai.

Aur poori life isi movement mein nikal sakti hai.

Not because destination exists.

But because searching has become an identity.

Shayad isi liye itne log physically nahi...

psychologically thak jaate hain.

Life ki wajah se nahi.

73
