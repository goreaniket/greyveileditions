hopes rehte hain

regrets rehte hain

expectations rehti hain

Aur sabse interesting baat...

ye hai ki hum us private world ko reality samajhne lagte hain.

Ek world hai jo actually ho raha hai.

Aur ek world hai jo uske baare mein socha ja raha hai.

Zyada tar log doosre world mein rehte hain.

Isi liye kabhi kabhi banda room mein akela baitha hota hai...

aur phir bhi uske andar poori battle chal rahi hoti hai.

Reality silent hoti hai.

Story loud hoti hai.

Aur loud cheez usually zyada real lagti hai.

Sabse dangerous prison wo nahi jisme deewarein hoti hain.

Sabse dangerous prison wo hai...

jisme tumhe lagta hai tum free ho.
