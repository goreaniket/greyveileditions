Koi cosmic reason nahi.

Koi grand purpose nahi.

Mind immediately uncomfortable feel karta hai.

Kyuki story certainty deti hai.

Aur certainty safety ka illusion deti hai.

Isi liye insaan sirf answers nahi dhoondta.

Wo narratives dhoondta hai.

Har story ko character chahiye.

Aur har character ko plot.

Identity character ban gayi.

Ab life plot ban gayi.

Ab har event ko kisi direction mein fit hona hai.

Har struggle ka meaning hona chahiye.

Har pain ka purpose hona chahiye.

Har loss se lesson nikalna chahiye.

Kyuki agar plot toot gaya...

toh character confuse ho jata hai.

Story comfort deti hai.

Lekin wahi story prison bhi ban sakti hai.

Kyuki ek point ke baad...

tum reality ko nahi dekhte.
