CHAPTER 5

The World Inside the Story

Kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta hai...

ki hum duniya mein nahi rehte.

Hum duniya ke baare mein bani hui apni stories mein rehte hain.

Aur shayad dono ke beech ka difference hi human suffering ka ek hidden source hai.

Kyuki duniya constantly change ho rahi hoti hai.

Lekin story...

stability chahti hai.

Duniya flow hai.

Story structure hai.

Duniya alive hai.
