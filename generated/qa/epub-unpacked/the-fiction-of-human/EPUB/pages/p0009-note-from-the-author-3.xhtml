Because sometimes the most important things in life are not hidden.

They are simply overlooked.

This series is not an attempt to tell you what is true.

It is an invitation.

An invitation to look.

To question.

To observe.

And perhaps...

to see something familiar with fresh eyes.

If these pages have given you comfort during difficult moments...

clarity during confusing moments...

or simply a moment of honest reflection...

then they have already done more than I could have hoped for.

And if they have left you with more questions than answers...

that may not be a failure.

It may be the beginning of a deeper conversation.

A conversation not with this book.

But with yourself.

Wherever you are in your journey...

whether you are searching...
