Searching ki wajah se.

Continuously improve karne ki wajah se.

Continuously compare karne ki wajah se.

Continuously arrive karne ki koshish ki wajah se.

Aur ek point ke baad...

insaan destination se zyada rest chahta hai.

Kabhi kabhi ek strange moment aata hai.

Not because everything is solved.

Not because every question is answered.

But because something inside quietly says:

"Bas."

Aur us moment mein...

search force se nahi rukti.

Wo naturally slow padne lagti hai.

Jaise ek river finally samundar tak pahunch gayi ho.

Ye realization shocking ho sakti hai.

Kyuki poori life hume sikhaya gaya:

Become more.
