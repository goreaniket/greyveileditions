Ye kitab answers dene ki koshish nahi karegi.

Ye tumhe koi new belief bhi nahi degi.

Ye tumse kuch maanne ko bhi nahi kahegi.

Ye sirf observe karegi.

Carefully.

Quietly.

Honestly.

Aur shayad observation ke dauraan...

kuch aisa nazar aa jaye...

jo pehle hamesha se saamne tha...

lekin kabhi dekha nahi gaya.

Agar tum certainty dhoond rahe ho...

toh shayad ye kitab tumhe disappoint kare.

Agar tum final conclusions dhoond rahe ho...

toh bhi.

Lekin agar tum genuinely dekhna chahte ho...

ki human experience ki deepest layers mein kya chhupa hai...

Toh shayad ye journey worth hogi.

Kyuki kabhi kabhi...

sabse extraordinary discovery koi nayi cheez nahi hoti.

Kabhi kabhi...
