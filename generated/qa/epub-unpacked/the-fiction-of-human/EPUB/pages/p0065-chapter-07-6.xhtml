Ye argument se nahi girti.

Ye belief change karne se nahi girti.

Ye sirf observation se dissolve hone lagti hai.

Jaise darkness light ke saamne fight nahi karti.

Waise hi separation seeing ke saamne survive nahi karta.

Aur phir dheere dheere...

ek soft realization emerge hoti hai.

Shayad story aur storyteller bhi utne alag nahi the.

Bas ek silence par.

Ek possibility par.
