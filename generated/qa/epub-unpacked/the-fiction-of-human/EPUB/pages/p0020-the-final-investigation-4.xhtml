Carefully.

Patiently.

Without rushing toward conclusions.

Hum identity ko dekhenge.

Narratives ko.

Perception ko.

Separation ko.

Aur us psychological structure ko...

jise hum "human" kehte aaye hain.

Ho sakta hai is journey ke end par tumhe koi final answer na mile.

Ho sakta hai koi ultimate conclusion bhi na mile.

Lekin shayad kuch aur mile.

Ek crack.

Ek opening.

Ek different way of seeing.

Kyuki kabhi kabhi transformation new beliefs se nahi aati.

Transformation us moment se aati hai...

jab pehli baar kuch aisa visible ho jata hai...

jo hamesha se saamne tha.

Is kitab ko certainty ke saath mat padhna.

Isse curiosity ke saath padhna.
