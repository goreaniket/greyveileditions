Is moment mein...

kuch missing nahi hai.

Aur shayad...

ye poori journey ka final realization bhi wahi hai.

Tum kabhi broken project nahi the.

Tum kabhi unfinished mistake nahi the.

Tum kabhi koi problem nahi the jise solve karna tha.

Tum sirf stories ke jungle mein bhatak gaye the.

Aur ek din...

search karte karte...

samajhte samajhte...

bante bante...

tootte tootte...

tumne mud kar dekha.

Aur jo dhoond rahe the...

wo kahin aage nahi tha.

Wo kabhi gaya hi nahi tha.

Wo yahin tha.

Isi saans mein.

Isi khamoshi mein.

Isi pal mein.
