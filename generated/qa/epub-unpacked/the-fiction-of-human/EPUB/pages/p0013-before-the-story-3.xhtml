Poore life hum ek assumption ke saath jeete hain.

Ek assumption itna basic...

ki wo almost invisible ho jata hai.

Wo assumption hai:

"Mujhe pata hai main kaun hoon."

Aur isi assumption ke upar...

identity build hoti hai.

Meaning build hota hai.

Goals build hote hain.

Relationships build hote hain.

Poora psychological world build hota hai.

Lekin agar foundation ko hi kabhi properly examine na kiya gaya ho...

toh?

Human sirf survive nahi karta.

Human imagine karta hai.

Remember karta hai.

Narrate karta hai.
