PART 5 - THE BREAK

When Holding On Feels Safer Than Starting Again:

Kabhi kabhi tum samajh jaate ho ki cheez tumhe tod rahi hai…

phir bhi tum usse chhod nahi paate.

Aur ye contradiction simple nahi hota…

ye ek silent internal conflict hota hai jo explain nahi hota.

Bahaar se sab log sochte hain:

“bas chhod de na”

par andar se itna simple kuch hota hi nahi.

Kyuki tum sirf cheez ko hold nahi kar rahe hote…

tum us cheez ke saath judi hui purani version of self ko hold kar rahe hote ho.

73
