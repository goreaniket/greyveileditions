DISCLAIMER

This book is not written to guide you.

It is written to make you notice what you are already doing inside your mind.

It does not claim to heal, fix, or improve anything.

It only observes how emotional experience is built — through repetition,

interpretation, and silent reinforcement of thought loops.

If at any point this feels personal…

it is not because the book is speaking about you.

It is because the mind recognizes its own structure.
