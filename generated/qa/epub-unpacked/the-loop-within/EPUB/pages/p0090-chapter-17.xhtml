PART 6 - THE SHIFT

When You Realize Nothing Is Coming Back :

Kabhi kabhi ek din aata hai…

jab tum kisi cheez ko “wait” karna band nahi karte…

par tumhe samajh aa jata hai ki wait ka koi endpoint nahi hai.

Na koi message aata hai…

na koi sign…

na koi sudden reversal…

bas silence same rehta hai.

Aur pehle tum is silence ko interpret karte the…

shayad abhi time nahi aaya”

shayad abhi kuch baaki hai”

“shayad abhi return possible hai”
