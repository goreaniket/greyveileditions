wo light hota hai.

Jaise kisi ne andar se pressure hata diya ho…

bina kuch replace kiye.

Aur is lightness ka koi explanation nahi hota…

sirf experience hota hai.

Aur tum notice karte ho:

cheez ab bhi yaad hai

par wo yaad ab pull nahi karti

Aur ye shift bahut silent hota hai…

par powerful hota hai.

Aur jab tum is state ko control nahi karte…

bas allow karte ho…

toh ek stable sa space develop hota hai.

Na excitement…

na pain spike…

bas ek grounded silence.

Aur is silence mein tumhe pehli baar lagta hai:

har cheez ko hold karna zaruri nahi hai

kuch cheezein sirf pass hone ke liye hoti hain

Aur jab tum pass hone dete ho…

toh andar ka load automatically light ho jata hai.
