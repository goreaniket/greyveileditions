baithte ho…

chal rahe hote ho…

par andar ek ongoing conversation chalti rehti hai.

Aur jab ye long enough chal jata hai…

toh ek feeling aati hai…

ki “kuch adhura hai”

Par agar thoda aur honestly dekho…

toh adhura kuch hota hi nahi…

bas mind usko baar baar chalu rakhta hai.

Aur jab tum ye notice karte ho…

toh pehli baar kuch forcefully break nahi hota…

bas ek chhota sa gap aata hai.

Aur us gap mein tum kuch change karne ki koshish nahi karte…

bas dekhte ho ki ye chal raha hai.

Aur jab dekhna shuru hota hai bina react kiye…

toh conversation apne aap dheemi ho jaati hai.

Na tum usko rok rahe hote ho…

na tum usko chase kar rahe hote ho…

bas tum uske witness ban jaate ho.

Aur yahi se ek soft release start hota hai…
