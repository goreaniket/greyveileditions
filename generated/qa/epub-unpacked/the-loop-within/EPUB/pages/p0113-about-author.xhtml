ABOUT THE AUTHOR

LALIT R MISHRA writes from the space between thought and observation.

His work explores the psychological patterns that shape human emotion —

especially the silent loops of memory, attachment, and meaning-making that

people often live inside without realizing.

Rather than focusing on events or stories, his writing focuses on the internal

structure of experience itself — how the mind repeats, distorts, and

reconstructs reality until it begins to feel personal.

He does not offer answers or instruction.
