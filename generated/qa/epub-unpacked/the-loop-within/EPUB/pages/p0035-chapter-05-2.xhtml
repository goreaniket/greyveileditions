Aur interesting cheez ye hoti hai…

wo memory sirf recall nahi hoti…

wo reconstruction hoti hai.

Mind us moment ko waise nahi rakhta jaise wo tha…

mind us moment ko waise recreate karta hai jaise wo feel hota tha.

Isliye tumhe lagta hai ki tum “yaad” kar rahe ho…

par asal mein tum us experience ko dubara build kar rahe hote ho

present ke context ke bina.

Aur jab koi cheez bina context ke repeat hoti hai…

toh wo reality se zyada strong feel hone lagti hai.

Kyuki reality change hoti rehti hai…

par memory fixed lagti hai.

Aur mind fixed cheez ko anchor bana leta hai.

Aur yahi pe ek subtle distortion start hota hai:

present unstable lagta hai

past stable lagta hai

Aur stability ka illusion mind ko attract karta hai.

Isliye tum present ko underestimate kar dete ho…

aur past ko overvalue.
