PART 1 - THE TRAP

When It Doesn’t End, It Just Repeats in Your Mind:

Kabhi kabhi problem ye nahi hoti ki kuch hua hai…

problem ye hoti hai ki kuch ho hi nahi raha hota…

par phir bhi mind mein sab kuch chal raha hota hai.

Tum physically present hote ho…

par mentally fully present nahi hote.

Ye cheez tum notice bhi nahi karte hamesha.

Kyuki tum kaam kar rahe hote ho, baat kar rahe hote ho, normal behave kar

rahe hote ho…

par uske peeche ek aur layer chal rahi hoti hai.

Ek hi memory

ek hi moment
