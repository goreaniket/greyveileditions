Aur jab ye pattern long time chal jata hai…

toh story ek external memory nahi rehti…

wo ek internal environment ban jati hai.

Jahan tum physically kisi aur moment mein hote ho…

par mentally wahi scene baar baar run ho raha hota hai.

Aur sabse subtle part ye hota hai…

tumhe lagta hai tum us story ko yaad kar rahe ho…

par asal mein tum us story ko phir se jee rahe hote ho.

Har baar thoda differently…

har baar thoda emotionally changed…

par essence same hota hai.

Aur jab tum baar baar us moment ko revisit karte ho…

toh wo moment “past” nahi rehta…

wo ek open loop ban jata hai.

Aur open loop ka nature hota hai…

wo closure maangta rehta hai…

par closure usi loop ke andar nahi milta.

Isliye tum jitna andar jaate ho…

utna hi aur stuck feel hota hai.
