PART 5 - THE BREAK

When You Are Afraid to Lose the Feeling, Not the Person :

Kabhi kabhi tumhe lagta hai tum kisi insaan ko yaad kar rahe ho…

par agar thoda aur andar jaake dekho…+

toh wo yaad actually insaan ki nahi hoti.

Wo ek state hoti hai.

Ek aisa emotional phase jahan tum:

zyada alive feel karte the

zyada connected lagte the

chhoti chhoti cheezein meaningful lagti thi

silence bhi heavy nahi, “full” lagta tha

Aur jab wo phase chala jata hai…

toh tumhe lagta hai insaan chala gaya.
