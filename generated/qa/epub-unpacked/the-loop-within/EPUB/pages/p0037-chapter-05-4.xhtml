tumhe lagta hai tum apni life jee rahe ho…

par tum apni memory ke through life ko filter kar rahe hote ho.

Aur jab filter strong ho jata hai…

toh asli reality soft ho jati hai.

jab tum khud se puchte ho:

“ye jo main feel kar raha hoon… kya ye abhi ka hai ya purana echo

hai?”

Aur us question ka answer loud nahi hota…

wo bas thoda sa silence create karta hai.
