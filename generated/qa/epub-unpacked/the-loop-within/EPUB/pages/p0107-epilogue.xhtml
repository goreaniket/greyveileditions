EPILOGUE

After everything ends… but nothing is forced to end inside you.

Kabhi kabhi jab tum is book ko band karte ho…

toh koi dramatic feeling nahi aati…

na relief jaisa burst…

na sadness ka weight…

bas ek normal sa silence hota hai…

jo pehle thoda unfamiliar lagta hai…

aur phir dheere dheere normal lagne lagta hai.

Aur yahi pe ek strange realization hota hai…

ki tumhe kisi cheez se “bahar nikalne” ki zarurat utni nahi thi…

jitni tum samajh rahe the.

Kyuki jo tum “phase” samajh rahe the…

wo ek emotional loop tha…

jo continuously repeat ho raha tha…

same thoughts… same interpretations… same meaning creation.

Aur jab tum ye observe karte ho…

toh ek simple si clarity aati hai:
