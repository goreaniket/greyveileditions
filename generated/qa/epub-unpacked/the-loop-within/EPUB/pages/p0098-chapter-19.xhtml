PART 6 - THE SHIFT

When You Finally Choose Yourself Without Realizing:

Kabhi kabhi life mein ek moment aata hai…

jo kisi decision ki tarah nahi lagta…

par baad mein tum samajh paate ho ki wahi turning point tha.

Na koi announcement hoti hai…

na koi dramatic goodbye…

na koi final message…

bas ek din tum us cheez ke baare mein utna nahi sochte…

jitna pehle sochte the.

Aur ye “kam sochna” intentional nahi hota…

ye natural exhaustion of attachment hota hai.
