wo simple presence hota hai.

Na kisi ka absence heavy lagta hai…

na kisi ka presence zaruri lagta hai…

Bas tum aur tumhari life hoti hai…

without constant comparison.

Aur ye point pe tum pehli baar samajhte ho:

“move on” ek struggle nahi tha

wo ek side effect tha of letting go of resistance

Aur ab tumhe kisi ko prove nahi karna padta…

ki tum theek ho.

Kyuki theek hona ab ek state nahi…

ek absence of noise jaisa lagta hai.

Aur yahin pe story khatam nahi hoti…

par loop khatam ho jata hai.

Ab jab tum yaad karte ho…

toh wo yaad tumhe todti nahi…

bas guzar jaati hai.

Aur jab kuch guzar jaata hai bina rukhe…

toh uska naam hota hai:

peace

Na loud… na dramatic…
