ek hi thought

baar baar… repeat hota rehta hai.

Aur kabhi kabhi tumhe khud bhi samajh nahi aata ki

ye soch rahe ho ya bas chal raha hai.

Aur honestly… ye clear bhi nahi hota pehle.

Bas ek strange sa background hota hai dimag mein…

jo kabhi fully band nahi hota.

Tum us cheez ko “soch” nahi rahe hote…

tum uske andar thode se phase hue hote ho.

Aur ye difference start mein samajh nahi aata.

Tumhe lagta hai tum control mein ho…

par agar thoda observe karo toh pata chalta hai ki

control present ka nahi hota…

control kisi purani cheez ke paas hota hai.

Aur ek aur cheez hoti hai…

tum ignore karne ki koshish karte ho…

par wo ignore nahi hota.

bas aur quiet ho jata hai…

par jata nahi.

Isliye tumhe lagta hai ki tum aage badh gaye ho…

par andar kuch same hi chal raha hota hai.
