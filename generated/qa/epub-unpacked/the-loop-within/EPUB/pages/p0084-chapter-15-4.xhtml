kyuki tum already “move” ho rahe hote ho…

par sirf apne hi circle mein.

Aur jab tum is ego-pain loop ko sirf observe karte ho…

na defend karte ho…

na justify…

toh ek chhota sa gap create hota hai.

Aur us gap mein tumhe pehli baar clearly dikhta hai:

tum apne pain nahi ho

tum apne ego nahi ho

tum sirf ek experience ke observer ho

Aur jab identification thodi loose hoti hai…

toh loop apna grip thoda chhod deta hai.

Na force se…

na effort se…

bas clarity se...
