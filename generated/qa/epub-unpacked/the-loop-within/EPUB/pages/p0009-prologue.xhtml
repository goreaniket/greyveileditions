PROLOGUE

( Don’t read this to understand it. Read it to notice what it does to you).

Kya tum kabhi kisi insaan ko miss karte ho…

ya tum bas apne us version ko miss kar rahe hote ho jo us insaan ke saamne

zinda feel karta tha?

Kya ho sakta hai ki tum jis “move on” ko failure samajh rahe ho…

wo failure nahi…

tumhara mind ka refusal to let a pattern die ho?

Aur ek aur uncomfortable sawal…

kya tum sach mein kisi insaan se stuck ho…

ya tum ek aise emotional loop se addicted ho…

jo tumhe lagta hai tum hi ho?

Aur ab thoda aur andar jao…

kyuki yahan se cheezein personal nahi lagengi…

cheezein pehli baar mechanical lagengi…

jaise tum apne hi thoughts ko observe kar rahe ho…

na ki unhe live kar rahe ho.
