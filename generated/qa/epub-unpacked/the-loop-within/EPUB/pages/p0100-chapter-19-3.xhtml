toh attachment apne aap loose hone lagta hai.

Aur jab attachment loose hota hai…

toh dard apni jagah pe hota hua bhi distant lagne lagta hai.

Jaise koi purani awaaz…

jo pehle andar loud chalti thi…

ab background mein chali gayi ho.

Aur tumhe samajh aata hai:

kuch cheezein complete nahi hoti

wo bas irrelevant ho jaati hain

Aur ye realization pehli baar dard nahi deta…

ye clarity deta hai.

Aur jab tum kisi cheez ko hold nahi karte…

na force se… na decision se…

bas naturally release hone dete ho…

toh ek chhota sa truth visible hota hai:

tum kabhi kisi cheez ke andar stuck the hi nahi

tum bas apne hi thoughts ke loop mein the

Aur jab loop toot-ta hai…

toh jo bachta hai…

wo empty nahi hota…
