Jaise mind baar baar ek hi room mein jaa raha ho…

aur har baar same answer mil raha ho…

toh dheere dheere jaana kam ho jata hai.

Aur sabse strange cheez ye hoti hai…

tumhe lagta hai tum kisi cheez se door ho rahe ho…

par asal mein tum kisi cheez se door nahi ja rahe…

tum apne andar wapas aa rahe ho.

Aur pehli baar tum notice karte ho…

ki tumhara din ab us ek cheez ke around orbit nahi karta.

Na phone check karna…

na overthinking loops…

na silent expectations…

na imaginary conversations…

Sab kuch thoda slow ho jata hai…

par heavy nahi lagta.

Aur yahi pe ek silent shift hota hai:

tum “wait” karna band nahi karte…

tum wait ko importance dena band kar dete ho

Aur ye difference sab kuch badal deta hai.

Kyuki jab importance kam hoti hai…
