Aur jab present us version ko break karta hai…

toh dard insaan se nahi hota…

dard illusion collapse se hota hai.

Aur interesting cheez ye hoti hai…

tum jitna zyada kisi version ko idealize karte ho…

utna hi present uske against weak lagta hai.

Par agar thoda aur deeply dekho…

toh samajh aata hai ki:

na present galat hai…

na past correct tha…

dono bas different states the mind ke.

Aur mind ka kaam hi ye hota hai…

wo states ko freeze karke identity bana deta hai.

Isliye tum kisi insaan se attach nahi hote…

tum ek frozen emotional frame se attach ho jate ho.

Aur jab wo frame crack hota hai…

toh confusion hota hai ki “kya change ho gaya?”

par change bahar nahi hota…

change perception ke andar hota hai.

Aur dheere dheere ek silent realization build hota hai…
