ye slow hota hai.

Ek normal moment hota hai…

aur usi ke beech mein achanak ek memory aa jati hai.

Aur interesting cheez ye hai…

wo memory puri nahi hoti.

Bas ek fragment hota hai.

Ek expression

ek word

ek situation ka chhota sa part

Par us ek chhote se fragment ka effect poore present moment pe pad jata hai.

Present thoda sa dull ho jata hai…

aur past thoda sa sharp.

Aur tumhe lagta hai ki tum bas “yaad” kar rahe ho…

par reality thodi different hoti hai.

Tum observe karoge toh samajh aata hai ki

tum yaad nahi kar rahe hote…

tum us cheez ko dubara experience kar rahe hote ho

but without the real situation.

Aur yahi pe mind ka pattern interesting ho jata hai…

wo memory ko memory ki tarah nahi rakhta…
