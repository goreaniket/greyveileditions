Aur ye “shayad” hi last thread hota hai…

jo tumhe loop mein rakhta hai.

Par ek din…

ye “shayad” bhi thoda weak pad jata hai.

Aur tumhe pehli baar clarity nahi…

par exhaustion clarity jaisa feel hota hai.

Jaise andar koi energy hi nahi bachi ho argument karne ki.

Aur wahi pe realization hit karta hai:

jo cheez tum rok rahe the… wo already ruk chuki thi

Aur jab tum is realization ko force nahi karte…

bas notice karte ho…

ki haan, ye ho raha hai…

toh ek chhota sa gap banta hai.

Na relief turant aata hai…

na sadness aur badhta hai…

bas ek neutral silence aata hai.

Aur us silence mein tumhe pehli baar ye feel hota hai:

expectation ab pressure nahi hai

Aur jab expectation drop hoti hai…
