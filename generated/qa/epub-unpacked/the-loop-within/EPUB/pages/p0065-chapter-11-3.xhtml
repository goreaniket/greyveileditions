Aur dheere dheere tum ek strange pattern notice karte ho…

ki tum har cheez ko thoda zyada effort se kar rahe ho.

Simple cheezein bhi heavy lagti hain…

reply karna…

sochna…

ya even bas “theek hoon” kehna.

Aur ye heaviness emotion nahi hoti…

ye emotional saturation hoti hai.

Jaise andar already bahut kuch bhara hua ho…

par koi spill nahi hua ho.

Aur jab spill nahi hota…

toh pressure andar hi rehta hai.

Aur interesting observation ye hai…

tum jab is state mein hote ho…

toh tum “problem” dhoondhne lagte ho.

Kyuki mind ko lagta hai:

agar reason mil gaya toh solve ho jayega

Par kabhi kabhi reason hota hi nahi…

sirf accumulation hota hai.
