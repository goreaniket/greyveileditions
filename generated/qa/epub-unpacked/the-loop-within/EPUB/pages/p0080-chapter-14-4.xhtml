par attachment tum carry karte ho.

Aur wahi attachment tumhe anchor karta hai…

chahe wo anchor tumhe rok hi kyun na raha ho.

Aur jab tum is holding ko fight nahi karte…

sirf notice karte ho ki ye ho raha hai…

toh ek chhota sa realization aata hai…

tum cheez se zyada apne fear ko hold kar rahe ho

Aur fear ko samajhna hi uska weight thoda kam kar deta hai.

Aur dheere dheere tumhe ye feel hota hai:

naya start loss nahi hai

wo ek empty space hai jisme naya version grow ho sakta hai

Aur space ko fill karne ki zarurat nahi hoti…

space ko allow karna hota hai.
