main samajh gaya hoon sab”

mujhe farak nahi padta”

Aur pain kehta hai:

main important tha”

main ignore nahi kiya ja sakta”

main incomplete hoon”

Aur jab ye dono saath chalne lagte hain…

toh ek strange sa internal system ban jata hai.

Jahan tum move on nahi karte…

tum justify karte rehte ho apne stuck hone ko.

Aur ye justification hi tumhara hidden comfort ban jata hai.

Kyuki jab tum pain ko justify kar rahe hote ho…

toh tum usse feel karne se bach rahe hote ho.

Aur jab tum ego se protect kar rahe hote ho…

toh tum vulnerability se bach rahe hote ho.

Aur dono milke ek safe loop create kar dete hain…

jisme tum “phase” mein rehte ho…

par us phase se bahar nahi aate.

Aur sabse subtle part ye hota hai…
