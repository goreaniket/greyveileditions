Wo gap silence jaisa nahi hota…

wo clarity jaisa hota hai.

Aur us clarity mein tumhe ye samajh aata hai:

tum broken nahi ho

tum bas over-referencing kar rahe ho

Aur jab referencing kam hoti hai…

toh present thoda zyada visible ho jata hai.

Aur present ko fix nahi karna padta…

use sirf dekha jata hai.
