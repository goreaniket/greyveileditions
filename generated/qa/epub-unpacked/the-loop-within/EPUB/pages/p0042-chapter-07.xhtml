PART 2 - THE DISTORTION

When Your Mind Starts Editing the Truth :

Kabhi kabhi tumhe lagta hai ki tumhe sab kuch clearly yaad hai…

par agar thoda ruk ke observe karo…

toh ek strange realization aata hai…

tum yaad nahi kar rahe hote…

tum edit kar rahe hote ho.

Aur ye editing jaan-bujhkar nahi hoti.

Ye mind ka ek natural process hota hai…

jo har baar memory ko thoda sa reshape karta rehta hai.

Shuruaat mein ek moment hota hai…

jaise wo ek simple memory ho.
