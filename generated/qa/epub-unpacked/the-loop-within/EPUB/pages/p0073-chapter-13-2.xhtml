Normal koi clear cheez nahi lagti…

wo ek blurry sa reference ban jata hai.

Aur jab reference blur ho jata hai…

toh har feeling thodi suspicious lagne lagti hai.

ye main theek feel kar raha hoon ya bas adjust kar liya hai?”

ye khushi hai ya temporary distraction?”

ye silence peace hai ya numbness?”

Aur yahi pe mind ek strange loop bana leta hai…

wo har state ko analyze karna start kar deta hai…

jabki pehle wo sirf jeeta tha.

Aur dheere dheere tum ek observer ban jaate ho…

par problem ye hai…

tum jeene ke saath observe nahi kar rahe hote…

tum jeene ke jagah observe kar rahe hote ho.

Aur jab observation overload ho jata hai…

toh experience thoda fade ho jata hai.

Aur tumhe lagta hai ki tum “lost” ho gaye ho…

par asal mein tum lost nahi hote…

tum bas continuous comparison mode mein hote ho.
