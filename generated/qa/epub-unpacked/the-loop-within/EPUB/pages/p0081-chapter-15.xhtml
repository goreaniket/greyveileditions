PART 5 - THE BREAK

When Ego and Pain Become

Comfort Zones :

Kabhi kabhi tumhe lagta hai tum aage badh rahe ho…

par agar thoda aur honestly dekho…

toh tum actually “aage” nahi ja rahe hote…

tum ek purani jagah ko hi naye naam de rahe hote ho.

Aur wo jagah hoti hai:

ego + pain ka mixture

Aur ye mixture dheere dheere ek comfort zone bana deta hai…

par aisa comfort jo soft nahi hota…

jo familiar hota hai.
