Aur silence ka sabse strange part ye hai…

wo tumhe disturb nahi karta…

wo tumhe expose karta hai.

Kyuki jab sab kuch quiet ho jata hai…

tab tumhe wo cheezein clearly dikhne lagti hain

jo din ke noise mein hide ho jaati hain.

Aur us moment pe tumhe samajh aata hai…

ki tumhara mind actually khali nahi hai…

wo overloaded hai.

Bas sound band hone se overload visible ho jata hai.

Isliye log silence se bhagte nahi…

log silence ko delay karte hain.

Busy rehkar…

scroll kar ke…

baat kar ke…

ya kisi aur distraction se.

Par silence apna kaam karta rehta hai…

wo disappear nahi hota…

wo wait nahi karta…

wo return hota rehta hai.

Aur dheere dheere ek pattern clear hota hai…
