Har feeling ko tum kisi purani feeling se compare karte ho…

aur jab match nahi hota…

toh wo feeling “wrong” lagti hai.

Par truth ye hota hai…

feelings ka koi fixed pattern nahi hota.

Har din tum same version mein nahi hote…

har moment thoda change hota hai.

Par mind stability ko ideal bana deta hai…

aur fluctuation ko problem.

Isliye jab tum fluctuate karte ho…

toh tum apne aap ko unstable samajhne lagte ho.

Aur sabse subtle part ye hota hai…

tum “normal” ko ek destination bana dete ho…

jabki wo ek moment-to-moment state hoti hai.

Aur jab destination nahi milta…

toh journey heavy lagne lagti hai.

Aur jab tum comparison ko thoda side rakhte ho…

sirf moment ko notice karte ho…

bina label diye…

toh ek strange sa gap aata hai.
