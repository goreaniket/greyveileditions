Par sach thoda aur subtle hota hai…

insaan nahi gaya hota

tumhara andar ka emotional environment change ho gaya hota hai

Aur mind ko environment ka loss insaan ka loss lagta hai.

Isliye tum baar baar usi insaan ko recall karte ho…

par har recall actually ek feeling ko activate karta hai…

na ki presence ko.

Aur yahi pe ek invisible confusion start hota hai…

tum relationship ko miss nahi kar rahe hote…

tum us version of yourself ko miss kar rahe hote ho jo us relationship mein

exist karta tha.

Aur wo version:

thoda softer tha

thoda more open tha

thoda more hopeful tha

thoda more emotionally alive tha

Aur jab wo version fade hota hai…
