Reality ye hai:

jo cheez time ke saath badalti nahi… wo ya toh truth hoti hai

ya phir sirf mind ki repeat ki hui image hoti hai.

Aur mind images ko truth bana deta hai…

kyuki repetition usse familiar lagta hai.

Aur familiar cheez safe lagti hai…

chahe wo painful hi kyun na ho.

Isliye tu “pain” ko chhod nahi pata…

kyuki tu pain ko nahi chhod raha hota…

tu familiarity ko chhod raha hota hai.

Aur yahi sabse real shift hai:

tu kisi insaan se nahi lad raha

tu apne hi mind ke pattern se lad raha tha

Aur jab ye samajh aata hai…

toh ek cheez automatic hoti hai:

control kam hota hai

aur clarity badhti hai

Aur clarity koi emotion nahi hoti…

wo ek simple recognition hoti hai:

ye main nahi hoon… ye sirf ek process hai”
