PART 2 - THE DISTORTION

When You Fall in Love With a Version That No

Longer Exists:

Kabhi kabhi tumhe lagta hai ki tum kisi insaan ko miss kar rahe ho…

par agar thoda aur quietly observe karo…

toh samajh aata hai ki tum actually kisi “insaan” ko nahi…

tum ek version ko hold kiye hue ho.

Wo version jo ek time pe real lagta tha…

par ab sirf memory ke andar live karta hai.

Aur interesting cheez ye hoti hai…

tum present insaan ko dekh rahe hote ho…
