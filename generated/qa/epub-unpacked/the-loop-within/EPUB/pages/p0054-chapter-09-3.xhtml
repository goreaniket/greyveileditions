par imagination ko tum repetition se truth bana dete ho.

Aur jab ye repetition baar baar hota hai…

toh ek illusion create hota hai…

ki shayad ye baat kabhi hui hi thi.

Par agar sach mein dekho…

toh wo sirf tumhare mind ke andar ka echo hota hai.

Aur interesting cheez ye hai…

har baar jab tum us conversation mein wapas jaate ho…

wo thodi si change ho jati hai.

Kabhi tum zyada strong reply dete ho…

kabhi tum chup rehte ho…

kabhi tum jeet jaate ho…

kabhi tum explain karte ho…

Aur dheere dheere ek parallel reality ban jati hai…

jisme tumhe lagta hai ki tum closure paa rahe ho…

par asal mein tum closure ko postpone kar rahe hote ho.

Aur yahi loop sabse silent hota hai…

kyuki bahar se kuch dikhai nahi deta.

Tum bas khade hote ho…
