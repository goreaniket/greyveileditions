Aur yahi pe ek silent realization aata hai…

ki shayad problem story nahi hai…

problem ye hai ki tum us story ko “complete” accept hi nahi kar pa rahe ho.

Aur jab ye samajh aata hai…

toh kuch forcefully break nahi hota…

bas ek gentle loosening hoti hai andar.

Na memories disappear hoti hain…

na feelings suddenly gayab hoti hain…

par unka hold thoda halka ho jata hai.

Aur pehli baar tum ye notice karte ho:

tumhe har story ko solve karne ki zarurat nahi

tumhe har emotion ko finish karne ki zarurat nahi

Kabhi kabhi bas itna kaafi hota hai…

ki tum maan lo:

“ye moment ab yahan complete hai”

Aur jab tum “complete” maan lete ho…

toh mind dheere dheere us loop ko release kar deta hai…

bina force ke…

bina fight ke…
