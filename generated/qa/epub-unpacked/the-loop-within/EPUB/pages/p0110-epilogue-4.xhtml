na kisi ki presence necessary…

Bas tum aur tumhara present moment…

ek normal sa coexistence.

Aur agar tum honestly dekho…

toh tumhe realize hoga:

tumhe kabhi kisi cheez se nikalna nahi tha

tumhe bas apne hi loop ko dekhna tha

Aur jab loop dikh jata hai…

toh wo tumhe pakad nahi pata.

Aur jab wo tumhe pakad nahi pata…

toh tum automatically free ho jate ho.

Na koi effort…

na koi fight…

na koi “move on karna”…

bas ek simple sa change:

pehle tum thought mein hote the

ab tum thought ko dekh rahe ho

Aur yahi pe sab kuch quietly change ho jata hai…

bina kisi announcement ke.

Not moving on…

just no longer being moved by the same loop.
