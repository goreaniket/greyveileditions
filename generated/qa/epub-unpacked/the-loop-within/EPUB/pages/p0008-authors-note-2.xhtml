you were not living events… you were reinterpreting them again and again

until they felt real enough to suffer from.

This book does not offer closure.

Because closure is just another form of continuation when the mind refuses to

drop the pattern.

It only does one thing…

it exposes repetition.

And repetition, once seen clearly, stops feeling like emotion…

and starts feeling like structure.

That is where everything changes.

Not because something ends…

but because something stops being believed in the same way.

And at that point, two sentences stop being opposites

“I can’t move on”

“I’m moving on”

Because both were never about movement.

They were about how deeply the loop was still active.

— Lalit.R.Mishra
