bina tumse pooche.

Aur tumhe lagta hai tum control mein ho…

kyuki thoughts tumhare hi dimaag mein aa rahe hain…

par reality ye hoti hai…

aana control mein hota hai

par rukna pattern decide karta hai

Aur yahi pe ek subtle deception hota hai…

tum sochte ho:

“main is baare mein soch raha hoon”

par asal mein:

“ye baat tumhe sochne par majboor kar rahi hai”

Aur difference chhota lagta hai…

par yahi difference poora loop banata hai.

Aur interesting observation ye hai…

tum jab overthink karte ho…

tum actually solution nahi dhoond rahe hote…

tum closure feel recreate karne ki koshish kar rahe hote ho.

Par closure memory se nahi milta…

closure acceptance se aata hai…
