log ho sakte hain, sound ho sakta hai, movement ho sakta hai…

par achanak ek moment aata hai…

aur sab kuch slow ho jata hai.

Aur us slowdown ke andar

tumhe koi nayi cheez nahi milti…

tumhe wahi purani cheez milti hai.

Same thought

same memory

same incomplete feeling

jo kabhi complete nahi hua.

Aur interesting observation ye hota hai…

tum silence ko avoid nahi karte…

tum silence mein “shift” ho jate ho.

Jaise dimag ke andar ek automatic route ho

jo tumhe wahi le jata hai baar baar.

Aur ye route tum consciously choose nahi karte…

ye already built hota hai.

Isliye lagta hai ki tum free ho…

par kuch cheezein already repeat mode mein chal rahi hoti hain.
