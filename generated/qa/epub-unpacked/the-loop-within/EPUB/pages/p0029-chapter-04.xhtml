PART 1 - THE TRAP

When You Confuse Missing with Meaning:

Kabhi kabhi tumhe lagta hai ki jo tum feel kar rahe ho…

wo sirf feeling nahi hai.

Wo kuch zyada hai.

Jaise tumhe lagta hai ki

agar tum kisi ko miss kar rahe ho…

toh uska matlab hai ki wo cheez important thi.

Aur yahin se ek silent assumption start hota hai…

“jo cheez mujhe affect karti hai… wo meaningful hogi”

Par agar thoda observe karo…

toh tumhe samajh aata hai ki

tum missing ko observe nahi kar rahe hote…
