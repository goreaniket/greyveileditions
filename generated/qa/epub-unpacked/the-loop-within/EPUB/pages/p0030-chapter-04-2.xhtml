tum usko interpret kar rahe hote ho.

Aur interpretation kabhi neutral nahi hoti.

Wo meaning create karti hai.

Isliye tum ek simple cheez ko

dheere dheere complex bana dete ho.

Ek memory → feeling ban jati hai

feeling → story ban jati hai

story → truth lagne lagti hai

Aur tum notice bhi nahi karte kab ye shift hua.

Kyuki mind intensity ko truth samajhne lagta hai.

Agar kuch cheez zyada feel hoti hai…

toh tumhe lagta hai wo zyada real hai.

Par reality mein…

feeling intensity ≠ truth clarity

Aur yahi pe distortion quietly enter karta hai.

Tum kisi ko miss karte ho…

par dheere dheere tum sirf insaan ko nahi miss karte…

tum us emotional state ko miss karne lagte ho

jo us insaan ke saath linked tha.

Aur ye difference tum start mein catch nahi karte.

Kyuki mind dono ko merge kar deta hai.
