PART 4 - THE WEIGHT

When Everything Feels Heavy Without Any

Reason:

Kabhi kabhi tum apni hi life mein hote ho…

par feel aisa hota hai jaise tum usmein fully present nahi ho.

Sab kuch normal hota hai…

phir bhi andar kuch “normal” nahi lagta.

Na koi bada incident hota hai…

na koi clear pain trigger hota hai…

par phir bhi ek weight hota hai…

jo explain nahi hota.

Aur sabse confusing part ye hota hai…

tumhe lagta hai ki tumhe “kuch samajh nahi aa raha”…
