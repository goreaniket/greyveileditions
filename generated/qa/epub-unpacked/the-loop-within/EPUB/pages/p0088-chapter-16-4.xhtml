pehle jaisa connection nahi banta

pehle jaisa intensity nahi aati

Par tum “pehle” ko present ke against measure kar rahe hote ho…

jo naturally mismatch create karta hai.

Aur jab tum is distinction ko fight nahi karte…

bas dekhte ho ki ye pattern ho raha hai…

toh ek subtle shift aata hai.

Tumhe pehli baar samajh aata hai:

jo feeling thi wo tumhari thi

insaan sirf usko activate kar raha tha

Aur jab ownership clarity aati hai…

toh dependency thodi si loosen ho jati hai.

Aur tumhe space milta hai ye samajhne ka:

wo feeling kisi ek insaan tak limited nahi thi

Aur yahi realization slowly ek naya opening create karta hai…

jahan tum purani feeling ko “loss” nahi…

“experience” ki tarah dekh paate ho.
