tumhe lagta hai tum aware ho.

Par awareness bhi kabhi kabhi ek shield ban jati hai…

jisme tum apne pain ko analyse karte ho…

par release nahi karte.

Aur analysis ek illusion deta hai:

main heal kar raha hoon”

par reality mein tum sirf repeat kar rahe hote ho.

Aur yahi pe ego quietly kaam karta hai…

wo tumhe vulnerable hone se rokta hai.

Kyuki vulnerable hona matlab hota hai:

main admit kar raha hoon ki main affected tha”

Aur ego ke liye ye accept karna difficult hota hai.

Isliye tum pain ko hi identity bana lete ho.

Aur dheere dheere tum ek aisi state mein aa jaate ho…

jahan tumhe apna pain hi familiar lagta hai…

aur peace unknown lagti hai.

Aur yahi inversion sabse dangerous hota hai.

Kyuki ab tum heal nahi karte…

tum pain ko maintain karte ho, bina realize kiye.

Aur jab koi tumhe kehta hai “move on”…

toh wo tumhe samajh nahi paata…
