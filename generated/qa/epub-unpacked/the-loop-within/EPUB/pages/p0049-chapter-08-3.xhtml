aur mind acceptance avoid karta hai kyuki wo unknown hota hai.

Isliye mind wahi safe route choose karta hai:

repeat

replay

re-analyze

re-imagine

Aur har repetition ke saath ek illusion build hota hai…

ki shayad ab kuch naya samajh aayega…

par mostly kuch naya samajh nahi aata…

sirf purana aur heavy ho jata hai.

Aur jab ye loop long enough chal jata hai…

toh ek strange situation aati hai…

tum same thought soch rahe hote ho…

par us thought ka emotion ab original nahi hota…

wo sirf echo emotion hota hai.

Jaise kisi purani recording ko baar baar sunna…

aur har baar lagta hai ki live ho raha hai.

Aur tum is echo ko reality samajhne lagte ho.

Aur yahi pe overthinking emotion nahi rehta…
