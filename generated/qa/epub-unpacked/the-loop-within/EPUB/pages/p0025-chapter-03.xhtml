PART 1 - THE TRAP

When Silence Becomes a Place You Visit Too

Often:

Kabhi kabhi silence bas silence nahi hota…

wo ek jagah ban jata hai.

Aisi jagah jahan tum physically toh kahin bhi ho sakte ho…

par tumhara mind baar baar wahi return karta hai.

Aur tumhe ye return karna notice bhi nahi hota.

Kyuki silence empty nahi hota…

wo filled hota hai —

bas awaaz nahi hoti usmein.

Tum kisi room mein ho sakte ho…
