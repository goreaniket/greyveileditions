Aur tum confuse ho jaate ho…

ki ye feeling aa kahan se rahi hai.

Kyuki saamne wala insaan kuch galat nahi kar raha hota…

wo bas apni normal presence mein hota hai.

Par tumhara mind us presence ko present nahi dekhta…

wo usko past ke filter se read karta hai.

Aur yahin pe ek silent distortion start hota hai…

insaan naya hota hai

reaction purana hota hai

Aur tumhe lagta hai ki tum “observe” kar rahe ho…

par asal mein tum reactive memory mode mein hote ho.

Jaise kisi naye chehre pe purani film chal rahi ho…

aur tum us film ko reality samajh lete ho.

Par agar thoda aur honestly dekho…

toh wo insaan trigger nahi hota…

wo sirf ek mirror ban jata hai…

jisme tum apna unresolved past reflect kar rahe hote ho.

Aur sabse interesting cheez ye hoti hai…

tum naye insaan se door nahi hote…
