“what if”

aur ek silent heaviness

Har thought ke saath ek reaction attached hota tha…

jaise mind har memory ko defend ya attack kar raha ho.

Par ek point pe…

wo same thought aata hai…

aur kuch nahi hota.

Na reaction fast hota hai…

na imagination jump karti hai…

na pura emotional loop start hota hai.

Bas ek blank pause.

Aur yahi pe tum confuse hote ho…

main feel kyun nahi kar raha jaise pehle karta tha?”

Par ye numbness nahi hoti…

ye over-fighting ka end hota hai.

Jaise koi cheez itni baar repeat ho chuki ho…

ki ab mind usse response dena hi band kar deta hai.

Aur jab response band hota hai…

toh pehli baar silence clearly sunai deta hai.

Aur ye silence pehle uncomfortable lagta hai…
