Jaise ek invisible mismatch ho:

jo saamne hai

aur jo andar stored hai

Aur tum is mismatch ko start mein catch nahi karte…

kyuki mind dono ko merge kar deta hai.

Person + Feeling + Memory = One illusioned identity

Aur dheere dheere tum us insaan ko “present reality” se nahi dekhte…

tum usko “past emotional frame” se dekhte ho.

Aur jab bhi present kuch alag behave karta hai…

toh tum confuse ho jate ho.

“ye pehle aisa nahi tha…”

par reality ye hoti hai…

wo kabhi waise tha hi nahi —

wo sirf us time ka interpretation tha.

Aur yahin pe ek deep observation aata hai…

tum kisi insaan ko nahi samajh rahe hote…

tum apne mind ke banaye hue version ko defend kar rahe hote ho.
