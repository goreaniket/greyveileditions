Aur yahi pe danger start hota hai…

kyuki jab tum apne thoughts ko live nahi karte…

sirf dekhte ho…

toh pehli baar tumhe samajh aata hai:

kuch bhi “naturally real” nahi chal raha tha

sab kuch repeat ho raha tha

Aur mind is realization ko accept nahi karta…

wo turant label lagata hai:

overthinking”

emotional phase”

normal attachment”

Par ek second ke liye ruk jao…

Kya tum notice kar sakte ho…

ki tumhara dard bhi structured hai?

same type of thoughts

same time pe triggers

same imagination patterns

same emotional spikes

Aur jo cheez structured hoti hai…

wo accident nahi hoti…

wo pattern hoti hai.
