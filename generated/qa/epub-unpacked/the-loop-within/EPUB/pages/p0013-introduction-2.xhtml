par continuously… jab tak wo cheez “reality” jaisi feel na hone lage.

Aur yahi pe real shift start hota hai:

Tum stuck isliye nahi ho kyuki kuch hua.

Tum stuck isliye ho kyuki tumhara mind usi cheez ko unfinished meaning

bana ke repeat kar raha hai.

Memory problem nahi hai.

Thought problem nahi hai.

Emotion bhi problem nahi hai.

Problem hai automatic repetition without awareness.

Aur jab ye repetition unnoticed hota hai…

toh dheere dheere ek system ban jata hai:

past present ban jata hai

imagination memory lagne lagti hai

interpretation truth ban jati hai

aur truth identity ban jati hai

Is process ko hi ye book “loop” kehti hai.

Na emotional sense mein…

na poetic sense mein…

par ek psychological structure ke roop mein.
