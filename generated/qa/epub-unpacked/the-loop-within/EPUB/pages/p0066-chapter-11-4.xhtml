Chhoti chhoti cheezein:

jo tumne avoid ki

jo tumne feel karke suppress ki

jo tumne ignore ki

jo tumne “later” pe chhod di

Wo sab milke ek invisible weight ban jaati hain.

Aur sabse subtle part ye hota hai…

tum khud ko weak samajhne lagte ho…

jabki tum overloaded hote ho.

Aur overloaded hona weakness nahi hota…

wo bas capacity ka full ho jana hota hai.

Aur jab tum is heaviness ko fight karna band kar dete ho…

aur sirf notice karte ho…

toh ek strange sa silence aata hai.

Na sudden relief…

na sudden change…

bas ek soft breathing space.

Jaise andar thoda sa room ban jata hai…

jahan pehle pressure tha.

Aur tumhe samajh aata hai:
