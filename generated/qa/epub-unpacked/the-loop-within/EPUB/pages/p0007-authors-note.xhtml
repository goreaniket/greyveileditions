NOTE FROM THE AUTHOR

(Don’t read this for comfort — read it for recognition)

This book was never written to tell you how to move on.

Because nothing here assumes you are stuck because of a person.

It assumes something more uncomfortable…

that you might be stuck because of a system inside your mind that keeps re

running unfinished meaning.

There is no “someone” at the center of this.

There is a loop.

And that loop is intelligent enough to disguise itself as love… memory… loss…

and even healing.

If you read this book casually…

you will call it emotional writing.

But if you read it completely…

you will notice something else:
