PART 4 - THE WEIGHT

When You Stop Knowing How to Feel Normal:

Kabhi kabhi tum apne hi din ke andar hote ho…

par tumhe lagta hai jaise tum “andar” ho hi nahi.

Sab kuch chal raha hota hai —

log, baatein, routine, noise…

par tum us flow mein fully fit nahi ho paate.

Aur ye cheez dramatic nahi hoti…

ye ek slow disconnect hota hai.

Jaise tum physically present ho…

par mentally kisi aur layer mein atak gaye ho.

Aur sabse confusing part ye hota hai…

ki tumhe yaad hi nahi hota “normal” feel kaisa hota tha.
