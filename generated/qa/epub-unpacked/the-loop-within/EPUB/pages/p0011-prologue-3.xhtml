Aur pattern ka matlab hota hai…

tum jo feel kar rahe ho… wo random nahi hai

Aur yahi sabse uncomfortable realization hota hai…

kyuki ab tumhe koi “insaan” villain nahi lagta…

na hi koi “loss” primary reason lagta hai…

Tumhe sirf ek cheez dikhti hai:

tumhara mind ek unfinished loop ko protect kar raha hai

Aur wo loop tumhe kabhi clearly end nahi hone deta…

wo hamesha ek line chhod deta hai:

shayad…”

Aur “shayad” hi wo jagah hai jahan tum atke ho…

na past mein… na present mein…

bas ek endless in-between state mein.

Aur sabse dangerous part ye hota hai…

tum is state ko “feelings” samajh lete ho…

jabki ye ek repeatable mental process hota hai.

Aur agar tum ye padh rahe ho… toh shayad tumhe jawab nahi chahiye…

tumhe apna pattern pehli baar clearly dikh raha hai.

Aur yahin se ye book shuru hoti hai…

jahan kuch bhi tumhe bataya nahi ja raha…

sirf expose kiya ja raha hai… tumhe, tumse.
