par kyuki tum usme phasna band kar dete ho.

Aur yahi pe healing quietly start hoti hai…

not by forgetting…

but by not re-editing it again and again.

Aur jab ye realization settle hota hai…

toh ek halka sa space banta hai andar.

Na dard complete disappear hota hai…

na yaadein gayab hoti hain…

par unka hold thoda loose ho jata hai.

Aur us space mein ek simple clarity aati hai:

jo hua tha… wo already complete ho chuka hai

jo yaad aa raha hai… wo sirf mind ka replay hai

Aur is realization ke saath…

pehli baar memory enemy nahi lagti…

bas ek old file lagti hai.
