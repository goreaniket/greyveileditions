Person + Emotion = One Identity

Aur jab identity ban jati hai…

toh release mushkil lagta hai.

Kyuki tum sochte ho tum kisi ko chhod rahe ho…

par asal mein tum ek feeling ko lose karne se dar rahe hote ho.

Aur interesting observation ye hai…

tum jitna zyada missing feel karte ho…

utna zyada tum usko meaning bana dete ho.

Aur jitna zyada meaning banta hai…

utna zyada wo cheez “important reality” lagne lagti hai.

Aur phir ek loop create hota hai:

missing → meaning → stronger missing → deeper meaning → emotional

attachment

Aur is loop ka sabse tricky part ye hai…

ye loud nahi hota.

ye logic se bhi support le leta hai…

“agar main itna miss kar raha hoon… toh kuch toh tha”

Par agar thoda aur deeply observe karo…
