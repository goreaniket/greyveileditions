PART 3 - THE LOOP

When Overthinking Becomes a Habit, Not Emotion:

Kabhi kabhi tumhe lagta hai ki tum soch rahe ho…

par agar thoda aur honestly observe karo…

toh ek aur layer saamne aati hai…

tum soch nahi rahe hote…

tum ek already-chal rahe pattern ko continue kar rahe hote ho.

Aur ye pattern shuruaat mein emotion hota hai…

kisi hurt ka… kisi confusion ka… kisi loss ka…

par time ke saath emotion fade ho jata hai…

aur reh jata hai sirf mental repetition mechanism.

Jaise ek track ban gaya ho mind ke andar…

aur thoughts us track pe automatically chal rahe ho…
