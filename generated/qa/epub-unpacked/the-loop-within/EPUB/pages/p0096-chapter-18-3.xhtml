kyuki tum usme meaning dhoondhne lagte ho.

Par kuch der baad…

tum meaning dhoondhna bhi chhod dete ho.

Aur yahi turning point hota hai.

Kyuki jab meaning nahi hota…

toh interpretation bhi kam ho jati hai.

Aur jab interpretation kam hoti hai…

toh burden bhi kam ho jata hai.

Aur tumhe samajh aata hai:

problem cheez mein nahi thi

problem tumhare continuous reacting mein thi

Aur sabse subtle realization ye hota hai…

ki tum “move on” nahi kar rahe…

tum stop fighting kar rahe ho.

Aur ye difference important hai…

kyuki move on ek effort lagta hai…

par stop fighting ek natural drop hota hai.

Aur jab fight drop hoti hai…

toh jo bachta hai…

wo acceptance jaisa lagta hai.

Par wo acceptance heavy nahi hota…
