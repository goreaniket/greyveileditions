aur bina kisi real insaan ke…

poori conversation complete ho jati hai.

Aur sabse strange cheez ye hoti hai…

tum isko “sochna” nahi kehte…

tum isko normal maan lete ho.

Par agar thoda aur observe karo…

toh samajh aata hai ki ye thinking nahi hai…

ye ek simulation hai.

Ek aisa simulation jisme tum dono side ho…

aur dono side tum hi ho.

Aur ye tab zyada hota hai jab kuch cheez incomplete reh jati hai…

kuch baatein jo bolni thi…

par bol nahi paaye…

ya bolke bhi pura feel nahi hua.

Aur mind us incompleteness ko tolerate nahi karta…

wo usko fill karne lagta hai.

Par fill jo hota hai…

wo real nahi hota.

Wo sirf imagination hota hai…
