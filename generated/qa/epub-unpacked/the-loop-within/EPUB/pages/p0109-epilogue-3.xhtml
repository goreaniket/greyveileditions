aur tumhe pehli baar realize hota hai ki wo heavy tha.

Aur yahi pe ek aur layer break hoti hai…

ki tum jo “insaan” ya “past” samajh rahe the…

wo actually tumhare andar ka ek meaning system tha.

Tum us insaan ko nahi chah rahe the

tum uske saath wali emotional identity ko hold kar rahe the

Aur jab identity loose hoti hai…

toh dard bhi apni jagah loose ho jata hai.

Aur sabse real moment ye hota hai…

ki tum kuch force nahi karte…

na khud ko samjhate ho…

na khud ko convince karte ho…

bas ek din tum notice karte ho:

ab wo thought aata hai… par rukta nahi

Aur yahi “move on” ka hidden form hota hai…

jo actually move nahi hota…

bas attachment ka grip loose hota hai.

Aur jab grip loose hota hai…

toh life suddenly kisi “story” jaisi nahi lagti…

bas life jaisi lagti hai.

Na kisi ki absence heavy…
