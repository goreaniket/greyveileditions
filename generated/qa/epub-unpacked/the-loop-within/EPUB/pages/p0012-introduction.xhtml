INTRODUCTION

(Before you call this a story… understand what is actually happening

inside you)

Ye book love ke baare mein nahi hai.

Ye book loss ke baare mein bhi nahi hai.

Aur ye bilkul bhi “move on ka guide” nahi hai.

Agar tum yahan comfort dhoondhne aaye ho…

toh shayad wo tumhe us tarah na mile jaisa tum expect kar rahe ho.

Kyuki ye book tumhare emotions ko soothe karne ke liye nahi bani.

Ye book isliye bani hai ki tum samajh sako ki emotions bante kaise hain.

Zyada tar log sochte hain ki unke saath jo hota hai, wahi unka dard hai.

Par asal mein jo unhe control karta hai…

wo event nahi hota.

wo hota hai mind ka us event ko baar-baar replay karna

Ek baar nahi… do baar nahi…
