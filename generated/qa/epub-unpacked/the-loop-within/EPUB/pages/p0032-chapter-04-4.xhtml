toh ek aur layer dikhne lagti hai.

Kabhi kabhi tum kisi insaan ko miss nahi kar rahe hote…

tum bas apne mind ke banaye hue version ko revisit kar rahe hote ho.

Wo version jo perfect nahi hota…

par complete lagta hai.

Aur present us comparison mein hamesha weak lagta hai.

Isliye present dull ho jata hai…

aur past sharp.

Aur yahi pe ek quiet realization start hota hai…

ki shayad tum jo meaning bana rahe ho…

wo reality se nahi aa raha…

wo repetition se aa raha hai.

Aur jab ye thought thoda sa settle hota hai…

toh pehli baar missing heavy nahi lagta…

wo bas ek signal lagta hai.

Na truth…

na sign…

bas ek mental echo.
