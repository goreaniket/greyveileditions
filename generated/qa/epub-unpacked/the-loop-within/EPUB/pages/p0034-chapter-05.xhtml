PART 2 - THE DISTORTION

When Memory Becomes Stronger Than Reality:

Kabhi kabhi tumhe lagta hai ki tum present mein ho…

par agar thoda deeply observe karo…

toh tumhe pata chalega ki present sirf ek layer hai.

Ek aur layer uske upar chal rahi hoti hai…

jo quietly sab kuch influence kar rahi hoti hai.

Tum kisi normal moment mein hote ho…

kuch khaas nahi ho raha hota…

par achanak ek memory shift hoti hai andar.

Aur ye shift visible nahi hota.

Koi sound change nahi hota…

koi scene break nahi hota…

par tumhara internal state change ho jata hai.
