Aur tum isko normal life samajhne lagte ho…

kyuki ye disruption loud nahi hota.

Ye quietly chalta hai.

Aur sabse interesting observation ye hai…

tumhe lagta hai tum move kar rahe ho

par tum actually circular motion mein hote ho.

Same emotional route…

different days…

same internal feeling.

Aur is loop mein ek point aata hai…

jab tum khud ko observe karte ho aur sochte ho:

“ye memory itni real kyun lag rahi hai jabki ye ab exist hi nahi karti?”

Aur us question ke andar hi pehli baar clarity aati hai…

ki problem memory nahi hai…

problem us weight ka hai jo memory carry kar rahi hai.
