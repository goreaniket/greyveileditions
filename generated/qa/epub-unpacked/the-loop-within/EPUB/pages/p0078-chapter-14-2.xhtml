Wo version jisme tumne kuch feel kiya tha…

kuch expect kiya tha…

kuch imagine kiya tha…

kuch “meaning” attach kar diya tha.

Aur jab tum us cheez ko chhodne ki sochte ho…

toh dimaag sirf object nahi dekhta…

wo pura emotional ecosystem dekh leta hai.

“agar ye gaya toh main kaun rahunga?”

Aur yahi question tumhe rok deta hai.

Kyuki insaan aksar logon ko nahi chhodta…

wo un logon ke saath attached apni identity ko chhodne se darta hai.

Aur identity koi fixed cheez nahi hoti…

wo experiences ka accumulation hoti hai.

Aur jab experience painful ho jata hai…

par familiar ho jata hai…

toh mind usko safe label kar deta hai.

Chahe wo safety tumhe continuously disturb hi kyun na kar rahi ho.

Aur isliye tum naya start karte hue bhi hesitate karte ho…
