ki shayad tum jo “loss” feel kar rahe ho…

wo insaan ka loss nahi hai…

wo version ka loss hai jo tumne apne andar bana rakha tha.

Aur jab ye realization thoda settle hota hai…

toh memory thodi light ho jati hai…

aur present thoda visible.

Aur jab tum ye observe karte ho…

toh ek strange sa calm aata hai.

Na forcefully move on…

na forcibly forget…

bas ek understanding:

ki jo tha… wo waise hi complete tha

aur jo hai… wo waise hi real hai.
