wo usko present ke saath merge kar deta hai.

Isliye confusion start hota hai.

Kyuki jo ab ho raha hota hai

wo fully real nahi lagta…

aur jo ho chuka hota hai

wo oddly real lagne lagta hai.

Aur ye shift itni subtle hoti hai ki

tum identify hi nahi kar pate kab hua.

Bas ek din tum notice karte ho ki

tumhara dimag present mein kam…

past mein zyada rehta hai.

Aur phir ek aur cheez hoti hai…

tum situation ko logically samajh rahe hote ho

par reaction emotional level pe already decided hota hai.

Tum sochte ho: “ye toh past hai…”

par body aur mind react aise karte hain

jaise ye abhi bhi ho raha ho.

Aur yahin pe ek invisible distortion ban jata hai…

present background ban jata hai

aur past foreground ban jata hai.
