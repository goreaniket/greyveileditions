PART 4 - THE WEIGHT

When New People Feel Like Old Pain :

Kabhi kabhi tum kisi naye insaan se milte ho…

aur sab kuch normal lagta hai.

Baat start hoti hai…

thoda curiosity hoti hai…

thoda interest bhi…

par phir ek invisible sa moment aata hai…

jab wo naya insaan…

naya lagta hi nahi.

Wo kisi purani yaad jaisa feel hone lagta hai…

kisi aise pain jaisa…

jo tumne pehle kabhi feel kiya ho — directly nahi, par internally.
