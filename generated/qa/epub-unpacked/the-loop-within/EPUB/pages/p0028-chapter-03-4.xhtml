ki tumhe problem situation se nahi hai…

tumhe problem us repetition se hai jo silence mein replay hota hai.

Aur jab tum ye observe karte ho…

toh pehli baar silence “heavy” nahi lagta…

wo thoda light lagne lagta hai.

Jaise tum usko fight nahi kar rahe…

bas dekh rahe ho.

Aur yahin pe ek small shift aata hai…

ki shayad silence enemy nahi hai…

shayd silence bas ek mirror hai

jo wahi dikhata hai jo already andar chal raha hota hai.
