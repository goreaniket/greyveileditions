Same triggers

same reactions

same thoughts… bas silently.

Aur kabhi kabhi tum sochte ho:

“ye itna important bhi nahi hai…”

par phir bhi dimag usi cheez ko importance de raha hota hai.

Ye contradiction hi trap hota hai.

Aur sabse ajeeb part ye hai…

tum us cheez mein nahi hote…

par us cheez se bahar bhi nahi hote.

Bas ek beech ka state hota hai…

jahan clear kuch bhi nahi hota.

Aur dheere dheere ye normal lagne lagta hai.

Tum isko problem bhi nahi bolte…

tum isko “main aisa hi hoon” maan lete ho.

Aur shayad wahi point hota hai…

jab tum notice karte ho:

“main yahan hoon… par main yahan ho hi nahi raha.”

Aur us moment pe pehli baar thoda sa clarity aata hai…
