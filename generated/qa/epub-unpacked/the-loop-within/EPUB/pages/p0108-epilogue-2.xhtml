jo bhi tum feel kar rahe the… wo fixed truth nahi tha

wo ek repeatable mental pattern tha

Aur sabse important part ye hota hai…

ki tum is cheez ko fight nahi karte…

tum isko “fix” nahi karte…

tum bas dekhna start karte ho.

Aur dekhna ek silent shift hota hai…

kyuki jab tum dekhte ho…

toh tum andar se usme fully merge nahi hote.

Aur jab merge kam hota hai…

toh intensity bhi kam ho jati hai.

Aur tumhe lagta hai:

main move on kar raha hoon”

par asal mein kuch aur ho raha hota hai…

tum apne hi mind ke repetition se detach ho rahe hote ho

Aur ye detachment koi loss jaisa feel nahi hota…

ye ek weight drop jaisa feel hota hai…

jo pehle pata bhi nahi tha ki carry ho raha hai.

Jaise tum kisi cheez ko roz uthate the…

aur tumhe aadat ho gayi thi us weight ki…

par ek din wo haath se gir jata hai…
