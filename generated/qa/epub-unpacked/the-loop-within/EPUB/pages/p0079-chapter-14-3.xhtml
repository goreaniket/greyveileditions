kyuki naya start ka matlab hota hai:

unknown version of self

Aur unknown cheez ko mind “risk” samajhta hai…

chahe wo actually growth hi kyun na ho.

Aur sabse subtle part ye hota hai…

tum daily life mein move kar rahe hote ho…

par internally tum ek invisible negotiation mein hote ho:

thoda aur ruk jao” vs “ab chhod do”

Aur ye negotiation kabhi end nahi hota…

wo bas repeat hota rehta hai.

Aur jab ye long enough chalta hai…

toh tum ek strange state mein aa jaate ho…

jahan pain bhi familiar lagta hai…

aur change bhi uncomfortable lagta hai.

Aur is point pe tum confuse ho jaate ho…

ki problem kya hai:

situation

ya attachment

Par agar thoda aur honest observation karo…

toh situation change ho sakti hai…
