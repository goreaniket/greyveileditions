Aur ye realization loud nahi hota…

ye bas ek silent loosening hota hai andar.

Aur jab ye samajh aata hai…

toh kuch toot ta nahi hai…

bas ek heavy grip dheere dheere halka ho jata hai.

Na thoughts band hote hain…

na memories vanish hoti hain…

par tum unme phasna kam kar dete ho.

Aur wahi se ek simple freedom start hoti hai:

“main har thought ka passenger nahi hoon”
