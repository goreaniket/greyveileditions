ENDING

(when everything stops feeling like emotion”)

Shayad tum kabhi kisi insaan ke andar stuck the hi nahi…

tum bas apne hi mind ke ek repeating system ke andar the…

jo har memory ko incomplete bana ke usse alive rakhta hai.

Aur sabse strange part ye hota hai…

jab tum observe karna start karte ho…

toh jo cheez pehle “emotion” lagti thi…

wo ek mechanical repetition pattern ban jaati hai.

Same triggers…

same thoughts…

same interpretations…

different intensity… but identical structure.

Aur jab structure dikh jata hai…

toh illusion break ho jata hai.

Kyuki illusion tab tak hi real lagta hai…

jab tak wo invisible hota hai.

tum move on nahi karte
