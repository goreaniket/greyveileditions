Bas ek scene… ek feeling… ek baat.

Par jab wahi memory baar baar repeat hoti hai…

toh wo same nahi rehti.

Wo har baar thodi si change ho jati hai…

kabhi emotion zyada strong…

kabhi meaning zyada heavy…

kabhi tumhara interpretation thoda different.

Aur tum notice bhi nahi karte…

kyuki ye changes bahut subtle hote hain.

Aur mind subtle cheezon ko ignore kar deta hai.

Par dheere dheere ek transformation hota hai…

original memory kam ho jati hai…

aur ek emotional version ban jata hai.

Aur interesting cheez ye hai…

tum us emotional version ko truth samajhne lagte ho.

Par agar honestly dekho…

toh tumhe realize hoga ki:

tum us cheez ko yaad nahi kar rahe

tum us cheez ko recreate kar rahe ho

Aur recreation kabhi neutral nahi hota…

wo hamesha present emotions se color hota hai.
