Aur jab story close nahi hoti…

toh wo “past” nahi rehti…

wo present repetition ban jati hai.

Tum aage chal rahe hote ho…

par andar wahi scene repeat hota rehta hai…

thoda different angle se…

thoda different emotion ke saath…

par content same hota hai.

Aur interesting cheez ye hoti hai…

tumhe lagta hai tum clarity dhoond rahe ho…

par tum actually clarity ko delay kar rahe hote ho.

Kyuki mind ko end accept karna uncomfortable lagta hai.

End ka matlab hota hai:

“ab kuch change nahi hoga”

aur ye sentence mind ko heavy lagta hai.

Isliye mind ek alternative banata hai…

“shayad agar main aur sochun toh kuch samajh aa jaye”

Par yahi pe loop start hota hai.

Kyuki “aur sochna” kabhi final answer nahi deta…

wo sirf usi question ko aur strong bana deta hai.
