PART 3 - THE LOOP

When You Keep Replaying Conversations That Never Happened:

Kabhi kabhi tum apni hi silence mein itne loud ho jaate ho…

ki bahar ki duniya normal lagti hai…

par andar ek poora scene chal raha hota hai.

Tum kisi se baat nahi kar rahe hote…

par tumhara mind already us baat ko start kar chuka hota hai.

Aur ye start tum control nahi karte…

ye bas ho jata hai.

Ek line tum bolte ho apne dimaag mein…

phir saamne se reply aata hai…

phir tum us reply ka response sochte ho…
