Toh agar tum soch rahe ho ki ye kisi insaan ke baare mein hai…

ek second ruk jao.

Kyuki ye book kisi ko point nahi kar rahi.

Ye us process ko point kar rahi hai jisse tumhare andar log, memories aur

meanings create hote hain.

Yahan koi steps nahi hain.

Na “move on ka tareeka”.

Na motivational escape.

Na forced healing.

Kyuki real change instruction se nahi aata.

Wo aata hai clear seeing se.

Aur jab tum clearly dekhna start karte ho…

toh ek dangerous cheez hoti hai:

Familiar patterns apni authority kho dete hain.

Fight ki wajah se nahi…

par isliye kyuki wo ab chhup nahi paate.

Toh ise dhyan se padhna.

Emotion se nahi.

Observation se.

Kyuki agar tum ise sahi tarah padh liya…
