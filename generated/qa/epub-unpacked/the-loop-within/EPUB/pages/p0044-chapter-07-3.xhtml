Isliye ek hi memory…

alag-alag din pe alag-alag lagti hai.

Kabhi strong…

kabhi painful…

kabhi confusing…

kabhi meaningful.

Par asal memory change nahi ho rahi hoti…

tumhara perception change ho raha hota hai.

Aur yahin pe ek soft realization aata hai…

ki jo tum “truth” samajh rahe the…

wo actually ek repeated interpretation tha.

Aur jab ye samajh aata hai…

toh ek strange sa moment hota hai…

jaise mind thoda sa relax ho jata hai.

Kyuki ab tumhe fight nahi karni padti memory se…

na usko prove karna hota hai…

na usko delete karna hota hai…

bas usko dekhna hota hai as it is.

Aur jab tum dekhte ho bina judgement ke…

toh memory apni intensity lose karne lagti hai.

Na kyuki wo gayab hoti hai…
