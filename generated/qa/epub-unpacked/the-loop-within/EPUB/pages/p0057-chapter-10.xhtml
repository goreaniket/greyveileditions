PART 3 - THE LOOP

When You Don’t Let the Story End :

Kabhi kabhi tumhe lagta hai ki sab kuch khatam ho gaya hai…

par agar thoda aur quietly observe karo…

toh samajh aata hai ki kuch khatam nahi hua hota…

bas tumne usko release nahi kiya hota.

Aur ye release na karna hamesha intentional nahi hota…

kabhi kabhi ye ek habit hoti hai…

ek invisible attachment jo tumhe pata bhi nahi hota.

Jaise ek story hoti hai…

jo physically toh finish ho chuki hoti hai…

par mind usko mentally close nahi karta.
