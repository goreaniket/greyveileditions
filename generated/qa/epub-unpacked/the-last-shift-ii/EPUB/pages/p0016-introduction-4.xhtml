Kyuki ye kisi nayi identity banane ki baat nahi karta.

Ye us moment ki baat karta hai jahan tu realize karta hai:

shift ho chuka tha…

par tu us shift ko thoughts ke through samajhne ki koshish kar raha tha.

Aur jab tu yahan tak aayega na…

To do possibilities hongi:

Tu isko sirf philosophy samjhega

Ya phir ek second ke liye… ruk jayega

Aur agar tu ruk gaya…

To tujhe ek strange sa gap feel hoga:

Thought aur awareness ke beech ka gap.

Aur us gap me…

kuch bolta nahi.

kuch explain nahi hota.

par sab clear hota hai.

Aur wahi se book start hoti hai.

Actually nahi…

wahi se tu notice karta hai ki tu kabhi “start” me tha hi nahi.
