ARC II - THE AWARENESS

YOU DON’T LOSE PEOPLE — YOU LOSE YOUR VERSION OF THEM

Ek stage aati hai logon ko samajhne ki jahan pe jo bhi tum connection ke baare mein sochte the, woh sab thoda unstable lagne lagta hai.

Isliye nahi ki duniya achanak badal jaati hai.

Na isliye ki log achanak different ho jaate hain.

Balki isliye ki tumhara unhe dekhne ka tareeka dheere dheere andar se tootne lagta hai.

Aur sabse ajeeb baat ye hai — koi obvious moment nahi hota jahan tum point karke keh sako, “yahin pe sab badal gaya.”

Na koi fight. Na koi ending. Na koi announcement.
