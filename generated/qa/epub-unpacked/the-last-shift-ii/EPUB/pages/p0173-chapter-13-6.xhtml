Tumhare failures se bada hai.

Tumhari mistakes se bada hai.

Aur sabse important—

tumhare thoughts se bhi bada hai.

Kyuki thoughts badalte rehte hain.

Worth nahi.

THE REAL MEANING OF HEALING

Shayad healing ka matlab...

kabhi bhi yeh nahi tha...

ki tum dobara kabhi nahi roge.

Kabhi overthink nahi karoge.

Kabhi attach nahi hoge.

Kabhi hurt nahi hoge.

Shayad healing ka matlab sirf itna tha...

ki jab yeh sab dobara hoga...

tab tum khud ka haath chhodoge nahi.

Aur shayad wahi...

real strength hai.

Na emotions ko control karna.

Na life ko control karna.

Bas har experience ke beech...
