WHEN WE STOP CORRECTING PEOPLE

Ek aur stage aati hai.

Pehle hum explain karte hain.

Phir prove karte hain.

Phir clarify karte hain.

Aur phir...

ek din hum chup ho jaate hain.

Log sochte hain—

"Isse farak hi nahi padta."

Par reality alag hoti hai.

Kabhi kabhi insaan isliye silent nahi hota...

kyuki uske paas words nahi hote.

Wo isliye silent hota hai...

kyuki usne hope lose kar di hoti hai ki koi samajh bhi payega.

Aur jab hope khatam ho jaye...

toh explanation bhi khatam ho jati hai.
