Life ka ek strange paradox hai.

Log chale jaate hain.

Par unki awaaz kabhi kabhi nahi jaati.

Ek teacher...

Jo saalon pehle school chhod chuka hai...

Aaj bhi exam se pehle tumhare dimaag mein bol raha hota hai.

Ek insaan...

Jisne sirf ek baar tumhe reject kiya tha...

Uski echo naye relationships mein bhi sunai deti hai.

Ek parent...

Jo shayad sirf discipline sikha raha tha...

Uske kuch sentences narrator ka permanent script ban jaate hain.

Aur phir...

Hum samajhte hain ki yeh humari self-talk hai.

Jabki kai baar...

Yeh borrowed voices hoti hain.

Awaazein jo kabhi kisi aur ki thi...

Par ab humari identity ka hissa ban chuki hain.

THE NARRATOR DOESN'T RECORD LIFE... IT EDITS IT
