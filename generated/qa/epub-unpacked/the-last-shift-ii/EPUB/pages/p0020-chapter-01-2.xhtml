Par andar kuch shift ho jata hai.

Ek rejection.

Ek expectation ka tootna.

Ek chhota sa emotional hit jo kisi aur ko pata bhi nahi hota.

Aur uske baad duniya change nahi hoti…

duniya ko dekhne wala system change ho jata hai.

THE MOMENT REALITY GETS FILTERED

Pehle cheezein direct hoti hain.

Ek message aata hai → message hota hai.

Ek silence hota hai → silence hota hai.

Ek reply hota hai → reply hota hai.

Bas.

Par jab andar koi emotional disturbance hoti hai…

tab yeh simplicity break ho jati hai.

Ab ek message sirf message nahi hota.

Wo become karta hai: “intention kya tha?” “tone kaisa tha?” “kyun aisa bola?” “kyun itna short tha?”

Aur jo cheez neutral thi…

wo suddenly loaded ho jati hai.
