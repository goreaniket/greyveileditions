WHY YOU START FILTERING EVEN EMOTIONS

Pehle sirf words filter hote the.

Phir behavior filter hua.

Phir reactions filter hue.

Aur phir ek point pe…

emotions bhi filter hone lagte hain.

Tum gussa feel karte ho…

par express nahi karte.

Tum hurt feel karte ho…

par justify nahi karte.

Tum care feel karte ho…

par show nahi karte.

Aur jo cheez express nahi hoti…

wo slowly clarity lose kar deti hai.
