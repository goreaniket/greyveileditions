THE WEIGHT OF AN INCORRECT IMAGE

Sabke dimaag mein humari ek image banti hai.

Aur problem image banne mein nahi hai.

Problem tab hoti hai...

jab woh image humse bilkul alag hoti hai.

Tum calm ho...

log tumhe arrogant bol dete hain.

Tum reserved ho...

log tumhe attitude samajh lete hain.

Tum emotionally tired ho...

log tumhe careless keh dete hain.

Aur dheere dheere...

tum sirf unke words se hurt nahi hote.

Tum is baat se hurt hote ho...

ki jis insaan ko wo judge kar rahe hain...

wo tum ho hi nahi.

WHEN WE START PERFORMING TO BE UNDERSTOOD

Ek point ke baad...

insaan natural rehna chhod deta hai.

Wo perform karna start karta hai.

Smile zyada.
