THE MOST PEACEFUL WAY TO LIVE

Shayad ab tumhe har cheez samajhne ki zarurat nahi.

Har cheez control karne ki bhi nahi.

Bas notice karna hai.

Jab hansi aaye...

has lena.

Jab aansu aaye...

ro lena.

Jab pyaar mile...

feel kar lena.

Jab loss aaye...

uske saath kuch der baith lena.

Life continuously flow kar rahi hai.

Usse pakadne ki zarurat nahi.
