ARC II - THE AWARENESS

YOU WERE NEVER REJECTED — YOU WERE JUST INTERPRETING SIGNALS

There is a point in human perception where pain stops being about events…

and starts becoming about interpretation itself.

And the strange thing is — most people never realize this shift happens.

They think they are reacting to what is happening.

But in reality, they are reacting to what they think what is happening means.

And meaning is not reality.

Meaning is construction.

You were never actually rejected in the way you believe.
