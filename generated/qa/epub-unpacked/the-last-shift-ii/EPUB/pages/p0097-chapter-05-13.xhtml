WHEN A THOUGHT STOPS BEING A THOUGHT

Har thought temporary hota hai.

Wo aata hai...

aur chale jana chahiye.

Par kuch thoughts ruk jaate hain.

Kyuki hum unse argue nahi karte...

hum unpe believe kar lete hain.

Aur jis moment ek thought belief ban gaya...

us moment usne tumhari perception pe ownership le li.

Ab tum thought ko nahi dekhte.

Tum thought ke through duniya dekhte ho.

Difference bahut chhota lagta hai...

par poori life wahi change kar deta hai.
