Par reality yeh hai—

Har insaan connection seek karta hai.

Difference sirf itna hai...

kuch openly seek karte hain...

aur kuch secretly deny karte hain.

WHEN DISTANCE BECOMES AN IDENTITY

Shuru mein distance choice hoti hai.

Baad mein habit.

Aur uske baad...

personality.

Log kehte hain—

"Main emotionally unavailable hoon."

"Main kisi pe trust nahi karta."

"Main kisi ko close nahi aane deta."

Jaise yeh unka nature ho.

Par aksar...

yeh nature nahi hota.

Yeh repeated protection hoti hai...

jo itni purani ho chuki hoti hai...

ki identity lagne lagti hai.
