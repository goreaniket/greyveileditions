Shuru mein dimaag ko lagta hai ki clarity ka matlab hai control milna.

Emotions control ho jayein.

Thoughts control ho jayein.

Situations control ho jayein.

Log samajh mein aa jayein.

Par dheere dheere ek uncomfortable truth samne aata hai:

Shayad problem kabhi “broken life” thi hi nahi.

Shayad problem sirf ye thi ki har cheez ko broken samjha ja raha tha.

Life same rehti hai.

Messages aate rehte hain.

Log apna behavior change karte rehte hain.

Situations shift hoti rehti hain.

Par ek cheez gradually change hoti hai:

Har cheez ko meaning dene ki urgency kam ho jaati hai.

Pehle har chhoti cheez ek question ban jaati thi:

“kyun aisa hua?”

“kyun aisa bola?”
