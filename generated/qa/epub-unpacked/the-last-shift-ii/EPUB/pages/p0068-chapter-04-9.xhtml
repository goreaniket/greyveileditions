WHEN HYPERVIGILANCE FEELS LIKE MATURITY

Kuch log har chhoti cheez notice karte hain.

Tone.

Timing.

Expressions.

Typing speed.

Reply ka gap.

Conversation ka flow.

Aur dheere dheere unhe lagne lagta hai ki yeh awareness hai.

Par har awareness wisdom nahi hoti.

Kabhi kabhi yeh sirf fear hota hai...

jo details collect kar raha hota hai.

Fear predict karna chahta hai.

Kyunki usse lagta hai—

"Agar sab pehle samajh gaya... toh hurt nahi hoga."

Par life prediction se nahi chalti.

Aur jab prediction impossible hoti hai...

toh anxiety permanent ho jaati hai.
