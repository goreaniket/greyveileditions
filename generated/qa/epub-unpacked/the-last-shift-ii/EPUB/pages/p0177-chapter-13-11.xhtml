Har jeet temporary hoti hai.

Aur har defeat personal lagti hai.

Shayad answer war jeetna nahi tha.

War dekh lena tha.

Aur dekhte hi...

uski necessity kam hone lagti hai.

THE STRANGEST KIND OF STRENGTH

Bachpan se hume sikhaya gaya—

Strong bano.

Mat ro.

Mat toot.

Handle karo.

Control karo.

Par life dheere dheere ek aur definition sikhati hai.

Kabhi kabhi strongest insaan wo nahi hota...

jo kabhi nahi rota.

Strong wo hota hai...

jo finally ro leta hai.

Jo finally maan leta hai—

"Haan...

mujhe dard hua tha."
