THE COST OF ALWAYS BEING READY

Constant alert rehna exhausting hota hai.

Par interesting baat yeh hai...

insaan is exhaustion ka bhi aadat bana leta hai.

Usse normal lagne lagta hai.

Har conversation analyse karna.

Har silence decode karna.

Har change notice karna.

Har possibility calculate karna.

Aur phir ek din...

mind itna tired ho jata hai...

ki peace bhi suspicious lagne lagti hai.

Kyunki chaos familiar tha.

Silence unfamiliar hai.

Aur unfamiliar cheez brain ko unsafe lagti hai.

WHY CONTROL FEELS SAFE

Control certainty ka illusion deta hai.

Hum sochte hain—

"Agar main emotionally invest kam karunga... toh kam hurt hoga."
