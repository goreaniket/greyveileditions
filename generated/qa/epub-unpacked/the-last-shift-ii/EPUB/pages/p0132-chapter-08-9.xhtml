reduced warmth

Individually ye sab ordinary events hote hain.

Lekin together woh ek structure banane lagte hain.

Aur jab structure ban jaata hai, mind use possibility nahi samajhta.

Woh use conclusion samajhne lagta hai.

Aur conclusion clarity jaisa feel hota hai.

Chahe woh sirf emotional reinforcement hi kyu na ho.

Isliye log feel karte hain ki doosre log badal gaye hain.

Hum kehte hain:

“Woh badal gaya.”

Lekin most cases mein person nahi badalta.

Hamari internal threshold for ambiguity badal jaati hai.

Jab hum emotionally stable hote hain, behavior normal lagta hai.

Jab hum emotionally attached hote hain, wahi behavior significant lagta hai.

Jab hum insecure hote hain, wahi behavior distant lagta hai.
