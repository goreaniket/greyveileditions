Wo normal lagne lagta hai.

Tumhe lagta hai:

“ye hi toh maturity hai.”

“ye hi toh control hai.”

“ye hi toh growth hai.”

Par andar se…

ye growth nahi hoti.

Ye suppression hota hai with better vocabulary.

WHEN LIFE BECOMES A PERFORMANCE EVEN IN SILENCE

Sabse strange phase wo hota hai:

jab tum akele hote ho…

aur phir bhi perform kar rahe hote ho.

Kisike liye nahi.

Apne hi idea ke liye.

“main aisa insaan hoon jo strong hai”

“main aisa insaan hoon jo care nahi karta”

“main aisa insaan hoon jo unaffected hai”

Aur slowly…

identity ek decision nahi rehti.

Wo acting ban jaati hai.
