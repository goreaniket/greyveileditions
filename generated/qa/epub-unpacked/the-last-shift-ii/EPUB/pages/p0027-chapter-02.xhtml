ARC I - THE DISTORTION

THE STORIES WE CREATE

Kabhi kabhi reality aur imagination ke beech ka gap itna thin hota hai

ki hum dono ko ek hi cheez samajh lete hain.

Aur wahi se ek silent transformation start hota hai—

hum jo dekh rahe hote hain, wo nahi…

hum jo samajh rahe hote hain ki hum dekh rahe hain, wo reality ban jata hai.

THE NEED TO COMPLETE WHAT IS INCOMPLETE

Mind incomplete cheezon ko tolerate nahi karta.

Ek message seen pe chhod diya jata hai.

Ek reply delay ho jata hai.

Ek conversation mid-way cut ho jati hai.
