And uncertainty always feels personal.

Even when it is not.

This is the most silent form of suffering:

Not what happened.

But what you assumed it meant.

And then believing the assumption so deeply that it becomes memory.

And memory feels like proof.

Even when it was originally just interpretation.

There is no villain in this structure.

No one is intentionally causing distortion.

Not the other person.

Not you.

Not the situation.

Only a system that tries to convert unknown into known at any cost.

Even if the known is wrong.

Because wrong certainty feels safer than correct uncertainty.

And that is where the real shift happens.

When you stop asking:
