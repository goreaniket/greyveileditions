WHEN SAFETY STARTS LOOKING BORING

Ek aur silent shift hota hai.

Chaos addictive ho sakta hai.

Jis insaan ne emotional highs aur lows mein relationships dekhi ho… uske liye calm connection emotionless lag sakta hai.

No drama.

No guessing.

No chasing.

Bas stability.

Par stability unfamiliar hoti hai.

Aur unfamiliar cheez ko mind excitement nahi… emptiness samajh leta hai.

Isliye kai baar log healthy cheezon se door chale jaate hain… sirf isliye kyunki unmein woh intensity nahi hoti jiske woh aadat ban chuke hote hain.

Intensity aur intimacy ek hi cheez nahi hote.

Par mind dono ko confuse kar deta hai.
