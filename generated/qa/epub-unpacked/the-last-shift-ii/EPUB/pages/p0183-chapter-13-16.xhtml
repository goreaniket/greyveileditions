Ki healing ka matlab...

hamesha happy rehna hai.

Nahi.

Kuch din ache honge.

Kuch din bahut heavy honge.

Kuch din bina wajah aansu aa jayenge.

Kuch din purani yaadein wapas aa jayengi.

Difference sirf itna hoga...

ab tum unse daroge nahi.

Tum turant yeh conclude nahi karoge—

"Main wapas wahi aa gaya."

Kyuki ab tum jaante hoge...

ki emotions weather ki tarah hote hain.

Identity ki tarah nahi.

Badal aate hain.

Badal chale bhi jaate hain.

Sky wahi rehta hai.
