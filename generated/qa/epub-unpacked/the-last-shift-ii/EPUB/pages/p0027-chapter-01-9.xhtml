Aur shayad sabse simple truth yeh hai:

Reality kabhi itni complex nahi hoti jitni hum usko bana dete hain.

Kabhi kabhi ek silence bas silence hota hai.

Ek delay bas timing hoti hai.

Ek message bas message hota hai.

Par jab hum khud distorted state mein hote hain…

toh hum duniya ko distorted hi padhte hain.

Aur shayad yahi pe pehla shift hai…

jab insaan yeh samajhne lagta hai ki:

“jo main dekh raha hoon… wo final truth nahi ho sakta.”
