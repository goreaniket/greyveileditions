Introduction

Tu abhi jo padh raha hai na…

wo ek book nahi hai.

Ye ek aisa mirror hai jisme tu khud ko tab dekhega jab tu dekhna band kar chuka hoga.

Aur problem ye nahi hai ki tu sochta hai.

Problem ye hai ki tu sochna band nahi kar pata.

Kabhi notice kiya hai?

Ek thought aata hai…

aur uske baad tu us thought ke andar chala jata hai.

Phir tu us thought ko observe nahi karta…

tu us thought ko live karta hai.

Aur us moment pe ek cheez quietly disappear ho jati hai:

“Tu jo observe kar raha tha, wo bhi tu hi tha.”

Yahin se start hota hai distortion.

Na koi crash.
