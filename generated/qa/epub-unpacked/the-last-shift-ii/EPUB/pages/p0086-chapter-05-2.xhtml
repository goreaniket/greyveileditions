hum apni identity inherit karte hain.

Thodi family se.

Thodi school se.

Thodi failures se.

Thodi un logon se…

jinhone kabhi humein define kar diya tha.

Aur dheere dheere…

ek description,

ek conclusion ban jaata hai.

THE FIRST NAME WE NEVER CHOSE

Jab ek bachcha paida hota hai…

usse apne baare mein kuch nahi pata hota.

Usse nahi pata ki woh intelligent hai ya average.

Strong hai ya weak.

Confident hai ya shy.

Ye sab baad mein aata hai.

Pehle log dekhte hain.

Phir log bolte hain.
