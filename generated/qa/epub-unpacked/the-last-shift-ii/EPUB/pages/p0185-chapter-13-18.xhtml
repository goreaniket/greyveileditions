Fear ke neeche.

Expectations ke neeche.

Comparison ke neeche.

Aur jab yeh sab dheere dheere halka hota hai...

toh tum koi naye insaan nahi bante.

Tum bas...

dobara wahi ban jaate ho...

jo tum hamesha se the.

THE QUIET PEACE

Peace loud nahi hoti.

Woh announce nahi karti—

"Congratulations, you are healed."

Woh bas ek din...

tumhare andar baith jaati hai.

Itni quietly...

ki shayad tum notice bhi na karo.

Aur phir ek din...

tum peeche mudkar dekhte ho...

aur realize karte ho—

"Dard ab bhi aata hai...

par woh mujhe lekar nahi chalta.
