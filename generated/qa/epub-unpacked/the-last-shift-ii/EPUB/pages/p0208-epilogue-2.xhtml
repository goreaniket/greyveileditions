ki duniya waise hi hai.

Log waise hi bolenge.

Kuch log samjhenge.

Kuch galat samjhenge.

Kuch tumhe chhod kar chale jayenge.

Kuch bina wajah tumhari life mein aa jayenge.

Kabhi baarish hogi.

Kabhi dhoop hogi.

Kabhi raat bahut lambi lagegi.

Kabhi ek chhoti si subah...

bahut gehri shanti de jaayegi.

Life nahi badlegi.

Lekin...

shayad tum usse dekhne ka tareeka badal doge.

Aur kabhi kabhi...

itna hi kaafi hota hai.

Ek waqt tha...

jab tum har emotion se ladte the.

Har thought ko analyse karte the.

Har silence ka meaning nikalte the.

Har goodbye ko apni value bana lete the.
