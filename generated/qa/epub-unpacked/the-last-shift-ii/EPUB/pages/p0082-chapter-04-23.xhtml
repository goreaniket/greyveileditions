Open mind ka matlab sirf itna hota hai...

ki belief aur self ek hi cheez nahi hain.

Aaj jo main maanta hoon...

kal usse better understanding mil sakti hai.

Aur agar mili...

toh mujhe apne purane version se ladna nahi padega.

Mujhe sirf evolve hona hoga.

Jo insaan evolve kar sakta hai...

usse apni image bachane ki zarurat nahi padti.

WHEN TRUTH STOPS BEING PERSONAL

Shayad hum truth se isliye nahi darte...

kyunki wo painful hota hai.

Hum truth se isliye darte hain...

kyunki wo personal lagta hai.

Agar meri belief galat nikli...

toh mujhe lagta hai main hi galat hoon.

Par belief aur insaan ek hi cheez nahi hote.

Beliefs badalte rehte hain.

Insaan unse bada hota hai.

Jis din yeh difference clear ho jata hai...

usi din learning effortless lagne lagti hai.
