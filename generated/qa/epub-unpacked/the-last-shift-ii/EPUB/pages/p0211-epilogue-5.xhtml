Jab anxiety aayegi...

shayad tum use enemy nahi...

visitor samjhoge.

Jab kisi ka jaana dukh dega...

shayad tum us dard ko bhi...

human hone ki price samjhoge.

Aur jab kabhi...

bina kisi wajah ke...

andar se halka mehsoos hoga...

toh us moment ko analyse mat karna.

Usse bas jeena.

Kyuki isi ke liye...

tum paida hue the.

Ye kitab...

tumhe perfect banane ke liye nahi likhi gayi thi.

Ye kitab...

tumhe wapas tum tak laane ke liye likhi gayi thi.

Aur agar is safar mein...

sirf ek pal ke liye bhi...

tumne apne mind ke shor ke peeche...

apni asli khamoshi ko mehsoos kiya...
