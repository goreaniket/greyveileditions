Aur pehle jahan ye ek emotional universe create karta tha…

ab wahan kuch nahi banta.

Na overthinking.

Na meaning.

Na analysis spiral.

Bas ek message.

Aur wahi khatam.

Aur yehi wo point hai jisse dimaag hamesha bachna chahta tha:

Simple reality.

Bina interpretation ke.

Kyuki interpretation hi sab kuch important banati thi.

Aur importance hi identity banati thi.

Aur identity hi suffering banati thi.

Jab interpretation dheere hoti hai…

importance girti hai.

Aur importance girte hi…

suffering ka weight bhi girne lagta hai.

Life control mein nahi aati.
