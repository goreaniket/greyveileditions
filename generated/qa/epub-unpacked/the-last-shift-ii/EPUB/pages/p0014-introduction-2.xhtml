Na koi breakdown.

Bas ek subtle shift…

Jahan reality wahi hoti hai,

par usko dekhne wala change ho jata hai.

Aur tujhe pata bhi nahi chalta.

Sabse dangerous illusion kya hai pata hai?

Ki tu sochta hai tu real time me jee raha hai.

Par majority time tu live nahi hota…

tu interpretation delay me hota hai.

Har cheez pe reaction,

har moment pe meaning,

har silence pe assumption.

Aur dheere dheere ek nayi reality ban jati hai:

Ek aisi reality jo cheezon se nahi…

tere analysis se chalti hai.

Aur ek point aata hai…

Jab silence bhi message lagne lagta hai.

Random thought bhi signal ban jata hai.

Normal cheez bhi “deep” lagne lagti hai.

Aur tu sochta hai — “main aware ho raha hu”
