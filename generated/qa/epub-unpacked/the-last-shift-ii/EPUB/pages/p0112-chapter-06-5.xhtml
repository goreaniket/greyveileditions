Ek invisible pattern:

tum har cheez ko normalize kar dete ho.

hurt → “it’s fine”

confusion → “it’s okay”

distance → “normal hai”

Aur jab sab kuch “fine” ho jata hai…

toh kuch bhi actually fine nahi hota.

Bas feel karne ki ability blunt ho jaati hai.

THE MOMENT YOU DON’T KNOW WHAT YOU FEEL ANYMORE

Sabse scary realization ye hota hai:

ki tumhe samajh hi nahi aata tum feel kya kar rahe ho.

Not because emotion missing hai…

but because it is constantly being edited before recognition.

Feeling → thought → judgment → suppression

Aur original emotion kabhi surface tak nahi aata.

WHY REAL LIFE FEELS FAR EVEN WHEN NOTHING IS WRONG

Externally sab normal hota hai.

No major problem.

No crisis.
