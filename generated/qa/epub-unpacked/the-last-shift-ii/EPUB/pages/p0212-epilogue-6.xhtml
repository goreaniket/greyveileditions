The Last Shift...

already ho chuka hai.

Ab kitab band karo.

Screen band karo.

Thodi der...

sirf saans lo.

Bahar dekho.

Aasmaan shayad wahi hai...

jo kal tha.

Par ho sakta hai...

dekhne wala insaan...

ab pehle jaisa na ho.

Aur agar aisa hai...

toh ye ending nahi.

Ye tumhari life ka...

pehla sachcha page hai.

Go.

Live.
