THE INVISIBLE PRISON

Sabse dangerous prison woh nahi hoti jisme walls dikhte hain.

Sabse dangerous prison woh hoti hai…

Jisme insaan ko lagta hai ki woh free hai.

Hum kehte hain—

"Main bas practical hoon."

"Main bas reality dekh raha hoon."

"Main bas experience se seekha hoon."

Kabhi kabhi yeh sab sach hota hai.

Par kabhi kabhi…

Yeh sirf purani stories ka refined version hota hai.
