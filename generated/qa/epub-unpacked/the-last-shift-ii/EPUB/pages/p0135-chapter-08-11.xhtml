Aur shayad yahi real shift hai jisme ye poora understanding point karta hai.

Tum ek moment mein logon ko lose nahi karte.

Tum dheere dheere unke raw reality ke saath interact karna band kar dete ho.

Aur us version ke saath interact karne lagte ho jo tumhara mind ne banaya tha.

Aur jab woh version finally toot jaata hai…

to jo bachta hai woh drama nahi hota.

Na closure. Na explanation.

Sirf reality hoti hai — bina interpretation ke upar layer kiye hue.

Aur woh reality unusually simple lagti hai.

Kabhi kabhi even empty.

Lekin woh pehli baar hoti hai jo actually real hoti hai.
