"Main better banunga."

"Main stronger banunga."

"Main overthinking khatam kar dunga."

"Main emotions control kar lunga."

Surface pe ye growth lagti hai.

Kabhi kabhi hoti bhi hai.

Lekin kai baar...

ye sirf self-rejection ka sophisticated version hoti hai.

Message hidden hota hai—

"Jab tak main better nahi ban jata...

tab tak main enough nahi hoon."

Aur yahin healing quietly postpone hoti rehti hai.

Kyuki future ka better version...

present wale insaan ko continuously reject karta rehta hai.

WHEN PEACE BECOMES ANOTHER GOAL

Mind har cheez ko achievement bana deta hai.

Even peace.

Ab wo kehta hai—

"Mujhe peaceful banna hai."
