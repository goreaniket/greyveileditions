THE NEED TO BE RIGHT

PART 1 — WHEN BEING RIGHT BECOMES MORE IMPORTANT THAN SEEING

Har insaan truth dhoondhne ki baat karta hai.

Par agar hum honestly observe karein…

toh zyada tar waqt hum truth nahi dhoondh rahe hote.

Hum confirmation dhoondh rahe hote hain.

Confirmation ki jo hum pehle se maante hain… wohi sahi nikle.

Aur yeh difference bahut chhota lagta hai.

Lekin shayad insaan ki psychology ka sabse dangerous difference bhi yehi hai.

Truth uncomfortable ho sakta hai.
