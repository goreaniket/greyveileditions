hum patterns dekhte hain.

“late replies = disinterest”

“short texts = coldness”

“silence = rejection”

Aur pattern safe hota hai kyunki predictable hota hai.

Par insaan unpredictable hote hain.

Isliye hum insaan ko pattern mein compress kar dete hain

taaki wo mentally manageable lage.

THE SELF-PROOF LOOP DEEPENS

Aur phir loop start hota hai:

Tum assume karte ho distance hai → tum withdraw karte ho

Tum withdraw karte ho → connection weak hota hai

Connection weak hota hai → tumhe proof milta hai

Aur phir mind kehta hai:

“See? I was right.”

Par jo tum miss kar dete ho wo yeh hai ki

tumne reality observe nahi ki… tumne reality shape ki.
