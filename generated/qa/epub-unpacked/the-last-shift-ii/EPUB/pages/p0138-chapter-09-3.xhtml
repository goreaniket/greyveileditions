But what it actually removes is reality itself.

The human mind is not built to wait for clarity.

It is built to survive ambiguity.

So when information is incomplete, it does not stay incomplete.

It gets filled.

Not with truth.

But with probability shaped by past pain.

And that is where distortion begins to feel like intuition.

You don’t actually see what is happening.

You see what your nervous system expects might be happening.

And expectation is always older than the present moment.

So every new situation becomes a shadow of an old emotional experience.

A delayed reply is never just a delayed reply.

It is every past silence combined.

Every past distance compressed.
