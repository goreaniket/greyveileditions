isi "shayad" ke andar...

(PART 4):

THE EXIT DOESN’T FEEL LIKE EXIT

Sabse ajeeb baat ye hoti hai…

ki psychological prison se nikalna koi dramatic moment nahi hota.

Koi explosion nahi.

Koi sudden awakening nahi.

Bas ek soft shift hota hai…

jahan tum pehli baar apni hi soch ko “final” nahi maante.

WHEN THE SYSTEM STARTS LOSING ITS AUTHORITY

Pehele ek thought aata tha…

aur uske baad turant belief ban jata tha.

Ab wahi thought aata hai…

par uska weight kam ho jata hai.

“Shayad ye sirf ek interpretation hai.”

Ye sentence hi system ko weaken kar deta hai.

Kyuki prison ka power bars se nahi aata…
