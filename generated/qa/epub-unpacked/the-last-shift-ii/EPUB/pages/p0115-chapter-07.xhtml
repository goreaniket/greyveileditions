ARC I - THE DISTORTION

YOU DON’T SEE PEOPLE — YOU SEE YOUR STATE

There is a point in life where you slowly start believing that you are understanding people.

You think you are reading them.

You think you are decoding their behaviour.

But slowly, almost invisibly, something else is happening underneath:

you are not seeing them at all.

You are only seeing yourself — in different emotional conditions.

And the worst part is, it feels like truth.

Not interpretation. Not assumption. Truth.
