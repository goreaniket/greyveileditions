jab pain ke upar story ban jaati hai.

"Dekha...

main weak hoon."

"Main kabhi happy nahi ho paunga."

"Mere saath hi aisa hota hai."

Pain kuch minute reh sakta tha.

Story usse saalon tak zinda rakhti hai.

Aur dheere dheere...

insaan pain se nahi...

pain ke meaning se suffer karne lagta hai.

THE MOST GENTLE SHIFT

Healing ka sabse beautiful moment wo nahi hota...

jab tum finally khush ho jaate ho.

Wo hota hai...

jab tum dukhi hokar bhi khud se nafrat karna band kar dete ho.

Emotion aata hai.

Tum usse notice karte ho.

Par tum uske against war nahi chhedte.

Anxiety aayi.

Theek hai.
