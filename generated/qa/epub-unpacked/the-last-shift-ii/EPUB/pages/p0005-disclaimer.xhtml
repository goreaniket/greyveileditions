This book exists in the space between observation and interpretation.

It is not a manual, nor a method. It does not prescribe outcomes, nor does it offer correction. The ideas presented here are contemplative constructs—meant to reflect patterns of thought, not define them.

Nothing within this text should be treated as clinical, diagnostic, or therapeutic instruction.

The reader is invited, not directed. What is seen here depends entirely on what is brought into it.
