But your mind refuses to stop there.

It starts building layers:

“busy hoga”

“ya ignore kar raha hai”

“ya interest kam ho gaya”

“ya maine kuch galat bola”

And slowly, what was a neutral silence becomes a full emotional story.

Not because something happened.

But because your mind cannot sit with “nothing happening.”

THE INTERNAL PROJECTION SYSTEM

Here is the uncomfortable truth:

You don’t interpret people’s behaviour.

You project your internal condition onto their behaviour.

If you are calm — everything looks normal.

If you are anxious — everything looks suspicious.

If you are attached — everything feels personal.

If you are emotionally drained — everything feels distant.
