THE INVISIBLE CAGE OF CERTAINTY

Insaan uncertainty se itna uncomfortable hota hai...

ki kabhi kabhi galat certainty choose kar leta hai.

"I know wo mujhe ignore kar raha hai."

"I know log kabhi change nahi hote."

"I know end yahi hona tha."

Notice karo.

Har sentence "I know" se start hota hai.

Lekin asal mein...

ye knowing nahi hoti.

Ye repeated assumption hoti hai.

Mind certainty ko truth samajhta hai.

Reality ko nahi.

WHY THE MIND LOVES FAMILIAR PAIN

Ek strange cheez hoti hai.

Mind unfamiliar happiness se zyada...

familiar pain choose karta hai.

Kyuki familiar pain predictable hota hai.

Uska pattern pata hota hai.

Uska ending pata hota hai.
