Ye tumhe change karne bhi nahi aayi.

Aur na hi ye promise karti hai...

ki iske baad tum kabhi overthink nahi karoge.

Ye kitab sirf ek invitation hai.

Ek baar...

sirf ek baar...

apne mind ko bahar se dekhne ka.

Without judging.

Without fixing.

Without fighting.

Kyunki shayad...

problem tumhare thoughts nahi the.

Problem sirf itni thi...

ki tumne har thought par yakeen kar liya.

Aur wahi se...

kahani shuru hui.

Har insaan ki life mein ek moment aata hai...

jo bahar se bahut chhota hota hai.
