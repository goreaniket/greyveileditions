Confirmation comforting hota hai.

Truth tumhe change karne ke liye force karta hai.

Confirmation tumhe wahi rehne deta hai jahan tum already ho.

Isliye mind naturally truth se pehle confirmation choose karta hai.

Na isliye kyunki wo evil hai.

Na isliye kyunki wo irrational hai.

Balki isliye kyunki certainty survive karna aasaan banati hai.

THE STRANGE COMFORT OF CERTAINTY

Socho tum kisi cheez ko lekar completely sure ho.

Chahe wo relationship ho.

Career ho.

Kisi insaan ka character ho.

Ya khud ke baare mein koi belief.

Us certainty ke andar ek ajeeb si peace hoti hai.

Questions khatam ho jaate hain.

Confusion kam ho jaati hai.

Mind relax kar leta hai.

Par certainty ka ek hidden cost bhi hota hai.
