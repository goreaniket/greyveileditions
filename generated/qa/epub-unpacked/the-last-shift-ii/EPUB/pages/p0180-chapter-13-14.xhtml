WHEN LIFE STOPS FEELING LIKE A PROBLEM

Ek din tum subah uthte ho.

Suraj waise hi ugta hai.

Phone waise hi vibrate karta hai.

Log waise hi messages bhejte hain.

Traffic waise hi hota hai.

Life mein kuch extraordinary nahi hota.

Par andar kuch alag hota hai.

Pehle har cheez solve karni hoti thi.

Ab har cheez observe hone lagti hai.

Pehle har emotion ko naam dena zaroori lagta tha.

Ab kabhi kabhi emotion sirf feel hota hai...

aur khatam ho jaata hai.

Tum uske peeche bhaagte nahi.

Tum usse bhagate bhi nahi.

Bas uske saath kuch der baithe rehte ho.

Aur shayad pehli baar...

life problem kam...

experience zyada lagne lagti hai.

THE BEAUTY OF ORDINARY MOMENTS
