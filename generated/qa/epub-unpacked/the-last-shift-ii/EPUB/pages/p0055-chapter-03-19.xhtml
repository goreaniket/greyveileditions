Jahan tum achanak apne hi thought ko dekh lete ho.

Thought aata hai—

"Wo tumhe ignore kar raha hai."

Aur is baar...

Pehli baar...

Tum us thought ke saath behte nahi.

Tum rukte ho.

Aur sirf itna puchte ho—

"Ya ye sirf ek interpretation hai?"

Ye question chhota lagta hai.

Par isi mein ek nayi direction chhupi hoti hai.

Kyunki pehli baar...

Narrator aur tum ek hi cheez nahi rehte.

Ek bol raha hota hai.

Aur ek sun raha hota hai.

WHEN SILENCE IS NO LONGER FILLED

Narrator ko silence pasand nahi.

Silence uske liye khaali jagah hai.

Aur khaali jagah usse uncomfortable lagti hai.

Isliye wo har gap ko words se bhar deta hai.

Har pause ko meaning de deta hai.
