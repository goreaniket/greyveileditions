Bas ek gradual shift hota hai jahan andar kuch aisa hota hai jo bahar jo ho raha hota hai usse match karna band kar deta hai.

Aur woh mismatch ek feeling create karta hai jise hum loss kehte hain.

Chahe externally kuch bhi khatam na hua ho.

Asal mein tum life mein kabhi raw person ke saath interact nahi karte.

Tum hamesha unka ek constructed version hi dekhte ho.

Ek mental structure jo time ke saath build hota hai — conversations, emotional highs, chhote patterns, expectations, silence aur memories se.

Aur jab woh structure tumhare mind mein stable ho jaata hai, tum person ko directly dekhna band kar dete ho.

Tum us version ko dekhne lagte ho jo tumne khud banaya hota hai.

To jab reality thodi si bhi us internal version se shift hoti hai, mind use “situation change” ki tarah register nahi karta.
