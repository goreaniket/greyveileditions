Kuch protections sirf purane pain ko repeat hone se rokne ki koshish karti hain... aur isi process mein nayi possibilities ko bhi andar aane nahi deti.

Yahin se emotional survival quietly emotional prison banne lagta hai.

THE INVISIBLE ARMOUR

Physical wound dikhta hai.

Emotional wound rarely dikhta hai.

Isliye body bandage pehenti hai...

mind patterns pehenta hai.

Ek baar trust toot gaya...

ab mind kehta hai:

"Next time pehle doubt karna."

Ek baar ignore feel hua...

"Next time zyada attach mat hona."

Ek baar expectations ne hurt kiya...

"Next time expect hi mat karna."

Mind ko lagta hai woh strong bana raha hai.

Reality mein woh sirf walls bana raha hota hai.

Aur walls ka problem yeh nahi hota ki woh pain ko rok deti hain.
