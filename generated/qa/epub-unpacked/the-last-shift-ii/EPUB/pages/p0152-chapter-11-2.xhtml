Hum hamesha assume karte hain ki ek fixed “main” andar baitha hai.

Jo dekh raha hai.

Soch raha hai.

Decide kar raha hai.

Par agar closely dekho…

toh aisa koi fixed point milta hi nahi.

Bas thoughts aate hain… feelings aati hain… reactions aate hain…

aur beech mein mind ek label chipka deta hai:

“ye main hoon”

Par wo “main” koi object nahi hai.

Wo bas continuity ka illusion hai.

Kyuki thoughts continuous hain, mind assume kar leta hai ki thinker bhi continuous hoga.

Par continuity identity proof nahi hoti.

Wo sirf flow proof hota hai.

Tum ek person nahi ho jo thoughts ko dekh raha hai.

Tum thoughts ka hi ek continuous process ho jo apne aap ko moment-to-moment “main” bana raha hai.

Aur har moment pe ye “main” recreate hota hai.
