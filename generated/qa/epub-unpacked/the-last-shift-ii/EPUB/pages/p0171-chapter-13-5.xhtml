YOU DON'T HAVE TO EARN YOUR WORTH

Sabse dangerous belief shayad yeh hai—

"Mujhe pehle better banna padega... tab main deserve karunga."

Love deserve karunga.

Peace deserve karunga.

Respect deserve karunga.

Acceptance deserve karunga.

Lekin worth kabhi earn nahi hoti.

Worth existence ke saath aati hai.

Achievement ke saath nahi.

Tumhara value...

tumhare marks se bada hai.
