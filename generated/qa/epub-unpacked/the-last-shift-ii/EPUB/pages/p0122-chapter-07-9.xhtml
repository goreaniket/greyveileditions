“I am not seeing what is happening. I am seeing what I am.”

And that realization doesn’t fix everything.

But it makes everything slightly transparent.

Less absolute.

Less personal.

Less final.

And maybe that is the real shift:

not understanding people better…

but understanding that your understanding itself is not neutral.

And once that is seen clearly…

life doesn’t become simpler.

But it becomes less falsely certain.
