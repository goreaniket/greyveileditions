You were not ignored in the absolute sense you felt.

You were not “less important” in the way your mind concluded.

What happened was far more subtle.

A signal appeared.

And your mind assigned meaning to it faster than reality could be verified.

A delayed reply.

A shorter message.

A different tone.

A pause.

A silence.

None of these are events with fixed emotional identity.

They are neutral fragments.

But the mind does not experience fragments.

It completes them instantly.

And the moment completion happens, interpretation begins.

And interpretation always feels like truth — because it removes uncertainty.
