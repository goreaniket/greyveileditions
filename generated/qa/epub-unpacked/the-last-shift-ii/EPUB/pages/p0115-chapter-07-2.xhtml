Human mind has this built-in illusion that it observes reality directly.

But observation is never clean.

It is always mixed with:

past memory

current emotion

expectation

fear

attachment level

And this mixture becomes the lens.

You don’t see the world first and then feel.

You feel first — and then the world gets shaped.

REAL LIFE SCENE: THE SAME MESSAGE, DIFFERENT REALITIES

Imagine this:

A simple message arrives — “ok”

That’s it. One word.

Now observe what happens internally.

On a normal, stable day: It is just a reply. Nothing more.

On a slightly low day: “ok… maybe he is busy.”
