THE LAST SHIFT

SHIFT kabhi awaaz karke nahi aata.

Isi liye...

zyadatar log use notice hi nahi karte.

Lekin uske baad...

andar kuch dheere dheere badalne lagta hai.

Aur bina pata chale...

insaan apni life jeena chhod deta hai...

aur apni life ko observe karna shuru kar deta hai.

Ye kitab...

usi pehle crack ki kahani hai.

Aur shayad...

isi crack ke doosri taraf...

woh insaan khada hai...
