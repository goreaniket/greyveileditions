Tum apni interpretation experience karte ho.

Shuru mein yeh subtle hota hai.

Ek doubt.

Ek thought.

Ek chhoti si discomfort.

Par mind usko wahi nahi chhodta.

Wo usko expand karta hai.

Kyuki uncertainty uncomfortable hoti hai.

Isliye wo uncertainty ko explanation se replace kar deta hai.

Chahe explanation galat hi kyun na ho.

Aur yahi se distortion identity ban jata hai.

Kyuki repeat hone wali interpretation familiar lagne lagti hai.

Aur familiarity ko mind truth maan leta hai.

Isliye baad mein wo yeh nahi kehta:

“shayad wo ignore kar raha hai”

wo kehta hai:

“wo ignore kar raha hai”

No space. No doubt. No observation.

Sirf conclusion.
