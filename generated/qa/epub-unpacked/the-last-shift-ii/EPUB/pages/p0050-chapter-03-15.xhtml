WHEN IDENTITY IS BUILT OUT OF SENTENCES

Identity ek din mein nahi banti.

Wo hazaar chhote sentences se banti hai.

"Main shy hoon."

"Main unlucky hoon."

"Main overthinker hoon."

"Main emotionally weak hoon."

Shuru mein yeh observations hote hain.

Phir labels ban jaate hain.

Aur dheere dheere...

Rules.

Jab identity rule ban jaaye...

Toh insaan waise hi jeena shuru kar deta hai.

Wo naye experiences ko chance hi nahi deta.

Kyuki usne pehle hi decide kar liya hota hai...

Ki wo kaun hai.
