Jaise kisi ne original painting ke upar baar baar naye colours chadha diye ho.

Aur ek din original canvas dikhna hi band ho gaya.

THE NEED TO ALWAYS BE RIGHT

Narrator ka ek hidden motive bhi hota hai.

Usse truth se zyada...

Consistency pasand hoti hai.

Agar usne ek baar decide kar liya—

"Log trust ke layak nahi hote."

Toh wo har naye insaan mein wahi proof dhoondhega.

Jo insaan trustworthy hoga...

Uske actions ignore ho jayenge.

Jo ek baar bhi mistake karega...

Narrator bolega—

"Dekha? Maine pehle hi kaha tha."

Usse khushi is baat ki nahi hoti ki wo sach tha.

Usse sukoon is baat ka hota hai...

Ki uski purani story tootne nahi paayi.

Aur isi tarah...

Kai log reality nahi jeete.

Wo apni purani kahaniyon ko bachate rehte hain.
