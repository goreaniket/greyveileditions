Psychological prison ki sabse dangerous quality ye hai...

wo khud ko protect karta hai.

"Shayad tum galat samajh rahe ho."

"Impossible."

"Tu nahi samjhega."

"Mujhe pata hai."

Prison nahi chahta...

ki tum uske existence pe doubt karo.
