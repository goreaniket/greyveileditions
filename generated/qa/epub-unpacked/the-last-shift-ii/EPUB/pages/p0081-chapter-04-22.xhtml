THE COURAGE TO CHANGE YOUR MIND

Log aksar consistency ko strength samajhte hain.

Par har consistency strength nahi hoti.

Kabhi kabhi wo sirf stubbornness hoti hai...

jo maturity jaisi dikh rahi hoti hai.

Real courage har baar apni baat prove karna nahi hai.

Real courage kabhi kabhi sirf itna hota hai—

"Main is cheez ko dobara dekhunga."

Ye sentence bahut simple lagta hai.

Lekin isi sentence ki wajah se civilizations badli hain.

Science aage badha hai.

Relationships heal hue hain.

Aur log apni purani identities se bahar nikle hain.

Growth ka matlab har baar naya answer paana nahi.

Kabhi kabhi purane answer ko respectfully chhod dena bhi growth hota hai.

THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN AN OPEN MIND AND AN EMPTY MIND

Open mind ka matlab har baat maan lena nahi hota.

Aur na hi har belief ko reject kar dena.
