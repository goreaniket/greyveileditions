WHEN FEAR BECOMES THE AUTHOR

Narrator hamesha neutral nahi hota.

Uski language aksar us emotion se decide hoti hai... jo us waqt andar sabse zyada active hota hai.

Aur un emotions mein...

Fear sabse loud writer hai.

Fear kabhi directly nahi kehta, "Main dar raha hoon."

Wo disguise pehen leta hai.

Wo logic ki tarah bolta hai.

"Expectation mat rakh."

"Zyada close mat ho."

"Trust mat kar."

"Pehle hi distance bana le."

Sunne mein yeh practical advice lagti hai.

Par kai baar...

Yeh wisdom nahi hoti.

Yeh sirf fear ka survival script hota hai.

Difference bahut subtle hai.

Wisdom tumhe reality ke paas le jaati hai.

Fear tumhe possibility se door le jaata hai.

Dono ki language kabhi kabhi same hoti hai...
