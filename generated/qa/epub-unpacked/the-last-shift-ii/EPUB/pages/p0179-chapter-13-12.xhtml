WHEN NOTHING NEEDS TO CHANGE FIRST

Mind kehta hai—

"Pehle life change hogi...

phir main peaceful ho jaunga."

Healing quietly kehti hai—

"Shayad pehle tumhara relation life ke saath change hoga...

phir baaki cheezein apni jagah par settle hone lagenge."

Ye guarantee nahi.

Ye invitation hai.

Invitation...

khud ko project ki tarah dekhna chhodne ka.

Khud ko problem samajhna chhodne ka.

Aur pehli baar...

sirf insaan ki tarah dekhne ka.
