THE STORIES WE START DEFENDING

Ek waqt ke baad narrator sirf stories nahi banata...

Wo unhe protect bhi karta hai.

Agar tumhari kahani hai...

"Main hamesha akela reh jaata hoon."

Toh narrator har naye experience ko usi frame mein fit karega.

Koi insaan genuinely tumhari care kare...

Narrator bolega,

"Bas shuru mein sab aise hi hote hain."

Koi tumhari tarif kare...

"Formality hogi."

Koi tumhare liye waqt nikale...

"Kisi kaam se aaya hoga."

Notice karo...

Yahan narrator truth prove nahi kar raha.

Wo apni purani story ko bachane ki koshish kar raha hai.

Aur insaan apni kahaniyon se itna attach ho jaata hai...
