WHEN OPINIONS STOP BEING OPINIONS

Shuru mein opinions flexible hote hain.

Aaj kuch aur socha.

Kal kuch aur samjha.

Par dheere dheere kuch opinions humare andar settle ho jaate hain.

Aur phir wo sirf opinions nahi rehte.

Wo identity ban jaate hain.

"I am this kind of person."

"Main hamesha logon ko sahi judge karta hoon."

"Main kabhi kisi pe jaldi trust nahi karta."

"Main practical hoon."

Ye statements harmless lagte hain.

Par inke andar ek invisible prison hota hai.

Kyunki ab agar reality in beliefs ke against jaati hai…

toh sirf belief nahi hilta.

Identity hilti hai.

Aur identity ko shake karna…

mind ko existence shake hone jaisa feel hota hai.
