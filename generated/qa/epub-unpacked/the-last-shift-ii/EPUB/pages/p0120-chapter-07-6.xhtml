WHY PEOPLE FEEL LIKE THEY CHANGE

Because you don’t meet people in one fixed state.

You meet them through different versions of yourself:

version that is secure

version that is doubtful

version that is attached

version that is detached

And each version creates a different “truth” about the same person.

So when you say:

“pehle aisa nahi tha”

what you are really saying is:

“pehle main aisa nahi tha jab main usko aise dekh raha tha.”

THE BREAKING POINT: WHEN ASSUMPTION FEELS LIKE REALITY

The most dangerous shift happens quietly.

At first:

“shayad aisa ho”

Then:

“lag raha hai aisa hi hai”
