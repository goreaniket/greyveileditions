THE LAST SHIFT

Aur shayad...

yehi poori journey ka final point tha.

Na overthinking ko destroy karna.

Na ego ko maar dena.

Na perfect insaan banna.

Bas itna dekh lena...

ki tumhare aur life ke beech...

sirf thoughts khade the.

Aur jab woh thode transparent hote hain...

toh life wahi rehti hai.

Par pehli baar...

tum usse directly dekh paate ho.

Na filter.

Na story.

Na fear.

Bas...

Life.
