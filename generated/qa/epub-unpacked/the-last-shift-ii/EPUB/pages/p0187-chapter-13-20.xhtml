Ek din uthna… aur phir kabhi hurt na hona.

Kabhi overthink na karna.

Kabhi anxious na feel karna.

Kabhi kisi ki yaad na aana.

Kabhi tootna na.

Aur isi expectation ki wajah se…

healing shuru hone se pehle hi impossible lagne lagti hai.

Kyuki reality kuch aur hai.

Healing ka matlab pain ka disappear ho jaana nahi hota.

Healing ka matlab hota hai—

Pain ke saath tumhara relationship badal jaana.

THE PERSON YOU KEEP TRYING TO BECOME

Notice karo...

Kitni baar tumne khud se kaha hoga—

"Bas thoda aur strong banna hai."

"Bas emotions control karne hain."

"Bas overthinking khatam karni hai."

"Bas better version banna hai."

Surface pe ye motivation lagta hai.
