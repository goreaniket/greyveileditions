On an insecure day: “ok… something is wrong.”

On an attached day: “ok… he doesn’t care anymore.”

Same message. Same person. Same situation.

But internally, four completely different worlds are created.

And the person on the other side didn’t change even once.

WHY THIS HAPPENS WITHOUT YOU NOTICING

Because your mind is not a camera.

It is a meaning-making machine.

It cannot tolerate empty space.

So when something is unclear — tone, silence, delay — it immediately fills it.

Not with truth.

With possibility.

And possibility is always shaped by your current emotional state.

SILENCE IS NEVER SILENCE

Take another example.

Someone sees your message and doesn’t reply.

Fact: no reply.
