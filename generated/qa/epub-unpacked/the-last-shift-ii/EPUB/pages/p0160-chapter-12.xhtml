ARC III - THE HEALING

WHEN EVERYTHING BECOMES CLEAR, NOTHING NEEDS TO BE SOLVED

Ek stage aata hai understanding mein jahan

lagta hai ki ab finally sab kuch clear ho jayega.

Ek final answer.

Ek ultimate clarity.

Ek aisa point jahan life “solve” ho jaye.

Par jo actually hota hai, wo completely different hota hai.

Silence.

Par ye silence bahar ka nahi hota… andar ka hota hai.

Jahan cheezon ko explain karne ki aadat dheere dheere kam ho jaati hai.
