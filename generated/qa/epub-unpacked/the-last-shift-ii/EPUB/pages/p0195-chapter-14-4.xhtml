Phir se.

Understanding ka last step...

understanding ko bhi chhod dena hota hai.

andar utarne se samajh aati hai.

ab chalne ka time hai.

PART 2 — STOP WAITING FOR LIFE TO BEGIN

Ek aur illusion hota hai...

jo bahut quietly humare andar grow karta hai.

"Ek din..."

Ek din sab theek ho jayega.

Ek din main completely heal ho jaunga.

Ek din overthinking khatam ho jayegi.

Ek din fear nahi rahega.

Ek din confidence aa jayega.

Aur us din...

main finally jeena shuru karunga.
