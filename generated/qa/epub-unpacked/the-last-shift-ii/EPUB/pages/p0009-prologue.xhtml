PROLOGUE

Before the First Crack

Agar tum ye padh rahe ho...

toh shayad tum kisi answer ki talaash mein ho.

Ya shayad...

tum answers se bhi thak chuke ho.

Ho sakta hai tumhari life bahar se bilkul normal dikhti ho.

Log tumhe dekhte hain... tum haste ho. Reply karte ho. Kaam karte ho. Padhte ho. Khate ho. Sote bhi ho.

Sab kuch normal lagta hai.

Sirf ek cheez normal nahi hoti...

Tumhare andar chal raha shor.

Ek shor... jo kabhi rukta nahi.

Kabhi kisi message ko lekar.

Kabhi kisi yaad ko lekar.

Kabhi kisi future ko lekar...

jo abhi tak aaya hi nahi.
