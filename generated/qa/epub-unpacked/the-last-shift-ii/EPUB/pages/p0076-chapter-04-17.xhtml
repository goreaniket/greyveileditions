Har insaan ke paas ek hidden identity hoti hai.

Koi khud ko "ignored person" maanta hai.

Koi "problem solver."

Koi "people pleaser."

Koi "unlucky."

Aur dheere dheere identity sirf description nahi rehti…

Wo filter ban jaati hai.

Uske baad jo bhi experience aata hai… mind usko identity ke according interpret karta hai.

Agar identity hai—

"Mujhe koi genuinely choose nahi karta."

Toh hundred caring moments bhi us belief ko change nahi karte.

Par ek rejection…

Wo instantly proof ban jaata hai.

Kyuki mind evidence collect nahi karta.

Wo confirmation collect karta hai.

WHY CHANGE FEELS LIKE A THREAT

Log sochte hain change difficult hota hai.

Reality thodi aur deep hai.
