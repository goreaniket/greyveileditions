ki hum pehchan lein—

PART 3 — WHY THE MIND CHOOSES THE FAMILIAR PAIN

Ek ajeeb paradox hai.

Insaan hamesha happiness nahi choose karta.

Wo familiarity choose karta hai.

Aur kai baar familiarity ka matlab hota hai — pain.

Agar kisi ne saalon tak criticism suna ho… toh appreciation usse fake lag sakti hai.

Agar kisi ne hamesha inconsistency dekhi ho… toh consistency suspicious lag sakti hai.

Agar kisi ne baar baar logon ko jaate dekha ho… toh jab koi rukta hai… tab bhi mind ready rehta hai uske jaane ka wait karne ke liye.

Kyuki mind ke liye unknown se zyada safe hota hai — known suffering.
