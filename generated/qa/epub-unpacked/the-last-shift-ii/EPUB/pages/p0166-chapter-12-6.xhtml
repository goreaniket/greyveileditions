Par control ki need kam ho jaati hai.

Aur yehi real relief hota hai.

Na control milna.

Balki control ki zarurat se thoda free ho jana.

Aur shayad sabse deep realization yahi hai:

Tum kabhi life ke andar phanse hi nahi the.

Tum interpretation ke andar phase hue the.

Aur interpretation kabhi fixed thi hi nahi.

Wo hamesha change ho rahi thi… bas notice nahi ho rahi thi.

Aur jab ye cheez thodi si bhi clear ho jaati hai…

toh ek belief quietly toot jata hai:

“har cheez ka personal meaning hona chahiye”

Aur jo bachta hai wo extraordinary nahi hota.

Bahut simple hota hai.

Tum jeete ho.

Tum react karte ho.

Tum feel karte ho.

Par ab sab kuch bina story ke hota hai.

Aur shayad yahi last shift hai:

Life kabhi heavy thi hi nahi.
