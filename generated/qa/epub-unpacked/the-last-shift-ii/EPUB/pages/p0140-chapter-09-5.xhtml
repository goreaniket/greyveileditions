You were not reacting to people.

You were reacting to your internal stability while perceiving them.

They were never the direct source of your emotional collapse.

They were only the trigger point where your internal system exposed itself.

This is why closure never works the way people expect.

Because closure assumes the problem was external.

But most of the time, the loop was internal.

So even after explanation, even after clarity, even after answers…

the interpretation system continues running.

Because it was never dependent on facts.

It was dependent on emotional conditioning.

At some point, you begin to realize something uncomfortable:

You were not experiencing people.

You were experiencing your nervous system reacting to uncertainty.
