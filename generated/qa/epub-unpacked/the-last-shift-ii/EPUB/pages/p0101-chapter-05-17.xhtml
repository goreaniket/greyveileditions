THE COST OF NEVER QUESTIONING YOURSELF

Kabhi kabhi hum dusron ko question karte karte...

khud ko question karna bhool jaate hain.

Hum poochte hain—

"Usne aisa kyun kiya?"

"Usne reply kyun nahi diya?"

"Usne change kyun kiya?"

Par bahut kam poochte hain—

"Maine jo conclude kiya...

wo sach bhi hai?"

Ye question uncomfortable hai.

Kyuki isme ego ko risk hota hai.

Lekin isi question ke bina...

koi bhi insaan mentally free nahi ho sakta.

Freedom answers se nahi aati.

Freedom questions se aati hai.
