living ki.

Lekin agar is poori kitab ko...

sirf ek sentence mein sametna ho...

toh shayad woh sentence ye hoga—

"Tum kabhi apne thoughts nahi the."

Bas.

Itna hi.

Baaki sab...

isi ek baat ki alag alag kahaniyan thi.

Ek din...

shayad tum ye kitab dobara padhoge.

Aur tumhe lagega...

ye pehli baar se alag hai.

Kitab nahi badlegi.

Reader badal chuka hoga.

Aur wahi...

har real journey ki pehchaan hoti hai.

Aaj ke baad...

jab bhi tum overthink karoge...

shayad tum khud se ladne ki jagah...

sirf muskura doge.
