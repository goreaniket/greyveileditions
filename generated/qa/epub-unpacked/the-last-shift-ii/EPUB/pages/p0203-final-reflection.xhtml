ENDING

Final Reflection — After the Shift

There is a point where introspection stops feeling like thought and starts feeling like exposure.

Not emotional. Not poetic. Structural exposure—like something inside consciousness quietly stepping out of hiding and realizing it was never separate from what it was observing.

If you have reached here, nothing external has changed. That was never the point. What has changed—if anything at all—is the collapse of the assumption that experience needs to be continuously translated into meaning.

The mind does not begin with truth. It begins with interpretation. And interpretation, repeated long enough, hardens into identity.

That is the first quiet distortion: not illusion, but over-definition.
