PART 4 — THE FREEDOM OF NOT KNOWING

THE HEAVIEST WORD IN THE HUMAN MIND

Human mind ke liye sabse uncomfortable word shayad "pain" nahi hai.

Wo hai—

"Maybe."

Maybe main galat hoon.

Maybe mujhe poori picture nahi dikh rahi.

Maybe jis certainty ko main truth samajh raha tha...

wo sirf meri current understanding hai.

Ye word ego ko pasand nahi.

Kyunki ego permanence chahta hai.

Usse fixed answers pasand hain.
