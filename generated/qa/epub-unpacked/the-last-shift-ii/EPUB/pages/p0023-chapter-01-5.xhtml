WHEN STORIES START FEELING MORE REAL THAN EVENTS

Ek point aata hai jahan hum actual event ko yaad hi nahi rakhte…

hum apni story ko yaad rakhte hain.

Event: “reply late aaya.”

Story: “woh avoid kar raha hai.”

Aur story repeat hoti hai.

Aur repetition belief ban jati hai.

Aur belief truth lagne lagta hai.

Aur truth identity ban jata hai.

“main aisa hi hoon jo sab samajh leta hai.”

“main pehle hi sense kar leta hoon.”

Par asal mein yeh understanding nahi hoti…

yeh trained expectation hoti haii
