Then:

“obviously aisa hi hai”

And finally:

“main jaanta hoon yeh sach hai”

At this point, assumption stops being flexible.

It becomes identity-backed belief.

And once belief forms, curiosity dies.

THE LOSS OF DIRECT EXPERIENCE

Slowly, life stops being experienced.

It starts being analyzed.

A laugh is no longer just a laugh.

A pause is no longer just a pause.

A message is no longer just a message.

Everything becomes a signal for something deeper.

And ironically, while trying to understand everything deeper…

you lose the surface completely.

But surface is where reality actually exists.

WHY THIS SYSTEM IS HARD TO NOTICE

Because it feels intelligent.

It feels like awareness.
