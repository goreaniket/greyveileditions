Par ab wo instantly “main” ban ke chipak nahi jaate.

Ab wo zyada tar aisa lagta hai:

“ye ho raha hai”

na ki

“ye mere saath hi kyun ho raha hai”

Aur ye change dramatic nahi hota.

Koi sudden enlightenment moment nahi hota.

Na koi explosion.

Bas ek slow loosening hoti hai.

Jaise koi cheez jo bahut tight pakdi hui thi… dheere dheere relax ho rahi ho.

Dimaag ab bhi stories banata hai.

Ab bhi interpretation hoti hai.

Ab bhi comparison hota hai.

Ab bhi prediction hoti hai.

Par farq yeh hai:

Ab wo authority nahi lagti.

Bas mental activity lagti hai.

Ek message aata hai:

“ok”
