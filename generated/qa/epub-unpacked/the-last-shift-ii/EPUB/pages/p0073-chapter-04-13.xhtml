Sabse ajeeb paradox yeh hai—

Jitna zyada mind safety create karta hai...

utni hi kam life feel hoti hai.

Risk kam.

Pain kam.

Lekin excitement bhi kam.

Curiosity bhi kam.

Depth bhi kam.

Aur dheere dheere...

insaan survive toh karta hai...

par fully live nahi karta.

Aur shayad isi liye...

sirf safe rehna...

jeena nahi hota.

END OF PART 2

Mind ka asli goal humein khush banana nahi hota.

Uska goal hota hai—

humein bachaye rakhna.

Aur survival ke liye woh kabhi kabhi wohi cheezein repeat karta rehta hai...

jo kabhi kaam aayi thi.
