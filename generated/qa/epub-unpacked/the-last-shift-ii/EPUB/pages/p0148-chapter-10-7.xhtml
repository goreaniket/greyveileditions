“main overthink karta hoon”

“main attach ho jata hoon”

“main zyada feel karta hoon”

Par yeh truth nahi hai.

Yeh sirf repeated emotional loops hain.

Ab sabse hidden part:

“main” jo hurt feel kar raha hai…

wo koi fixed entity nahi hai.

Wo har moment pe thought ke saath identify hone wala moving center hai.

Par kyuki wo continuous lagta hai…

isliye wo permanent lagta hai.

Ek thought aata hai:

“wo care nahi karta”

Aur instantly body react karti hai.

Emotion aata hai.

Aur us emotion ko truth label mil jata hai.

“That’s true.”

Aur ab thought thought nahi raha.

Wo reality ban gaya.

Aur jab thought reality ban jata hai…
