Aur layers illusion create karti hain.

Ek point pe aake yeh dikhta hai:

Jo “main” tum protect kar rahe the…

wo kabhi stable tha hi nahi.

Wo har moment change ho raha tha.

Har thought ke saath.

Har feeling ke saath.

Har reaction ke saath.

Toh sawaal yeh bachta hai:

Tum actually protect kya kar rahe the?

Ek moving image.

Ek story jo continuously update ho rahi thi.

Aur sabse shocking realization yeh hota hai:

Na koi fixed center tha.

Na koi permanent identity.

Bas experience ho raha tha…

aur awareness usko notice kar rahi thi.

Par mind empty space ko accept nahi karta.

Isliye wo continuously ek center banata rehta hai.

Kyuki bina center ke usko “loss” feel hota hai.

Par jo loss lag raha hota hai…
