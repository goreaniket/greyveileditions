Par mind wahan stop nahi karta.

“busy hoga…”

“ya ignore kar raha hai…”

“ya interest kam ho gaya…”

“ya maine kuch galat bola…”

Aur interesting baat yeh hai —

yeh sab assumptions lagte hi nahi.

Yeh clarity lagti hai.

Aur jab assumption clarity ban jaye…

toh distortion already complete ho chuka hota hai.

WHY THE MIND CANNOT ACCEPT NOTHING

Mind ka sabse basic discomfort yeh hai: uncertainty

Agar kuch explain nahi hota…

mind usko empty nahi chhodta.

Wo usme meaning inject karta hai.

Kyuki “unknown” se zyada uncomfortable cheez uske liye kuch nahi hota.

Isliye:

Silence → rejection ban jata hai

delay → distance ban jata hai

confusion → story ban jata hai
