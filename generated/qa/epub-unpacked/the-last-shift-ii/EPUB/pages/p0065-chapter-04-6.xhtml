THE INVISIBLE DEFENSE

Isi liye jab koi humse disagree karta hai…

toh kabhi kabhi hum uski baat sunte hi nahi.

Hum sirf wait karte hain…

kab wo bolna khatam kare.

Taaki hum apna answer de sakein.

Surface pe lagta hai conversation chal rahi hai.

Reality mein do monologues chal rahe hote hain.

Dono sun nahi rahe.

Dono defend kar rahe hain.

Aur jab defense active ho…

tab understanding impossible ho jaati hai.

Kyunki understanding tab aati hai…

jab tum temporarily apni certainty side par rakh do.

Aur ego ko ye sabse mushkil kaam lagta hai.

THE FIRST SILENT SHIFT

Shayad problem galat hone mein kabhi thi hi nahi.

Problem us fear mein thi…

ki agar hum galat nikle…

toh hum kam ho jayenge.

Kam intelligent.
