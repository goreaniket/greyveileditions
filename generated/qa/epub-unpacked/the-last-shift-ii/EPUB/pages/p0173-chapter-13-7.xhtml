PART 2 — THE DAY YOU STOP TRYING TO FIX YOURSELF

Ek strange moment aata hai healing ke beech...

Jahan tum suddenly realize karte ho ki tum saalon se heal nahi ho rahe the.

Tum khud ko repair karne ki koshish kar rahe the.

Aur repair aur healing ek hi cheez nahi hote.

Repair ka matlab hota hai—

"Kuch toot gaya hai."

Healing ka matlab hota hai—

"Shayad kuch toot hi nahi tha."

Bas kuch samjha nahi gaya tha.

Aur ye realization pehle comfort nahi deta.

Ye confusion deta hai.

Kyuki agar main broken nahi tha...

Toh phir itne saal main kis cheez ko theek karne ki koshish kar raha tha?
