“kyun reply aisa aaya?”

“kyun mujhe aisa feel ho raha hai?”

Aur ye questions sirf questions nahi hote the…

ye loop ban jaate the.

Par ab ek difference aata hai.

Ye questions aate toh hain…

par wo tumhe consume nahi karte.

Wo tumhe apne andar kheench nahi lete.

Wo bas pass hoke chale jaate hain.

Aur sabse ajeeb shift yahi hota hai:

Silence ab uncomfortable nahi lagta.

Silence bas silence lagta hai.

Na usme koi hidden meaning hota hai.

Na koi signal hota hai.

Na koi emotional message.

Bas absence.

Aur wo absence ab threatening nahi lagti.

Ek aur shift hota hai:

Pain aata hai.

Thoughts aate hain.

Feelings aati hain.
