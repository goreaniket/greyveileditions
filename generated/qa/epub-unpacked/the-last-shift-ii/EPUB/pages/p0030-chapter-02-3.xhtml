Aur dheere dheere silence blank nahi rehta…

wo filled ho jata hai interpretation se.

Aur filled silence hamesha heavy hota hai.

THE INNER WRITER THAT NEVER STOPS

Humare andar ek automatic writer hota hai.

Wo continuously scenes likh raha hota hai:

late reply → “distance aa gaya”

short message → “cold behavior”

seen without reply → “intentional ignorance”

change in tone → “emotional shift”

Aur problem yeh nahi hai ki wo write karta hai…

problem yeh hai ki hum us writing ko read karke react karte hain as if it’s real.

WHEN ASSUMPTION FEELS LIKE UNDERSTANDING

Sabse dangerous phase yeh hota hai:

jab assumption confusion nahi lagta.

wo clarity lagta hai.

“ab samajh aa gaya.”

“yeh toh obvious hai.”

“main pehle hi sense kar raha tha.”

Aur clarity ka illusion mind ko relax kar deta hai.
