toh frustration sirf misunderstanding ka nahi hota.

Pain is baat ka hota hai ki...

tumhari reality kisi aur tak pahunch hi nahi paayi.

Aur us moment pe insaan sirf explain nahi karta...

wo almost apni existence defend kar raha hota hai.

Kyuki har explanation ke peeche ek silent sentence hota hai—

"Please mujhe waise dekho jaisa main hoon."

WHY WE KEEP EXPLAINING OURSELVES

Kuch log ek baar explain karte hain.

Kuch log dus baar.

Kuch log saalon tak karte rehte hain.

Aur bahar se lagta hai...

yeh over-explaining hai.

Par andar...

yeh validation ki craving nahi hoti.

Yeh ek silent hope hoti hai—

"Shayad is baar koi sach mein samajh le."

Har extra explanation ke peeche information kam hoti hai...

aur acceptance ki need zyada hoti hai.
