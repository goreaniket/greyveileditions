YOU START CURATING YOUR PERSONALITY

family ke saath ek version

alone ek version

unknown logon ke saath ek defensive version

Aur problem ye nahi hai ki multiple versions hain…

problem ye hai ki koi bhi version fully “you” nahi lagta.
