ARC II - THE AWARENESS

YOU WERE NEVER A FIXED SELF — JUST A MOVING EXPERIENCE

Ek point aata hai understanding mein jahan sab kuch “mera life” jaisa feel hona kam hone lagta hai…

aur cheezein ek flow ki tarah dikhne lagti hain jo apne aap ho rahi hoti hain.

Aur sabse uncomfortable part yeh hota hai

bahar kuch change nahi hota.

Log same hote hain. Life same hoti hai.

Par jo “center” tha jahan se tum sabko interpret kar rahe the… wo stable rehna band kar deta hai.

Na toot-ta hai.

Bas solid feel hona band kar deta hai.
