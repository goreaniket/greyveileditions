WHEN PEOPLE BECOME SYMBOLS

Aur jab interpretation aur strong ho jati hai…

log log nahi rehte.

wo symbols ban jate hain:

“safe person”

“risky person”

“confusing person”

“temporary person”

Aur jab koi insaan symbol ban jata hai…

toh usko samajhne ki journey khatam ho jati hai.

Bas label reh jata hai.

THE LOSS OF RAW MOMENTS

Ab wo intention hota hai.

Ek silence bas space hota tha.
