Aur dheere dheere…

bachcha un baaton ko sunna shuru karta hai.

"Ye bahut shaant hai."

"Ye bahut ziddi hai."

"Isse maths nahi aati."

"Ye toh hamesha emotional rehta hai."

Shuru mein ye sirf observations hote hain.

Phir labels ban jaate hain.

Aur ek din…

woh labels identity ban jaate hain.

Sabse ajeeb baat?

Insaan ko yaad bhi nahi rehta…

ki usne ye identity kab accept ki.

Par mind unmein se kuch ko choose karta hai.

Ek exam fail hua.

Ek friendship toot gayi.

Ek stage pe words bhool gaye.

Ek baar kisi ne compare kar diya.
