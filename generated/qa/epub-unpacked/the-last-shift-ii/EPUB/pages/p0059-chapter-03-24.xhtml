Aur jis din woh pehli baar yeh dekh leta hai ki

"Mere andar jo bol raha hai... zaroori nahi ki wahi main hoon."

Us din koi miracle nahi hota.

Duniya bhi nahi badalti.

Log bhi waise hi rehte hain.

Bas ek bahut subtle shift hota hai.

Aur kabhi kabhi...

Puri zindagi badalne ke liye...

Sirf utna hi kaafi hota hai.
