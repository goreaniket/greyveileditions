Ab connection dhoondhte ho.

Pehle tum prove karna chahte the...

ki tum enough ho.

Ab tum sirf present rehna chahte ho.

Aur interesting baat yeh hoti hai...

jo log sirf tumhare performance ke saath the...

woh dheere dheere door ho jaate hain.

Aur jo log tumhare existence ke saath the...

woh quietly reh jaate hain.

Tab samajh aata hai...

ki relationship effort se nahi...

presence se bante hain.

THE RETURN TO YOURSELF

Sabse lambi journey duniya ghoomne ki nahi hoti.

Khud tak wapas aane ki hoti hai.

Aur jab tum finally wapas aate ho...

toh pata chalta hai...

jise tum itne saal dhoondh rahe the...

woh kabhi gaya hi nahi tha.

Woh bas...

thoughts ke shor ke neeche chup gaya tha.
