Aur yahi real freedom start hoti hai.

Life same rehti hai.

Log same rehte hain.

Par tumhara relation unse change ho jata hai.

Aur sabse important realization:

Tum toot nahi rahe ho.

Tum sirf thoughts ko truth maan rahe the.

Aur jab ye illusion break hota hai…

toh pain disappear nahi hota…

par uska hold toot jata hai.
