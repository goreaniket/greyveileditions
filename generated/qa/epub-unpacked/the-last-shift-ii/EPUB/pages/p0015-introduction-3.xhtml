Tu aware nahi hota…

tu over-interpret kar raha hota hai.

Ye book tujhe kuch naya nahi sikha rahi.

Ye sirf ek cheez todne ki koshish kar rahi hai:

Tera automatic meaning-making system.

Kyuki jab tak har experience ko meaning milega…

tab tak experience kabhi raw nahi hoga.

Ek simple sa truth:

Tu apni life ko direct nahi jeeta.

Tu uska continuous narration jeeta hai.

Aur narration itna fast hota hai…

ki tu kabhi notice hi nahi karta ki original moment kab chala gaya.

Aur ab sabse uncomfortable part:

Jo tu “main” samajhta hai na…

wo ek fixed entity nahi hai.

Wo ek ongoing explanation hai.

Har second update hota hua…

har memory se edit hota hua…

har fear se reshape hota hua.

Isliye is book ka naam “The Last Shift” hai.
