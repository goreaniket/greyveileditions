Ek baar nahi.

Har baar.

Isliye identity stable lagti hai.

Kyuki wo continuously recreate hoti rehti hai bina break ke.

Isliye wo ek solid cheez jaisa feel hoti hai.

Par asal mein wo repetition hai.

Ek thought aata hai:

“main hurt hoon”

Phir next:

“main hamesha aisa hi feel karta hoon”

Phir:

“yehi main hoon”

Aur bas ek temporary emotion identity ban jata hai.

Na kyuki wo permanent hai…

par kyuki usko repeat kar diya gaya.

Aur jab cheez identity ban jati hai…

toh usko question karna difficult ho jata hai.

Kyuki identity ko question karna apne aap ko question karne jaisa lagta hai.

Isliye mind usko protect karta hai.
