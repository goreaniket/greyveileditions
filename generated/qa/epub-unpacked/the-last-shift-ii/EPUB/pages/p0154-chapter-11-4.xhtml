Chahe wo hurt hi kyun na kare.

Par agar thoda slow down karke dekho…

toh ek strange truth dikhta hai:

Koi fixed thinker nahi hai.

Bas thinking ho rahi hai.

Koi fixed feeler nahi hai.

Bas feeling ho rahi hai.

Koi fixed “main” nahi hai.

Bas experience ho raha hai.

Aur yeh samajh aate hi suffering ka structure change hone lagta hai.

Kyuki suffering ko ek “owner” chahiye hota hai.

Aur owner ko “main” chahiye hota hai.

Aur “main” ko thought se identification chahiye hota hai.

Tum emotions feel karte ho…

par tum un emotions ban nahi jate.

Tum anxiety feel karte ho…

par tum anxiety identity nahi ho.

Tum confusion feel karte ho…

par tum confusion “ho” nahi.
