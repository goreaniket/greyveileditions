Jis din tum sure ho gaye…

usi din tum dekhna kam kar dete ho.

Observation dheere dheere khatam hone lagti hai.

Kyunki observation tab hoti hai jab possibility open ho.

Certainty possibilities ko quietly band kar deti hai.

Aur jab possibilities band ho jaati hain…

tab reality andar aana bhi band ho jaati hai.

THE MOMENT CURIOSITY DIES

Curiosity ka matlab sirf questions poochna nahi hota.

Curiosity ka matlab hota hai—

"Ho sakta hai main jo dekh raha hoon, uske alawa bhi kuch ho."

Ye sentence simple lagta hai.

Lekin ego ke liye ye sentence bahut heavy hota hai.

Kyunki is sentence ke andar ek risk chhupa hota hai.

Risk of being wrong.

Aur ego ko galat hona pasand nahi.

Isliye wo curiosity ki jagah certainty choose karta hai.

Pehle hum puchte hain—
