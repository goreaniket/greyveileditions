Har question important lagta tha.

Aur tum answers collect karte gaye.

Page after page.

Chapter after chapter.

Lekin dheere dheere ek ajeeb cheez hui.

Questions kam hone lage.

Na isliye ki saare answers mil gaye.

Balki isliye...

kyunki kuch questions dissolve ho gaye.

Jaise subah hone ke baad flashlight ki zarurat khatam ho jaati hai.

Flashlight buri nahi thi.

Bas ab darkness utni nahi rahi.

Sabse dangerous addiction knowledge bhi ho sakti hai.

Mind ko lagta hai—

"Bas ek aur understanding...

ek aur realization...

ek aur breakthrough...

phir main finally free ho jaunga."

Lekin freedom information se nahi aati.

Freedom tab aati hai...
