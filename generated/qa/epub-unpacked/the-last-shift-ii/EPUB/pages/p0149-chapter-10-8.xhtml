toh wo perception control karne lagta hai.

Tum life se suffer nahi kar rahe…

tum thought ko truth maan rahe ho.

Aur truth absolute hota hai.

Isliye questioning band ho jati hai.

Aur perception freeze ho jata hai.

Par ek gap hamesha hota hai.

Thought aur “tum” ke beech.

Chhota sa gap.

Almost invisible.

Par real.

Us gap mein thought sirf movement hota hai.

Na truth.

Na identity.

Bas movement.

Aur jab wo gap dikhta hai…

toh ek shift hota hai:

Thought aata hai… par tum usme merge nahi hote

Emotion aata hai… par tum usme kho nahi jate

Intensity wahi rehti hai…

par ownership kam ho jati hai.
