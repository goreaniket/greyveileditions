THE SHIFT FROM SEEING TO INTERPRETING

Pehle hum dekhte the.

Phir hum samajhne lage.

Phir hum interpret karne lage.

Aur phir humne decide kar liya ki:

dekhna optional hai

interpretation necessary hai

Aur jab interpretation necessary ban jata hai…

toh direct experience lose ho jata hai.
