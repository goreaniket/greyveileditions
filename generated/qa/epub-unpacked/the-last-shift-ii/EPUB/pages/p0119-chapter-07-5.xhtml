REAL LIFE SCENE: FRIENDSHIP SHIFT

A friendship starts.

At first everything feels natural.

Replies don’t matter. Timing doesn’t matter. Silence doesn’t matter.

Then slowly attachment increases.

Now delay starts having weight.

A late reply is not just delay anymore — it is a signal.

Then expectation enters.

And expectation is the turning point where interpretation begins replacing experience.

Finally, one day you say:

“Woh badal gaya.”

But what actually changed was not the person.

It was your sensitivity to distance.
