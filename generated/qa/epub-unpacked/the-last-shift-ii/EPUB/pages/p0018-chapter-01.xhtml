ARC I - THE DISTORTION

WHAT WE FEEL VS WHAT IS!

Kabhi kabhi sabse bada illusion yeh hota hai ki hum sochte hain hum duniya dekh rahe hain…

jabki hum sirf apni internal state ka reflection dekh rahe hote hain.

Aur yeh baat samajhna simple lagti hai…

par jeete waqt yeh almost invisible hoti hai.

Ek normal sa din hota hai.

Koi insaan milta hai.

Koi baat hoti hai.

Kuch messages aate hain.

Kuch silence hota hai.

Aur externally sab kuch same lagta hai.
