Every past emotional uncertainty replayed in one instant.

And slowly, something dangerous happens.

You stop interacting with people.

You start interacting with predictions.

You are no longer responding to what they did.

You are responding to what you think they are about to do.

And once you begin living in prediction, reality becomes secondary.

This is why the same person can feel completely different to you on different days.

Not because they changed.

But because your interpretive system changed.

On a secure day, they are normal.

On an anxious day, they are distant.

On a sensitive day, they are cold.

On a triggered day, they are harmful.

And yet the input from them is often unchanged.

Only the receiver changed.

There is a deeper layer most people never reach.
