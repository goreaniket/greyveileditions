Yeh detachment nahi hai.

Yeh clarity hai.

Life same rehti hai.

Conversations same rehti hain.

Problems same rehte hain.

Par andar merging kam ho jati hai.

Ek aur illusion break hota hai:

Tum thoughts ke thinker nahi ho.

Tum us space ho jahan thoughts aate aur jaate hain.

Aur thoughts instructions nahi hain.

Wo sirf mental events hain.

Temporary movements.

Par kyuki wo “tumhare andar” ho rahe hote hain…

tum unko apna maan lete ho.

Aur jo cheez “apni” lagti hai…

wo serious ban jati hai.

Chahe wo sirf noise hi kyun na ho.

Isliye log apne mind se zyada suffer karte hain reality se.

Kyuki reality simple hoti hai.

Par interpretation layered hoti hai.
