Temporary.

"Main loser hoon."

"Main public speaking ke layak nahi."

"Main enough nahi hoon."

Ek moment khatam ho gaya.

Par us moment se nikla hua sentence…

saalon tak zinda raha.

Aur phir har naya experience…

usi sentence ke against judge hone laga.

utni baar woh sentence sach jaisa feel hone lagta hai.
