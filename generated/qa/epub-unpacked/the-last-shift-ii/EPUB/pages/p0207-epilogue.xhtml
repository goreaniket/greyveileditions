THE LAST SHIFT

Agar tum yahan tak aa gaye ho...

toh shayad...

ye kitab khatam ho gayi hai.

Par shayad...

tumhari journey abhi shuru hui hai.

Ek ajeeb si baat hoti hai na...

Hum sochte hain ki books hume answers dene ke liye likhi jaati hain.

Par shayad...

achhi books answers nahi deti.

Woh sirf...

galat questions gira deti hain.

Aur jab galat questions gir jaate hain...

toh kai answers ki zarurat hi nahi rehti.

Shayad tum notice karoge...
