Aur kabhi... sirf isliye... kyunki mind ko chup rehna aata hi nahi.

Shayad tumne bhi notice kiya hoga...

Jab room mein sab log chale jaate hain...

tab asli conversation shuru hoti hai.

Tumhare aur tumhare thoughts ke beech.

Ek thought aata hai.

Uske peeche dus aur.

Phir sau.

Aur dheere dheere...

ek chhoti si baat...

poori duniya ban jaati hai.

Mind stories likhne lagta hai.

Jo hua hi nahi...

woh bhi real lagne lagta hai.

Jo kisi ne socha bhi nahi...

woh bhi personal lagne lagta hai.

Aur ek din...

tum realize karte ho...

ki tum life kam...

apni interpretation zyada jee rahe ho.
