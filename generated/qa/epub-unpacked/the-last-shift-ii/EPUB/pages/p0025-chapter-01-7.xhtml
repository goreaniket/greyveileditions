Hum insaan ko nahi dekhte…

hum patterns dekhte hain.

“late reply → disinterest”

“short text → coldness”

“silence → ending"

Aur patterns safe lagte hain…

kyunki wo predict karne layak hote hain.

Par insaan predict nahi hote.

Isliye hum insaan ko simplify kar dete hain…

taaki hum unhe control kar sakein mentally

THE INTERNAL LOOP THAT FEEDS ITSELF

Aur phir ek loop start hota hai:

Tum assume karte ho distance hai → tum thoda withdraw karte ho

Tum withdraw karte ho → dusra bhi distant ho jata hai

Distant response aata hai → tumhe proof mil jata hai

Aur tum kehte ho: “I was right.”

Par tum yeh nahi dekhte ki tumne reality observe nahi ki…

tumne reality co-create ki
