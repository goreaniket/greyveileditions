CHAPTER 1

The Illusion of “Who I Will Become”

Hum apne aap ko aksar present mein nahi dekhte…

hum apne aap ko future ke through dekhte hain.

Ek invisible image continuously chal rahi hoti hai:

“Main aage jaake kya banunga”

Aur ye image itni natural lagti hai…

ki hum usko imagination nahi samajhte.

Hum usko direction samajhte hain.

Lekin agar closely dekha jaye…

toh jo “future self” hai…

wo present experience mein exist hi nahi karta.

Wo sirf ek mental projection hota hai.

Mind ek current moment ko uthata hai…
