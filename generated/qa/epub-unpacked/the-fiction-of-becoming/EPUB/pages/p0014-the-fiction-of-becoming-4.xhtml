toh ek strange possibility open hoti hai:

jo “becoming” lagta hai…

wo actually “avoiding the present” bhi ho sakta hai.

Aur jo “present” lagta hai…

wo already complete experience ho sakta hai…

bina kisi future definition ke.

Is book ka purpose ye nahi hai ki tumhe stop kar de.

Na goals ko reject karna.

Na life ko deny karna.

Ye sirf ek subtle shift point dikhata hai:

ki jo movement tumhe define karta hai…

wo tum ho nahi.

Jab becoming clearly visible ho jata hai…

toh ek strange clarity aati hai:

tum kisi cheez ki taraf ja nahi rahe…

tum sirf ek continuous unfolding ko interpret kar rahe ho.

Aur jab interpretation soft hota hai…

toh experience apne aap zyada direct lagne lagta hai.

Na pressure.

Na comparison.

Na hidden finish line.
