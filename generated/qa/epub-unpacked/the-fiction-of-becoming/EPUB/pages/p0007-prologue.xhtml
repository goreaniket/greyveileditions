PROLOGUE —

BEFORE BECOMING BEGINS

Har insaan ke andar ek silent pressure continuously chalta rehta hai…

jo kabhi visible nahi hota, par har moment ko shape karta rehta hai.

Ye pressure ek simple sa question se start hota hai:

“Main kya banunga?”

Lekin agar is question ko sirf thought na samjha jaye…

toh ye ek psychological architecture hai.

Ek invisible structure jo present ko hold karta hai…

aur usko continuously ek imagined future ke against measure karta hai.

Mind sirf present ko experience nahi karta…

wo present ko project bhi karta hai.

Aur projection kabhi neutral nahi hoti…
