Lekin “real main” hamesha future mein shift hota rehta hai.

Isliye becoming kabhi complete nahi hota.

Wo ek moving horizon hota hai.

Tum jitna uske paas jaate ho…

wo utna hi door chala jata hai.

Aur mind is movement ko progress samajhta hai.

Lekin agar deeply dekha jaye…

toh ye progress nahi…

ye delay system hai.

Ek continuous postponement of being.

Aur jab ye clearly dikhta hai…

toh ek strange realization aata hai:

jo “I will become” hai…

wo actually “I am avoiding what is” bhi ho sakta hai.

Aur present moment…

kabhi insufficient nahi hota.

Bas comparison usko insufficient bana deta hai.

Aur comparison ka root hamesha future image hota hai.

Isliye “who I will become” ek innocent thought nahi hai…
