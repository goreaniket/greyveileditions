Sirf present bachta hai…

without measurement.

Future self koi destination nahi hota…

wo ek reference point hota hai jo present ko shape karta rehta hai.

Aur jab ye reference point visible ho jata hai…

toh ek strange clarity aati hai:

jo “future me main hoon”…

wo abhi imagination hai.

Aur jo “abhi main hoon”…

wo already complete experience hai, bina definition ke.

Becoming ka structure tab tak powerful lagta hai…

jab tak projection invisible hota hai.

Jab projection dikh jata hai…

toh future dissolve nahi hota…

bas uski authority gir jati hai.

Aur tab ek simple recognition bachta hai:

experience ko complete hone ke liye future ki zarurat nahi hoti.

Sirf ek continuous present hota hai…
