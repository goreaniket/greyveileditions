complete me

Ye sab ek parallel existence ki tarah present ke saath rehte hain.

Aur present un sab ke against silently measured hota rehta hai.

Is structure mein koi bhi moment neutral nahi hota.

Har moment ek comparison field mein enter hota hai:

what I am vs what I should be

Aur jahan “should be” exist karta hai…

wahan “is” kabhi enough nahi hota.

Becoming koi event nahi hai…

ye ek continuous psychological motion hai.

Ek loop:

present experience

future projection

emotional gap

effort

temporary shift

new projection

Aur ye loop itni smooth speed se chalta hai…

ki lagta hai ye natural reality hai.

Hum is motion ko “growth” kehte hain.

“improvement” kehte hain.
