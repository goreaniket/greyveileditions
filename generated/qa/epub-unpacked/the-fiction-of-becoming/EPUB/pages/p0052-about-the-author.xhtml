ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Lalit R. Mishra is the creator of The Human Fiction series.

He writes from observation, not ideology.

From watching how thought builds identity, how meaning attaches itself to experience, and how a “self” is continuously constructed through narrative.

His central question is simple:

Why does experience feel like it is happening to someone?

His work does not aim to provide answers or beliefs.

It aims to make the structure of thinking visible.

In his view, clarity is not something added to life.

It appears when unnecessary interpretation quiets down.

The Human Fiction is not a philosophy to follow.

It is an exploration of how human experience becomes a story — and how that story is mistaken for reality.

He does not claim authority over truth.

He only points toward the process through which truth is interpreted.
