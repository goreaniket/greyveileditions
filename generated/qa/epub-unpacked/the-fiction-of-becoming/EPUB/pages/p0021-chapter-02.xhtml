CHAPTER 2

The Future Self Projection Mechanism

Future self koi real entity nahi hota…

ye ek mental projection system hota hai jo present ko continuously extend karta rehta hai.

Mind ek simple kaam karta hai:

wo present moment ko uthata hai…

aur usko time ke aage project kar deta hai.

Jaise ek shadow ko light ke aage stretch kar diya jaye…

aur phir maan liya jaye ki wo shadow already “next stage” hai.

Lekin wo extension kabhi actual reality mein exist nahi karta.
