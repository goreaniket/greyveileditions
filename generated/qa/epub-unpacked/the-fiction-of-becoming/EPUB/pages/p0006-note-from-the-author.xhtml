INTRODUCTION

NOTE FROM THE AUTHOR

The Human Fiction is not written to convince, correct, or conclude.

It is written to observe.

What it observes is not the world as an external reality…

but the way reality is continuously constructed through thought, memory, and interpretation.

Most of what feels like “understanding” is not final truth…

it is ongoing narration.

A process that turns raw experience into structured meaning.

This work does not oppose meaning.

It only points to how meaning is formed in the first place.

Nothing in these pages requires belief.

And nothing requires rejection.

Because both belief and rejection arise from the same mechanism — interpretation.

The intention here is not to provide answers…

but to make the process of asking visible.

And once the process is seen clearly…

its automatic authority begins to soften on its own.

The Human Fiction does not end with a conclusion.

It ends with a shift in seeing —

that experience does not need to be explained to be real.

It only needs to be seen.
