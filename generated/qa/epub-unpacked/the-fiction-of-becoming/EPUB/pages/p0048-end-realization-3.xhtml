nahi tha.

Na “becoming” start hua tha…

na “becoming” end hota hai.

Sirf ek continuous unfolding…

jo apne aap ko “someone becoming” ki tarah read karta raha.

Na future self real hai.

Na present self fixed hai.

Na journey linear hai.

Sirf shifting perception hai…

jo khud ko story bana leta hai.

Aur jab ye sab bhi unnecessary lagne lagta hai…

toh koi new state nahi aati.

Sirf ye obvious ho jata hai:

jo ho raha hai… wo explain hone se pehle hi complete hai.

Aur explanation…

sirf usko delay karta hai.

Isliye Book III end nahi hoti…

wo sirf ek illusion of direction drop kar deti hai.

Aur jo bachta hai…

wo kisi conclusion ki tarah nahi hota.

Wo bas hota hai:
