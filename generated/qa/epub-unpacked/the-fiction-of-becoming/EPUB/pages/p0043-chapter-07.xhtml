CHAPTER 7

The Desire to Change Oneself

Hum aksar ye sochte hain ki problem hum mein hai…

isliye hume change hona chahiye.

Lekin agar is desire ko closely dekha jaye…

toh ye sirf change ki wish nahi hoti.

Ye ek rejection hota hai present self ka.

Desire to change usually is tarah kaam karta hai:

present self observe hota hai

judgment aata hai (“ye enough nahi hai”)

ideal self imagine hota hai

gap create hota hai

change ka pressure build hota hai

Change ka desire aksar improvement nahi hota…
