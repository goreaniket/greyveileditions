Ye gap uncomfortable hota hai…

aur mind us discomfort ko reduce karna chahta hai.

Isliye ek movement start hota hai:

“main wahan tak pahunchunga”

Jaise hi tum move karte ho…

ideal image bhi shift ho jata hai.

Target constantly aage chala jata hai.

Aur tum kabhi “arrive” nahi karte.

future image → present comparison → incompleteness → effort → updated future image → repeat

Is loop ka hidden effect ye hai:

tum hamesha “becoming” mein rehte ho…

never “being”.

Mind ye belief create karta hai:

“Main abhi incomplete hoon, isliye future important hai.”

Lekin ek deeper observation ye hai:

incompleteness ek real condition nahi…

wo comparison ka byproduct hai.

Agar comparison drop ho jaye…

toh “incomplete” bhi disappear ho jata hai.
