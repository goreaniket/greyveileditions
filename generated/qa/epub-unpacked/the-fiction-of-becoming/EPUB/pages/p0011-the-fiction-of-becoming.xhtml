INTRODUCTION

THE FICTION OF BECOMING

Har insaan ke andar ek silent current chal raha hota hai…

jo kabhi rest nahi leta.

Ye current ek simple question se start hota hai:

“Main kya banunga?”

Lekin ye question utna innocent nahi hota jitna lagta hai.

Iske andar ek hidden assumption hota hai:

“Jo main ab hoon… wo final nahi hai.”

Aur yahi assumption poore human experience ko shape karta hai.

Ek future version hamesha present ke saath chalta rehta hai.

Wo visible nahi hota…

lekin feel hota hai.

better me

stronger me

healed me

successful me
