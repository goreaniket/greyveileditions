Wo sirf thought-space mein exist karta hai.

Aur isi thought-space mein ek identity create hoti hai:

“jo main ban sakta hoon”

Ye “ban sakta hoon” statement innocent lagta hai…

lekin iske andar ek hidden structure hota hai:

present ko incomplete maanna.

Projection ka mechanism simple nahi hota…

ye multi-layered hota hai.

Mind ek ideal image banata hai:

better version

successful version

healed version

stable version

complete version

Ye images real nahi hoti…

lekin emotionally real feel hoti hain.

Jaise hi ideal image create hota hai…

present automatically “less” lagne lagta hai.

Aur yahi gap generate hota hai:

what is vs what should be
