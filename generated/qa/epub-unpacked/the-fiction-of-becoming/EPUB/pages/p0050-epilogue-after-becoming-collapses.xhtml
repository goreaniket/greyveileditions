EPILOGUE — AFTER BECOMING COLLAPSES

Agar is poore structure ko ek distance se dekha jaye…

toh ek subtle collapse notice hota hai.

Becoming kabhi disappear nahi hota…

lekin uski authority dissolve ho jati hai.

Future ab bhi imagine hota hai…

lekin wo present ko define nahi karta.

Effort ab bhi hota hai…

lekin uske peeche ek silent clarity hoti hai:

ye movement necessary nahi hai, bas happening hai.

Pehele experience ek project lagta tha…

ab experience ek unfolding lagta hai.

Pehele har cheez ko improve karna zaroori lagta tha…

ab improvement ek optional movement lagta hai.

Loop ab bhi exist karta hai…

lekin uska emotional charge kam ho jata hai:
