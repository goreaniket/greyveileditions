THE INNER STRUCTURE

Becoming ka sabse subtle layer ye hai:

present ko incomplete feel karwana.

Aur jab present incomplete lagta hai…

toh ek invisible urgency activate hoti hai:

“abhi kuch aur hona chahiye.”

Is urgency ke andar hi poora psychological identity system build hota hai.

self-worth

ambition

success

healing

transformation

Sab isi movement ke different expressions hain.
