toh “change” ka urgency structure weak ho jata hai.

Iska matlab ye nahi ki movement band ho jata hai.

Kaam, learning, evolution — sab continue rehta hai.

Bas ek internal pressure dissolve ho jata hai:

“main abhi wrong hoon”

Hum change isliye nahi chahte kyuki hum grow karna chahte hain…

hum change isliye chahte hain kyuki hum present ko reject karte hain.

Jab present self ko bina label ke dekha jata hai…

toh wo ek project nahi lagta…

wo ek happening lagta hai.

Aur happening ko fix karne ki urgency kam ho jati hai.

Na resistance.

Na forced transformation.

Sirf awareness of what is already unfolding.
