CHAPTER 6

The Broken Self Narrative

Hum apne aap ko aksar ek story ke through define karte hain…

aur us story ka ek common version hota hai:

“I am broken.”

Ye sirf ek feeling nahi hoti…

ye ek narrative structure hota hai.

Ek experience hota hai jo uncomfortable hota hai…

aur mind usko ek stable identity mein convert kar deta hai.

painful experience
