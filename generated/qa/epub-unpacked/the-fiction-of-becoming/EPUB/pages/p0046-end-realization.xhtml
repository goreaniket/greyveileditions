ENDING

END REALIZATION

Agar yahan tak aaya hai…

toh shayad ek cheez notice hone lagi ho…

ki kuch “solve” nahi hua.

Aur shayad yahi point hai.

Humne hamesha ye assume kiya:

ki understanding se kuch change hoga.

Lekin ab ek strange clarity aati hai:

understanding bhi ek movement hai…

arrival nahi.

“Main kya banunga” — ye question kabhi future ke baare mein nahi tha…

ye present ke dissatisfaction ka echo tha.

Aur dissatisfaction kabhi reality ka part nahi tha…

wo comparison ka side-effect tha.

“Main incomplete hoon”
