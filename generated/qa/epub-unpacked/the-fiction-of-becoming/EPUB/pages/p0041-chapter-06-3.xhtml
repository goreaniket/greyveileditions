experience aur story ek cheez nahi hain.

story usko define karti hai.

jab har experience ko “proof” ki tarah use karna band ho jata hai.

Shayad “broken” koi actual state nahi…

sirf ek repeated interpretation pattern hai.

Aur jab interpretation pattern visible ho jata hai…

toh identity ka weight naturally kam ho jata hai.

Na because kuch fix hua…

but because kuch define karna urgent nahi lagta.
