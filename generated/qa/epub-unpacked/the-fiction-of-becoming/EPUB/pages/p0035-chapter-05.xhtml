CHAPTER 5

The Fiction of Healing

Hum healing ko ek final state samajh lete hain…

jaise ek point ho jahan sab theek ho jayega.

Lekin agar healing ko closely observe kiya jaye…

toh wo koi fixed end-state nahi hoti.

Wo ek continuous interpretation shift hota hai.

Ek experience hota hai jo painful lagta hai…

aur usko hum “damage” label kar dete hain.

Phir ek future imagine hota hai:

“jab main heal ho jaunga…”

Lekin is imagination ke andar ek hidden structure hota hai:

present self ko broken maanna
