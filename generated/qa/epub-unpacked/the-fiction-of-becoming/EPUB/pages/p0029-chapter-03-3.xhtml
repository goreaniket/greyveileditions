ye internal dissatisfaction ko sustain karne ka system bhi hai.

Aur dissatisfaction hi fuel hai.

Agar thoda sa gap clearly dikha liya jaye…

toh ek strange clarity aati hai:

growth khud problem nahi hai…

problem ye hai ki growth ko identity bana diya gaya hai.

Jab identity “becoming” pe based hoti hai…

toh “being” always incomplete lagta hai.

Agar ideal self ka pressure soften ho jaye…

toh growth stop nahi hoti…

bas uska emotional weight gir jata hai.

Aur jo bachta hai…

wo ek simple action hota hai:

without urgency, without identity crisis.

Na chase.

Na escape.

Sirf movement.
