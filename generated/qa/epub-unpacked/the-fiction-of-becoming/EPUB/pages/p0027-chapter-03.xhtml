CHAPTER 3

The Fiction of Growth

Hum growth ko ek natural truth ki tarah treat karte hain.

Jaise life ka default rule ho:

“hamesha better banna hai”

Lekin agar growth ko directly observe kiya jaye…

toh ek uncomfortable pattern dikhta hai.

Growth kabhi “being” se start nahi hoti…

wo hamesha “lack” se start hoti hai.

Ek feeling:

“abhi main complete nahi hoon.”

Aur isi feeling se ek direction create hoti hai:

“mujhe improve hona hai.”
