no urgency

no identity panic

no existential pressure

Sirf motion rehta hai…

without psychological weight.

Jo “becoming” lagta tha…

wo actually ek continuous interpretation of presence tha.

Aur jab interpretation soft ho jati hai…

toh presence apne aap zyada visible ho jata hai.

Na koi final self milta hai…

na koi perfect version arrive hota hai.

Sirf ek simple clarity bachi rehti hai:

jo bhi ho raha hai…

wo already ho raha hai.

Aur usko complete banane ki zarurat…

kabhi thi hi nahi.
