future shifts again

loop restarts

Aur ye loop itni smooth speed se chalta hai…

ki lagta hai ye hi reality hai.

Mind ek illusion create karta hai:

“Main ek fixed point se ek better point ki taraf ja raha hoon.”

Lekin dono points imagination mein hote hain…

sirf movement real lagta hai.

Becoming ka sabse hidden function ye nahi hai ki tum change ho…

ye hai ki tum kabhi settle na ho.

Kyuki settling ka matlab hota hai:

future projection ka pause ho jana.

Aur mind ko pause unsafe lagta hai.

Is book ka entry point koi philosophy nahi hai…

ye ek lived structure hai:

jo har insaan silently carry karta hai.

Aur jab ye structure visible hota hai…

toh ek strange discomfort aata hai:

ki jo “main ban raha hoon” lagta tha…

wo ek continuous mental motion tha.

Aur jo “main hoon” lagta hai…
