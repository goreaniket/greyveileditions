aur usko forward time mein extend kar deta hai.

Aur phir ek character create hota hai:

better version of me

successful version of me

healed version of me

complete version of me

Lekin interesting baat ye hai:

ye “version” kabhi available nahi hota.

Wo hamesha ahead rehta hai.

Aur isi “ahead” hone ki wajah se…

wo ek permanent motivation ban jata hai.

Hum chase karte rehte hain…

ek identity ko jo abhi exist nahi karti.

Aur is chasing mein…

present experience secondary ho jata hai.

Becoming ka illusion yahan se start hota hai:

ki jo main hoon abhi…

wo enough nahi hai.

Aur jo main banunga…

wo real main hoga.
