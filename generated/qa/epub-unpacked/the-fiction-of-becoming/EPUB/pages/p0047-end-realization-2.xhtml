“Main complete ho sakta hoon”

Dono ek hi system ke do poles the.

Aur jab system dikhta hai…

toh poles meaningful nahi rehte.

Na incompleteness bachi…

na completeness ka idea.

Sirf ek silent fact:

experience is happening.

Without explanation.

Without hierarchy.

Without timeline.

Becoming ka sabse hidden illusion ye nahi tha ki tum grow kar rahe ho…

illusion ye tha ki tum “someone” ho jo grow kar raha hai.

Aur wo “someone” kabhi directly milta hi nahi.

Sirf thought ke andar ek reference point hota hai.

Jab ye clearly dikhta hai…

toh ek strange reversal hota hai:

Tum experience ko live karna start nahi karte…

tum realize karte ho ki experience kabhi stop hi nahi hua tha.

Sirf interpretation uske upar chal raha tha.
