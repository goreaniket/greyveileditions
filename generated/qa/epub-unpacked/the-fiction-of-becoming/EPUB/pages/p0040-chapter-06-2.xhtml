identity formation (“I am broken”)

Aur ek baar ye identity set ho jaye…

toh har naya experience usi lens se filter hota hai

normal moment → “temporary relief”

painful moment → “proof of brokenness”

Aur isi asymmetric filtering se narrative strong hota jata hai.

Broken self koi fixed entity nahi hota…

ye selective interpretation ka result hota hai.

Mind sirf kuch moments ko highlight karta hai…

aur baaki ko ignore.

Aur jo highlighted hota hai…

wo identity ban jata hai.

Brokenness ka sense tab strong hota hai jab:

past ko repeatedly revisit kiya jaye

present ko comparison se dekha jaye

future ko “fixing project” bana diya jaye

Aur is combination se ek stable story emerge hoti hai:

“Something is fundamentally wrong with me”
