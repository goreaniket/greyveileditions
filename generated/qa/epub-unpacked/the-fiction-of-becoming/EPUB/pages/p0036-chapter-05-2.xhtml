Healing ka process aksar aise chalta hai:

pain occur hota hai

label attach hota hai (“trauma”, “hurt”, “damage”)

identity form hoti hai (“I am broken”)

future self imagine hota hai (“I will be healed”)

continuous self-monitoring start hoti hai

loop repeat hota hai

Healing ka idea khud ek comparison system pe based hota hai:

present self vs ideal healed self

Aur jahan comparison hota hai…

wahan incompleteness automatic hoti hai.

Jo cheez healing lagti hai…

wo kabhi kabhi sirf:

reinterpretation

reframing

meaning restructuring

hoti hai.

Experience change nahi hota…

uska story change hota hai.

Pain ko jab meaning diya jata hai…
