From there, everything follows.

Goals.

Comparison.

Becoming.

Endless adjustment of self.

Endless adjustment of life.

Not necessarily because reality demands it…

but because the mind refuses to accept what is already here.

This work does not try to correct life.

It does not try to improve reality.

It does not try to offer a better system of thinking.

It only tries to expose one subtle distortion:

the belief that something is missing right now.

Because if that belief is seen clearly…

something unexpected happens.

Not resolution.

Not completion.

Not final peace.

But the collapse of the need to constantly resolve.

And that collapse can feel uncomfortable.

Not because something is wrong…
