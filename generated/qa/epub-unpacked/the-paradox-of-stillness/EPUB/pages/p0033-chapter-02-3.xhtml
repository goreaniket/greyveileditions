"Kya life mein sach mein koi meaning hai?"

Questions alag hote hain.

Lekin unke peeche jo feeling hoti hai...

woh aksar same hoti hai.

Ek hope.

Ki ek din answer milega.

Aur sab settle ho jayega.

Ek din clarity aayegi.

Ek din confusion khatam hoga.

Ek din andar jo noise hai woh chup ho jayega.

Aur isi hope ke saath log saalon tak jeete hain.

Kabhi kabhi poori zindagi.

Mind ko lagta hai ki har question answer deserve karta hai.

Har problem ka solution hota hai.

Har mystery ka explanation hota hai.

Har uncertainty ke end par certainty khadi hoti hai.

Lekin kuch questions alag nature ke hote hain.
