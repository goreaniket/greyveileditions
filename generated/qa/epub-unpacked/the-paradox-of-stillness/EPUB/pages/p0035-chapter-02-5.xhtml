Aur yahan ek ajeeb realization saamne aati hai.

Jab tum question mein busy the...

life chal rahi thi.

Jab tum samajhne ki koshish kar rahe the...

life chal rahi thi.

Jab tum certainty dhoond rahe the...

life chal rahi thi.

Barish aati rahi.

Mausam badalte rahe.

Log milte rahe.

Log jaate rahe.

Din saalon mein badalte rahe.

Aur existence ko koi farq nahi pada ki tum answer ke kitne kareeb ho.

Phir dheere dheere tum ordinary moments ko notice karna shuru karte ho.

Ek random laugh.

Ek cup chai.

Ek shaam ki hawa.

Ek familiar jagah.

Ek khidki se aati roshni.
