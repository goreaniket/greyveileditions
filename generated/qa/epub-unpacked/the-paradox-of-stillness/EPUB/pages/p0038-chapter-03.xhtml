THE PARADOX OF STILLNESS

THE BEAUTY OF ORDINARY THINGS

Life mein ek ajeeb habit develop ho jaati hai.

Aur zyada tar logon ko pata bhi nahi chalta.

Hum moments ko categories mein divide karna shuru kar dete hain.

Kuch moments important hote hain.

Kuch ordinary.

Kuch moments yaad rakhne layak hote hain.

Kuch ignore karne layak.

Aur dheere dheere humare andar ek invisible hierarchy ban jaati hai.

Hum wait karna shuru kar dete hain.

Kisi bade din ka.
