Life puzzle nahi thi.

Puzzle banane wala mind tha.

Life problem nahi thi.

Problem yeh belief tha ki life ko solve karna zaruri hai.

Aur jab yeh belief dheere dheere weak padta hai...

toh kuch magical nahi hota.

Aur us din...

jab bahar kuch bhi change nahi hua...

shayad wahi din tha...

jab andar sab kuch quietly change hona shuru hua.
