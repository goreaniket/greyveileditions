THE PARADOX OF STILLNESS

LIFE AFTER THE QUESTION

Kuch questions aise hote hain jo sirf mind mein nahi rehte.

Woh life ka hissa ban jaate hain.

Pehle woh bas ek thought lagte hain.

Ek curiosity.

Ek cheez jise samajhna hai.

Lekin dheere dheere...

woh question tumhare saath rehna shuru kar deta hai.

Tumhare dinon mein.

Tumhari khamoshiyon mein.

Tumhari walks mein.

Tumhari sleepless nights mein.
