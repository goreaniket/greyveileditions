ya journey tumhe chala rahi hai?

Aur yahin se yeh book shuru hoti hai.

Na kisi answer ke saath.

Na kisi conclusion ke saath.

Balki us moment ke saath...

jahan observer aur observed ke beech ki line blur ho jaati hai.

Aur shayad tab samajh aata hai...

ki tum kabhi bahar the hi nahi.

Tum hamesha se usi movement ke andar the...

jisse tum samajhne ki koshish kar rahe the.
