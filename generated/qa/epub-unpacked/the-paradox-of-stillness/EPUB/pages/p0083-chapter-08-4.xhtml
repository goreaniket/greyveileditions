Phir jeeyenge.

Lekin saalon baad...

ek uncomfortable question saamne khada milta hai.

Agar life future mein start honi thi...

toh ab tak kya chal raha tha?

Agar life answers ke baad start honi thi...

toh answers se pehle jo saare saal guzre...

woh kya the?

Agar life certainty ke baad start honi thi...

toh uncertainty ke andar jo saansein li gayi...

woh kya thi?

Aur yahin poori journey dheere dheere ulatne lagti hai.

Kyuki tum realize karte ho...

tum life ko observe nahi kar rahe the.

Tum life ke andar the.

Hamesha se.

Jab tum confused the...

woh bhi life thi.

Jab tum overthink kar rahe the...

woh bhi life thi.
