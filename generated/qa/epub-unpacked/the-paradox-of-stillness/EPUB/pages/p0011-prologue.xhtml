PROLOGUE

Kuch journeys start nahi hoti.

Woh bas notice ki jaati hain.

Jaise tum kisi din achanak realize karte ho...

ki tum already andar ho.

Kisi process ke andar.

Kisi search ke andar.

Kisi invisible movement ke andar.

Aur shayad tab se sab kuch chal raha hota hai.

Without announcement.

Without permission.

Without clarity.

Hum aksar sochte hain ki shuruaat ek moment hoti hai.

Ek decision.

Ek turning point.

Ek realization.
