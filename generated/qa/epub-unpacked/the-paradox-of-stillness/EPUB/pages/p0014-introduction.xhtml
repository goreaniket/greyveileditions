INTRODUCTION

Har journey ka ek ajeeb end hota hai.

Woh end nahi jahan sab answers mil jaate hain.

Woh bhi nahi jahan saare questions khatam ho jaate hain.

Balki woh jahan insaan achanak realize karta hai...

ki shayad woh poore waqt galat direction mein nahi ja raha tha.

Shayad woh poore waqt us cheez ko dhoond raha tha...

jiske andar woh pehle se hi khada tha.

Yeh realization pehle beautiful nahi lagti.

Yeh confusing lagti hai.

Kabhi kabhi almost disappointing bhi.

Kyuki poori life hume ek hi cheez sikhayi jaati hai.

Ki jo chahiye...

woh kahin aur hai.

Future mein.

Kisi destination par.
