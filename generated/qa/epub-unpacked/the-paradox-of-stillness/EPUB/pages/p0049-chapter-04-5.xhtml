Story alag hoti hai.

Aur shayad isi jagah ek weightless moment paida hota hai.

Not when life becomes perfect.

Not when all problems disappear.

Not when every question gets answered.

But when, for a brief moment...

the mind stops carrying everything.

Bas ek moment ke liye.

Past ko side rakh deta hai.

Future ko side rakh deta hai.

Aur sirf yahan hota hai.

Aur us second mein kuch magical nahi hota.

Sky nahi khulta.

Universe secrets reveal nahi karta.

Lekin kuch aur hota hai.

Weight kam ho jaata hai.

Achanak saans halki lagti hai.

Achanak room zyada clear lagta hai.

Achanak life utni complicated nahi lagti.

Not because reality changed.
