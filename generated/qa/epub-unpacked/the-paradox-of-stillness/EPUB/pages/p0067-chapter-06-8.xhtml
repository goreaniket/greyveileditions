Wahi khamoshi jo tab paida hoti hai...

jab ek lambi ladai khatam ho jaati hai.

Aur pehli baar...

saalon baad...

tumhari energy life jeene mein lagti hai.

Reality ko rewrite karne mein nahi.

Aur shayad isi ko healing without closure kehte hain.

Jab answer nahi milta.

Ending nahi milti.

Explanation nahi milti.

Phir bhi...

tumhara dil dheere dheere reality ke saath chalna seekh leta hai.

Not because the story was completed.

But because you finally stopped waiting for a different one.
