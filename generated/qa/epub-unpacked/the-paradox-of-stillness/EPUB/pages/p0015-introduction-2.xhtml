Kisi achievement ke baad.

Kisi relationship ke andar.

Kisi answer ke peeche.

Kisi understanding ke baad.

Aur hum maan lete hain.

Hum us belief ke saath bade hote hain.

Us belief ke saath sapne dekhte hain.

Us belief ke saath struggle karte hain.

Ek din sab theek ho jayega.

Ek din sab clear ho jayega.

Ek din hum finally arrive kar jayenge.

Aur phir...

zindagi start hogi.

Lekin saalon baad ek strange question saamne aata hai.

Agar life future mein start honi thi...

toh ab tak kya chal raha tha?

Agar peace kisi destination par milni thi...

toh raste ke saare moments kya the?

Agar completeness kisi achievement ke baad aani thi...

toh usse pehle jo saari saansein li gayi...

woh kya thi?
