NOTE FROM THE AUTHOR

Lalit R.Mishra

There is a strange assumption most minds carry without ever questioning it.

That reality is incomplete.

That something is missing.

That somewhere, somehow, life is not yet enough.

This assumption does not always feel like a belief.

It feels like common sense.

Like background truth.

Like something obvious.

And because it feels obvious…

it is rarely seen.

Most of what humans call “growth”, “progress”, or “search” is built on top of this single invisible premise:

that the present is insufficient.
