THE WOUND STOPS ASKING

Har wound pain nahi deti.

Kuch wounds questions deti hain.

Aur kai baar...

questions pain se bhi zyada dard dete hain.

Kyuki pain ko feel kiya ja sakta hai.

Questions ko nahi.

Pain aata hai.

Rehta hai.

Aur dheere dheere kam ho jaata hai.

Lekin questions...

woh saalon tak zinda reh sakte hain.
