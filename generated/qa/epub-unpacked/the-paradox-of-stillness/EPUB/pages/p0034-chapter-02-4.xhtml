Jitna unke peeche bhaago...

utne door lagte hain.

Jitna unko pakadne ki koshish karo...

utna woh shape change kar dete hain.

Aur dheere dheere tum realize karte ho...

ki tum answer ke kareeb nahi ja rahe.

Tum bas question ke aas paas circles bana rahe ho.

Aur phir ek din kuch unexpected hota hai.

Answer nahi milta.

Koi revelation nahi hoti.

Life achanak explain nahi ho jaati.

Bas question halka lagne lagta hai.

Pehle jaise weight nahi rehta.

Pehle jaise urgency nahi rehti.

Pehle jaise desperation nahi rehti.

Aur yeh bahut strange feel hota hai.

Kyuki saalon tak tumne socha tha ki answer important hai.

Lekin ab lagne lagta hai...

ki shayad answer se zyada important woh life thi jo answer ka wait karte karte nikal rahi thi.
