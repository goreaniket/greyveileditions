Aur jab yeh finally dikhai deta hai...

toh koi fireworks nahi hote.

Koi cosmic revelation nahi hoti.

Koi ultimate answer nahi milta.

Bas ek halki si khamoshi.

Jaise koi bahut purani search apne aap gir gayi ho.

Jaise koi darwaza khula nahi...

sirf dikh gaya ho ki woh kabhi band tha hi nahi.

Aur us moment mein...

pehli baar...

shayad tum life ko samajhte nahi.

Shayad pehli baar...

tum bas uske saath hote ho.

THE END
