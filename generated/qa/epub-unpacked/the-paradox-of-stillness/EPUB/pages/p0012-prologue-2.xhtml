Lekin shayad shuruaat itni dramatic nahi hoti.

Shayad shuruaat bas ek subtle dissatisfaction hoti hai.

Ek halka sa feeling.

Ki kuch abhi complete nahi hai.

Na tum clearly keh paate ho.

Na tum clearly samajh paate ho.

Bas ek movement start ho jaata hai.

Aur tum us movement ko naam dene lagte ho.

Search.

Growth.

Understanding.

Meaning.

Healing.

Lekin shayad yeh sab naam baad mein diye gaye labels hain.

Original cheez sirf movement thi.

Ek andar ki directionless push.

Jo kisi destination ki taraf nahi ja rahi thi.

Balki sirf chal rahi thi.

Aur jab tum pehli baar is movement ko observe karte ho...

toh ek strange question uthta hai.

Kya tum is journey ko chala rahe ho...
