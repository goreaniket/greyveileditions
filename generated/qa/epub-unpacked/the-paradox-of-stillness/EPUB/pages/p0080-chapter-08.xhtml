THE PARADOX OF STILLNESS

LIFE CONTINUES

Poore life hum ek strange wait mein rehte hain.

Kabhi consciously.

Kabhi unconsciously.

Hum wait karte hain us moment ka...

jab sab finally clear ho jayega.

Jab confusion khatam ho jayegi.

Jab fear khatam ho jayega.

Jab uncertainty khatam ho jayegi.

Jab hum ready feel karenge.

Aur phir...

hum jeena start karenge.

Kam se kam mind yahi kehta hai.
