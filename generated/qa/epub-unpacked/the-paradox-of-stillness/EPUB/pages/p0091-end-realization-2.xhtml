ki movement ka matlab progress hai.

Lekin jab sab shant hota hai…

toh ek uncomfortable clarity aati hai.

Ki tum kabhi kahin “pahunch” hi nahi rahe the.

Tum sirf chal rahe the.

And movement itself was the entire story.

Na start.

Na end.

Na destination.

Sirf continuation.

Aur sabse ajeeb realization yeh nahi hota ki tumne kuch miss kar diya.

Balki yeh hota hai ki…

kuch tha hi nahi jise miss kiya ja sake.

Na koi hidden answer.

Na koi final layer.

Na koi last secret.

Sirf wahi cheezein jo hamesha se thi:

saans lena

dekhna

mehsoos karna
