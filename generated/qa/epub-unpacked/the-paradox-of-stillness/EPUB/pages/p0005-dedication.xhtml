DEDICATION

This book is dedicated to every mind that has ever tried to find certainty in an uncertain experience.

To those who have spent years trying to become “enough” without knowing when that idea began.

To those who have mistaken thought for truth, and search for necessity.

To the ones who believed something was missing…

and built entire lives around that belief.

And to those who may one day notice…

that the feeling of incompleteness was never evidence of incompleteness.

Only a pattern of thought repeating itself.
