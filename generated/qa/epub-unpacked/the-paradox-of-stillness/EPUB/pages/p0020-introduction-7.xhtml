Yeh book answers dene ki koshish nahi karegi.

Yeh book tumhe convince karne ki bhi koshish nahi karegi.

Yeh sirf kuch cheezon ko dheere dheere dissolve karegi.

Kuch assumptions.

Kuch fears.

Kuch invisible pursuits.

Aur jab woh girne lagenge...

toh shayad pehli baar kuch naya dikhai nahi dega.

Shayad pehli baar wahi dikhai dega...

jo hamesha se yahan tha.

Ek saans.

Ek moment.

Ek existence.

Aur ek strange realization.

Ki shayad poori journey ka final destination koi jagah nahi tha.

Shayad woh ek dekhna tha.

Ek aisa dekhna...

jahan achanak samajh aaye...

ki darwaza kabhi khula nahi.

Bas dikh gaya...

ki woh kabhi band tha hi nahi.
