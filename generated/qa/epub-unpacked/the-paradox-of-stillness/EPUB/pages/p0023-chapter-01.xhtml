THE PARADOX OF STILLNESS

THE DAY NOTHING CHANGED

Ek ajeeb si disappointment hoti hai...

jiske liye koi prepare nahi karta.

Isliye nahi kyunki woh rare hai.

Balki isliye kyunki uske baare mein lagbhag koi baat hi nahi karta.

Saalon tak tum search karte ho.

Question karte ho.

Sochte ho.

Observe karte ho.

Samajhne ki koshish karte ho.

Khud ko.

Dusre logon ko.
