Aur dheere dheere...

ek crack appear hota hai.

Us story mein jo humne poori life khud ko sunayi thi.

Ki hum incomplete hain.

Ki hum late hain.

Ki hum ready nahi hain.

Ki hum enough nahi hain.

Aur sabse dangerous baat?

Hum in beliefs ko beliefs ki tarah nahi dekhte.

Hum inhe reality samajh lete hain.

Itni baar repeat karte hain...

ki woh humari identity ban jaate hain.

Hum room mein enter karte hain...

aur apne saath ek invisible burden lekar jaate hain.

Ki mujhe aur banna hai.

Aur paana hai.

Aur prove karna hai.

Aur samajhna hai.

Aur dheere dheere hum jeena bhool jaate hain.

Hum existence ko experience nahi karte.

Hum existence ko improve karne ki koshish karte rehte hain.
