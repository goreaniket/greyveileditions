Life ko.

Pain ko.

Reality ko.

Aur un sab cheezon ko jo raat ke 2 baje achanak important lagne lagti hain.

Aur dheere dheere andar ek assumption build hone lagti hai.

Ek silent belief.

Ki ek din...

yeh sab searching kahin na kahin le jayegi.

Ek din kuch click karega.

Ek din sab make sense karega.

Ek din clarity aayegi.

Aur us din life different feel hogi.

Shayad world halka lagega.

Shayad mind quiet ho jayega.

Shayad existence finally understandable lagne lagegi.

Tum exactly nahi jaante ki kya change hoga.

Par tumhe lagta hai...

kuch toh hoga.

Aur phir ek din...
