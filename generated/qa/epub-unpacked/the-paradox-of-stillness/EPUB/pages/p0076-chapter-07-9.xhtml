Zindagi tumse permission nahi maang rahi ki woh complete hai ya nahi.

Woh bas hai.

Aur pehli baar...

tum dekhte ho...

ki jo insaan poori life completeness dhoond raha tha...

Wahi insaan completeness aur apne beech khada tha.

Kyuki jab tak tum believe karte rahoge ki kuch missing hai...

har answer temporary lagega.

Har achievement chhoti lagegi.

Har destination incomplete lagegi.

Mind hamesha ek aur gap create kar dega.

Not because life incomplete hai.

Because the belief survives by creating another gap.

Aur phir...

poori journey ka sabse shocking realization saamne aata hai.

Tumhe peace nahi mili.

Tumhe enlightenment nahi mili.
