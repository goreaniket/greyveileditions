THE PARADOX OF STILLNESS

NOTHING WAS MISSING

Poori life hum kuch na kuch dhoondte rehte hain.

Kabhi success.

Kabhi love.

Kabhi purpose.

Kabhi peace.

Kabhi certainty.

Kabhi khud ko.

Object change hota rehta hai.

Search nahi.

Bachpan mein lagta hai kuch aur chahiye.

Jawani mein lagta hai kuch aur chahiye.
