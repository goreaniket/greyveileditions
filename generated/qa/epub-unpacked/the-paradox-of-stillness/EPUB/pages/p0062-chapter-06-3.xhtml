Lekin past dobara nahi likha ja sakta.

Aur shayad isi liye kuch wounds saalon tak zinda rehti hain.

Not because we don't understand what happened.

But because somewhere deep inside...

we cannot accept that what happened...

actually happened.

Hum memory ke saath nahi lad rahe hote.

Hum reality ke saath lad rahe hote hain.

Aur yeh fight bahut exhausting hoti hai.

Kyuki reality argue nahi karti.

Reality explain nahi karti.

Reality defend nahi karti.

Woh bas hoti hai.

Aur phir bhi mind saalon tak uske saamne khada rehta hai.

"Aisa nahi hona chahiye tha."
