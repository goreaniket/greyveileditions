Jo har achievement ke baad naya gap create kar deta tha.

Aur shayad sabse dangerous realization yeh hai...

Ki incompleteness reality nahi thi.

Woh ek thought tha.

Ek thought jo itni baar repeat hua...

ki usne fact ka disguise pehen liya.

Ek thought jise tumne question karna band kar diya.

Ek thought jiske upar tumne poori identity build kar di.

Aur phir ek din...

saalon ki searching ke baad...

mind thak jaata hai.

Not defeated.

Not broken.

Bas thak jaata hai.

Aur us thakan mein...

ek ajeeb si stillness paida hoti hai.

Wahan koi revelation nahi hoti.

Koi cosmic answer nahi milta.

Koi hidden truth sky se nahi utarta.
