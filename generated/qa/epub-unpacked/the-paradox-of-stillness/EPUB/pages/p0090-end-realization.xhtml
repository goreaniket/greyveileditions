END REALIZATION:

Akhir mein sab kuch ek simple jagah par aake ruk jaata hai.

Na answers.

Na clarity.

Na final understanding.

Sirf ek strange silence.

Aur us silence ke andar ek baat dheere se dikhai deti hai…

jo pehle kabhi clearly nahi dekhi gayi thi.

Tum jis cheez ko poori life “solve” karne ki koshish kar rahe the…

woh shayad kabhi problem thi hi nahi.

Woh sirf ek pattern tha.

Ek repeating motion.

Mind ka apne aap ko busy rakhne ka tareeka.

Kyuki jab tak question zinda rehta hai…

tab tak tum chase mein rehte ho.

Aur chase mein ek illusion hota hai…
