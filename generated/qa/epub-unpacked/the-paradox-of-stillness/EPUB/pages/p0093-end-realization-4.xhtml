Sirf unnecessary burden ka gir jaana.

Aur uske baad bhi life chalti rehti hai.

Same duniya.

Same log.

Same noise.

Bas ek difference hota hai.

Ab tum usse explain karne ki koshish nahi karte.

Aur shayad yahi sabse uncomfortable truth hai:

Ki kuch bhi kabhi complete nahi hua tha…

kyuki kabhi kuch incomplete tha hi nahi.
