Hum breakfast karte hue future ke baare mein soch rahe hote hain.

Hum walk karte hue past ke conversations repeat kar rahe hote hain.

Hum kisi ke saath baithe hote hain...

lekin mentally kahin aur hote hain.

Aur shayad isi wajah se life kabhi kabhi itni heavy lagti hai.

Not because the moment is heavy.

But because the moment ke andar dus hazaar aur moments uthakar laaye ja rahe hote hain.

Ek regret.

Ek fear.

Ek memory.

Ek expectation.

Ek imaginary future.

Aur dheere dheere present moment ek storage room ban jaata hai.

Jahan mind har cheez collect karke rakhta rehta hai.

Purani baatein.

Purani galtiyan.
