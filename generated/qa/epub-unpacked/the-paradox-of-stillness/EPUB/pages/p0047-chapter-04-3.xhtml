Purane versions of ourselves.

Aur saath hi future ki worries.

Future ki plans.

Future ki uncertainties.

Phir hum kehte hain:

"Life heavy lag rahi hai."

Lekin kabhi kabhi...

life heavy nahi hoti.

Mind jo weight carry kar raha hota hai...

woh heavy hota hai.

Socho.

Iss exact moment mein.

Abhi.

Jis second tum yeh padh rahe ho.

Kya problem actually present hai?

Sach mein present.

Not memory.

Not imagination.
