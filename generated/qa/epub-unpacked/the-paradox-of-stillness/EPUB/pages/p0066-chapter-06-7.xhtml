Jo gaya...

woh gaya.

Jo toot gaya...

woh toot gaya.

Aur yeh acceptance weakness nahi hoti.

Yeh surrender bhi nahi hoti.

Yeh reality ke saamne haar maan-na bhi nahi hota.

Yeh sirf reality ko reality rehne dena hota hai.

Kyuki kai baar suffering event nahi hoti.

Suffering woh argument hota hai...

jo mind reality ke saath saalon tak karta rehta hai.

Aur shayad healing tab start hoti hai...

jab woh argument finally thak jaata hai.

Jab tum reality ko approve nahi karte.

Reality ko justify nahi karte.

Reality ko pasand bhi nahi karte.

Bas uske existence ko deny karna band kar dete ho.

Aur us moment mein...

koi grand revelation nahi aati.

Koi dramatic peace nahi milti.

Bas ek ajeeb si khamoshi aati hai.
