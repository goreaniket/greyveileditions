Kuch dreams toot jaate hain.

Aur tum saalon baad bhi nahi samajh paate ki kyun.

Aur phir bhi...

life continue karti rehti hai.

Yeh realization pehle cruel lagta hai.

Kyuki hum believe karte hain ki understanding healing laayegi.

Ki agar hum reason jaan gaye...

toh dard khatam ho jayega.

Lekin saalon baad kai log discover karte hain...

ki unhe answer nahi mila.

Aur phir bhi woh zinda hain.

Woh muskura rahe hain.

Woh chal rahe hain.

Woh naye experiences create kar rahe hain.

Answer ke bina.

Aur yahin se kuch change hona start hota hai.

Not outside.

Inside.

Ek din woh memory wapas aati hai.

Wahi memory jo pehle raaton ki neend le jaati thi.
