Healing ka matlab yeh nahi ki wound disappear ho gayi.

Healing ka matlab yeh nahi ki tum bhool gaye.

Healing ka matlab yeh bhi nahi ki ab dard feel nahi hota.

Kabhi kabhi healing ka matlab sirf itna hota hai...

Ki wound ne explanation maangna band kar diya.

Ki wound ne roz courtroom lagana band kar diya.

Ki wound ne roz "why" puchna band kar diya.

Aur us silence mein ek ajeeb si freedom hoti hai.

Not because the story finally made sense.

But because you no longer need it to.

Kuch cheezein explain nahi hoti.

Kuch chapters conclude nahi hote.

Kuch questions answer nahi hote.

life rukti nahi.
