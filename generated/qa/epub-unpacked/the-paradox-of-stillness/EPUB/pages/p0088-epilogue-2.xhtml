Ki life ko solve kiya ja sakta hai.

Ki existence ko complete kiya ja sakta hai.

Ki inner world ko finally settle kiya ja sakta hai.

Lekin har answer ke baad ek naya silence aata raha.

Ek aisa silence jo koi final statement nahi deta.

Sirf space deta hai.

Aur us space mein ek ajeeb sa clarity hoti hai.

Na excitement.

Na disappointment.

Bas ek soft understanding.

Ki jo cheez tum poori life dhoond rahe the...

woh kisi final moment mein nahi thi.

Woh har moment mein thi.

Aur jab yeh realization aata hai...

toh journey khatam nahi hoti.

Journey dissolve ho jaati hai.

Jaise koi line jo kabhi important thi...

ab bas page par faint ho jaati hai.

Aur bachti hai sirf life.

Na explained.

Na solved.
