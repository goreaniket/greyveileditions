Wahi cheezein jo pehle background mein thi.

Aur achanak woh important lagne lagti hain.

Not because they contain answers.

But because they never needed answers.

Aur shayad yahi point hai jahan question apni power lose karna start karta hai.

Question gayab nahi hota.

Woh exist karta rehta hai.

Kabhi kabhi wapas bhi aata hai.

Kabhi kabhi raat ko phir saamne khada milta hai.

Lekin ab woh throne par nahi baitha hota.

Ab woh tumhari life ka center nahi hota.

Ab woh sirf ek question hota hai.

Aur tum...

sirf ek insaan.

Jo uske bina bhi jee sakta hai.

Aur shayad life after the question ka matlab bhi yahi hai.

Answer mil jaana nahi.

Balki itna jee lena...

ki answer ki zarurat dheere dheere kam padne lage.
