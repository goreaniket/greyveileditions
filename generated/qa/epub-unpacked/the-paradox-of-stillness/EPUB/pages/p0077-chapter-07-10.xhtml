Tumhe completion nahi mili.

Tumne sirf ek illusion ko girte hue dekha.

Wahi illusion jo saalon se keh raha tha:

"Abhi nahi."
"Abhi enough nahi."
"Abhi kuch missing hai."

Aur uske neeche...

jo bacha...

woh koi achievement nahi thi.

Koi answer nahi tha.

Koi final destination nahi thi.

Bas ek simple truth tha.
