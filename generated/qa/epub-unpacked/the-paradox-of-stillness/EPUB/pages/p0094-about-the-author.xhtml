ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Lalit R. Mishra is a writer whose intent is to bring human perception back to a simpler and more grounded reality.

He writes with the aim of helping people see life without unnecessary psychological noise — to reduce internal complexity and restore clarity in how everyday experience is understood.

His core intention is not to create new philosophies, but to dissolve overcomplication.

To make life feel less fragmented.

Less mentally heavy.

And more directly observable.

He believes that much of human suffering is not created by life itself, but by the layers of interpretation we build around it.

Thoughts about thoughts.

Feelings about feelings.

Stories built on top of experiences.
