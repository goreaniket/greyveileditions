The danger is in what it quietly removes.

The need for an imagined missing piece.

The need for a future arrival.

The need for a final version of experience.

And when those structures weaken…

life does not become extraordinary.

It becomes unexplainably ordinary.

So ordinary…

that the mind struggles to continue its usual story.

And in that struggle…

there is a pause.

Not as achievement.

Not as enlightenment.

Not as escape.

But as a direct encounter with what was always already here.

Unedited.

Uninterpreted.

Unfinished only in thought.

And complete without it.
