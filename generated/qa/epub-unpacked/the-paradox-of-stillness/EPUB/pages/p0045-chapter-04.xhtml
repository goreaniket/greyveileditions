THE PARADOX OF STILLNESS

THE WEIGHTLESS MOMENT

Insaan ek ajeeb jagah par jeeta hai.

Physical world mein nahi.

Sirf present moment mein bhi nahi.

Balki aksar...

yaadon aur possibilities ke beech.

Body yahan hoti hai.

Mind kahin aur.

Kabhi past mein.

Kabhi future mein.

Kabhi dono mein ek saath.

Aur dheere dheere yeh itna normal lagne lagta hai ki hum notice hi nahi karte.
