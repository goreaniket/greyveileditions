aur phir se bhool jaana

Aur shayad yahi point hai jahan mind resist karta hai.

Kyuki mind meaning chahata hai.

Aur yahan sirf existence hoti hai.

Without explanation.

Without permission.

Without reason.

Aur jab explanation gir jaata hai…

toh jo bachta hai woh peace nahi hota.

Woh clarity bhi nahi hoti.

Woh sirf ek raw exposure hota hai…

ki reality kabhi tumhare questions ka jawab dene ke liye bani hi nahi thi.

Aur tumhara poora struggle…

shayad jawab paane ka nahi tha.

Balki ek imaginary problem ko solve karne ka tha.

Aur jab yeh samajh aata hai…

toh kuch “achha lagta hai” aisa nahi hota.

Sirf ek weight drop hota hai.

Na victory.

Na loss.
