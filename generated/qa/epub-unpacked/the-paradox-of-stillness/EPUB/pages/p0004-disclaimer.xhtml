INTRODUCTION

DISCLAIMER

This book is not written to guide, fix, or improve the reader.

It is not psychology.

It is not therapy.

It is not self-help.

It is an observation of how the human mind constructs meaning and then begins to live inside its own construction.

Nothing in this book should be treated as instruction or advice.

There are no techniques here.

No steps to follow.

No system to apply.

The ideas presented may feel uncomfortable at times…

because they question assumptions that are usually never questioned.

The reader is not being asked to believe anything.

Not being asked to reject anything either.

Only to notice what is already happening within experience.
