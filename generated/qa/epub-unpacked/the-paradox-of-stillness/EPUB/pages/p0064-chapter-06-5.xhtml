woh toot-ta nahi.

woh ruk jaata.

Hope ki shayad jo khatam ho gaya...

woh bach jaata.

Aur isi liye closure itna difficult lagta hai.

Kyuki closure answer milne ka naam nahi hai.

Ek future ko nahi.

Ek imagined past ko.

Aur yeh kaam aasaan nahi hota.

Bilkul bhi nahi.
