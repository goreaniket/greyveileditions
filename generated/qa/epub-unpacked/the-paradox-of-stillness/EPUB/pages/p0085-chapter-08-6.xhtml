Itni suffering ke baad.

Itni understanding ke baad.

Itne paradoxes ke baad.

Ek almost embarrassing realization saamne aati hai.

Tum poori life arrival ka wait kar rahe the.

Lekin koi arrival tha hi nahi.

Tum poori life complete hone ka wait kar rahe the.

Lekin existence ne kabhi tumhe incomplete maana hi nahi.

Tum poori life life tak pahunchne ki koshish kar rahe the.

Lekin tum kabhi life se door the hi nahi.

Not for a single second.

Na jab tum toot rahe the.

Na jab tum jeet rahe the.

Na jab tum samajh rahe the.

Na jab tum kho gaye the.

Kabhi nahi.

Aur shayad poori journey ka final paradox yahi tha.

Tum jis cheez tak pahunchna chahte the...

tum kabhi usse alag the hi nahi.
