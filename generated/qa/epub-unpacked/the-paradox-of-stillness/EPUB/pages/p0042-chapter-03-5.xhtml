Ek shaam ki hawa.

Ek familiar road.

Ek khidki se aati roshni.

Ek normal conversation jo us waqt important nahi lag rahi thi.

Yeh sab cheezein background mein thi.

Aur phir ek din pata chalta hai...

background hi poori picture tha.

Hum sochte rehte hain ki kuch bada hume change karega.

Lekin aksar transformation quietly hota hai.

Itna quietly ki notice bhi nahi hota.

Ek fear thoda weak ho jaata hai.

Ek wound thodi soft ho jaati hai.

Ek perspective change ho jaata hai.

Ek burden halka lagne lagta hai.

Aur yeh sab kisi grand event ki wajah se nahi hota.

Yeh ordinary life ke beech hota hai.

Wahi life jise hum usually important nahi maante.

Shayad isi liye ordinary moments itne powerful hote hain.
