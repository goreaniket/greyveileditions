EPILOGUE:

Kuch journeys end nahi hoti.

Woh bas apne aap quiet ho jaati hain.

Na kisi final answer ke saath.

Na kisi dramatic closure ke saath.

Balki ek simple realization ke saath...

ki shayad end ka concept hi galat tha.

Poore safar mein tumne bahut kuch dekha.

Awareness.

Understanding.

Certainty.

Meaning.

Stillness.

Loss.

Acceptance.

Aur har layer ke saath ek belief toot-ta gaya.
