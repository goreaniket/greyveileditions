Is level pe reality stable lagti hai.

Har cheez ka answer hota hai.

Ya kam se kam lagta hai ki hai.

Phir ek moment aata hai…

jab ek conclusion fully stick nahi karta.

Tum sochte ho:

“shayad yeh aisa hai”

lekin turant ek aur thought aata hai:

“ya shayad nahi hai”

Aur yeh conflict pehli baar noticeable hota hai.

Ab mind pehli baar “single answer” nahi bana pata.

Ab cheezein aur complex ho jaati hain.

Ek hi situation ke andar:

ek version truth lagta hai

ek version doubt lagta hai
