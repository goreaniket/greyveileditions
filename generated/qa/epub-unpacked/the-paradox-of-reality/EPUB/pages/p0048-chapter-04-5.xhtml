“reality kya hai?”

Question yeh ho jaata hai:

“kaunsi reality ban rahi hai?”

Aur yeh “ban rahi hai” word important hai.

Kyuki reality fixed nahi hai.

woh construction hai.

Aur construction ke andar har cheez input hoti hai:

past experience

emotional history

fear patterns

desire structure

social conditioning

Aur mind in sab ko use karke ek continuous model run karta hai.

Isliye tumhara world static nahi hota.
