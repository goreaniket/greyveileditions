THE PARADOX OF REALITY

CHAPTER 7 — WHEN THE WORLD HAS NO FIXED MEANING

Kabhi kabhi ek phase aata hai…

jab duniya bilkul normal chal rahi hoti hai…

par us normal ke andar ek strange instability develop hone lagti hai.

Na koi event hota hai.

Na koi breakdown hota hai.

Na koi obvious change hota hai.

Bas ek cheez quietly shift karti hai:

cheezon ka “fixed sense”

Pehle har cheez apni jagah hoti hai.

Har situation ka ek direct meaning hota hai.

Har moment ka ek automatic interpretation hota hai.
