Aur yeh label-less state confusing nahi hoti…

par unfamiliar hoti hai.

Kyuki mind ko hamesha classification ki aadat hoti hai:

yeh good hai

yeh bad hai

yeh safe hai

yeh unsafe hai

Lekin ab yeh classification stick nahi karti.

Har label thoda sa try karta hai attach hone ka…

aur phir drop ho jata hai.

Jaise reality kisi bhi fixed container mein fit nahi ho rahi ho.

Aur yahan pe ek deeper realization slowly form hota hai:

shayad duniya kabhi defined thi hi nahi

shayad definition sirf ek mental overlay tha

Aur jab yeh overlay weak hota hai…

toh world khatam nahi hota…

bas uska “fixed interpretation” dissolve ho jata hai.
