Aur har world internally consistent hota hai.

Isliye har insaan ko apni reality “true” lagti hai.

Aur yeh truth conflict create karta hai.

Conflict is not because someone is wrong.

Conflict is because:

koi single shared reality exist hi nahi karti in pure form

Sirf overlap zones hoti hain.

Temporary agreement.

Context-based alignment.

Aur jab woh alignment toot jaati hai…

toh clash hota hai.

Mind naturally ek tendency rakhta hai:

ek reality ko “real” label karne ki

aur baaki ko “distorted” label karne ki

Lekin yeh labeling bhi constructed hoti hai.

Memory + conditioning + fear + past reinforcement.

Jab tum zyada observe karte ho…

toh ek strange realization aata hai:

tum ek world nahi dekh rahe ho
