Interpretations ab bhi generate hoti hain.

Mind ab bhi stories banata hai.

Par ab un stories ko hold karne ki zarurat feel nahi hoti.

Woh aati hain…

aur chali jaati hain…

without becoming “truth”.

Pehle experience ko control karna hota tha.

Ab experience ko sirf notice kiya jaata hai.

Na usko change karne ki urgency hoti hai.

Na usko fix karne ka pressure hota hai.

Bas ek simple awareness hoti hai…

ki jo bhi ho raha hai…

woh ho raha hai.

Aur yahan pe ek strange clarity aati hai:

koi final understanding exist nahi karti

Na end point hota hai.

Na complete picture hoti hai.

Na ultimate truth hota hai.

Sirf ongoing flow hota hai…

jo khud ko continuously generate karta rehta hai.

Duniya same rehti hai.
