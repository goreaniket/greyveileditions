Yahan pe conflict opinions ka nahi hota.

Yahan pe conflict interpretation layers ka hota hai.

Ab ek deeper shift start hota hai.

Jo cheezein pehle stable meaning rakhti thi…

woh ab thodi loose lagne lagti hain.

Words same rehte hain…

par unka weight change ho jata hai.

Ek sentence ab ek fixed direction mein lock nahi hota.

Woh multiple directions open kar deta hai.

Aur mind usko hold nahi kar pata.

Na koi meaning fully settle hota hai.

Na koi meaning fully reject hota hai.

Sab kuch temporary lagta hai.

Aur yeh temporary nature pehli baar visible hota hai.

Kabhi kabhi kisi simple cheez ko dekhte hue…

ek strange neutrality aa jaati hai.

Na usmein emotional push hota hai.

Na usmein rejection hota hai.

Bas ek “unassigned” feeling hoti hai.

Jaise world abhi label ke bina exist kar raha ho.
