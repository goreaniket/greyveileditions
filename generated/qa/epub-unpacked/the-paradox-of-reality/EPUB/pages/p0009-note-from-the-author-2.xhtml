For a long time, I believed that understanding would eventually bring final clarity.

That if I looked deeply enough, questioned carefully enough, and thought honestly enough, I would eventually arrive somewhere permanent.

But the deeper I looked, the more something unexpected revealed itself.

Life was not becoming simpler.

It was becoming wider.

The questions were not disappearing.

They were changing shape.

And the answers I once searched for were slowly losing their importance.

This book was written from that space.

Not from certainty.

Not from expertise.

Not from a position above the reader.

But from the same human condition we all share.

The condition of trying to understand an experience while being inside the experience itself.

Perhaps that is one of the strangest things about being human.
