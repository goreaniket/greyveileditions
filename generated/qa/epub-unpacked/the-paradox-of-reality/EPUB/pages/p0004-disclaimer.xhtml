INTRODUCTION

DISCLAIMER

This book is a work of philosophical reflection, personal observation, and exploratory thought.

The ideas presented within these pages are not intended to be absolute truths, scientific conclusions, psychological diagnoses, professional advice, or universal explanations of reality.

They are observations.

Questions.

Perspectives.

Experiments in looking.

The purpose of this book is not to tell the reader what to believe, but to invite deeper examination of experience itself.

Different readers may interpret these ideas differently.

That is not a weakness of the work

It is part of its subject.
