memory reality ko replace nahi karti

memory reality ko continuously edit karti rehti hai

Aur edited reality ko hum “truth” bol dete hain.

Ab present ek raw thing nahi lagta.

woh ek filtered output lagta hai.

Aur jab yeh samajh aata hai…

toh duniya change nahi hoti.

bas uska “directness” kam ho jaata hai.

Na clarity fully aati hai.

Na confusion fully stable hota hai.

Bas ek in-between state:

reality as it is

vs reality as remembered

aur dono ek saath collide karte rehte hain.
