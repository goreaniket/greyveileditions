THE PARADOX OF REALITY

CHAPTER 9 — WHEN NOTHING NEEDS TO BE UNDERSTOOD

Aakhir mein ek strange phase aata hai…

jab duniya same rehti hai…

par andar ka “explain karna” dheere dheere khatam hone lagta hai.

Na questions strong rehte hain.

Na answers urgent lagte hain.

Na clarity ek goal bachi rehti hai.

Pehle har cheez ek problem hoti thi.

Har moment ko samajhna hota tha.

Har feeling ko decode karna hota tha.

Har thought ko fix karna hota tha.
