THE PARADOX OF REALITY

CHAPTER 3 — MEMORY IS REWRITING REALITY

Har insaan yeh maanta hai ki woh present mein jee raha hai.

Ki jo ho raha hai, wahi reality hai.

Simple.

Direct.

Clean.

Lekin yeh “clean” sirf surface pe hota hai.

Neeche ek aur system chal raha hota hai…

silent, automatic, invisible.

memory

Memory sirf past store nahi karti.

Woh present ko recreate bhi karti hai.
