THE PARADOX OF REALITY

CHAPTER 8 — WHEN THOUGHT LOSES ITS AUTHORITY

Kabhi kabhi ek phase aata hai…

jab mind chal raha hota hai…

par jo chal raha hota hai, us par belief kam hone lagta hai.

Thoughts rukte nahi.

Unka flow same rehta hai.

Shayad aur zyada bhi.

Lekin ek cheez quietly change hoti hai:

unka weight

Pehle ek thought aata tha…

aur uske saath ek natural seriousness hoti thi.

Jaise woh important hai.
