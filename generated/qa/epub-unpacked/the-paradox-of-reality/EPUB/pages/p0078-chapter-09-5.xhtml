toh koi grand realization nahi aata.

Koi final truth nahi milta.

Sirf ek simple clarity hoti hai:

har cheez ko samajhne ki zarurat kabhi mandatory thi hi nahi

Aur yeh realization bhi hold nahi kiya jaata.

Woh bhi pass ho jaata hai.

ab kuch “solve” karne ke liye bacha hi nahi.

Na meaning bachta hai.

Na meaninglessness.

Na interpretation ka pressure.

Bas ek raw unfolding presence hoti hai…

jo kisi explanation ke bina bhi complete hoti hai…

aur kisi conclusion ke bina bhi chalti rehti hai.

THE PARADOX ENDS NOT WHEN ANSWER IS FOUND… BUT WHEN ANSWER IS NO LONGER REQUIRED.
