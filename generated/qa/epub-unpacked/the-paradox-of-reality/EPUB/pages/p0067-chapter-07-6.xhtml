Aur ab ek final realization slowly settle hota hai:

ki shayad meaning reality ka property nahi hai…

shayd meaning sirf ek temporary construction hai…

jo mind continuously attach aur detach karta rehta hai.

Aur jab yeh attachment loose hota hai…

toh world empty nahi hota…

bas unlabelled ho jata hai.

Na meaning hota hai.

Na meaninglessness hoti hai.

Bas ek raw presence hoti hai…

jo kisi explanation ke bina bhi complete lagti hai…

aur kisi conclusion ke bina bhi ongoing rehti hai.

Hai.
