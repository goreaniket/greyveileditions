language bhi ab stable nahi lagti.

Ek hi sentence:

kabhi truth lagta hai

kabhi assumption lagta hai

kabhi incomplete lagta hai

Aur koi final reading exist nahi karti.

Aur jab language hi unstable ho jati hai…

toh reality ka structure bhi loose lagne lagta hai.

Ab ek strange phase aata hai:

tum kisi bhi cheez ko “define” karne ki koshish karte ho…

aur definition incomplete lagti hai.

Aur yeh incompleteness failure nahi lagti…

yeh nature lagne lagti hai.

Aur yahin pe ek silent shift hota hai:

reality lose nahi hoti

bas uska interpretation system visible ho jata hai

Aur jab system visible hota hai…

toh cheezein pehli baar “as it is” nahi lagti.

Sab kuch ek flow jaisa lagta hai…

jo kisi fixed meaning ko hold nahi karta.
