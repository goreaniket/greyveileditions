INTRODUCTION

NOTE FROM THE AUTHOR

If you are reading this page, then we have already traveled a long distance together.

Not through places.

Not through events.

But through questions.

The kind of questions that rarely leave visible marks on the outside, yet quietly reshape the way a person sees everything.

When I began writing The Human Paradoxes, I did not start with answers.

I started with confusion.

With observation.

With moments that felt too strange to ignore.

Moments where certainty felt fragile.

Moments where identity felt unstable.

Moments where reality itself seemed less solid than it appeared.
