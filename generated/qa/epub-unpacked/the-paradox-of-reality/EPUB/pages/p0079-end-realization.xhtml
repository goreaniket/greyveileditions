ENDING

END REALIZATION

Aakhir mein jab sab layers thodi der ke liye quiet ho jaati hain…

toh koi grand conclusion nahi bachta.

Na answers ka pressure rehta hai.

Na questions ka urgency.

Na kisi cheez ko finalize karne ki need.

Bas ek simple sa seeing reh jaata hai…

jo kisi explanation ke bina bhi chal raha hota hai.

Duniya mein kuch change nahi hota:

log waise hi baat karte hain

situations waise hi unfold hoti hain

din apni normal speed mein chalta rehta hai

Par difference duniya mein nahi hota…

difference “dekhne ke tareeke” mein hota hai.

Ab cheezein pehle jaisi rigid nahi lagti.

Kisi bhi moment ka ek fixed meaning nahi banta.
