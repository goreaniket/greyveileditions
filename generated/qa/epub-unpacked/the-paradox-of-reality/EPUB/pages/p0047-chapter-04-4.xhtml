Ab ek aur subtle distortion:

jab mind reality construct karta hai…

toh woh khud ko invisible rakhta hai.

Isliye tumhe lagta hai:

“main duniya dekh raha hoon”

Lekin truth yeh hota hai:

tum duniya nahi dekh rahe

tum apne mind ke output ko dekh rahe ho

Aur yeh distinction initially noticeable nahi hota.

Kyuki system ke andar hi system chal raha hota hai.

Jaise kisi screen ke andar ek aur screen render ho rahi ho…

aur tum sirf final image dekh rahe ho.

Ab ek aur deeper collapse:

do log same event dekhte hain…

lekin dono ka reality experience different hota hai.
