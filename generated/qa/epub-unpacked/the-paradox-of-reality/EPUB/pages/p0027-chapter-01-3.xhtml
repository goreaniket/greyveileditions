Tum kisi cheez ko dekhte ho…

aur saath hi yeh bhi feel hota hai:

“yeh main dekh raha hoon… ya yeh mere through create ho raha hai?”

Aur us second mein clarity nahi aati…

us second mein stability thodi si hil jaati hai.

Kyuki pehli baar yeh suspicion aata hai:

shayad reality ek fixed thing nahi hai

shayad reality ek continuously processed experience hai

Aur agar reality processed hai…

toh question shift hota hai:

“phir original kya hai?”

Lekin yeh question bhi jaldi dissolve ho jaata hai…

kyuki “original” bhi ek idea ban jaata hai.
