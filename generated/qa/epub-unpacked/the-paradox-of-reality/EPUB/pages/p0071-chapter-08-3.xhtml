Pehle thought = reality update.

Ab thought = possibility.

Aur yeh shift subtle hai…

par irreversible lagta hai.

Ab kuch bhi aata hai mind mein…

toh woh immediately “true” nahi lagta.

fear aata hai → but full reality nahi banta

anger aata hai → but identity nahi banata

hope aati hai → but command nahi ban jaati

Sab kuch present hota hai…

par binding nahi hota.

Pehle mind ek courtroom jaisa hota tha:

har thought ek verdict deta tha.

Ab woh courtroom dissolve ho jaata hai.

Aur thoughts sirf “passing voices” lagte hain.

Koi voice permanent nahi.

Koi voice final nahi.

Ab reaction automatic nahi hota.

Ek micro pause aa jaata hai.

Thought → space → response

Aur is space mein kuch naya nahi add hota…
