DEDICATION

This book is dedicated to the moments that never had names.

To the questions that arrived without warning.

To the nights that felt longer than they should have.

To the thoughts that refused to leave.

To the silent observations nobody else could see.

And to every person who has ever looked at the world and felt that something beneath the surface was waiting to be noticed.

This book is dedicated to those who kept questioning when certainty would have been easier.
