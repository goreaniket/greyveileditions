PROLOGUE

Kuch bhi samajhne se pehle…

kuch already ho raha hota hai.

Ek moment exist karta hai…

jo na fully visible hota hai…

na fully hidden hota hai.

Bas “happening” hota hai.

Aur interesting baat yeh hai…

hum us happening ko directly nahi jeete.

Hum usko turant interpret kar lete hain.

Jaise hi kuch experience hota hai…

mind us par ek layer add kar deta hai:

yeh kya tha

iska matlab kya tha

yeh kyun hua

Aur yeh questions itne fast aate hain…
