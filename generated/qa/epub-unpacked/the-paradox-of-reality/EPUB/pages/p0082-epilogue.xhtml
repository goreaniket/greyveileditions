EPILOGUE

Jab sab kuch observe kar liya jata hai…

toh bahar ki duniya mein kuch alag nahi hota.

Log waise hi baat karte hain.

Situations waise hi chalti rehti hain.

Life apni normal speed mein rehti hai.

Par andar ek cheez change ho chuki hoti hai…

urgency of meaning

Pehle har cheez ko turant samajhna hota tha.

Har moment ko define karna hota tha.

Har feeling ko explain karna hota tha.

Lekin ab yeh compulsion dheere dheere weak ho jaati hai.

Kisi cheez ko dekh kar ab turant label nahi banta.

Aur agar banta bhi hai…

toh woh final nahi lagta.

Thoughts ab bhi aate hain.
