We are both the observer and the observed.

The question and the one asking it.

The creator of meaning and the one trapped inside it.

And somewhere within that contradiction, an entire life unfolds.

If this book leaves you with more questions than answers, that is not a failure.

Because some questions are not doors meant to be closed.

They are windows.

They do not give us conclusions.

They give us perspective.

And sometimes perspective changes a life more deeply than certainty ever could.

So as you continue this journey, I hope you do not simply agree or disagree with these pages.

I hope you observe.

I hope you question.

I hope you notice the invisible structures beneath your own experience.

And most importantly, I hope you remain curious enough to keep looking, even when no final answer appears.
