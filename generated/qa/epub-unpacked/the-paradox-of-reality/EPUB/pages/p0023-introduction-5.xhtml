Aur jo bachta hai…

woh koi answer nahi hota.

Koi conclusion nahi hota.

Koi final truth nahi hota.

Bas ek continuously moving field hota hai…

jo apne aap ko explain nahi karta…

sirf unfold hota rehta hai.
