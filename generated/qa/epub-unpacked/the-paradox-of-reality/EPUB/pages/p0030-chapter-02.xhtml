THE PARADOX OF REALITY

CHAPTER 2 — WHEN SEEING STOPS BEING DIRECT

Pehle lagta hai dekhna sabse simple cheez hai.

Aankhen khuli → duniya dikhi → matter over.

Lekin yeh simplicity sirf surface pe hoti hai.

Kyuki jo “dikhta” hai…

woh kabhi raw nahi hota.

Har cheez jo tum dekhte ho…

uske saath ek invisible layer already attach hoti hai.

Meaning ki.

Interpretation ki.

Memory ki.

Expectation ki.
