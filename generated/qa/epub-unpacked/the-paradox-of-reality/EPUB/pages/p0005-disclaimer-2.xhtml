Reality, meaning, identity, perception, and understanding have been explored by philosophers, psychologists, scientists, and thinkers for centuries. This book does not claim to settle those discussions.

It simply participates in them.

Readers are encouraged to think independently, question freely, and form their own conclusions.

Or perhaps, at times, to remain without conclusions.

If any value exists within these pages, it is not in providing certainty.

It is in encouraging observation.

Nothing more.

Nothing less.
