kuch chhupa raha hai

deep soch mein hai

Aur actual silence kho jaata hai.

Wahi cheez har jagah hoti hai.

Ek neutral moment aata hai…

aur mind usko immediately ek story mein convert kar deta hai.

Aur yeh story tum experience karte ho as “reality”.

Ab ek aur layer:

mind ek passive mirror nahi hai.

woh ek prediction engine hai.

Woh sirf present ko nahi dekh raha hota…

woh continuously predict kar raha hota hai ki:

aage kya hoga

iska matlab kya hai

iska outcome kya ho sakta hai

Aur yeh prediction present ke upar chipak jaati hai.

Isliye tum jo “now” samajhte ho…

woh actually:

now + prediction + memory ka mix hota hai

Aur is mix ko tum reality bol dete ho.
