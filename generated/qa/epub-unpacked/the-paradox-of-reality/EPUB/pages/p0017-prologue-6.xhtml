ek cheez slowly clear hoti hai:

Reality kisi fixed structure ka naam nahi hai.

Woh ek ongoing process hai…

jo har moment apne aap ko generate kar raha hai.

Aur hum us process ko “life” ke naam se jee rahe hain…

without noticing the construction behind it.

Aur sabse interesting part yeh hai:

tumhe lagta hai tum reality dekh rahe ho

Lekin tum reality nahi dekh rahe hote…

tum reality ka interpreted version dekh rahe hote ho.

Aur yeh distinction normally visible nahi hota.

Book IV us moment ko explore karta hai…

jab yeh invisible system visible hone lagta hai.

Jab realization aata hai ki:

perception direct nahi hai

meaning automatic nahi hai

reality fixed nahi hai

Sab kuch continuously “ban raha” hota hai…

inside experience.

Aur jab yeh samajh aata hai…

toh ek strange phase start hota hai.
