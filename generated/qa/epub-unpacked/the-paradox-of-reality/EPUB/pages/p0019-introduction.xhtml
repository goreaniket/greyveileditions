INTRODUCTION

Kabhi kabhi life bahar se bilkul normal chal rahi hoti hai…

par andar ek strange sa silence grow kar raha hota hai.

Na kuch collapse hota hai.

Na kuch dramatically change hota hai.

Na koi clear reason hota hai.

Bas ek subtle shift hota hai…

jo pehle notice bhi nahi hota.

Tum dekhte ho same duniya ko.

Same log.

Same faces.

Same routine moments.

Par un moments ka “feel” change hone lagta hai.

Pehle jo cheezein direct lagti thi…

ab unke beech ek invisible gap feel hone lagta hai.

Jaise reality seedhi nahi mil rahi…
