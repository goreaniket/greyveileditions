Na kuch break hota hai.

Na kuch collapse hota hai.

Na kuch dramatically change hota hai.

Bas ek subtle awareness grow hoti hai:

jo bhi tum “world” bol rahe ho… woh already ek constructed experience hai

Aur yahan pe focus change hota hai:

from:

what is reality?

to:

reality kaise ban rahi hai?

Aur jaise jaise yeh observation deepen hota hai…

ek cheez slowly clear hoti hai:

Reality kisi fixed structure ka naam nahi hai.

Woh ek ongoing process hai…

jo har moment apne aap ko generate kar raha hai.

Aur hum us process ko “life” ke naam se jee rahe hain…

without noticing the construction behind it.
