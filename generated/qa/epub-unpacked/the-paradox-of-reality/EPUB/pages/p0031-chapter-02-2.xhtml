Aur yeh sab itni fast hota hai…

ki tumhe lagta hai tum seedha reality dekh rahe ho.

Par ek point aata hai…

jab yeh process thoda slow dikhne lagta hai.

Not because it changed.

But because awareness thodi sharp ho jaati hai.

Tum kisi normal cheez ko dekhte ho.

Ek face.

Ek room.

Ek movement.

Ek silence.

Aur usi moment pe ek aur cheez parallel chal rahi hoti hai:

“yeh kya hai?”
“iska matlab kya hai?”
