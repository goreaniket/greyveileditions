Na kuch completely real lagta hai.

Na kuch completely unreal lagta hai.

Bas ek unfolding experience hota hai…

jo kisi final meaning pe rukta nahi.

Aur yeh phase uncomfortable bhi nahi hota…

aur peaceful bhi nahi hota.

Bas ek raw awareness hoti hai.

Unfiltered. Unnamed. Uncontrolled.

Jaise tum kisi cheez ko pehli baar “as it is” dekh rahe ho…

without forcing it into meaning.

Aur yahin pe Book IV ka real entry point hota hai:

jab reality ko samajhne ki koshish kam ho jaati hai… aur usko observe karne ka awareness zyada ho jaata hai

Aur jaise jaise yeh awareness grow karti hai…

ek cheez slowly dissolve hone lagti hai:

fixed interpretation ki addiction

Reality disappear nahi hoti.

Life stop nahi hoti.

World change nahi hota.

Bas ek layer quietly gir jaati hai:

meaning ko lock karne ki compulsion
