but tum “dekhne wale” se zyada…

“translate hone wale system” jaisa feel karte ho.

Aur yeh shift subtle hai…

lekin irreversible feel hota hai.

Kyuki ab tum kisi cheez ko dekh kar usko stop nahi kar sakte…

interpret hone se.

Aur yahi pe ek strange discomfort arise hota hai:

agar har cheez interpreted hai… toh original kya hai?

Lekin yeh question bhi quickly dissolve ho jaata hai…

kyuki “original” bhi ek interpretation hi hai.

Aur yeh loop thoda heavy ho jaata hai.

Na clarity fully stable rehti hai.

Na confusion fully solid hota hai.

Bas ek in-between state banti hai…

jahan seeing aur thinking merge ho chuke hote hain…

without permission.

Aur ab ek cheez slowly visible hoti hai:

tum duniya ko nahi dekh rahe… tum duniya ko construct hote hue experience kar rahe ho

Aur jab yeh realization settle hone lagta hai…
