“yeh safe hai ya nahi?”
“yeh important hai ya not?”

Aur yeh questions baad mein nahi aate.

Yeh saath-saath chal rahe hote hain.

Aur yahin pe ek strange realization start hota hai:

tum dekh nahi rahe ho… tum classify kar rahe ho

Har cheez instant category mein chali jaati hai:

good / bad

important / ignore

threat / safe

relevant / irrelevant

Aur jab classification ho rahi hoti hai…

toh direct experience lost ho jata hai.

Ab ek simple scene lo:

koi insaan chup hai.
