Aur sab kuch slightly unstable lagta hai…

par broken nahii

Yeh confusion nahi hai.

Yeh collapse of single interpretation habit hai.

Aur jab yeh state stable hone lagti hai…

toh ek silent understanding aati hai:

certainty truth nahi thi

certainty sirf mind ka compression tool tha

Aur jab compression weak hota hai…

toh reality open ho jaati hai.

Na fully clear.

Na fully unclear.

Bas ek flowing field…

jahan answers fixed nahi rehte…

sirf possibilities rehti hain.
