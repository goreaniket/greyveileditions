Tum usko define nahi kar rahe.

Bas notice ho raha hai.

jo cheez mujhe “soch” lagti thi… woh actually ek ongoing mental activity hai, not an instruction

ab visible ho jaata hai.

Na right decide karta hai.

Na wrong decide karta hai.

Bas generate karta hai.

Aur jab yeh generation visible ho jaata hai…

toh ek silent stability aati hai:

thoughts aate hain…
