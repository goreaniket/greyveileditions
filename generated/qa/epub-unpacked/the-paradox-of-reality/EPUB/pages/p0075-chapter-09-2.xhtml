Lekin ab yeh sab ek unnecessary movement lagne lagta hai.

Explanation ek time pe comfort thi.

Ek control mechanism.

Ek stability illusion.

“agar main samajh gaya toh safe ho jaunga”

Lekin ab yeh logic hold nahi karta.

Kyuki samajhne ke baad bhi cheezein wahi rehti hain.

Aur na samajhne ke baad bhi cheezein wahi rehti hain.

Ek moment aata hai…

jab kuch bhi dekhte hi usko define karne ka pressure drop ho jaata hai.

Koi situation hoti hai…

aur mind automatically usko label nahi karta.

Na good.

Na bad.

Na meaningful.

Na meaningless.
