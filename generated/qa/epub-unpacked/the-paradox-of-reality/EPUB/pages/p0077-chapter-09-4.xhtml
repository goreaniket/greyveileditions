Everything becomes non-final.

Aur yahan pe ek subtle understanding aati hai:

shayad problem reality kabhi thi hi nahi

problem reality ko continuously interpret karne ki habit thi

Aur jab habit weak hoti hai…

toh world nahi badalta.

Sirf pressure drop hota hai.

Ab mind har cheez ko samajhne ke liye push nahi karta.

Woh sirf notice karta hai.

Aur notice karna bhi effort nahi lagta.

Duniya same hoti hai.

Lekin uske around ek invisible frame gir jaata hai.

Jo frame pehle define karta tha:

kya important hai

kya meaningless hai

kya true hai

kya false hai

Woh frame ab silent ho jaata hai.

Aur jab yeh silence settle hota hai…

73
