Jaise woh decision hai.

Jaise woh truth hai.

Lekin ab woh same thought…

sirf ek movement lagta hai.

Ek passing structure.

SOMETHING DROPS

Mind ab bhi generate kar raha hota hai:

analysis

judgement

prediction

comparison

memory replay

Lekin ab in sab ke andar ek distance aa jaata hai.

Thought aata hai…

aur turant ek silent gap appear hota hai:

“yeh sirf ek thought hai”

Aur yeh sentence nahi hota.

Yeh ek direct perception hota hai.
