Par uska emotional weight change ho jaata hai.

Cheezein ab bhi meaningful ho sakti hain…

par unhe “forcefully meaningful” banana zaruri nahi lagta.

Aur jab yeh state settle hoti hai…

toh ek simple si understanding reh jaati hai:

shayad kabhi kuch solve karna tha hi nahi

Aur yeh realization bhi hold nahi kiya jaata.

Woh bhi flow mein dissolve ho jaata hai.

Na meaning bachta hai.

Na meaninglessness.

Na explanation ka pressure.

Bas ek quiet presence hoti hai…

jo kisi conclusion ke bina bhi complete hoti hai…

aur kisi question ke bina bhi chalti rehti hai.
