Bas “is happening”.

Thoughts aate hain.

Analysis aata hai.

Memory aati hai.

Prediction aati hai.

Lekin ab koi inner urgency nahi hoti unko follow karne ki.

Woh bas pass ho jaate hain.

Jaise hawa mein movement.

Pehle mind chahta tha:

clarity

conclusion

final answer

stable meaning

Ab yeh “chahna” hi weak ho jaata hai.

Aur yeh weakness loss nahi lagti.

Yeh relief lagta hai.

Ab normal cheezein bhi different lagti hain:

koi baat → immediate interpretation nahi

koi silence → meaning assign nahi hota

koi reaction → permanent identity nahi banta
