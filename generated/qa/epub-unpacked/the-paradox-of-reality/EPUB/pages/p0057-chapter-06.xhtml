THE PARADOX OF REALITY

CHAPTER 6 — WHEN CERTAINTY STOPS WORKING

Kabhi kabhi ek phase aata hai…

jab duniya badalti nahi hai…

par usko samajhne ka tareeka tootne lagta hai.

Aur yeh tootna loud nahi hota.

Silent hota hai.

Slow hota hai.

Aur andar se feel hota hai.

Shuru mein mind simple hota hai:

yeh sahi hai

yeh galat hai

yeh safe hai

yeh unsafe hai
