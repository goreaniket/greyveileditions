“main dekh raha hoon reality ko”

Ab lagta hai:

“main reality ko interpret kar raha hoon”

Aur phir ek aur layer aati hai:

“shayad main reality ko construct kar raha hoon”

Aur yeh realization ek thought nahi hota.

Yeh ek lived experience ban jaata hai.

Jo cheez pehle stable thi…

woh ab fluid lagne lagti hai.

Aur jo cheez pehle solid lagti thi…

woh ab continuously change hoti hui lagti hai.
