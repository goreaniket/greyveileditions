dono perfectly align nahi kar rahe.

Shuruaat mein yeh bahut small hota hai.

Almost ignoreable.

Tum kisi cheez ko dekhte ho:

ek room, ek face, ek situation, ek silence.

Aur sab normal lagta hai.

Lekin us normal ke andar ek layer hoti hai:

yeh normal kyu lag raha hai?”

Aur yahi pe first fracture start hota hai.

Reality mein nahi…

perception mein.

Tum notice karte ho ki:

jo cheez “real” lag rahi hai…

woh actually directly present nahi lag rahi…

woh processed lag rahi hai.

Jaise mind ke andar se pass ho kar aa rahi ho.

Aur ek aur uncomfortable realization aata hai:

tum reality nahi dekh rahe ho

tum reality ka interpretation dekh rahe ho

Ab yeh thought nahi lagta.

Yeh ek observation jaisa feel hota hai.
