THE PARADOX OF REALITY

CHAPTER 1 — THE FEELING THAT REALITY IS NOT STABLE

Kabhi kabhi ek normal sa moment hota hai…

kuch bhi special nahi.

na koi event.

na koi shock.

na koi emotional trigger.

Bas tum hotey ho… aur duniya chal rahi hoti hai.

Lekin phir ek strange sa gap feel hota hai.

Not visible.

Not explainable.

Bas ek subtle mismatch.

Jaise jo tum dekh rahe ho…

aur jo tum “samajh rahe ho ki tum dekh rahe ho”…
