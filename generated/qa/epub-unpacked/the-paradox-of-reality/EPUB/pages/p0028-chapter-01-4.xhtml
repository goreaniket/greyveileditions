Aur yahan pe mind ek loop mein chala jaata hai:

real kya hai?

jo dikh raha hai ya jo ho raha hai?

ya jo samjha ja raha hai?

Aur koi answer stable nahi rehta.

Aur sabse dangerous part yeh hai:

reality change nahi hoti

sirf uska “certainty layer” weaken hota hai

World wahi hota hai.

Log wahi hote hain.

Situation wahi hoti hai.

Lekin ek invisible shift ho chuka hota hai:

“this is real” ab automatic nahi lagta

Aur yeh doubt nahi hai.

Yeh confusion nahi hai.

Yeh ek perceptual instability hai.

Jaise mind ab pehli baar notice kar raha ho:
