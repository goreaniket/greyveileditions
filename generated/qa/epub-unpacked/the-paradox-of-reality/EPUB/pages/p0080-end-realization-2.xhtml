Ek cheez ko dekh kar ab turant final label nahi aata.

Aur agar aata bhi hai…

toh woh hold nahi karta.

Ab experience ko todne ki koshish kam ho jaati hai.

Samajhne ki urgency kam ho jaati hai.

Explain karne ka pressure kam ho jaata hai.

Aur jo reh jaata hai…

woh sirf experience hota hai.

Without extra layer.

Without extra weight.

Jo pehle “solve karna” lagta tha…

ab sirf “dekhna” lagta hai.

Na kuch control karna hota hai.

Na kuch fix karna hota hai.

Na kuch conclude karna hota hai.

Bas cheezein apne flow mein hoti rehti hain.

Kahin bhi ek final point nahi milta.

Na thinking mein.

Na feeling mein.

Na understanding mein.

Har cheez ongoing lagti hai…
