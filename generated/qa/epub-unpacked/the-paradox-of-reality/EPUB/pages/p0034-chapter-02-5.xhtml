Silence ab silence nahi rehta.

Silence ek story ban jaata hai.

Aur yeh story itni fast generate hoti hai…

ki tumhe lagta hai wahi reality hai.

Lekin jab thoda gap create hota hai…

ek micro pause…

toh ek uncomfortable clarity aati hai:

jo main dekh raha hoon… woh already edited hai

Edited by memory.

Edited by expectation.

Edited by fear.

Edited by past experience.

Aur jab yeh editing visible ho jaati hai…

toh ek aur layer crack hoti hai:

“direct seeing” ka illusion

Ab reality distant nahi hoti…

par “unprocessed” bhi nahi lagti.

Woh ek continuous translation ban jaati hai.

World → perception → interpretation → meaning → reaction

Aur tum beech mein ho…
