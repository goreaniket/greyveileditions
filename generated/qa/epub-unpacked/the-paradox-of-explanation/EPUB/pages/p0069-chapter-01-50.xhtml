Aur shayad isi liye kai log saalon baad bhi ussi memory ke paas laut jaate hain.

Not because the memory is unfinished.

Because the search is.

Unhe lagta hai ki ek aur interpretation mil jaaye...

ek aur explanation mil jaaye...

ek aur meaning mil jaaye...

toh finally peace mil jayegi.

Lekin kai baar peace answer milne se nahi aati.

Peace search rukne se aati hai.

Aur yeh accept karna bahut mushkil hai.

Kyuki humari poori life humein sikhati hai:

Questions poochho.

Answers dhoondo.

Understand karo.

Explain karo.

Decode karo.

Lekin bahut kam log humein yeh sikhate hain:

Ki kuch cheezein sirf jeene ke liye hoti hain.

Samajhne ke liye nahi.

Kuch dard sirf mehsoos karne ke liye hote hain.
