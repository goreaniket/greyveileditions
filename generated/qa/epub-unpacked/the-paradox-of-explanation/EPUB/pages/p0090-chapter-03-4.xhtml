Isliye hum labels use karte hain:

I am introvert

I am overthinker

I am emotional

I am strong

I am broken

But labels reality nahi hote.

Labels bas understanding ko simplify karte hain.

Aur jo cheez simplify hoti hai…

woh truth lose kar deti hai.

Ek aur dangerous layer hai.

Hum apne past ko apni identity bana dete hain.

Jo hum the…

woh hum ban jaate hain.

Ek rejection → “I am rejected type”

Ek failure → “I am failure prone”

Ek mistake → “I am wrong person”

Aur dheere dheere past ek mirror nahi rehta.

Past ek cage ban jaata hai.

Lekin agar tum genuinely observe karo…

toh ek strange gap dikhega.
