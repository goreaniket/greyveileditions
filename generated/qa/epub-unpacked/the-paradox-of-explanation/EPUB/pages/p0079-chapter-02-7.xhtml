filters humein pata bhi nahi hote.

Hum unhe “truth” samajh lete hain.

Ek aur pattern quietly chalta rehta hai.

Selective memory.

Hum un moments ko zyada clarity dete hain jo humari existing story ko support karte hain.

Aur un moments ko ignore kar dete hain jo us story ko break karte hain.

Agar hum believe karte hain:

“I always fail”

toh dimaag 5 failures ko cinematic detail mein replay karta hai.

Aur 20 small wins ko completely blur kar deta hai.

Not because they are irrelevant.

But because they are dangerous to the narrative.

Kyuki agar story break ho gayi…

toh identity unstable ho jaayegi.

Aur dheere dheere ek illusion ban jaata hai:
