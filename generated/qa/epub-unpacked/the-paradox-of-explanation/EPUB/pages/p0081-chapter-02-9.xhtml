Yeh curiosity nahi hoti.

Yeh reinforcement hota hai.

Existing belief system ko stable banane ka mechanism.

Because “always” is not reality.

It is emotional pattern turned into belief.

Aur shayad sabse uncomfortable realization yeh hai:

You are not living your past.

You are living your explanation of your past.

Aur explanation ka problem yeh hai…

it feels more solid than truth.

Reality fluid hoti hai.

Situations evolve karti hain.

People change karte hain.

Circumstances shift hoti hain.

But story stable lagti hai.

Aur human mind stability ko safety samajhta hai.

Chahe woh false hi kyun na ho.

Stories comfort deti hain.

Kyuki stories chaos ko structure bana deti hain.

Even if that structure is imaginary.

Even if it is incomplete.
