A silence arrives.

And it does not become emotional analysis.

It remains silence.

And in this simple existence of things…

something very deep starts shifting.

Not outside.

Inside perception itself.

Because what is happening is not “new understanding”.

It is the slowing down of interpretation.

And when interpretation slows down…

reality becomes sharper.

Not because it changed…

but because it is no longer filtered immediately.

But this clarity is not comfortable at first.

Because comfort was built on meaning.

If everything had meaning…

then everything felt manageable.

Even pain.

Even confusion.

Even loss.

But now…
