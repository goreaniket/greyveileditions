life becomes strangely immediate.

Not extraordinary.

Not mystical.

Just immediate.

And in that immediacy…

there is no distance between “you” and experience.

Only participation.

Not observer versus observed.

But simply unfolding.

And for a brief moment…

there is no need to explain what is happening…

because nothing is missing from what is happening.

And that is the strange edge:

Life was never incomplete.

Only the interpretation was.
