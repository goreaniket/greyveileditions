THE PARADOX OF EXPLANATION

WHEN NOTHING NEEDS A MEANING

There comes a point in human thinking…

where everything has already been explained.

Not by truth.

But by the mind.

Every pain has a reason.

Every failure has a lesson.

Every person has an intention.

Every silence has a hidden message.

Every coincidence has a pattern.

Every emotion has a psychological root.

And at first…

this feels like understanding.
