Even confusion becomes organized into identity.

Even randomness is converted into explanation.

Because the mind is not designed to tolerate unclassified reality.

It wants labels.

It wants conclusions.

It wants closure.

But there comes a point…

where even closure stops working.

Not because life becomes clear…

but because clarity itself becomes unnecessary.

Something begins to happen quietly.

You are still thinking…

but thoughts are no longer sticky.

You are still feeling…

but feelings are no longer defining you.

You are still remembering…

but memories are no longer constructing identity.

It is not detachment.

It is not emotional distance.

It is not spiritual achievement.
