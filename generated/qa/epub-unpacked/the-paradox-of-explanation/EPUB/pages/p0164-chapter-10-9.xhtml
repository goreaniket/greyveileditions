Because the mind always searches for something special.

But what is here…

was never special.

It was always present.

Just ignored.

And now…

attention stops interfering so aggressively.

And in that reduced interference…

something becomes obvious:

You were never separate from life.

You were never outside it observing it.

You were never inside it controlling it.

You were the space in which it was appearing.

Not as identity.

Not as concept.

Not as conclusion.

But as simple presence that does not need definition.

And even this presence…

does not need to be held.

Because holding implies fear of loss.
