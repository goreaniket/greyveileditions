"Yeh mujhe mere baare mein kya dikha raha hai?"

Aur kai baar life lesson nahi deti.

Life mirror deti hai.

Pain ke moments mein pehli baar hum dekhte hain:

Hum kin cheezon par dependent the.

Hum kin logon par dependent the.

Hum kitna validation chahte the.

Hum kitna approval chahte the.

Hum kitna control chahte the.

Aur sabse important...

Hum kitna darr rahe the.

Kyuki comfort bahut cheezein chhupa leta hai.

Comfort doubts ko mute kar deta hai.

Comfort insecurities ko cover kar deta hai.

Comfort illusions ko survive karne deta hai.

Lekin pain...

pain cover nahi karta.
