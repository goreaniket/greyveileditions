EPILOGUE-

WHEN EVEN THE QUESTION DISAPPEARS

Agar tum yahan tak aa gaye ho…

toh kuch tumhare andar already change ho chuka hai.

Lekin shayad tumhe pata bhi nahi.

Tum shuru mein answers dhoondh rahe the:

yeh kyun ho raha hai?

iska meaning kya hai?

mujhe kya samajhna chahiye?

Lekin dheere dheere…

yeh questions heavy lagne lage.

Kyuki answers milne ki jagah…

questions hi dissolve hone lage.

Aur jab questions dissolve hote hain…

toh clarity nahi aati.

Space aata hai.

Aur us space mein…
