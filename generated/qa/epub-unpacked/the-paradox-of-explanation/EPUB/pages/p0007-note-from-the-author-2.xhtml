It will quietly interrupt the habit of turning every experience into meaning.

Because somewhere along the way…

we stopped seeing life directly.

We started translating it.

And that translation became heavier than the thing itself.

I am not asking you to believe anything written here.

Belief is not the point.

Observation is.

And observation is uncomfortable…

because it removes the comfort of fixed meanings.

So if at any point this book feels too direct…

too close…

or too familiar…

don’t rush to label it.

Just notice it.

Because the intention was never to change your thinking.

It was to make you aware that thinking is already happening without your permission.

And maybe that is where the real conversation begins.

Not between you and this book…
