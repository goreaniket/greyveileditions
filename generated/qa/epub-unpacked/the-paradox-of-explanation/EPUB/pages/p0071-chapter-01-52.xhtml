Ek aur story.

Ek aur explanation.

Jaise kahin na kahin hum yeh maan chuke hain ki agar humne sab kuch samajh liya...

toh hum finally safe ho jayenge.

Lekin shayad life samajhne ke baad safe nahi hoti.

Life sirf explainable ho jaati hai.

Aur explainable aur livable ek hi cheez nahi hote.

Aur shayad yahi is chapter ka sabse uncomfortable realization hai:

Insaan answers ki bhookh se nahi chalta.

Insaan certainty ki bhookh se chalta hai.

Aur kai baar...

certainty ki bhookh hi usse reality se door le jaati hai.

Aur shayad isi liye insaan answers se zyada narratives collect karta hai.

Kyuki narrative ke andar certainty hoti hai.

Aur certainty ke andar comfort.

Lekin reality ko comfort ki koi zaroorat nahi hoti.

Reality sirf reality hoti hai.
