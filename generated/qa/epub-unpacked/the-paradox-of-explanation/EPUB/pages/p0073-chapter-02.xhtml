THE PARADOX OF EXPLANATION

THE STORIES WE BUILD

There is a strange thing about human

memory.

It does not store life.

It stores meaning.

Not what happened…

but what it felt like it meant.

Aur dheere dheere, bina realize kiye, hum apni life jeena band kar dete hain.

We don’t live experiences anymore.

We start editing them.

Not once.

Not twice.
