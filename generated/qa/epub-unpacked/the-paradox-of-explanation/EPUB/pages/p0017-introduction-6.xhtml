Aur yeh moment mind ko disturb karta hai.

Kyuki agar tum apni thoughts nahi ho…

toh phir tum ho kya?

Mind turant answers banata hai:

self, soul, identity, consciousness…

But har answer…

sirf ek aur thought hota hai.

Aur thought kabhi observer nahi ho sakta.

Aur yahan pe ek crack aata hai perception mein.

Shayad main life ke andar nahi hoon…

shayad life mere andar appear ho rahi hai.

Not as belief.

But as direct, uncomfortable seeing.

Aur jab yeh seeing hoti hai…

toh explanation ki need kam hone lagti hai.

Kyuki jab reality direct ho…

toh meaning extra lagne lagta hai.

Aur yahin se yeh book start hoti hai.

Not to give you answers.

But to interrupt the habit…

of turning everything into meaning.
