Because earlier, the mind never left empty space.

It filled everything immediately.

With meaning.

With story.

With judgment.

With prediction.

But now…

sometimes nothing appears immediately.

And that “nothing” feels unfamiliar.

A person thinks something like:

“I should do this…”

And suddenly another awareness appears:

“Is this a thought… or a fact?”

And in that small pause…

the thought loses its grip.
