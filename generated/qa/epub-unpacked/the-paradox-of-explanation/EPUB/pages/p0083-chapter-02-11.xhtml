“I am always rejected.”
“I am not meant for this.”
“I am stuck.”

Aur phir ek silent shift hota hai.

Hum experience karna band kar dete hain.

Hum interpret karna start kar dete hain.

But stories are not real structures.

They are mental constructs.

And anything constructed…

can collapse.

But collapse tabhi hota hai jab contradiction allow kiya jaaye.
