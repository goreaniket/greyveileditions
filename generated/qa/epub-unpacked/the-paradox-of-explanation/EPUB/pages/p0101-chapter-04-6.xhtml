“If I had done something different… this wouldn’t have happened.”

Maybe true sometimes.

But not always.

Yet the mind treats it as always true.

Because it gives illusion of control.

And illusion feels better than helplessness.

But control has a limit.

A very uncomfortable limit.

At some point, life will happen without permission.

Without warning.

Without preparation.

A conversation will change everything.

A person will leave suddenly.

A plan will collapse.

A moment will shift direction completely.

And nothing you rehearsed will apply.

And in that moment…
