explaining existence.

And this failure is not destruction.

It is exhaustion of interpretation.

Because every explanation requires distance.

And here…

there is no stable distance left.

Everything is too immediate.

Too direct.

Too present.

And in this immediacy…

life stops needing narration.

A sound is just sound.

A breath is just breath.

A moment is just moment.

Not symbols.

Not messages.

Not lessons.

Not signs.

Just what is.

And something very strange happens here:

The pressure to understand starts dissolving.
