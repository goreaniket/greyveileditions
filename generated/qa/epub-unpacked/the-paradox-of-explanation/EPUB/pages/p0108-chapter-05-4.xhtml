Future failures are being simulated.

And the body reacts as if it is real.

Because the body does not distinguish clearly between real threat and imagined threat.

And this is the core trap:

The mind suffers in advance.

Most pain is not experienced.

It is pre-experienced.

A situation might never even arrive.

But the stress of it has already been lived multiple times in the head.

And the irony is:

If the situation finally arrives…

the mind says:

“I knew this would happen.”

Even if it created that fear itself.

This is how fear becomes self-confirming.

It creates preparation.
