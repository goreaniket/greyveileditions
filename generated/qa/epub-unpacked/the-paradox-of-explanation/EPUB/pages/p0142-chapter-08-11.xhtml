Memory becomes a thought.

Not self.

Even “I am like this” becomes a thought.

Not fact.

And slowly…

a deeper stability appears.

Not in thought.

But behind thought.

A kind of silence that does not argue.

Does not explain.

Does not defend.

Does not conclude.

Just watches.

And in that watching…

something very subtle becomes clear:

Maybe you were never your thoughts.

Maybe you were the space in which thoughts happen.

And if that is true…

then nothing that thoughts say about you…

was ever final.

And in that realization…
