You stop meeting life.

You start meeting your interpretation of life.

The mind becomes addicted to meaning.

Because meaning reduces uncertainty.

And uncertainty feels unsafe.

So the mind prefers explanation…

even if explanation is incomplete.

But meaning has a hidden cost.

It does not just explain life.

It replaces life.

A child sees rain.

No analysis.

No symbolism.

No interpretation.

Just experience.

Cold. Sound. movement. presence.

An adult sees rain.
