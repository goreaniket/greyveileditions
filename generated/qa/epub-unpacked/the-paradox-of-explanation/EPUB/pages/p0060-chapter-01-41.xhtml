Aur observation tab shuru hoti hai jab insaan apni favourite story par doubt karne lagta hai.

Woh story jo woh khud ko saalon se suna raha tha.

Ki main unlucky hoon.

Ki duniya unfair hai.

Ki log samajhte nahi.

Ki mere saath hi aisa hota hai.

Aur phir ek din woh rukta hai.

Aur pehli baar poochta hai:

"Kya ho agar main galat story ko protect kar raha tha?"

Yeh dangerous moment hai.

Kyuki yahin se transformation shuru hota hai.

Aur yahin se fear bhi.
