Even if it is biased.

A painful truth unstable lagta hai.

But a meaningful lie stable lagta hai.

Aur insaan aksar truth nahi choose karta.

Insaan stability choose karta hai.

Isliye closure addictive hota hai.

Hum sirf answers nahi dhoondh rahe hote.

Hum dhoondh rahe hote hain ek final explanation.

Ek aisi story jo sab kuch end kar de.

Jo confusion ko permanently seal kar de.

But life rarely behaves like that.

So hum us gap ko khud fill kar dete hain.

Aur jab story stable ho jaati hai…

toh hum apni identity us story se fuse kar dete hain.

“I am unlucky.”
