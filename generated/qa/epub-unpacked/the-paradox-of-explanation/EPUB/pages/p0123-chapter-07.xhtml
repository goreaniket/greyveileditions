THE PARADOX OF EXPLANATION

WHEN THE MIND STOPS EXPLAINING

There is a point in every deeply thinking human…

where explanations stop working.

Not because truth arrives.

But because the mind gets exhausted.

Exhausted of translating everything.

Exhausted of converting experience into meaning.

Exhausted of trying to stay in control through understanding.

And this exhaustion is not loud.

It does not announce itself.

It does not feel like a breakthrough.

It feels like silence inside thought itself.
