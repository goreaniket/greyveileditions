meaning itself is not arriving instantly.

And that creates a strange exposure.

Not of danger.

But of rawness.

A moment where nothing is being added to experience.

No story.

No explanation.

No future projection.

No past reference.

Just now.

Unclaimed.

And the mind struggles here.

Because it wants to complete experience.

It wants to “finish” reality into understanding.

But reality refuses completion.

It keeps unfolding.

Without permission.

Without conclusion.

And slowly…

a strange observation becomes possible:

Not everything that happens is pointing somewhere.
