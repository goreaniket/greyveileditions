Aur yahan ek invisible transformation hota hai.

Ek interpretation, agar repeat ho jaaye…

toh woh perception ban jaata hai.

Aur perception, reality ko reshape karne lagta hai.

Agar ek baar dimaag ne decide kar liya:

“I am not enough”

toh duniya neutral nahi rehti.

Duniya already interpreted hoti hai.

Har silence rejection lagta hai.

Har delay disrespect.

Har change abandonment.

Har unfamiliar tone threat.

Reality same rehti hai.

But lens distorted ho jaata hai.

Aur distorted lens ko reality lagta hai.

Hum log reality se directly nahi milte.

We meet reality through filters.

Aur sabse dangerous baat yeh hai:
