“mere saath hi aisa hota hai.”

Aur yeh ek baar nahi hota.

Yeh baar baar hota hai.

Itna baar…

ki tum reality ko nahi yaad rakhte.

Tum uska interpretation yaad rakhte ho.

Aur yahi se ek silent transformation hoti hai.

Tum life ko experience nahi karte…

tum life ko explain karte ho.

Aur explanation ka trap yeh hai:

woh kabhi stop nahi hota.

Ek thought → ek identity

Ek identity → ek pattern

Ek pattern → ek personality

Aur dheere dheere…

tum ek living story ban jaate ho.

Jo apne aap ko jee rahi hai…

but realize nahi kar rahi ki woh story hai.
