Because meaning does not only describe reality.

It slowly starts replacing reality.

At some point, nothing is left untouched.

Everything becomes something else:

experience becomes insight

silence becomes message

pain becomes lesson

joy becomes phase

people become patterns

life becomes theory

And slowly…

life becomes heavier.

Not because it is heavy.

But because it is constantly processed.

Even peace is not left alone.

Peace becomes:

“I am healing.”
