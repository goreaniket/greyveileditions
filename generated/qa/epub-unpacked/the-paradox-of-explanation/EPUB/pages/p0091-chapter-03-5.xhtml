Tum wahi person nahi hote jo tum 3 saal pehle the.

Na thoughts same hain.

Na reactions same hain.

Na desires same hain.

Even memories same nahi feel hoti.

Phir bhi tum insist karte ho:

“I am the same person.”

Kyuki continuity comfort deti hai.

Aur comfort truth se zyada powerful hota hai.

Ek moment aata hai jab self breakdown start hota hai.

Jab tum realize karte ho:

Main jis “self” ko defend kar raha tha…

woh actually ek narrative tha.

A set of repeated thoughts.

A collection of reactions.

A memory-based identity.

Not something solid.

Aur yahan ek uncomfortable silence aata hai.
