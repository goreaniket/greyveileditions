Aur jab koi experience us image ko break karta hai…

hum us experience ko reject kar dete hain.

Instead of updating the image.

Aur yeh hi loop hai:

Reality → contradiction → denial → story repair → identity reinforcement

But kabhi kabhi life forcefully break karti hai ye structure.

Not to punish.

But to show:

Tum jo defend kar rahe the… woh real tha hi nahi.

Aur yahan pe pehli baar ek clarity aati hai:

Tum ek fixed self nahi ho.

Tum ek unfolding process ho.

Aur jo process ko hold karne ki koshish karta hai…

woh stress create karta hai.

Because you cannot hold movement.

You can only observe it.

Aur shayad sabse deep realization yeh hai:

Tumhe khud ko “find” nahi karna hai.

Tumhe khud ko “stop defining” karna hai.
