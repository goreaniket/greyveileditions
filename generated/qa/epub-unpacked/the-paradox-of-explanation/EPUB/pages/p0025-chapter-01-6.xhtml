Yeh pattern sirf ek insaan ka nahi.

Puri humanity ka hai.

Hum events ko yaad nahi rakhte.

Hum unke meanings ko yaad rakhte hain.

Aur shayad isse bhi zyada dangerous baat yeh hai:

Insaan reality se nahi jeeta.

Insaan reality ke baare mein bani hui apni favourite story se jeeta hai.

Aur jab reality aur story takraati hain...

toh aksar story jeet jaati hai.

Kyuki truth ko accept karne ke liye courage chahiye hota hai.

Lekin kahani ko protect karne ke liye sirf ego.

Isi liye do log ek hi cheez experience karke do alag realities mein jee sakte hain.

Ek insaan rejection ko end samajhta hai.

Dusra usse direction samajhta hai.

Event same.

Meaning alag.

Reality same.

Story alag.
