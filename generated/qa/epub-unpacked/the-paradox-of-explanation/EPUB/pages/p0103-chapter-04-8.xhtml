Silence is uncertainty without interpretation.

And that feels unfamiliar.

So we escape it.

With thoughts.

With distractions.

With explanations.

With control loops.

But no matter how far we go…

life still remains partially uncontrollable.

Not as punishment.

But as structure.

Because unpredictability is not a flaw in life.

It is life itself.

And slowly, a deeper realization appears:

Maybe control was never real.

Maybe it was just a story to make randomness feel less terrifying.

And when that realization arrives…

the question changes again.
