Is book ka purpose tumhe kuch naya dena nahi hai.

Iska purpose tumhari ek purani cheez disturb karna hai:

automatic interpretation system

Kyuki real question yeh nahi hai:

“Life kya hai?”

Real question yeh hai:

“Kaun hai jo tumhare andar har cheez ko meaning bana raha hai?”

Aur agar tum yahan comfort dhoondhne aaye ho…

toh tum already us illusion ke andar ho jisse yeh book break karna chahti hai.

Isliye jaldi mat padhna.

Kyuki fast reading mind ko bachati hai.

Aur slow reading mind ko expose karti hai.

Aur jo aage aayega…
