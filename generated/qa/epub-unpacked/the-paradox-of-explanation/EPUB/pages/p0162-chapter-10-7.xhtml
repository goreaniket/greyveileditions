Not because understanding is achieved…

but because understanding is no longer demanded.

And in that space…

a silence becomes visible.

Not external silence.

Not absence of noise.

Not mental blankness.

But silence that exists even inside activity.

Even inside thought.

Even inside emotion.

Even inside confusion.

A silence that does not react.

Does not interpret.

Does not interfere.

And at first…

the mind tries to claim it:

“This is peace.”
