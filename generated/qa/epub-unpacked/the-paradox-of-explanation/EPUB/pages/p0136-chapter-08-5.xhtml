Not completely.

But noticeably.

And this begins to repeat.

Again and again.

Thought appears.

And is observed.

Not reacted to instantly.

Just seen.

And slowly…

something very fundamental starts shifting:

You are no longer inside every thought.

You are also witnessing them.

And this creates a strange internal split.

Not between good and bad thoughts.

But between:

thoughts that arise

and something that notices their arising

At first, this feels destabilizing.

Because identity was built on uninterrupted thinking.

If thoughts stop feeling trustworthy…

then what was “you” actually based on?
