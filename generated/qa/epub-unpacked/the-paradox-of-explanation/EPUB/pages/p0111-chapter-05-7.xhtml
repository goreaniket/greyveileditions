But internally.

Because when you try to avoid every possible pain…

you also avoid many possible experiences.

And then a strange realization arrives:

You were not protecting your life.

You were protecting yourself from a story your mind created.

And the question shifts again.

Not:

“What can go wrong?”

But:

“Why am I living inside something that hasn’t happened?”

And in that question…

fear loses its authority for a moment.
