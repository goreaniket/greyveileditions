PROLOGUE-

BEFORE YOU TRUST YOUR OWN MIND

Is book ko padhne se pehle…

ek baat samajh lo.

Jo bhi tum apne baare mein “jaante” ho…

woh direct truth nahi hai.

Woh filtered hai.

Filter kis cheez ka?

interpretation ka

fear ka

past experience ka

aur tumhari apni expectations ka

Tumne kabhi reality ko raw form mein nahi dekha.

Tumne sirf uska meaning dekha hai.

Aur dheere dheere…

yeh filter itna normal ban gaya…

ki tumhe lagta hai yeh hi “tum” ho.
