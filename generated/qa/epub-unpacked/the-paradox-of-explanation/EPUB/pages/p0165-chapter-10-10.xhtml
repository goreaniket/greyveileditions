And here…

nothing is being lost.

Nothing is being gained.

Life continues exactly as before.

But the internal commentary weakens.

Not completely gone.

But no longer absolute.

And in that weakening…

there is a strange lightness.

Not happiness.

Not sadness.

Not neutrality.

Just absence of internal resistance.

And in that absence…

everything becomes more real.

Not because reality changed…

but because it is no longer being filtered through constant interpretation.

And slowly…

even the need to describe this state begins to fade.

Because description itself feels like separation.
