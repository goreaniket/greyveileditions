what you are

what you should be

what went wrong

what might go wrong

what others think

what you lack

And without realizing it…

you stop meeting life.

You start meeting interpretation of life.

And this interpretation becomes so continuous…

that silence feels strange.

Stillness feels suspicious.

Non-thinking feels empty.

Because identity was never built on being…

it was built on thinking about being.

And then one day…

something very subtle breaks.

Not externally.

But internally.

A moment arrives where:

You are not actively defining yourself.
