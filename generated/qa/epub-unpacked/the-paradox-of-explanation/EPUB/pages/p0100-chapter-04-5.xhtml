So the mind fills it immediately.

With assumptions.

With predictions.

With imagined outcomes.

Most of which never happen.

But the body still reacts.

Heart races.

Thoughts spiral.

Sleep becomes lighter.

Not because something is happening.

But because something might happen.

And this is where human suffering quietly increases.

Not from reality.

But from imagined control failure.

We don’t suffer only from what happens.

We suffer from what we think we should have prevented.
