a realization becomes unavoidable:

You were never actually inside the story.

You were the space in which the story appeared.

The story changed.

The roles changed.

The emotions changed.

The memories changed.

The interpretations changed.

But something remained constant.

Not as identity.

But as awareness of change.

And that awareness was never really noticed…

because attention was always absorbed inside content of life.

Not the witnessing of it.

And now…

attention shifts slightly.

Not away from life.

But away from automatic self-narration.

And in that shift…

life feels strangely direct.
