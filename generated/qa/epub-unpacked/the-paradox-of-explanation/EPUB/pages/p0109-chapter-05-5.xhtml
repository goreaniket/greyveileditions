Preparation creates anxiety.

Anxiety feels like evidence.

And evidence strengthens fear.

A loop.

Not of reality.

But of interpretation.

And inside this loop…

life becomes heavier than it actually is.

Because reality is usually simple.

But anticipation is not.

Reality is one moment.

Fear is hundreds of imagined moments.

And human beings often confuse the two.

They think they are reacting to life.

But they are reacting to mental simulations.

This is why peace feels rare.

Not because life is chaotic.

But because mind never stops projecting chaos forward.

Even when nothing is wrong…

the mind keeps asking:
