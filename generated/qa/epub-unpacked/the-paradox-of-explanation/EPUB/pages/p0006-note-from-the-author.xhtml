INTRODUCTION

NOTE FROM THE AUTHOR

If you are reading this…

it already means something in you stopped scrolling.

Not because this book is special.

But because something in it felt familiar in a way you didn’t expect.

I am not writing this as someone who has understood life.

I am writing this as someone who stopped trusting the automatic explanations the mind keeps producing.

Because most of what we call “understanding”…

is actually repetition of old thoughts in new words.

And most of what we call “confusion”…

is the moment reality stops matching the story in our head.

This book is not here to give you answers.

If anything, it will do the opposite.
