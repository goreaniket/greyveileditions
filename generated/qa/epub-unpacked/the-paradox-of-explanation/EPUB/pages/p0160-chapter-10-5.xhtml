without effort.

without direction.

without ownership.

And slowly…

this awareness becomes noticeable not as a concept…

but as the background of everything.

A thought appears…

and disappears.

A fear appears…

and disappears.

A desire appears…

and disappears.

A memory appears…

and disappears.

But something does not appear and disappear.

It only notices appearing and disappearing.

And for the first time…

you cannot locate this “noticer” as an object.

Because anything you try to define it as…

becomes another thought inside it.
