Lekin kaunsa “me”?

Kyuki jo “tum” kal the…

woh aaj ka tum nahi hai.

Aur jo aaj ka tum hai…

woh kal nahi rahega.

But mind ek trick karta hai.

Woh in changing moments ko ek single identity mein compress kar deta hai.

Taaki confusion kam ho.

Taaki life manageable lage.

Aur yahin se ek illusion create hota hai:

A stable self.

Hum apni identity ko ek object bana dete hain.

Jaise woh permanent ho.

Jaise woh fixed ho.

Jaise woh change nahi hota.

But reality yeh hai:

Self is not an object.

Self is a process.

Aur process ko define karna hamesha incomplete hota hai.
