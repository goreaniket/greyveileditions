Moment becomes personality.

Event becomes definition.

And that is how a story is built.

Quietly.

Repeatedly.

Unnoticed.

At first, this story feels helpful.

Because chaos becomes structured.

Pain becomes explainable.

Life becomes narratable.

And human mind loves narration.

Because narration reduces uncertainty.

Uncertainty feels like danger.

Story feels like control.

But slowly…

something changes.

The story starts running even when life is not happening.

Even when nothing is wrong.

Even when nothing is happening at all.

The mind keeps describing:
