“What if something becomes wrong?”

And this question has no limit.

There is always another “what if”.

Always another layer.

Always another scenario.

So fear never reaches completion.

It only expands.

And slowly, a person starts living cautiously.

Not because danger exists…

but because imagined danger feels real enough.

They hesitate.

They delay.

They overthink.

They avoid.

Not based on reality…

but based on possibility.

And life shrinks.

Not externally.
