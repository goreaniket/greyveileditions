Not emotional.

Not philosophical.

Just immediate.

A moment is no longer “your memory”.

It is just happening.

A feeling is no longer “your pattern”.

It is just arising.

A thought is no longer “your identity”.

It is just appearing.

And in this stripping away of ownership…

something unexpected happens.

Life becomes lighter.

Not because problems disappear.

But because they are no longer being turned into identity.

And in that lightness…

you begin to see:

Every story you told about yourself…

was an interpretation of something that was never fully captured.

Because life was always moving.
