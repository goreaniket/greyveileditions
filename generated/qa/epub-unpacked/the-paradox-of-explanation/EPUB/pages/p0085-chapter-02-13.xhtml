“Why does everything feel the same again and again?”

Aur answer uncomfortable hota hai:

Because reality same nahi hoti.

Lens same hota hai.

Aur shayad sabse disturbing realization yeh hai:

Tum apni life nahi jee rahe.

Tum apni life ki story jee rahe ho.

Aur jab yeh samajh aata hai…

toh pehli baar ek gap open hota hai.

Between event and interpretation.

Between experience and meaning.

Between reality and story.

And that gap…

is not confusion.

That gap is awareness starting for the first time.

Aur shayad yahin se Chapter 2 actually end nahi hota…

yahin se ek naya question start hota hai:
