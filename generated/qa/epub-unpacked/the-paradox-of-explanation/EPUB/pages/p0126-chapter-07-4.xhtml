a discomfort arises.

Not fear exactly.

But unfamiliar emptiness.

A gap without explanation.

And the mind tries to fix it.

It reaches again.

For story.

For reason.

For pattern.

For conclusion.

But sometimes…

nothing fits.

And for the first time…

the mind is forced to remain with the moment…

without owning it.

A sound is heard.

And it does not become a memory.

It remains a sound.

A face is seen.

And it does not become a judgment.

It remains a face.
