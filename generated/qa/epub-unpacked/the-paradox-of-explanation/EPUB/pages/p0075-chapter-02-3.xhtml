“Something is wrong with me.”
“This always happens with me.”

Aur yeh jo meaning hai…

yeh dheere dheere ek event ko rewrite kar deta hai.

Not in reality.

But in perception.

Hum sochte hain hum apni life ko yaad kar rahe hain.

But most of the time, we are not remembering life.

We are reconstructing it.

Memory ek static file nahi hoti.

It is a living system.

Har baar jab hum kisi purani memory ko revisit karte hain…

we don’t retrieve it like a recording.

We rebuild it like a story.
