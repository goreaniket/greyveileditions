But identity was always freezing it.

And what you called “me”…

was mostly a frozen explanation of a moving reality.

And now that freezing loosens…

something deeper becomes visible.

Not a new identity.

But the absence of need for one.

And in that absence…

there is no pressure to define yourself completely.

No pressure to finalize who you are.

No pressure to become a fixed idea.

Because nothing living is ever fully complete as a story.

Only as experience.

And maybe that was the misunderstanding all along:

You were not meant to be finished.

You were meant to be lived.

And in that realization…

you meet yourself again.

Not as a story.

But as something far more immediate.
