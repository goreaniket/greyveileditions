ABOUT THE AUTHOR-

Lalit R Mishra is not trying to be understood.

And maybe that’s exactly why people end up trying to understand him.

He doesn’t speak like someone who is “explaining life.”

He speaks like someone who is still watching it happen in real time.

There is something unusual about his writing

it doesn’t feel like information.

It feels like someone is sitting in your head and pointing out thoughts you usually ignore.

He is not a philosopher.

Not a guru.

Not a motivational voice.

Just someone who noticed a strange pattern:

people don’t suffer because life is complicated…
