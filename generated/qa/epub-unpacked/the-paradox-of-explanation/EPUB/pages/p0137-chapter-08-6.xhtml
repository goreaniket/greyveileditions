This question is not philosophical.

It is existential disruption.

Because for most of life:

Thought = reality

Thought = identity

Thought = decision-maker

Thought = safety system

And now all of that becomes questionable.

You start noticing something unsettling:

Thoughts are not instructions.

They are events.

They come and go without permission.

They contradict each other without shame.

They change depending on mood, memory, fear, environment.

And yet…

you were treating them as truth.

And this realization does not arrive like insight.

It arrives like instability.

Because suddenly…
