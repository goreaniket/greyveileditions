Kyuki har transformation se pehle ek cheez tootni padti hai.

Identity.

Wahi identity jo explanations se bani thi.

Wahi identity jo meanings se bani thi.

Wahi identity jo har failure ko destiny aur har success ko personal greatness samajh kar survive kar rahi thi.

Aur shayad isi liye kai log change nahi karte.

Unhe naya version banne se problem nahi hoti.

Unhe purani kahani chhodne se problem hoti hai.

Kyuki kahaniyan comfortable hoti hain.

Reality nahi.

Reality seedhi hoti hai.

Kabhi kabhi brutal.

Kabhi kabhi silent.

Aur kabhi kabhi woh bas itna kehti hai:

"Yeh punishment nahi tha."
