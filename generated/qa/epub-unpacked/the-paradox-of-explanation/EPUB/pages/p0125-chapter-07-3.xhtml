Still reaches for meaning.

Still tries to label.

Still tries to connect patterns.

But something feels off.

Like the system is running…

but slower than before.

And in that slowness…

something unusual happens.

A moment is not immediately consumed by thought.

It just stays.

Raw.

Unprocessed.

Untranslated.

And this feels almost unnatural.

Because the mind was never trained for “non-interpretation”.

It was trained for survival through meaning.

Meaning = safety.

Meaning = control.

Meaning = predictability.

So when meaning does not appear immediately…
