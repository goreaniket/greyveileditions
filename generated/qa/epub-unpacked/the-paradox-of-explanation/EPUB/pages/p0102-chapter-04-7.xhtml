the illusion cracks.

Because real control was never about stopping events.

It was only about feeling prepared.

But life does not ask for preparedness.

It asks for presence.

And presence and control are opposites.

Control lives in the future.

Presence lives in the now.

Control needs certainty.

Presence exists without it.

And this is the conflict most humans live in:

They are physically in the present…

but mentally trying to manage the future.

So even when nothing is wrong…

the mind is still working.

Planning.

Replaying.

Correcting.

Simulating.

As if silence itself is dangerous.

But silence is not danger.
