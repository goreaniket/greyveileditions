“I don’t do that.”
“I always react this way.”

But truth yeh hai…

hum ek fixed person nahi hote.

Hum situations ke response mein bante hue versions hote hain.

Ek din tum confident hote ho.

Ek din tum silent ho jaate ho.

Ek din tum sab kuch samajh rahe hote ho.

Ek din tum khud ko hi nahi samajh paate.

Aur phir bhi tum kehte ho:

“This is me.”
