And separation feels unnecessary.

So what remains cannot be concluded.

Cannot be finalized.

Cannot be captured.

Only lived.

And in that living…

a final understanding appears…

that is not really an understanding:

You were never meant to become something through life.

You were meant to experience life as it unfolds…

without turning it into a story every second.

And when that pressure dissolves…

nothing dramatic remains.

No ultimate truth.

No final answer.

No philosophical conclusion.

Just life.

Untranslated.

Unclaimed.

Unfinished.
