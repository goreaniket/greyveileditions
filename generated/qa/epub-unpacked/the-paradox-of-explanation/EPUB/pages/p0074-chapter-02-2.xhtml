But every single time we remember them.

Ek moment hota hai.

A silence after an argument.

A message that never comes back.

A reply that feels different from before.

A door that stays closed a little longer than expected.

A person jo suddenly unfamiliar lagne lagta hai.

Outwardly, nothing dramatic happens.

But inwardly, something shifts.

And the mind, almost instantly, fills that silence.

Not with facts…

but with meaning.

“They don’t care anymore.”
“I was never important.”
