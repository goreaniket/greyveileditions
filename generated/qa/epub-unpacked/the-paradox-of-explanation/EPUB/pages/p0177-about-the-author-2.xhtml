And the more you read him…

the more uncomfortable it becomes to pretend you don’t relate.

His writing style is simple, but not soft.

Direct, but not loud.

Deep, but not trying to impress.

It doesn’t tell you what to think.

It makes you notice what you are already thinking.

And somewhere in between reading him…

you don’t feel like you are reading a book anymore.

You feel like you are being observed by your own thoughts.

He is not offering answers.

He is exposing the habit of chasing them.

And if you ever feel like talking to him after reading this…

that is not curiosity.

That is recognition.
