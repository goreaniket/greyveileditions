So something shifts.

Not externally.

But internally.

The need to obey thoughts starts weakening.

Not because thoughts stop.

But because obedience is no longer automatic.

And this is where the separation begins.

Between:

thoughts that appear

and awareness that notices them appearing

At first, this separation feels strange.

Almost unreal.

Because earlier, everything was fused.

“I think” was “I am”.

But now…

“I think” is just an event.

Not identity.

Not truth.

Not command.

And in this shift…

a silent space begins forming.
