“This is enlightenment.”
“This is clarity.”

But even these labels feel heavy.

Because they try to freeze something that is not frozen.

So even labeling starts falling away.

And what remains is extremely simple:

life is happening…

without being constantly converted into meaning.

And this simplicity is not dramatic.

It is not emotional.

It is not extraordinary.

It is almost too ordinary to notice.

And that is the point.
