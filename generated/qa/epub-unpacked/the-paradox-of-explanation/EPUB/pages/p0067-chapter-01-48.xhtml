Pain expose karta hai.

Isi liye kai log life ke sabse painful periods ko yaad karte hue kehte hain:

"Woh mera sabse difficult phase tha."

Aur kuch der baad wahi log kehte hain:

"Wahi phase tha jahan maine khud ko pehli baar dekha."

Kyun?

Kyuki pain insaan ko special nahi banata.

Pain insaan ko honest bana deta hai.

Aur honesty hamesha comfortable nahi hoti.

Kabhi kabhi honesty ka matlab hota hai yeh accept karna ki tum utne strong nahi the jitna tum sochte the.

Kabhi kabhi honesty ka matlab hota hai yeh accept karna ki tum galat the.
