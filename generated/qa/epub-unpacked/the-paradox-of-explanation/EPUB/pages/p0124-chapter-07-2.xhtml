A strange mental fatigue where even questions start losing urgency.

Earlier, everything demanded explanation.

A delay had meaning.

A silence had interpretation.

A reaction had psychology.

A failure had lesson.

A success had validation.

A person had intention.

A moment had hidden message.

Nothing was allowed to simply exist.

Everything had to become something else.

But slowly…

a subtle shift begins.

Not in reality.

In response to reality.

There is a gap.

Between what happens…

and what the mind does with it.

A small delay in interpretation.

At first, the mind still tries.
