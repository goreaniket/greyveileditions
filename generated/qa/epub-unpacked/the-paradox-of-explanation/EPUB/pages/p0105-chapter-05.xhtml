THE PARADOX OF EXPLANATION

THE FEAR OF WHAT HAS NOT HAPPENED

There is a strange kind of suffering human beings create.

It does not come from reality.

It comes from imagination.

Not imagination in a creative sense…

but imagination in a predictive sense.

Most people are not living their present.

They are living possible futures that have not happened yet.

And most of those futures…

will never happen.

But the mind does not care about probability.
