Aur har reconstruction mein thoda sa change hota hai.

Kuch parts highlight ho jaate hain.

Kuch fade ho jaate hain.

Aur kuch completely naye meanings ke saath rewrite ho jaate hain.

Not because we are lying.

But because raw reality ko carry karna uncomfortable hota hai.

Hum closure dhoondhte hain.

Even when none exists.

First illusion:

We believe we are recalling life.

But actually, we are rewriting it.

Ek rejection hota hai.

Technically simple.

A “no”.

Bas itna.

But mind us “no” ko simple nahi rehne deta.

Woh usse expand karta hai.
