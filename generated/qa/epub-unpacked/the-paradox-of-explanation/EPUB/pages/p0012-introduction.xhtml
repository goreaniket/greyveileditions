INTRODUCTION-

YOU ARE NOT WHAT YOU THINK YOU ARE

There is a point in human life…

jab tum sochna start nahi karte…

tum soch ke andar rehna start kar dete ho.

Aur sabse dangerous baat yeh hoti hai…

tumhe yeh difference kabhi pata hi nahi chalta.

Tum bas ek din uthte ho…

aur tum already ek story ke andar jee rahe hote ho.

Not life.

But meaning of life.

Aur woh story kisi ek bade moment se nahi banti.

Woh chhoti chhoti cheezon se banti hai:

ek rejection…

ek failure…

ek delay…

ek silence…
