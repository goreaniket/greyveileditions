Not judging yourself.

Not explaining yourself.

Not narrating yourself.

And still…

you are here.

Breathing.

Aware.

Present.

Without a sentence running in the background.

And this creates a shock that is not emotional…

but existential.

Because if you are not the story running in your mind…

then what exactly are you?

The mind immediately tries to answer.

It produces labels:

personality

soul

identity

past

nature
