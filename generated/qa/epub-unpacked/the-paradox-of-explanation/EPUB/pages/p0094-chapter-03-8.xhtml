Kyuki definition ruk jaati hai.

Lekin life nahi rukti.

Aur jab definition kam hoti hai…

tab experience zyada visible hota hai.

Aur yahi se ek shift start hota hai:

From “Who am I?”

to

“What is happening inside me right now?”

Aur yeh shift chhota nahi hota.

Yeh identity ka gravity break karta hai.

Aur shayad yahi is chapter ka end nahi hai…

yahi ek doorway hai.

Aise space ka jahan tum apne baare mein sochna kam karte ho…

aur apne hone ko notice karna start karte ho.
