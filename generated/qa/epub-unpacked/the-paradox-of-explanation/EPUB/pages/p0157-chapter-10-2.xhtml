And for most of life…

we live inside these layers without noticing them.

We don’t just live events.

We translate them.

We don’t just feel pain.

We explain it.

We don’t just experience life.

We narrate it.

And slowly…

this narration becomes so continuous…

that silence starts feeling unusual.

Because silence is not empty.

Silence is just unprocessed reality.

And the mind is addicted to processing.

So the mind keeps running:

What does this mean?

Why is this happening?

What should I learn from this?

What is the purpose?

What is my role in this?

And even suffering becomes structured into meaning.
