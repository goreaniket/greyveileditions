there is a difference between:

living experience

and explaining experience

And it becomes visible that most of life was not lived…

it was processed.

And this realization does not create excitement.

It creates stillness.

A very unfamiliar stillness.

Because nothing is being extracted from the moment anymore.

No meaning.

No lesson.

No insight being forced.

And in that stillness…

something breaks quietly.

Not identity.

Not memory.

But the need to constantly convert life into understanding.

And when that pressure reduces…
