mind ek last baar poochta hai:

“Toh main hoon kaun?”

Lekin ab woh question bhi different lagta hai.

Less urgent.

Less solid.

Less desperate.

Aur tum notice karne lagte ho:

jo question pooch raha tha…

woh question se alag nahi tha.

Woh bhi same movement ka part tha.

Same loop.

Same search.

Same need to define.

Aur ab…

woh structure poori tarah gayab nahi hua hai…

lekin uski grip weak ho gayi hai.

Aur us gap mein…

ek simple sa realization bachta hai:
