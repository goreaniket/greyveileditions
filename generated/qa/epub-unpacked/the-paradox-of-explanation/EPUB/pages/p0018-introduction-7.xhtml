Kyuki shayad problem yeh nahi hai ki tumhe answers nahi milte.

Problem yeh hai ki…

tum har cheez ko answer bana dete ho.

Aur agar yeh habit ek second ke liye bhi toot jaaye…

toh life pehli baar direct lagti hai.

Unfiltered.

Unclaimed.

Uninterpreted.

Aur us moment mein…

tumhe koi nayi cheez nahi milti.

Tumhe sirf yeh dikh jaata hai:

tum kabhi lost the hi nahi

tum sirf interpretation ke andar busy the.
