Familiar.

But now something breaks.

Thoughts are still coming…

but authority is gone.

And for the first time…

you notice something uncomfortable:

You have been following something that was never consistent.

Every decision in life was built on thought.

Every fear came from thought.

Every expectation came from thought.

Every identity was maintained by thought.

Even your sense of “I” was built from thought continuity.

And now that continuity starts cracking.

Not in a dramatic way.

But in small interruptions.

Small doubts inside certainty.

Small pauses inside reaction.

Small gaps between mental sentences.

And these gaps are new.
