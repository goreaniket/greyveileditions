YOU WERE NEVER LOST

If you have reached here…

don’t rush to close this book.

Not because something important is about to be said…

but because nothing new actually needs to be said anymore.

There was never a real “problem” to solve.

There was only a continuous process happening inside you:

experience → interpretation → repetition → identity

And you called that process life.

But here is the uncomfortable part:

You were never inside life as clearly as you believed.

You were inside your explanation of life.

And that explanation was running so fast…

that reality never got a chance to appear without it.

That is why everything felt heavier than it actually was.

Not because life was heavy…
