Very quietly.

Not belief.

Not identity.

But the automatic need to explain everything.

For a brief moment…

something is experienced without translation.

A sound is just a sound.

A moment is just a moment.

A feeling is just a feeling.

No meaning.

No story.

No conclusion.

And the mind does not know how to respond.

Because it cannot categorize it.

It cannot complete it.

It cannot control it.

And that inability…

creates space.

A space where experience exists before thought.

Before meaning.

Before interpretation.
