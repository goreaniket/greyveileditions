Sometimes uncomfortably simple.

No final answer was ever hidden here.

Because nothing was missing in the first place.

Only attention was elsewhere.

And now…

even this realization does not need to become a memory.

Because memory becomes story again.

And story becomes identity again.

So let it stay light.

Without ownership.

Without conclusion.

Without meaning overload.

Just this:

You are still here…

but slightly less inside the interpretation than before.
