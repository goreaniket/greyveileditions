Agar main fixed nahi hoon…

toh main hoon kya?

Yeh question scary lagta hai.

Kyuki insaan ko structure chahiye hota hai.

Identity structure deti hai.

Even if it is false.

Isliye hum apne upar stories chipka dete hain:

I am like this

I am not like that

I can’t do this

I always do that

Aur har baar jab life contradict karti hai…

hum ya toh ignore kar dete hain…

ya explanation bana dete hain.

Aur slowly ek cheez aur hoti hai:

We stop observing ourselves.

And start protecting a version of ourselves.

Self-awareness ka illusion yeh hai:

Hum sochte hain hum khud ko samajh rahe hain.

But actually hum ek image ko maintain kar rahe hote hain.
