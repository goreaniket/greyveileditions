hum apni stories ko protect karte hain…

even when they are limiting us.

Because agar story toot gayi…

toh lagta hai “main” bhi toot jaunga.

Aur yeh belief itna deep hota hai ki:

past tumhe define karta hai

memory tumhe shape karti hai

experience tumhe identity deta hai

Lekin kabhi kabhi…

ek gap aata hai.

Very small.

Almost invisible.

Jahan koi thought nahi hota.

Koi identity active nahi hoti.

Koi explanation running nahi hota.

Bas awareness hoti hai.

Silent.

Unclaimed.

Unlabeled.

Aur woh awareness kisi cheez ko define nahi karti.

Woh sirf notice karti hai.
