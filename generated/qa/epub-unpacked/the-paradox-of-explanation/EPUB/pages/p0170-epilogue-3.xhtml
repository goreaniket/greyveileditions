life kabhi missing nahi thi.

sirf interpretation usse door bana rahi thi.

Aur jab interpretation thodi kam hoti hai…

toh kuch “naya” nahi banta.

Sab kuch direct ho jaata hai.

thought sirf thought hota hai

emotion sirf emotion hota hai

moment sirf moment hota hai

Bina meaning ke.

Bina ownership ke.

Bina story ke.

Aur yeh cheez hold nahi ki ja sakti.

Kyuki jaise hi tum isse pakadne ki koshish karte ho…

yeh phir se idea ban jaati hai.

Isliye yeh sirf ek direction reh jaati hai:

kuch bhi fix karne ki zarurat nahi thi

sirf dekhne ki zarurat thi

Aur shayad yahi poora point tha:

khud ko badalne ka nahi…

sirf yeh dekhne ka…

ki tum sirf apni story nahi ho.
