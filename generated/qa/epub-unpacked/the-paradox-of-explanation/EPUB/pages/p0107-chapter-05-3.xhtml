Nothing has happened.

But internally, everything has already collapsed.

This is not sensitivity.

This is prediction addiction.

Human mind is built to scan for danger.

But modern life has no clear predators.

So the mind starts inventing them.

In relationships.

In career.

In self-image.

In future outcomes.

And slowly, silence becomes threatening.

Because silence leaves space.

And space allows imagination.

And imagination usually turns negative faster than it turns positive.

A person lies in bed at night.

Nothing is happening.

But internally, everything is happening.

Conversations are being replayed.

Mistakes are being re-analyzed.
