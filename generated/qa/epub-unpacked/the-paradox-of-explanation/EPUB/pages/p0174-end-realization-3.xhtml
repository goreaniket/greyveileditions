Nothing here is asking you to believe anything.

Belief was never the point.

Because belief is just another form of interpretation.

And interpretation is exactly what this book was quietly pointing toward all along.

Not to destroy it.

But to make you see it.

Because once you see it clearly…

you cannot fully unsee it again.

And maybe that is the only “ending” this book can offer:

Not a conclusion.

Not a solution.

Not a transformation.

Just a quieter relationship with your own thinking.

And if that feels small…

that is only because the mind is used to dramatic endings.

Reality is not dramatic.

It is simple.
