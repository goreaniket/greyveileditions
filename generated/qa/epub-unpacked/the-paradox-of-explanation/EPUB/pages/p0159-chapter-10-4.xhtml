It is simply:

things are happening…

without being converted into a story immediately.

And this is where the real shift begins.

Because for the first time…

there is experience without interpretation.

And this creates confusion.

Because the mind says:

“If I am not interpreting, then what am I doing?”

And the answer is uncomfortable:

Nothing.

Just witnessing.

And even “witnessing” is not a correct word.

Because it still implies separation.

What remains is simpler:

things are appearing.

and something is aware of them.
