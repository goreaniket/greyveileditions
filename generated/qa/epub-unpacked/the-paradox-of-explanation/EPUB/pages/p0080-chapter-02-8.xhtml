Life predictable lagne lagti hai.

But life repeat nahi ho rahi hoti.

Hum repeat kar rahe hote hain apni interpretation.

Aur jab interpretation strong ho jaati hai…

toh ek aur shift hota hai.

Question change ho jaata hai.

Pehle:

“What happened?”

Phir:

“Why did this happen?”

Aur phir finally:

“Why does this always happen to me?”
