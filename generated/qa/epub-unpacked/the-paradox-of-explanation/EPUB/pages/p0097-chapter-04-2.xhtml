And slowly, control becomes a need.

Not a skill.

A need.

We start controlling small things first.

Messages.

Reactions.

Tone of voice.

Timing.

Plans.

Future scenarios in our head.

We rehearse conversations that haven’t even happened yet.

We prepare responses for situations that don’t even exist yet.

And we call it planning.

But often, it is not planning.

It is fear wearing the mask of preparation.

Because somewhere deep inside…

there is discomfort with uncertainty.

Uncertainty feels like loss of grip.

And the mind hates anything it cannot hold.
