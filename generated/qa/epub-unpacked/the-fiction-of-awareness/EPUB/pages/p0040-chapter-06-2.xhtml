Ye “self-story” actually kabhi directly exist nahi karti.

Ye sirf moments ke beech ka interpretation hota hai.

Har moment apne aap complete hota hai.

Uske andar koi “unfinished identity” nahi hoti.

Lekin mind un moments ko connect karta rehta hai…

aur un connections ko ek single flow bana deta hai.

Aur jab ye flow strong ho jata hai…

toh lagta hai jaise ek consistent “someone” exist kar raha ho.

But agar attention thoda aur direct ho jaye…

toh ye flow tootne lagta hai.

Na past fully present hota hai.

Na future actually accessible hota hai.

Na present koi narrative carry karta hai.

Sirf isolated appearances hoti hain.

Aur in appearances ke beech jo “bridge” hota hai…

wo actually real nahi hota.

Wo constructed continuity hoti hai.

Self ka poora structure isi bridge pe khada hota hai.

Isliye jab ye bridge visible ho jata hai…
