CHAPTER 1

The Illusion of a Fixed “I”

Hum ek aisi cheez ke saath jeete hain jisko hum kabhi directly observe nahi karte…

phir bhi uske bina hum kisi experience ko imagine nahi kar sakte.

Wo cheez hai — “I”.

Ye “I” kabhi experience ke andar ek object ki tarah present nahi hota.

Agar tum dhyaan se dekho, toh na thoughts ke andar koi “I” milta hai…

na emotions ke andar…

na memories ke andar…
