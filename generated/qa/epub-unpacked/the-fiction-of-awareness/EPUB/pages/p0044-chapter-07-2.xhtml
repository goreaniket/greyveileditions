Na koi location.

Na koi fixed identity.

Sirf awareness jaisa “presentness” hota hai…

lekin usko bhi kisi entity mein convert nahi kiya ja sakta.

Aur yahin pe final illusion break hota hai:

“there is someone who is aware”

Lekin awareness ke andar “someone” kabhi found nahi hota.

Sirf awareness hoti hai.

Without owner.

Without center.

Without name.

Thoughts aate hain…

par koi fixed thinker nahi milta.

Memory appear hoti hai…

par koi permanent holder nahi milta.

Experience unfold hota hai…

par koi central experiencer locate nahi hota.

Aur phir ek aur layer dissolve hoti hai:

ye idea ki “I am the witness”.
