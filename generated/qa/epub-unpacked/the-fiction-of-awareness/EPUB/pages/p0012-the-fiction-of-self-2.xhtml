jo decisions leti hai

jo memories rakhti hai

jo future plan karti hai

Lekin agar direct experience ko dekha jaye…

toh koi ek fixed center kabhi directly milta hi nahi.

Sirf moments hote hain.

Thoughts hote hain.

Feelings hote hain.

Memories hoti hain.

Aur in sab ke beech ek invisible story chal rahi hoti hai:

“ye sab mera hai”

Aur yahin pe fiction start hoti hai.

Ye book koi belief change karne ke liye nahi hai.

Na ye kehta hai ki “self exists nahi karta”

Na ye kehta hai ki “self ek illusion hai” in a philosophical slogan sense.

Ye sirf ek cheez dikhata hai:

kaise ek “center” continuously banaya ja raha hai…

without ever being found.

Har chapter ek layer remove karta hai…

lekin koi violence nahi hoti.
