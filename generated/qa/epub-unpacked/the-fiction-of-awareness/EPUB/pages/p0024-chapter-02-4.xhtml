reconstructed past

Aur dono milke ek continuous identity create karte hain.

Lekin ye continuity real continuity nahi hoti.

Ye stitched continuity hoti hai.

Aur stitching itni smooth hoti hai…

ki mind usko truth maan leta hai.

But truth simple hai:

Memory proof nahi hai ki “you existed”

Memory sirf ye prove karti hai ki narration continuously chal raha hai.

Aur narration hi identity ban jata hai.

Isliye “my past” actually past nahi hota…

wo present ka reconstructed story hota hai.

Aur ye story kabhi stop nahi hoti…

kyuki har moment naye reconstruction ka base ban jata hai.

Aur jab ye cycle visible hota hai…

toh ek strange realization aata hai:

jo “me” lagta hai…
