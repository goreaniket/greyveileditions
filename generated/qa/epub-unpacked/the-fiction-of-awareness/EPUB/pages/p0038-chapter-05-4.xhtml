future suddenly motivation nahi lagta…

wo ek illusionary distance lagta hai.

Aur present pehle se complete nahi banta…

wo bas less distorted lagne lagta hai.

Becoming yahin dissolve hota hai:

jab future ko destination nahi…

sirf thought projection maana jaye.

Aur tab sirf ek simple reality bachi rehti hai:

jo abhi hai… wo already happening hai.

without delay.

without upgrade requirement.
