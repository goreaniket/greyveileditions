toh self ka idea unstable lagne lagta hai.

Lekin interesting baat ye hai:

kuch “collapse” nahi hota physically.

Sirf ek belief weaken hota hai.

Belief that:

“there is someone living this life”

Aur jab ye belief thoda loose hota hai…

toh experience change nahi hota…

sirf interpretation change hoti hai.

Same thoughts.

Same sensations.

Same world.

But ownership ka weight kam ho jata hai.

Aur isi state mein ek strange clarity appear hoti hai:

cheezon ko chalane ke liye kisi “center” ki zarurat nahi hoti.

Self-story collapse ka matlab disappearance nahi hai.

Matlab hai:

story ko literal truth na maanna.

Aur jab story literal truth nahi lagti…
