Isliye “I” kabhi questioned nahi hota.

Kyuki wo experience ke beech mein nahi hai…

wo experience ke upar overlay ki tarah chal raha hota hai.

Agar tum direct attention se dekho…

toh ek strange thing samajh aati hai:

“I” never appears before experience.

It always appears after experience.

As a commentary.

As a conclusion.

Phir bhi hum us commentary ko origin maan lete hain.

Aur isi misunderstanding se ek aur layer build hoti hai:

“I am the one experiencing.”

Lekin agar deeply dekha jaye…

toh experience pehle aata hai…

aur “I” baad mein interpret kiya jata hai.

Matlab:

experience independent hai

but identity dependent hai

Aur jo cheez dependent hoti hai…
