PROLOGUE

PROLOGUE:

Shuruaat hamesha ek story se nahi hoti…

kabhi-kabhi shuruaat ek assumption se hoti hai.

Aur yahan bhi wahi hua.

Ek assumption silently already present thi:

“Main yahan hoon.”

Is assumption ko kabhi prove nahi kiya gaya…

kyuki isko prove karne ki zarurat kabhi feel hi nahi hui.

Ye bas felt as obvious.

Aur isi “obviousness” ke andar pura human experience build ho gaya.

Thoughts aaye…

aur unko “main soch raha hoon” bana diya gaya.

Feelings aaye…

aur unko “main feel kar raha hoon” bana diya gaya.

Memories aayi…
