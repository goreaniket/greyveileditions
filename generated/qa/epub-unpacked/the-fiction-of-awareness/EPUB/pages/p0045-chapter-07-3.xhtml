Kyuki witness bhi ek subtle identity ban sakti hai.

Ek refined ego form.

Lekin jab observation aur deep hota hai…

toh even witness ko hold karne wala koi point nahi milta.

Sirf experiencing remains.

Without labeling.

Without separation.

Na “inside”

Na “outside”

Bas appearance.

Aur ye appearance kisi ko show nahi ho rahi hoti…

wo bas ho rahi hoti hai.

Is stage pe language apni limit dikha deti hai…

kyuki language hamesha “someone” assume karti hai.

Lekin yahan koi “someone” support nahi karta.

Aur jab ye clear hota hai…

toh ek strange simplicity bachi rehti hai:

Na self.

Na witness.

Na story.
