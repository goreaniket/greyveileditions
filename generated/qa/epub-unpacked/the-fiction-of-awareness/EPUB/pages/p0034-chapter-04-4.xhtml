Lekin agar deeply dekha jaye…

toh ye “hidden purpose” bhi mind ka creation hai.

Reality ko purpose ki zarurat nahi hoti.

Sirf mind ko hoti hai.

Aur jab ye samajh aata hai…

toh ek uncomfortable simplicity bachi rehti hai:

cheezon ka hona…

bina explanation ke bhi ho raha hai.

Meaning wahan se collapse hota hai…

jahan cheezon ko explain karne ki necessity hi dissolve ho jati hai.

Aur tab ek shift aata hai:

From “Why is this happening to me?”

To simply…

“This is happening.”
