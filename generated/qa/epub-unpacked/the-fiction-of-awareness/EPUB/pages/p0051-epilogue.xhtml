EPILOGUE :

Agar tumne is book ko kisi conclusion ki tarah padha hai…

toh ek subtle misunderstanding reh jaati hai.

Kyuki yahan koi conclusion tha hi nahi.

Jo kuch bhi chapters mein hua…

wo ek dismantling process jaisa lag sakta hai…

lekin asal mein kuch dismantle nahi hua.

Sirf ek cheez visible hui:

jo “center” lagta tha…

wo kabhi locate hi nahi hua.

Na andar.

Na bahar.

Na thoughts mein.

Na silence mein.

Sirf experience chal raha tha.

Aur us experience ke saath ek story attach ho rahi thi:
