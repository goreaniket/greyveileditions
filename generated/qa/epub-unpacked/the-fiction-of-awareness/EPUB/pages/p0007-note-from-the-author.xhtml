INTRODUCTION

NOTE FROM THE AUTHOR

This book is not written to convince you of anything.

It is not a philosophy to adopt, nor a belief system to follow.

It is an invitation to look at something very ordinary…

but rarely observed clearly:

the sense of “I” that sits behind every experience.

Most of what we call life is already filtered through interpretation.

Thoughts are immediately labeled.

Memories are immediately owned.

Feelings are immediately personalized.

And in that instant, a story begins:

“This is happening to me.”

The Human Fiction does not try to change your experience.

It only points toward the structure through which experience becomes personal.
