wo kabhi original source nahi ho sakti.

Phir bhi mind us dependent structure ko center bana deta hai…

aur us center ke around life organize hone lagti hai.

Is organization ke bina hum scattered feel karte…

isliye mind ek false continuity create karta hai.

Aur ye continuity hi “self” ban jati hai.

Important baat ye hai:

Ye self kabhi fully formed nahi hota.

Ye har moment recreate hota hai…

har new experience ke saath.

Without exception.

Isliye jo hum “me” kehte hain…

wo ek fixed object nahi hai.

Wo ek ongoing construction hai.

Jo kabhi complete nahi hota…

sirf continue hota hai.

Aur kyuki ye continue hota rehta hai…

hum usko permanent maan lete hain.

Lekin permanence yahan illusion hai.

Continuity ka effect hai.
