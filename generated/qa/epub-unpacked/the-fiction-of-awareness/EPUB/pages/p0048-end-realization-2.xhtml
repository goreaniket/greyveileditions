And when interpretation is seen clearly…

it stops being mistaken for reality.

Nothing needs to disappear for clarity to exist.

No thought needs to stop.

No memory needs to be erased.

No experience needs to change.

Only the unnecessary claim of ownership loses its weight.

What remains is not an altered state…

but a simpler recognition:

experience is happening, without requiring a center.

No observer outside it.

No controller behind it.

No permanent identity within it.

Just movement.

Just appearance.

Just unfolding.

And even this description is only a pointer…

because what is being indicated cannot be held by language.

So there is no final conclusion here.

Only a quiet seeing:

that what was always searched for as “I” was never missing…
