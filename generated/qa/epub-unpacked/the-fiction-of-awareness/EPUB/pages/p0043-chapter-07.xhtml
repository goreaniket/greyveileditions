CHAPTER 7

The Witness Without Identity

Agar Chapter 6 mein self-story collapse hoti hui dikhi thi…

toh ab ek aur deeper shift aata hai:

jo “collapse dekh raha tha” — uska bhi koi fixed form nahi milta.

Hum aksar sochte hain ki ek “witness” hoga…

jo thoughts ko dekh raha hai…

emotions ko observe kar raha hai…

aur life ko experience kar raha hai.

Lekin jab is witness ko directly search kiya jaye…

toh ek strange silence milta hai.

Na koi shape.
