CHAPTER 3

The Fiction of the Inner Voice

Hum sabko lagta hai ki andar ek “voice” chal rahi hoti hai.

Jo continuously comment karti rehti hai

Sochti hai.

Judge karti hai.

Explain karti hai.

Lekin agar carefully dekha jaye…

toh ye “voice” koi actual voice nahi hoti.

Na sound hota hai

Na speaker hota hai.

Na clear origin hota hai

Ye sirf ek thought stream hoti hai…
