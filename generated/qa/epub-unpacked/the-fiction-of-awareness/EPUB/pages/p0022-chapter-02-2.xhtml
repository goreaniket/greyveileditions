Aur jab bhi koi memory aati hai…

wo kabhi “pure past” nahi hoti.

Wo hamesha ek present interpretation hoti hai.

Aur usi instant pe mind ek cheez add karta hai:

“ye mere saath hua tha”

Lekin interesting baat ye hai:

memory khud kabhi ownership claim nahi karti.

Ownership baad mein attach hota hai.

Automatically.

Memory ke andar sirf fragments hote hain:

image

feeling

sensation

scene

Bas.

Lekin mind un fragments ko jod deta hai…

aur ek story bana deta hai:

“ye mera past hai”

Aur yahi se “personal history” start hoti hai.

Lekin agar deeply dekha jaye…

toh koi “past self” directly available nahi hota.
