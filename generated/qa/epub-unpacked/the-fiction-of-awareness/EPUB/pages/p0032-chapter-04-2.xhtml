iska matlab kya hai

ye mere liye kya signal hai

Lekin situation khud kuch nahi kehti.

Wo sirf event hoti hai.

Neutral.

Raw.

Meaning ek overlay hai.

Ek filter jo experience ke upar lag jata hai.

Aur interesting baat ye hai:

hum meaning ko truth samajh lete hain…

jabki wo sirf interpretation hota hai.

Har cheez ko hum kisi story mein fit karna chahte hain.

Agar kuch painful hai…

toh uska meaning dhundhte hain.

Agar kuch good hai…

toh uska purpose assign karte hain.

But reality doesn’t respond to meaning.

Reality simply unfolds.

Meaning ka sabse strong illusion ye hai:

“If I understand why, I will feel better.”
