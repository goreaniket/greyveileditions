aur unko “mera past” bana diya gaya.

Aur bina kisi notice ke…

ek invisible center create ho gaya:

“I”

Lekin interesting baat ye hai:

ye center kabhi directly dekha nahi gaya.

Ye sirf assume kiya gaya.

Aur jab koi cheez continuously assume ki jaye…

toh wo reality jaisi lagne lagti hai.

The Human Fiction yahin se start nahi hoti…

ye yahin se notice hoti hai.

Aur ab jo aage aane wala hai…

wo koi new truth nahi hai.

Wo sirf ek structure ko dheere-dheere visible hote hue dekhna hai…

jo hamesha already chal raha tha.
