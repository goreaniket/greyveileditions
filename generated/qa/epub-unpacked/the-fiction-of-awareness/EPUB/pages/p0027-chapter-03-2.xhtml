jo language ke form mein appear hoti hai.

Aur mind usko “inner voice” bana deta hai.

Aur yahin pe ek subtle illusion start hota hai:

«“Main soch raha hoon”»

Lekin reality mein aksar aisa hota hai ki:

soch ho rahi hoti hai…

aur uske baad “main” attach hota hai

Inner voice pehle nahi hota.

Wo baad mein recognized hota hai.

Jaise hi koi thought aata hai…

mind turant usko label karta hai

- ye mera thought hai

- main ye soch raha hoon

- main decide kar raha hoon

Lekin agar thoda gap observe karo…

toh ek strange thing dikhegi:

thought apne aap arise ho raha hota hai

Uske pehle koi announcement nahi hoti.

Na permission.

Na intention.
