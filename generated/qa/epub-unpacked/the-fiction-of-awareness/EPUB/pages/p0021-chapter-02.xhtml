CHAPTER 2

The Illusion of Memory Ownership

Agar Chapter 1 ne ye show kiya ki “I” koi fixed cheez nahi hai…

toh Chapter 2 ye batata hai ki memory us illusion ko kaise alive rakhti hai.

Hum generally memory ko ek storage system samajhte hain…

jaise brain mein past store ho raha ho.

Lekin lived experience mein memory aisa behave nahi karti.

Memory past ko “retrieve” nahi karti…

wo present moment mein past ka reconstruction karti hai.
