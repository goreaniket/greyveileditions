Koi force nahi hota.

Sirf clarity hoti hai.

Jaise-jaise book aage badhti hai:

thought ka narrator dissolve hota hai

memory ka ownership structure visible hota hai

inner voice ka illusion breakdown hota hai

meaning ek interpretation system lagne lagta hai

becoming ek endless projection ban jata hai

aur even “witness” bhi identity ban jata hai

Aur finally…

jo bachta hai…

wo koi “answer” nahi hota.

Wo sirf ek strange simplicity hoti hai:

experience ho raha hai…

without asking “kiske liye”.

Is book ka aim tumhe kuch naya dena nahi hai.

Iska aim hai:

jo already chal raha hai usko dekhne ki clarity dena.

Kyuki jab koi structure clearly dekh liya jata hai…

toh wo apni authority quietly lose kar deta hai.

Aur tab ek uncomfortable but honest realization aata hai:

9
