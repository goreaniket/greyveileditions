mind ek aur version create kar deta hai.

Isliye becoming kabhi complete nahi hota.

Kyuki wo completion pe based hi nahi hai.

Wo deferral pe based hai.

Har achievement ke baad…

ek naya “next” create ho jata hai.

Aur identity us “next” ke around re-assemble hoti rehti hai.

So life ek journey nahi hoti…

life ek continuous postponement ban jati hai.

Aur ye postponement hi identity ko alive rakhta hai.

Agar koi instant completeness feel ho jaye…

toh becoming collapse ho jata hai.

Aur mind us collapse ko tolerate nahi karta.

Isliye wo turant ek new lack create kar deta hai.

Becoming ka sabse deep fiction ye hai:

“I am not enough yet, but I will become enough.”

Lekin “enough” kabhi define hi nahi hota.

Isliye arrival kabhi possible nahi hota.

Aur jab ye structure clearly dikhta hai…

toh ek strange shift hota hai:
