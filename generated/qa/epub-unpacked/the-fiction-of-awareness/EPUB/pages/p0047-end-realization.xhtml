ENDING

END REALIZATION

If you have reached this point…

there is nothing new left to add.

Because everything this book pointed toward was never hidden in words.

It was always already present in experience itself.

The entire structure of “self” — thoughts, memories, meaning, inner voice, becoming — was never something separate from awareness.

It was awareness interpreted through narrative.

And narrative always creates a center.

A “someone.”

A “me.”

A continuity that feels real only because it is continuously assumed.

But assumptions are not truths.

They are habits of interpretation.
