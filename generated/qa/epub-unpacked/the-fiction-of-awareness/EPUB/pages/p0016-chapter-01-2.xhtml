Phir bhi har experience ke saath ek invisible center feel hota hai.

Jaise har cheez kisi ek point se belong kar rahi ho.

Lekin wo point kabhi dikhai nahi deta.

Sirf assume kiya jata hai.

Aur yahin se fiction start hoti hai.

Har moment jo experience hota hai, uske saath mind ek silent operation karta hai:

wo experience ko label karta hai.

Ek thought aata hai…

aur turant uske upar ownership attach ho jati hai:

“main soch raha hoon.”

Lekin agar sirf thought ko dekha jaye…

toh wo bas ek movement hai.

Us movement ke andar koi signature nahi hota.

Na “mine”, na “not mine”.

Phir ek memory aati hai…

aur wo memory ek personal timeline bana deti hai:

“ye mere saath hua tha.”

But memory khud kabhi ye claim nahi karti.

Claim mind add karta hai.
