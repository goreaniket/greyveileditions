Har present moment ke saath mind ek parallel image banata hai:

“jo main hona chahiye tha.”

“jo main ho sakta hoon.”

“jo main ban raha hoon.”

Lekin ye image kabhi present reality mein exist nahi karti.

Ye sirf imagination mein exist karti hai.

Aur interesting baat ye hai:

hum present ko live nahi karte…

hum present ko compare karte rehte hain.

Future ke against.

Is comparison se ek gap create hota hai:

“main abhi kaafi nahi hoon.”

Aur isi gap ko hum growth samajh lete hain.

Lekin growth aksar reality change nahi karti…

wo sirf dissatisfaction ko organize karti hai.

Becoming ka illusion ye hai:

“Ek final version exist karta hai jahan main finally okay ho jaunga.”

Lekin jaise hi wo version imagine hota hai…
