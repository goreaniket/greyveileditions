Lekin aksar understanding pain ko dissolve nahi karti.

Wo sirf usko structure deti hai.

Pain raw hota hai…

meaning usko organized bana deta hai.

But organized pain still pain hi hota hai.

Mind ko uncertainty intolerable lagti hai.

Isliye wo har cheez ko explainable banana chahta hai.

Aur jab explanation mil jata hai…

toh ek temporary relief milta hai:

“ab samajh aaya”

Lekin wo understanding reality ko change nahi karti.

Sirf perception ko settle karti hai.

Meaning ek coping mechanism ban jata hai.

Jo chaos ko story mein convert karta hai.

Aur story hamesha comfortable hoti hai…

chaos se zyada.

Isliye hum truth nahi chahte…

hum meaning chahte hain.

Aur isi desire se ek aur illusion banta hai:

“Har cheez ka ek hidden purpose hota hai”
