“maine socha”

Is sense ko hi hum “inner voice” samajh lete hain.

Lekin ye voice actually continuous narration hai…

jo automatically generate hoti rehti hai.

Aur kyuki ye narration language mein hoti hai…

toh lagta hai jaise koi “someone” bol raha ho.

But agar deeply dekha jaye:

- koi speaker locate nahi hota

- koi origin point nahi milta

- sirf thoughts appear hote hain

Inner voice ka sabse strong illusion ye hai:

«it feels like control center of self»

Lekin control center khud ek output hai.

Source nahi

Thoughts aate hain…

aur un thoughts ke upar ek additional layer attach hoti hai:

“main ye dekh raha hoon”

Aur us second layer ko hum awareness samajh lete hain.
