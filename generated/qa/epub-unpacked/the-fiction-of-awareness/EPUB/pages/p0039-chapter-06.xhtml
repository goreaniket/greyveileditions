CHAPTER 6

The Collapse of the Self-Story

Agar pichle chapters mein self ek continuous story ki tarah dikh raha tha…

toh ab ek aur layer samajh aati hai:

ye story utni solid nahi hai jitni lagti hai.

Self ka pura structure ek narrative stitching par based tha:

memory se past

thought se present

imagination se future

aur “I” se continuity

Lekin jab in sab ko ek saath dekha jaye…

toh ek strange gap appear hota hai.
