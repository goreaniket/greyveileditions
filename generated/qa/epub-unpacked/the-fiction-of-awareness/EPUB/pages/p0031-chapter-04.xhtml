CHAPTER 4

The Fiction of Meaning

Hum apni life ko ek strange lens se dekhte hain.

Har cheez ka “meaning” hona chahiye.

Har event ka “purpose” hona chahiye.

Har pain ka “reason” hona chahiye.

Lekin agar direct experience ko dekha jaye…

toh reality khud koi meaning carry nahi karti.

Wo bas happen ho rahi hoti hai.

Meaning baad mein add hota hai.

Mind ke through.

Ek situation hoti hai…

aur turant mind usko interpret karta hai:

ye kyu hua
