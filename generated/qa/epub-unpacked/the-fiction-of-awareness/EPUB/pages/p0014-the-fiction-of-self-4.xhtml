jo “main” lagta tha…

wo kabhi ek cheez tha hi nahi.

Wo sirf ek continuously written story thi…

jo itni fast likhi ja rahi thi ki lagta tha wo already complete hai.

The Human Fiction yahi puchta hai:

agar story hi self hai…

toh writer kahan hai?

Aur phir ye question bhi dissolve ho jata hai…

kyuki writer ki zarurat bhi story ke andar hi thi.

Is book ka end kisi conclusion pe nahi hota.

Ye ek shift pe hota hai.

Ek silent shift…

from believing a center exists…

to noticing that center was never located in the first place.
