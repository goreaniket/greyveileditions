CHAPTER 5

The Fiction of Becoming

Hum sab ek invisible direction mein jee rahe hote hain.

Ek future version ki taraf.

Jo abhi exist nahi karta…

par hamesha present hota hai.

Ye idea simple lagta hai:

“Main better banunga.”

“Main heal ho jaunga.”

“Main complete ho jaunga.”

Lekin agar deeply dekha jaye…

toh “becoming” kabhi end point nahi hota.

Ye ek continuous projection system hota hai.
