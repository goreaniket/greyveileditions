If you read carefully, you may notice something subtle:

that what you assumed to be a fixed “self” is actually a continuous process of narration.

A story that never announces itself as a story.

There is no demand here to believe or reject anything written inside these pages.

Because belief is not the point.

Seeing clearly is.

And when something is seen clearly…

it no longer needs to be defended as true or dismissed as false.

It simply becomes obvious.

If this book creates any shift at all…

it will not be through adding new knowledge.

But through softening the certainty of old assumptions.

And in that softening…

what remains is not confusion…

but a quieter way of seeing experience itself.
