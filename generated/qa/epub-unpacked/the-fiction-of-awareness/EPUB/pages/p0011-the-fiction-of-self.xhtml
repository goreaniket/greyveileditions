INTRODUCTION

THE FICTION OF SELF

Ek cheez hum sab silently assume karte hain…

without even questioning it:

“Main yeh life jee raha hoon.”

Lekin agar is sentence ko thoda aur slowly dekha jaye…

toh ek strange question nikalta hai:

ye “main” actually hai kya?

Kya ye koi fixed cheez hai jo body ke andar baithi hai…

aur life ko experience kar rahi hai?

Ya phir ye sirf ek ongoing feeling hai…

jo har moment recreate hoti rehti hai?

The Human Fiction — Book I isi question ko explore nahi karta…

ye is assumption ko dheere-dheere dissolve hota hua dikhata hai.

Hum “self” ko ek solid entity maan lete hain:

jo sochti hai

jo feel karti hai
