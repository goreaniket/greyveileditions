ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Lalit R. Mishra is the author of The Human Fiction series.

He is not writing from a position of authority, but from observation — of thought, identity, and the subtle narratives through which human experience organizes itself.

His work explores a simple but uncomfortable question:

what if the “self” is not something we find, but something that is continuously constructed?

Rather than presenting fixed answers, his writing focuses on examining the hidden structures behind everyday experience — memory, language, interpretation, inner narration, and the sense of becoming.

He is particularly interested in how a stable “I” appears to exist, even though it is never found as a single, fixed entity in direct experience.
