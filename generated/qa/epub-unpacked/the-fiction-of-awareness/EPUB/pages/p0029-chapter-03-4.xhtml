Lekin wo awareness bhi ek thought-based interpretation hai.

Ek label

Ek narrative position.

Isliye inner voice ek single entity nahi hai.

Ye multiple fragments ka fast sequence hai…

jo ek continuous conversation jaisa feel hota hai.

Aur kyuki gap visible nahi hota…

mind usko “I am thinking” ke form mein compress kar deta hai.

But truth is:

thinking is happening

not someone thinking

Aur ye difference subtle hai…

lekin entire identity structure isi pe based hai.

Kyuki agar “someone thinking” collapse ho jaye…

toh “thinker” bhi dissolve ho jata hai

Aur jab thinker dissolve hota hai…

toh sirf thinking bachi rehti hai.

without ownership.

without center.
