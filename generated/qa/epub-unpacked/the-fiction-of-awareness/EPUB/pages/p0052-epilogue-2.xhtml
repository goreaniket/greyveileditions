“ye mere saath ho raha hai”

Aur jab ye story thodi transparent hoti hai…

toh experience change nahi hota…

sirf interpretation soften ho jati hai.

Is book ka end koi disappearance nahi hai.

Na self vanish hota hai.

Na world dissolve hota hai.

Sirf ek cheez shift hoti hai:

ownership ka weight.

Thought ab bhi aata hai…

lekin wo “mera thought” kam lagta hai.

Feeling ab bhi hoti hai…

lekin wo “meri feeling” kam tight lagti hai.

Life ab bhi chalti hai…

lekin usko explain karne ki urgency kam ho jati hai.

Aur jo bachta hai…

wo koi mystical state nahi hota.

Wo sirf ek simple clarity hoti hai:

jo bhi ho raha hai… ho raha hai.

Aur is “ho raha hai” ke andar…

koi hidden owner dhoondhne ki zarurat nahi bachi hoti.
