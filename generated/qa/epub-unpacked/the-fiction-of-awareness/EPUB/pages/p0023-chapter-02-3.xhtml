Sirf reconstructed moments hote hain.

Memory ka real function truth preserve karna nahi hai…

balke continuity preserve karna hai.

Kyuki agar continuity toot jaye…

toh “I” ka idea weak pad jata hai.

Isliye mind memory ko ek chain ki tarah connect karta hai:

Present → Past → Identity

Aur is chain ke beech ek assumption silently add hota hai:

“There was someone experiencing it.”

Lekin wo “someone” memory ke andar kabhi visible nahi hota.

Wo hamesha inference hota hai.

Aur yahin pe illusion deepen hota hai.

Kyuki ab “self” sirf present experience se nahi banta…

wo past reconstruction se bhi banta hai.

So self ek double-layer system ban jata hai:

present experience
