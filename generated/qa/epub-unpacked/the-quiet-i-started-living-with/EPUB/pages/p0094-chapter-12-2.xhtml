Pehle main har cheez analyze karta tha.

Har message.

Har pause.

Har tone.

Phir ek point aaya… jab main analyze karna bhi

postpone karne laga.

Not because I understood everything.

But because I stopped caring enough to solve it.

Aur yeh difference mujhe bahut der baad samajh aaya.

Understanding aur numbness same nahi hote.

Main samajh raha tha ki main theek ho raha hu.

Lekin shayad main sirf feel karna kam kar raha tha.

Kabhi kabhi main apne aap ko observe karta hu.

Main kisi cheez ka reaction nahi deta.

Na khushi.

Na sadness.

Na anger.

Bas ek blank response.

Aur pehle mujhe lagta tha yeh maturity hai.

Aaj lagta hai yeh thakan hai.
