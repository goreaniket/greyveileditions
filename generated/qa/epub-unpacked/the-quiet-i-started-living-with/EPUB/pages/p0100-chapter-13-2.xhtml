Lekin andar se kuch trigger nahi hota.

Aur yeh baat mujhe pehle ajeeb lagti thi.

Ab normal lagne lagi hai.

Shayad isi ko log control bolte hain.

Ya shayad disconnect.

Mujhe khud bhi clear nahi.

Kabhi kabhi main kisi cheez par react karna chahta hu…

Lekin reaction aata hi nahi.

Jaise beech mein koi invisible gap aa gaya ho… mere

aur life ke beech.

Main us gap ko explain nahi kar sakta.

Bas feel kar sakta hu.

Aur woh feeling bhi half hi hoti hai.

Main purane version ko yaad karta hu… toh

woh mujhe real lagta hai.

Aur main jo ab hu… woh temporary

lagta hai.

Jaise main kisi transition phase mein atak gaya hu.

Na pura wahan.

Na pura yahan.
