Between interpretation and truth.

Between what happens… and what is made of what happens.

I used to think I was trapped inside my mind.

Now I see it differently.

The mind was never a prison.

It was only a place I forgot I could stand beside instead of inside.

Nothing external resolved itself.

No past was corrected.

No version of events was restored to purity.

Everything remained imperfectly remembered.

And that was enough.

Because the need to perfect it slowly stopped feeling necessary.

There is a quiet kind of ending that does not announce itself.

It does not close a story.

It simply removes the urgency to continue rewriting it.

Even now, silence is not absolute.

It never became total.

It simply stopped needing to be defined.

Sometimes it is still interrupted by thought.

Sometimes by memory.
