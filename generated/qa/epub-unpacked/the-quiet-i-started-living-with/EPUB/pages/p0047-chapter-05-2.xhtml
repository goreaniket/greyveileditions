Kyunki mujhe woh ladka yaad hai.

Aur phir bhi nahi hai.

Usse cheezon ka excitement hota tha.

Woh bina soche message bhej deta tha.

Bina darr ke attach ho jaata tha.

Bina calculate kiye trust kar leta tha.

Usse nahi pata tha...

ki ek din woh har word ko bolne se pehle do baar sochega.

Har feeling ko feel karne se pehle analyse karega.

Aur har insaan se milne se pehle distance maintain karega.

Kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta hai... main

overthinking se nahi thaka.

Main khud se thak gaya hu.

Har waqt alert rehna.

Har waqt strong dikhna.

Har waqt yeh pretend karna ki sab control mein hai.

Ek point ke baad yeh exhausting ho jaata hai.

Bahut zyada.
