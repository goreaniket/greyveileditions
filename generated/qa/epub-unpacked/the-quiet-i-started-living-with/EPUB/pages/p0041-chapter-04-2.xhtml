Jo har baar gale tak aakar ruk gaya.

Jo har baar "chhod" ke peeche dab gaya.

Kabhi kabhi main imagine karta tha...

ki woh pooch rahi hai:

"Sach batao, hua kya tha?" Aur

us moment mein... mere paas

answer hota tha.

Pehli baar.

Na gussa.

Na ego.

Na explanation.

Bas sach …..

Lekin ajeeb baat yeh hai... real life mein kisi ne kabhi

woh sawaal poocha hi nahi.

Aur shayad isi liye main aaj tak jawab deta rehta hu.

Apne dimaag mein.
