Maine itna time us story ke saath spend kiya tha... ki woh

story meri identity banne lagi thi.

Logon ko nahi pata tha.

Lekin mujhe pata tha.

Har baar jab main akela hota tha...

mera dimaag automatically wahi chala jaata tha.

Jaise kisi ne default setting set kar di ho.

Aur sabse dangerous baat?

Main usse stop bhi nahi karta tha.

Kyunki mujhe lagta tha main healing kar raha hu.

Aaj lagta hai...

main sirf repeat kar raha tha.

Ek hi chapter.

Ek hi scene.

Ek hi feeling.

Bas alag alag dinon mein….

Ek raat maine genuinely khud se poocha:

Agar kal subah uthkar sab pain gayab ho gaya...

toh kya hoga?

Aur jo answer aaya...
