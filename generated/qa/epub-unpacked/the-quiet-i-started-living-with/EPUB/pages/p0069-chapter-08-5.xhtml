Ek situation.

Ek memory.

Ek loss.

Ab mere paas kuch nahi tha.

Sirf ek mirror tha.

Aur mirror kabhi kabhi bahut cruel hota hai.

Woh excuses nahi maanta.

Woh bas dikhata hai.

Aur mujhe jo dikh raha tha...

woh mujhe pasand nahi aa raha tha.

Main thak gaya tha.

Lekin sirf kisi aur ki wajah se nahi.

Khud ki wajah se bhi.

Khud se bhaagte bhaagte.

Khud ko samjhaye bina.

Khud ko ignore karte karte.

Ek point ke baad insaan kisi aur ko nahi...

khud ko lose kar deta hai.

Aur shayad mere saath bhi wahi hua tha.

Mujhe lagta tha meri life ka sabse difficult part wait karna tha.
