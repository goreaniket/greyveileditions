"Tu itna dheere kyun chal raha hai?"

Kabhi kisi ne mujhse poocha: "Tu

itna chup kyun rehta hai?" Aur us

waqt mujhe realise hua... mere

paas answer hi nahi tha.

Main chup kyun rehta hu?

Kyunki main thak gaya hu?

Kyunki mujhe darr lagta hai?

Kyunki mujhe lagta hai koi samjhega nahi?

Ya kyunki main khud ko nahi samajhta?

Pata nahi.

Aur jab answer hi nahi hota... toh explanation

kahan se laun?

Kabhi kabhi main normal baitha hota hu.

Kuch galat bhi nahi hota.

Koi problem bhi nahi hoti.

Lekin andar ek ajeeb si heaviness rehti hai.
