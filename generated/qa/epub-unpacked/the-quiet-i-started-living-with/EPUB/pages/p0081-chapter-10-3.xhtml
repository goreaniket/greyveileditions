Khud se decide karna mushkil.

Mujhe yaad hai kitni baar main woh cheezein karta tha...

jo mujhe karni nahi thi.

Aur jab koi poochta:

"Tu theek hai na?"

Main automatically bol deta:

"Haan."

Na isliye ki main theek tha.

Na isliye ki main jhooth bol raha tha.

Bas isliye kyunki mujhe pata tha saamne wala kis answer ki expectation kar raha hai.

Aur dheere dheere...

maine notice karna band kar diya...

ki mujhe actually kya feel ho raha hai.
