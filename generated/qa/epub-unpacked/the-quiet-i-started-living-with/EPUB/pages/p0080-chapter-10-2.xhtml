Kam se kam mujhe aisa nahi lagta.

Main jhooth bolne wala insaan bhi nahi tha.

Lekin phir bhi...

kuch toh tha jo natural nahi lagta tha.

Jaise main har jagah thoda alag insaan ban jaata tha.

Kisi ke saamne funny. Kisi ke saamne mature.

Kisi ke saamne detached.

Kisi ke saamne completely fine.

Aur yeh sab versions itne time tak saath chalte rahe... ki

mujhe samajh nahi aaya... asli wala kaunsa hai.

Kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta tha...

main logon ko disappoint karne se darrta hu.

Phir baad mein realise hua...

main unki expectations todne se zyada...

unki expectations ke bina rehne se darrta tha.

jab log tumse kuch expect karte hain... toh

kam se kam tumhe ek role mil jaata hai.

Ek character.

Ek script.

Aur script follow karna aasaan hota hai.
