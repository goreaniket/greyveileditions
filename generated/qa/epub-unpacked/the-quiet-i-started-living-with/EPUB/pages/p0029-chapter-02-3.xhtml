Woh thoda thoda silent hota hai.

Aaj ek baat nahi batata. Kal ek

feeling nahi batata. Phir ek din poora

din guzar jaata hai...

aur usne kisi ko apne baare mein kuch nahi bataya hota.

Mere saath bhi shayad aisa hi hua.

Log poochte the:

"Sab theek?"

Aur mere paas jawab hota tha.

Bahut lamba jawab.

Lekin muh se sirf ek word nikalta tha.

"Haan."

Aur us ek word ke peeche kitni cheezein chhupi hoti thi...
