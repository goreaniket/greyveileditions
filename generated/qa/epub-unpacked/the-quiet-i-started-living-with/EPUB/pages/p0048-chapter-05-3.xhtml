"Tu change ho gaya."

Aur mujhe sabse zyada gussa is baat par nahi aata.

Mujhe gussa is baat par aata hai...

ki woh sahi hote hain.

Main change ho gaya hu.

Aur mujhe nahi pata kab.

Mujhe yaad nahi ki maine kab openly baat karna chhoda.

Kab maine logon ko distance par rakhna shuru kiya.

Kab maine har situation ke worst outcome ke liye mentally prepare rehna shuru kiya.

Bas itna pata hai...

ki ab yeh sab normal lagta hai.

Aur shayad yahi sabse dangerous part hai.

Jab pain normal lagne lage.

Jab silence normal lagne lage.

Jab loneliness normal lagne lage.
