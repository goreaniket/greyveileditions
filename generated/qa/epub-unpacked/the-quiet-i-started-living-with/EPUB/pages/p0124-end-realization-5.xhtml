Just movement.

And the ground beneath it was always here.

Unchanged. Unaffected.

I had mistaken movement for self.

That mistake ended quietly.

Not through resolution.

But through the disappearance of the need for resolution.

Now, thoughts still arise.

But they no longer determine where I stand.

I am no longer inside every thought.

I can see them pass without entering them.

And for the first time, I am not trying to explain my life.

I am simply inside it.

And that is all.
