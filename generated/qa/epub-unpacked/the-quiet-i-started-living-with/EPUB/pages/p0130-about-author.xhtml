ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Lalit R. Mishra is a student who began writing out of curiosity rather than ambition.

What started as scattered notes, unfinished thoughts, and observations gradually turned into stories

that explored experiences difficult to capture in ordinary conversations.

He is less interested in giving answers and more interested in paying attention to the questions

people carry with them.

His work is influenced by everyday moments, long periods of reflection, and the belief that some

of the most meaningful experiences in life are often the hardest to describe.

"The Quiet I Started Living With" is his second book.
