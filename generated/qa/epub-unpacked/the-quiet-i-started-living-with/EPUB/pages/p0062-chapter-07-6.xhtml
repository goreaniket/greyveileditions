Mujhe closure bhi nahi chahiye.

Mujhe jo chahiye tha….

woh kisi ke paas tha hi nahi

Mujhe time reverse karna tha.

Mujhe un moments mein wapas jaana tha...

jab cheezein complicated nahi thi.

Jab mujhe har cheez analyse nahi karni padti thi.

Jab future itna heavy nahi lagta tha.

Jab kisi naam ko sunke dimaag itna loud nahi ho jaata tha.

Aur tab mujhe samajh aaya...

ki main impossible cheez ka wait kar raha thaa….

Shayad isi liye main itna thak gaya.

Kyunki insaan jab kisi real cheez ka wait karta hai...

toh ek din answer mil jaata hai.

Lekin jab insaan impossible cheez ka wait karta hai...

toh woh sirf wait karta rehta hai.

Kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta hai...

main move on nahi kar pa raha tha.
