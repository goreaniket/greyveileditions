Phir ek din...

pata nahi kaise...

wait khatam ho gaya.

Na koi announcement hui.

Na koi dramatic moment.

Na koi realization.

Bas ek din maine notice kiya... main

dekh nahi raha.

Main expect nahi kar raha.

Main imagine nahi kar raha.

Aur pehli baar mujhe laga... shayad

yeh khatam ho gaya.

Lekin jo cheez uske baad hui... uske

liye main ready nahi tha.

Relief nahi aaya.

Silence aaya.

Ek ajeeb sa silence.

Jaise bahut time se background mein koi machine chal rahi thi...

aur achanak band ho gayi.

Aur phir mujhe pehli baar room ki khamoshi sunai di.
