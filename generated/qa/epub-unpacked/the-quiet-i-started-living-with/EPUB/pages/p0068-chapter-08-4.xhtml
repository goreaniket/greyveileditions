Tum kisi aur ke baare mein soch sakte ho.

Kisi aur ki absence ke baare mein.

Kisi aur ki mistakes ke baare mein.

Lekin jab woh sab hat jaata hai... tab

tumhare paas sirf tum bachte ho.

Aur har insaan khud ke saath comfortable nahi hota.

Main bhi nahi tha.

Mujhe lagta tha...

agar woh chapter close ho gaya...

toh meri life automatically better lagne lagegi.

Lekin chapter close hua... aur

main wahi tha.

Wahi insecurities.

Wahi confusion.

Wahi habits.

Aur pehli baar mujhe realise hua...

ki kuch problems us chapter ne create nahi ki thi.

Kuch problems pehle se mere andar thi.

Main bas unhe dekh nahi raha tha.

Yeh realization achha nahi tha.

Kyunki us point tak mere paas ek villain tha.
