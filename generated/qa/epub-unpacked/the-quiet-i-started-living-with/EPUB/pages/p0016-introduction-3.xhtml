Aur healing kisi motivational quote se nahi aati.

Ye book sirf ek invitation hai.

Observe karne ka.

Bina immediately judge kiye.

Bina har feeling ko fix karne ki koshish kiye.

Bina har silence ko bharne ki zarurat mehsoos kiye.

Kyuki kabhi kabhi...

problem thought nahi hota.

Problem ye hota hai...

ki hum har thought ko apni identity bana lete hain.

Aur jab identity thought ke saath attach ho jaati hai...

toh har memory personal lagti hai.

Har rejection permanent lagta hai.

Har uncertainty dangerous lagti hai.

Lekin...

agar tum ek pal ke liye ruk jao...

aur sirf notice karo...

ki tumhare andar kya chal raha hai...

toh shayad tumhe ek aur cheez dikhe.

Thoughts aa rahe hain.

Ja rahe hain.
