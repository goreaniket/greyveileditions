Sometimes by nothing at all.

But none of it feels like disturbance anymore.

Only weather.

Passing through an unchanged sky.

If there is something I understand now, it is this:

Nothing was ever waiting to be fixed inside me.

Only observed.

And when observation stopped trying to become control…

something unnecessary fell away on its own.

Not the mind.

But the need to believe everything it said.

That is what remained.

Not answers.

Not resolution.

Not final understanding.

Just a quieter relationship with thought.

And perhaps that is the closest thing to silence there ever was.

Not the absence of noise.

But the absence of resistance to it.

And in that absence…

life was always here.
