Pretend karna padta hai.

Aur pretend karna...

explain karne se aasaan lagta hai.

Kam se kam shuru mein.

Lekin phir ek waqt aata hai...

jab tum realise karte ho ki tumne sirf baatein chhupayi nahi.

Tumne khud ko chhupa diya.

Jo cheezein tum feel karte ho.

Jo cheezein tum sochte ho.

Jo cheezein tumhe raat ko jagaye rakhti hain.

Sab kuch.

Aur sabse buri baat?

Kisi ko pata bhi nahi chalta.

Log sochte hain tum strong ho.

Mature ho.

Handle kar lete ho. Lekin reality mein... tum bas aadat

daal chuke hote ho akela handle karne ki.

Aur aadat aur strength mein bahut farq hota hai.

Bahut zyada.
