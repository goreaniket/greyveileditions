A late-night street.

A paused world.

I was wrong.

The loudest place I had ever been was inside my own head.

And it wasn’t trying to harm me.

It was trying to resolve uncertainty.

Because uncertainty feels unfinished.

And the mind prefers a painful explanation over no explanation at all.

So it keeps building stories.

Not because it wants suffering—but because it cannot tolerate gaps.

What is unfinished becomes replayed.

What is replayed becomes familiar.

What is familiar becomes identity.

That is how quietly it happens.

You don’t notice the transition.

One day you are thinking.

And one day you are being thought through.

For years, I mistook this for awareness.

But awareness expands life.
