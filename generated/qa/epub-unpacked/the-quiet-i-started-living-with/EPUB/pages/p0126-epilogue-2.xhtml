And strangely, nothing collapsed because of it.

There was a time I believed clarity meant resolution.

That peace meant solving everything inside the mind.

Now it felt different.

Peace was not resolution.

It was distance.

Not escape.

Not suppression.

Just a quiet separation between what is experienced… and what is narrated.

There were still moments when the old pattern returned.

A conversation replaying itself.

A memory reshaping its tone.

A future being rewritten in advance.

But something in me no longer followed it all the way.

The loop could continue.

But I did not have to live inside it.

That was the change I never expected.

Not silence.

But space.

Space between thought and identity.
