Kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta hai… main apne emotions ko door

se observe karna seekh gaya hu.

Jaise woh kisi aur ke hon.

Aur shayad yeh hi survival hai.

Kyunki agar main har cheez ko pehle jaisa feel karu… toh

shayad main handle hi na kar paun.

Lekin ek sawaal kabhi kabhi aata hai.

Agar main itna door ho gaya hu… toh main ab “main” kitna hu?

Ya main sirf ek habit hu… jo chal

rahi hai bina full presence ke.

Kabhi kabhi main khud ko test karta hu.

Koi purani memory yaad karta hu.

Dekhta hu reaction aata hai ya nahi.

Aur kabhi aata hai… kabhi

nahi aata.

Aur dono hi cheezein mujhe confuse karti hain.

Reaction aana bhi strange lagta hai.

Reaction na aana bhi.
