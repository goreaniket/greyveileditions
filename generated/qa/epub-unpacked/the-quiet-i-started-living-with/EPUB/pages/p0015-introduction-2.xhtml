toh shayad hum life se zyada apne thoughts experience kar rahe hote hain.

Ek hi conversation ko baar baar replay karna.

Ek hi memory ko naye angles se dekhna.

Future ke imaginary scenes banana.

Aur phir unhi scenes par emotional reaction dena.

Jaise mind sirf reality nahi jeeta...

wo reality ke andar ek aur reality create karta rehta hai.

Aur sabse dangerous baat?

Ye sab itna gradually hota hai...

ki hume pata bhi nahi chalta kab hum present se zyada apni interpretation ke saath rehne lage.

Kab humne moments ko jeena kam...

aur unhe explain karna zyada start kar diya.

Shayad isi liye hum itne mentally exhausted rehte hain.

Life ki wajah se nahi.

Us version ki wajah se...

jo humne apne dimaag ke andar bana rakhi hoti hai.

Ye book tumhe positive banna nahi sikhayegi.

Na hi ye bolegi ki overthinking bas stop kar do.

Mind kisi switch ki tarah kaam nahi karta.
