Lekin woh silence pehle jaisa nahi tha.

Pehle silence mein sawal hote the.

Ab silence mein sirf space tha.

Empty space.

Kabhi kabhi main kisi purani baat ko yaad karta hu… aur mujhe

lagta hai main us insaan ko jaanta hi nahi tha.

Jo itna feel karta tha.

Jo itna react karta tha.

Jo har cheez ko apne andar le leta tha.

Woh version ab door lagta hai.

Lekin gayab nahi.

Bas inaccessible.

Jaise koi purani file jiska password bhool gaya ho.

Main ab bhi zindagi jee raha hu.

Lekin ek difference ke saath.

Main ab zindagi ke andar fully sink nahi karta.

Main thoda bahar hi rehta hu.

Aur shayad ye protection hai.

Ya phir avoidance.

Mujhe khud bhi nahi pata.
