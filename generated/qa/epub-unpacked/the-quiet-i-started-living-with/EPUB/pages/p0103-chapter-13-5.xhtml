Jaise koi cheez jo mere saath ho rahi hai… lekin mere andar nahi.

Aur kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta hai…

yeh peace nahi hai.

Yeh distance hai.

Aur distance hamesha visible nahi hota.

Woh slowly build hota hai.

Bina noise ke.

Bina warning ke.

Bas ek din tum realize karte ho… ki

tum wahan nahi ho jahan pehle the.

Aur shayad wahi din… sabse

confusing hota hai.
