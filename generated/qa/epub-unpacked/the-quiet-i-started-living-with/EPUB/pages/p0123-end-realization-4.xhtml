It felt like loss at first.

Loss of internal narration.

Loss of constant engagement.

Loss of the sense that I was always “working through something.”

But even that loss dissolved.

What remained was distance.

A separation between what happens and what I make of it.

That separation is everything.

Because thoughts no longer define the space they appear in.

They move through it.

And the space remains unchanged.

I did not create that stillness.

I stopped interfering with it.

And in that absence of interference, something became clear:

Nothing inside needed correction in the way I believed.

Only observation without control.

When control dropped, the mind stopped behaving like a problem.

It became movement.

Not identity.

Not truth.

Not command.
