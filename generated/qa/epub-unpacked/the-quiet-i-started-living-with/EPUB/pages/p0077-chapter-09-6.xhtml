Shayad problem yeh thi ki main answer aane ka wait kar raha tha.

Jaise ek din uthunga...

aur achanak sab clear ho jayega.

Jaise identity koi notification hai... jo

ek din phone screen par aa jayegi.

Lekin aisa nahi hota.

Bahut saari cheezein clarity se nahi...

movement se milti hain.

Pehle chalna padta hai.

Baad mein samajh aata hai ki kahan ja rahe the.

Aur mujhe lagta hai...

yeh baat accept karne mein mujhe bahut time laga.

Kyunki main certainty chahta tha.

Main guarantee chahta tha.

Main pehle answer chahta tha.

Phir action.

Life ulta kaam karti hai.

Pehle action.

Phir answer.
