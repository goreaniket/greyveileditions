Lekin woh wapas nahi aate.

Sirf ek faint echo aata hai.

Jaise koi awaaz jo kisi aur room se aa rahi ho.

Main ab bhi wahi insaan hu… jo

sochta hai. jo samajhta hai. jo

chal raha hai.

Lekin woh urgency nahi rahi.

Woh intensity nahi rahi.

Woh “everything matters” wala feel dheere dheere kam ho gaya hai.

Aur mujhe darr nahi lagta is baat se.

Bas main notice karta hu.

Ki main kisi cheez ke andar poori tarah doob nahi pa raha.

Main hamesha thoda bahar hi reh jaata hu.

Jaise main life ko live nahi kar raha… main

usse read kar raha hu.

Aur kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta hai… shayad main ab kisi

ek fixed version mein fix hogya hu

mai har jagah thoda sa mismatch

Log ke saath bhi.
