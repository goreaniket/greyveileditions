Woh illusion ki main heal kar raha hu...

jabki main sirf repeat kar raha tha.

Aur shayad wahi pehli baar tha...

jab maine apne aap par taras khana thoda kam kiya.

Aur apne aap ko observe karna thoda zyada.

Kyunki us point ke baad sawaal yeh nahi tha:

"Mere saath yeh kyun hua?"

Sawaal yeh tha:

"Ab main iske saath aur kitna time jeene wala hu?"

Aur sach kahu...

mere paas uska answer aaj bhi nahi hai.

Lekin pehli baar...

maine answer dhoondhne se zyada... sawaal se

bhaagna band kiya.
