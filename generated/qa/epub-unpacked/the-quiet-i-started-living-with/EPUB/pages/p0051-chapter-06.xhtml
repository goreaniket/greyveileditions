PHASE II - WHEN SILENCE BECOMES YOUR LANGUAGE

THE VERSION OF ME NOBODY EVER KNEW

Mujhe bahut time tak lagta raha ki log mujhe samajhte nahi.

Main is baat se irritate bhi hota tha.

Kabhi kabhi hurt bhi.

Aur kabhi kabhi mujhe sach mein lagta tha ki shayad meri life mein koi aisa insaan hai hi nahi jo

mujhe poori tarah samajh sake.

Phir ek din pata nahi kyun...

maine khud se ek sawaal poocha.

Agar koi mujhe samajhta nahi...

toh maine kitni baar kisi ko mauka diya?
