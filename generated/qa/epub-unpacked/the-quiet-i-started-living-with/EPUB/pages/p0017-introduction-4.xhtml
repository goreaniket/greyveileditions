Emotions bhi aa rahe hain.

Ja rahe hain.

Memories bhi badal rahi hain.

Phir bhi...

kuch hai jo in sab ke beech kabhi change nahi hota.

Ye book usi cheez ke baare mein hai.

Silence ke baare mein nahi.

Us distance ke baare mein...

jo dheere dheere tum aur tumhari real experience ke beech ban jaata hai.

Aur us possibility ke baare mein...

ki shayad us distance ko force se nahi...

sirf awareness se dekha ja sakta hai.

Ho sakta hai is book mein tumhe answers na mile.

Aur shayad wahi iska purpose bhi hai.

Kyuki kuch questions solve nahi kiye jaate.

Unke saath baithna seekha jaata hai.

Aur kabhi kabhi...

jab tum unhe solve karna chhod dete ho...

tab woh khud dissolve hone lagte hain.

Isliye is book ko jaldi mat padhna.
