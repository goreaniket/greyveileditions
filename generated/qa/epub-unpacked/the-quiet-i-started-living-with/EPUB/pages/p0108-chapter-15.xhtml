PHASE IV - BECOMING SOMEONE THE SILENCE COULD NO LONGER HIDE

THE VERSION OF ME THAT DOESN’T FIT ANYMORE

Mujhe lagta hai main ab us insaan jaisa nahi raha jo main pehle tha.

Par ajeeb baat yeh hai… main usse poori

tarah chhod bhi nahi paaya.

Jaise koi purani skin ho…

jo ab tight ho gayi ho… par

utarti bhi nahi.

Kabhi kabhi main khud ko yaad karta hu.

Aur mujhe lagta hai…

“haan, main aisa tha.”

Lekin turant baad lagta hai…

“ya shayad main kabhi tha hi nahi.”
