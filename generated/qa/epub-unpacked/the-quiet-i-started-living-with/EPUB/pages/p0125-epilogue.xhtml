What Remained After the Noise

In the end, nothing dramatic changed.

There was no final moment where everything suddenly made sense.

No clear ending where the mind went silent forever.

Life continued in the same ordinary way it always had.

But something subtle had shifted in how it was being lived.

The thoughts were still there.

They still arrived uninvited, still repeated themselves, still tried to complete unfinished things.

But they no longer felt like directions.

Only movements.

Passing through.

Without ownership.

I stopped treating every thought as a message.

Stopped assuming every inner sentence required an answer.

Some questions were still unanswered.

They remained unanswered.
