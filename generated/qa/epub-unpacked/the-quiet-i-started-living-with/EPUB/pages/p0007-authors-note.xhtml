FRONTMATTER

NOTE FROM THE AUTHOR

This book is not written to explain life.

It is written to document what happens when thoughts stay longer than emotions, and

silence slowly replaces reaction.

There are no fixed meanings hidden between these pages.

No advice.

No conclusions.

Only observations.

Only inner shifts that were too subtle to notice in real time.

This is not a story of healing.

It is a story of states.

Of phases where you stop recognizing your own patterns, but

still continue living inside them.

If you find yourself somewhere in these pages, it

is not because the book is about you.

It is because these states are more common than we admit… just rarely

spoken in words.
