ENDING

Simply Inside It

I used to think I was trying to understand myself.

But I wasn’t.

I was trying to complete unfinished thoughts about myself.

There is a difference between understanding and completion.

Understanding expands. Completion tries to close.

For a long time, I lived without noticing that difference.

Every thought felt necessary.

Every memory felt incomplete.

Every silence felt like something missing.

I did not experience life directly.

I experienced it through constant interpretation.

And slowly, interpretation replaced experience.

I replayed what was already over.

Reconstructed what could no longer change.

Assigned meaning to fragments the mind refused to release.

It felt like awareness.
