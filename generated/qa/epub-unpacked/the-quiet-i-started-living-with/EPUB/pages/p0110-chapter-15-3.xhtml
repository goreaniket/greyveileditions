Aur phir sochta hu…

“kya yeh main hu?”

“kya yeh main hu?”

Aur jawab nahi aata.

Bas ek purani si memory aati hai… jab

main cheezein feel karta tha bina soche.

Ab main sochta hu pehle…

feel baad mein aata hai… ya

kabhi kabhi aata hi nahi.

Main kisi cheez ko miss karta hu… lekin us missing

ka intensity bhi change ho gaya hai

Jaise koi cheez door ho…

par us door hone ka dard bhi dheere dheere fade ho gaya ho.

Aur mujhe samajh nahi aata… yeh

healing hai ya loss ka next stage.

Kabhi kabhi main khud ko force karta hu… feel

karne ke liye.

Purani yaadein.

Purane moments.

Purane emotions.
