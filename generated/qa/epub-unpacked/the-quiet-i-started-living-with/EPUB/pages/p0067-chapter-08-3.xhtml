Main bahut time tak samajhta raha...

ki mera struggle us cheez se tha jiska main wait kar raha tha.

Lekin wait khatam hone ke baad realise hua...

mera struggle us cheez se tha jo main avoid kar raha tha.

Khud ko.

Kyunki jab tak main wait kar raha tha... mere

paas focus karne ke liye kuch tha.

Ek possibility.

Ek memory.

Ek unfinished chapter.

Ab woh nahi tha.

Aur ab mujhe apni life dekhni thi.

Wahi life jise maine kitni baar postpone kiya tha.

Wahi questions jinke answers maine kabhi dhoonde hi nahi.

Kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta hai...

main move on nahi karna chahta tha.

Main distract rehna chahta tha.

Kyunki distraction comfortable hoti hai.
