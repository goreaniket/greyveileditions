PROLOGUE

The Quiet I Started Living With

There is a moment in every life that almost no one remembers.

Not because it is dramatic.

But because nothing seems to happen.

No tragedy announces it.

No celebration marks it.

The world continues unchanged.

Morning still arrives on time.

Messages still get read.

People still ask how you are—and you still answer automatically.

From the outside, nothing shifts.

But somewhere beneath that continuity, something quietly turns inward.

Not the body.

Not the circumstances.

But attention itself.
