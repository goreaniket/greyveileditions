Dono thoughts ek saath chalte hain.

Aur dono hi mujhe confuse nahi karte ab.

Bas thoda sa empty kar dete hain.

Main pehle zyada react karta tha.

Chhoti chhoti cheezein.

Log

Messages.

Silence.

Presence.

Absence.

Ab kuch bhi directly hit nahi karta.

Aur pehle mujhe lagta tha yeh progress hai.

Ab lagta hai… main bas thoda kam

available ho gaya hu.

Main kisi se milta hu…

baat karta hu… smile bhi

karta hu…

Lekin beech beech mein ek gap hota hai.

Jaise main poori tarah wahan nahi hu.

Main observe karta hu apne aap ko.

Ki main kaise react kar raha hu.
