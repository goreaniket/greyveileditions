When surviving thoughts becomes so normal that living quietly stops feeling necessary.

If these pages resemble you in any way, do not rush to find conclusions.

Conclusions end movement.

And this story is not meant to end anything.

Only to notice what has been happening for a long time without being noticed.

Perhaps silence was never the absence of sound.

Perhaps it was the gradual disappearance of the person who once experienced life before translating it.

And perhaps that is why it was so difficult to recognize.

Silence did not arrive.

It was learned.

Slowly.

And eventually… it was lived.

And maybe that is where the story actually begins.
