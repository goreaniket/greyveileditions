Aur wahi jagah thi jahan mera poora argument toot gaya.

Kyunki sach yeh tha...

main logon ko apne baare mein batata tha.

Lekin sirf utna...

jitna comfortable lagta tha.

Jo cheezein easy thi...

main unke baare mein bol deta tha.

Jo cheezein funny thi... unpar joke

maar deta tha.

Jo cheezein normal thi... unhe share

kar deta tha.

Lekin jo cheezein sach mein important thi...

unpar main chup ho jaata tha.

Aur phir mujhe lagta tha ki log mujhe nahi jaante.

Aaj sochta hu toh ajeeb lagta hai.

Main ek locked room mein baitha tha.

Darwaza khud band kiya tha.

Chabi bhi mere paas thi.

Aur phir main complain kar raha tha ki koi andar kyun nahi aata.
