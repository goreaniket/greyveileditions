Itna ki mujhe khud samajh na aaye main kya bol raha hu?

Ya itna ki saalon se dabayi hui har cheez ek saath bahar aa jaye?

Kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta hai...

main kisi insaan ko nahi dhoond raha.

Main ek jagah dhoond raha hu.

Jahan main finally pretend karna band kar sakun.

Jahan mujhe har sentence explain na karna pade.

Jahan koi yeh na pooche: "Par

hua kya hai?" Kyuki sach toh yeh

hai... kabhi kabhi kuch hua hi

nahi hota.

Aur phir bhi sab kuch toot raha hota hai.

Na bola.
