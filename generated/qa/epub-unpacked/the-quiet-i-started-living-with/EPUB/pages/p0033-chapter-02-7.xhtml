Kabhi kabhi raat ko lagta tha...

kaash koi hota.

Aisa nahi jo solution deta.

Bas koi jo sun leta.

Beech mein roke bina.

Judge kiye bina.

Bas sun leta.

Lekin phir wahi thought aata:

"Chhod."

Aur main phir chup ho jaata.

Shayad isi tarah... dheere

dheere... maine bolna nahi

chhoda. Maine umeed karna

chhod diya... ki koi waqai samajh

payega.
