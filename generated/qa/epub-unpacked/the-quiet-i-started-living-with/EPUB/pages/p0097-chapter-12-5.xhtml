Kabhi kabhi main sochta hu… ki agar main apne

aap ko fully feel karne du… toh kya main wapas

wahi chaos mein chala jaunga?

Ya phir shayad main sirf us chaos ka darr carry kar raha hu.

Main sure nahi hu.

Aur shayad yahi problem hai.

Main sure hona hi chhod chuka hu.

Main bas exist kar raha hu.

Quietly.

Without asking too many questions.

Without expecting answers.

Aur kabhi kabhi lagta hai… yeh

peace nahi hai.

Yeh pause hai.

Long pause.
