Aur wahi moment tha jab mujhe darr laga.

Situation se nahi.

Past se nahi.

Memory se nahi.

Khud se.

Kyunki mujhe pehli baar laga...

ki kahin main unknowingly apne hi cage ka door pakad kar khada hu.

Aur bahar nikalne se mana bhi main hi kar raha hu.

Us raat kuch change nahi hua.

Main suddenly better nahi ho gaya.

Main strong bhi feel nahi kar raha tha.

Bas ek illusion toot gaya.

Woh illusion ki main heal kar raha hu...

jabki main sirf repeat kar raha tha.

Aur shayad wahi pehli baar tha...

jab maine apne aap par taras khana thoda kam kiya.

Aur apne aap ko observe karna thoda zyada.

Main suddenly better nahi ho gaya.

Main strong bhi feel nahi kar raha tha.

Bas ek illusion toot gaya.
