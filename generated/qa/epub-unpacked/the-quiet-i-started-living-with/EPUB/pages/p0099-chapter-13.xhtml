PHASE IV - BECOMING SOMEONE THE SILENCE COULD NO LONGER HIDE

THE DISTANCE

I DIDN’T NOTICE GROWING

Kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta hai… main apni hi

zindagi se thoda peeche ho gaya hu.

Jaise main ab uska part nahi… sirf

ek observer ho.

Pehle main har cheez ke beech hota tha.

Har emotion ke andar.

Har situation ke center mein.

Ab main bas side se dekh raha hu.

Log baat karte hain.

Main sunta hu.

Log react karte hain.

Main notice karta hu.
