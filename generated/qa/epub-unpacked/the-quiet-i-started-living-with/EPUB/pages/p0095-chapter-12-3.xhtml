Ek aisi thakan jahan tumhe kuch feel karne ke liye bhi permission chahiye hoti hai.

Main logo se normal baat karta hu.

Main smile karta hu.

Main reply deta hu.

Sab kuch normal lagta hai.

Lekin andar kuch bhi fully present nahi hota.

Jaise main life ko participate nahi kar raha.

Bas usko observe kar raha hu.

Aur kabhi kabhi mujhe darr lagta hai.

Ki main iss state mein itna comfortable na ho jaun… ki

wapas feel karna hi ajeeb lagne lage.

Kyunki numbness bhi ek habit ban sakti hai.

Aur habits quietly dangerous hoti hain.

Woh tumhe todti nahi hain.

Woh tumhe slowly change kar deti hain.

Mujhe yaad hai ek din maine khud se poocha…

“Main kya ban raha hu?”

Aur mere paas answer nahi tha.

Sirf ek silence tha.
