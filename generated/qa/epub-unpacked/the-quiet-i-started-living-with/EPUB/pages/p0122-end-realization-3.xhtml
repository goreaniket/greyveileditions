Only a gradual reduction of urgency.

The need to resolve every thought slowly weakened.

At first, that felt wrong.

Because constant mental activity had become normal.

Stillness felt like something missing.

But nothing was missing.

There was only space where reaction used to be.

And for a while, I almost filled it again out of habit.

But the pattern broke when I noticed something simple:

Not every thought requires continuation.

Not every memory requires meaning.

Not every feeling requires interpretation.

Some things exist only to pass through awareness.

That realization did not stop thoughts.

It changed my relationship with them.

Thoughts were no longer instructions.

Memories were no longer demands.

Feelings were no longer problems to solve.

They became events.

Temporary. Impersonal. Passing.

This was not immediately peaceful.
