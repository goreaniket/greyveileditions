mujhe lagta hai main logon se kam...

khud se zyada confuse tha.

Main nahi jaanta tha mujhe kya chahiye.

Bas itna jaanta tha...

ki jo mere paas hai woh nahi chahiye.

Aur jo chahiye...

usse lene ki himmat nahi thi.

Shayad isi liye meri life ka ek version sabne dekha.

Aur ek version kisi ne nahi.

Na doston ne.

Na un logon ne jo mere close the.

Aur shayad... na

maine khud.

Mujhe lagta tha meri problem yeh hai ki koi mujhe poori tarah nahi jaanta.

Aaj lagta hai...

sabse pehle mujhe khud hi mujhe jaan lena chahiye tha.
