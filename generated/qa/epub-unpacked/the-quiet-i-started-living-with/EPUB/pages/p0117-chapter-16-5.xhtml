Some things don’t end.

They just become quieter over time.

I don’t know if this was a story.

Or just a phase that kept repeating itself in different forms.

Maybe nothing was meant to be solved here.

Maybe everything was only meant to be noticed.

And maybe that is all there ever is.

Not answers.

Not endings.

Just awareness that something changed…

without ever announcing itself.

If there is a final

feeling left, it is not

clarity.

It is not peace.

It is simply this:

Somewhere along the way, I stopped searching for an end.

I thought I was losing myself

I thought I was losing all the memories
