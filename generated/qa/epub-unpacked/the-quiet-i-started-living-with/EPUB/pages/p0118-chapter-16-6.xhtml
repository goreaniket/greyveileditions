Losing of every moments

Losing of every connections.


I don’t know if this was a story.

Or just a phase that kept repeating itself in different forms.

Maybe nothing was meant to be solved here.

Maybe everything was only meant to be noticed.

And maybe that is all there ever is.

Not answers.

Not endings.

Just awareness that something changed… without

ever announcing itself.

If there is a final feeling left, it

is not clarity.

It is not peace.

It is simply this:

Somewhere along the way, I stopped searching for an end.

I thought I was afraid of

losing myself.

Turns out, I was afraid of meeting myself
