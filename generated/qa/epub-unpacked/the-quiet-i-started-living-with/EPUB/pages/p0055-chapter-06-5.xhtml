Ki agar main kisi thought ko loud nahi bolunga...

toh shayad woh itna real nahi lagega.

Ki agar main kisi feeling ka naam nahi lunga... toh

shayad mujhe usse deal nahi karna padega.

Lekin feelings naam na lene se gayab nahi hoti.

Woh bas background mein chalti rehti hain.

Aur ek din tum realise karte ho...

ki tumhari aadhi energy un cheezon ko ignore karne mein ja rahi hai...

jinhe tumne kabhi accept hi nahi kiya.

Kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta hai... main

connection chahta tha.

Lekin vulnerability nahi.

Main chahta tha koi mujhe samjhe.

Lekin main explain nahi karna chahta tha.

Main chahta tha koi ruk jaye.

Lekin main kisi ko itna paas aane nahi deta tha ki woh ruk sake.

Aur phir jab log door ho jaate the...

main sochta tha ki sab chale kyun jaate hain.

Aaj yeh likhte hue...
