Lekin agar main poori honesty se bolu...

toh shayad main move on se nahi darr raha tha.

Main accept karne se darr raha tha.

Accept karne se...

ki kuch stories waise khatam hoti hain jaisa hum nahi chahte.

Koi final message nahi.

Koi perfect ending nahi.

Koi last explanation nahi

Bas ek din sab ruk jaata hai.

Aur tum bahut der baad realise karte ho...

ki story khatam ho chuki thi.

Sirf tum uske last page par baithe hue the.

Aur shayad isi liye aaj bhi jab main khud se poochta hu:

"Tu kis cheez ka wait kar raha tha?"

Toh mere paas perfect answer nahi hota.

Bas itna pata hai...
