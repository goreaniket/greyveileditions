PHASE IV - VOID

Isolation (Self-Distance)

Kabhi kabhi insaan duniya se nahi,

khud se door ho jaata hai.

Aur yeh distance bahar se dikhayi nahi deta.

Kyuki tum logon ke beech hote ho.

Tum baat bhi kar rahe hote ho.

Tum hass bhi rahe hote ho.

Tum replies bhi de rahe hote ho.

Par andar… tum wahan nahi hote.

Self-distance ka start hamesha obvious nahi hota.

Pehle tumhe lagta hai tum bas “busy” ho.

Phir lagta hai tum “tired” ho.

Phir lagta hai tum “overthinking” kar rahe ho.
