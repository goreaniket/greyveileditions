Kaash main pehle samajh jaata.

Par truth yahi hai:

samajh tab aati hai jab tum us waqt se bahar nikal chuke hote ho jahan uski sabse zyada zarurat thi.

Aur shayad “too late” ka matlab yahi hai—

ki life ko samajhne ke liye hamesha thoda distance chahiye hota hai…

jo us waqt available nahi hota jab hum uske beech hote hain.
