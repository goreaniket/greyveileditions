Kam react karte ho.

Kam expect karte ho.

Aur log sochte hain tum change ho gaye ho.

Par truth simple hota hai

tum change nahi hote…

tum bas thak jaate ho.

Bahut thak jaate ho.

Aur jo log sabse zyada exhausted hote hain…

woh aksar sabse normal dikhte hain.

Kyuki unhone apna breakdown kisi ko show nahi kiya hota.

Unhone bas sab kuch quietly carry kiya hota hai…

bahut der tak.

Aur phir ek din ek simple realization aata hai.

Koi dramatic insight nahi.

Bas clarity:

Ki har cheez ko continuously hold karte rehna zaroori nahi hota.

Kuch feelings solve nahi hoti.

Kuch situations fix nahi hoti.

Kuch answers milte hi nahi.
