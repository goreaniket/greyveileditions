Na koi perfect closure.

Bas ek quiet acceptance hoti hai —

ki sab kuch hold karna zaroori nahi tha.

Aur jo cheez tumhe tod rahi thi…

wo kabhi tumhe complete kar rahi thi, tumhe pata hi nahi tha.

Aur shayad yahi “Last Shift” hai —

jab tum zindagi ko samajhne ki koshish chhod dete ho,

aur usse jeena shuru kar dete ho… jaise woh already enough ho.
