phir ek aisa phase aata hai jahan unka hona ya na hona din ke structure ka part banne lagta hai.

Aur insaan usse “normal” hi maanta rehta hai.

Ek naam jo pehle sirf ek naam tha…

dheere dheere familiar lagne lagta hai.

Aur familiarity ka sabse dangerous part yahi hota hai—woh bina permission ke comfort ban jaati hai.

Aapko lagta hai ki aap bas baat kar rahe ho.

Lekin dheere dheere aap wait karne lagte ho.

Ek notification jo pehle bas ek notification tha…

ab thoda sa attention kheench leta hai.

Aur aap khud ko pakad bhi nahi paate ki kab aapne us screen ko zyada baar check karna start kar diya.

Bina kisi reason ke.

Bina kisi plan ke.

Bas ek expectation ke saath jo kabhi define nahi kiya gaya.

Aur yahi se shuruaat hoti hai—silent shift ki.

Shuruaat mein yeh “attachment” jaisa feel hi nahi hota.

Woh bas normal lagta hai.
