PHASE III - DETACHMENT

Detachment

(The Quiet Disappearance)

Detachment kabhi ek decision nahi hota.

Na hi ek clear moment hota hai jahan insaan decide kare — “ab mujhe farak nahi padta.”

Aisa sirf kahaniyon mein hota hai.

Real life mein detachment ek slow disappearance hota hai.

Itna slow ki tumhe pehle pata hi nahi chalta ki tum jaa rahe ho… kisi se nahi, balki apne hi emotions se.

Pehle jo cheez tumhara pura din control kar leti thi…

ab woh sirf ek chhota sa thought ban ke reh jaati hai.
