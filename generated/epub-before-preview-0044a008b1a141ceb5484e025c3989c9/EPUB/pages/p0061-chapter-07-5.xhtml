Repair nahi karti.

Aur jab distractions bhi kaam karna band kar dete hain…

tab insaan apne saath reh jaata hai.

Bina escape ke.

Aur wahi pe ek uncomfortable truth saamne aata hai:

Tum lonely logon se nahi hue ho.

Tum apne hi andar se disconnected ho gaye ho.

Aur yeh loneliness nahi hoti.

Yeh absence hoti hai.

Self ka absence.

Phir kabhi kabhi tum feel karte ho:

“Main kisi aur ke saath hoon… toh better lagta hai.”

Par woh better feeling bhi unstable hoti hai.

Kyuki problem “log” nahi hote.

Problem “self” hota hai.

Aur jab self unstable hota hai,

toh kisi bhi connection pe stable rehna mushkil ho jaata hai.

Self-distance ka end dramatic nahi hota.

Koi breakdown nahi.
