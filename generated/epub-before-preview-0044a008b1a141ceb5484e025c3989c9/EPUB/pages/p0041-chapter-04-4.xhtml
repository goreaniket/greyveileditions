Aur pehle jo instant reaction hota tha…

woh ab nahi hota.

Na khushi.

Na sadness.

Bas ek neutral silence.

Aur tum khud se poochte ho:

“Main theek hoon ya bas feel karna band kar diya hai?”

Lekin dono answers ek jaise lagte hain.

Din normal chalta rehta hai.

Tum kaam karte ho.

Logon se baat karte ho.

Has bhi dete ho.

Par andar ek strange overload rehta hai

jaise dimag mein bahut saare tabs khule hon,

aur koi bhi properly close nahi ho raha.

Aur yeh overload loud nahi hota.

Yeh silent hota hai.

Isliye notice bhi late hota hai.

Phir ek aur phase aata hai:

Tum kam bolte ho.
