Wohi chats jo pehle baar baar khul jaati thi… ab sirf notification ban ke reh jaati hain.

Wohi moments jo pehle mood change kar dete the… ab bas pass ho jaate hain.

Jaise kuch bhi “andar” tak nahi jaa raha.

Aur sabse strange cheez:

Tumhe is absence ka bhi shock nahi hota.

Na loss feel hota hai.

Na gain feel hota hai.

Bas indifference.

Kabhi kabhi tum phone uthate ho bina reason.

Unlock karte ho.

Scroll karte ho.

Phir lock kar dete ho.

Aur baad mein realize hota hai…

tumhe yaad hi nahi tha tum kya dhoondh rahe the.

Yeh state loud nahi hoti.

Isliye dangerous hoti hai.

Tum normal life continue karte ho.

Kaam.
