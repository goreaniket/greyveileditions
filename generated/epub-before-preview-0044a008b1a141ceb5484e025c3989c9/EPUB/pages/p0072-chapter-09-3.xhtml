Aur kabhi kabhi problem yeh hoti hai ki hum us change ko rokne ki koshish bhi nahi karte…

hum bas uske saath adjust kar lete hain bina samjhe ki hum kya kho rahe hain.

Kabhi purani photos dekhi hain?

Us waqt sab normal tha.

Koi emotional weight nahi.

Koi meaning overload nahi.

Bas ek random moment.

Par saalon baad wahi photo…

sirf ek image nahi rehti.

Woh ek proof ban jaati hai ki kuch cheezein sach mein exist karti thi…

aur ab nahi hain.

Aur yahin ek uncomfortable realization aata hai:

Shayad main us moment mein tha hi nahi…

sirf physically present tha.

Mentally kahin aur.

Future mein. Past mein. Logon mein. Apne thoughts mein.

Aur jab hum peeche dekhte hain…
