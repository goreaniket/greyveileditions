Zyada explain karne lagte ho.

Zyada samajhne ki koshish karte ho.

Kabhi kabhi itna zyada ki tum khud ko hi side pe rakh dete ho.

Pehle tum cheezon par seedha mana kar dete the.

Ab tum sochne lagte ho—

“Chal koi baat nahi.”

Aur dheere dheere yeh compromise nahi hota… aadat ban jaati hai.

Aur sabse dangerous part yeh hota hai ki yeh sab itna slowly hota hai ki tumhe khud pata nahi chalta ki tum badal chuke ho.

Tumhe lagta hai tum connection maintain kar rahe ho.

Lekin kai baar tum sirf khud ko peeche rakh rahe hote ho.

Thoda thoda.

Roz.

Aur yeh invisible sacrifices ek din mein hurt nahi karte—

yeh accumulate hote hain.

Silently.
