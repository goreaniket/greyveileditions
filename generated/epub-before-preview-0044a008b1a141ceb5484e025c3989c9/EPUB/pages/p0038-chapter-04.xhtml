PHASE II - DISTANCE

Emotional Exhaustion

Attachment jab start hoti hai, sab kuch

naturally alive lagta hai.

Messages mein ek energy hoti hai.

Replies mein ek meaning hota hai.

Aur har interaction ek small importance carry karta hai.

Par mind ek hi intensity ko continuously maintain nahi kar sakta.

Woh slow shift hota hai.

Aur yeh shift kabhi ek event se nahi aata.

Woh chhoti chhoti cheezon se build hota hai.

Pehle tum message type karte ho…
