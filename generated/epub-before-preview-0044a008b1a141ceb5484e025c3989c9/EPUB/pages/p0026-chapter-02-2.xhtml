Koi expectations nahi hoti.

Phir ek subtle shift aata hai

topic nahi badalta…

par importance badhne lagti hai.

Ek specific person ka message…

din ke flow ko interrupt karne lagta hai.

Ek naam screen par aate hi attention automatically shift ho jaata hai.

Aur bina realize kiye insaan kisi ki presence ka emotionally “used to” ho jaata hai.

Jaise woh person officially life ka part nahi hota…

phir bhi uski absence noticeable hone lagti hai.

Silent attachment ka dangerous part yahi hai

kyuki kuch bhi openly define nahi hota.

Na “relationship” hota hai.

Na “distance” hota hai.

Bas ek in-between space hota hai jahan emotions quietly grow karte rehte hain.

Outside everything looks normal.

“We’re just talking.”

“It’s nothing serious.”
