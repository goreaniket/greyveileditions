Aur sabse confusing baat—yeh sab wrong nahi lagta pehle.

Yeh sab natural lagta hai.

Even comforting.

Kyuki attachment apne starting phase mein kabhi pain jaisa feel nahi hota.

Woh comfort jaisa lagta hai.

Woh familiarity jaisa lagta hai.

Woh ek soft dependency jaisa lagta hai jo dangerous nahi lagti.

Aur isi wajah se insaan us shift ko miss kar deta hai.

Woh samajhta hai ki woh bas thoda zyada care kar raha hai.

Thoda zyada involved ho gaya hai.

Bas itna hi.

Lekin reality mein ek aur layer quietly build ho rahi hoti hai—

Aap kisi insaan ke baare mein unse zyada sochne lagte ho jo aap apne baare mein soch rahe hote ho.

Aur jab koi insaan thoughts ka center ban jaata hai…
