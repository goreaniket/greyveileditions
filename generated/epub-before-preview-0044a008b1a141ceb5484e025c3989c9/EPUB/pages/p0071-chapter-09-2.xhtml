Jaise yeh sab hamesha yahin rahega.

Phir time move karta hai.

Aur sab kuch quietly shift hone lagta hai.

Koi announcement nahi hoti.

Koi warning nahi hoti.

Koi dramatic moment nahi hota.

Bas ek din tum notice karte ho…

ki jo cheezein hamesha jaisi lagti thi…

woh waisi nahi rahi.

Ek dost door ho gaya.

Ek group kam ho gaya.

Ek conversation band ho gayi.

Ek connection fade ho gaya.

Aur tum samajh bhi nahi paate kab.

Aur yahi life ka sabse strange part hai:

Change kabhi announce nahi hota.

Woh itni dheemi tarah aata hai ki mehsoos hi nahi hota.

Aur jab tum dekhte ho…

tab tak woh tumhari reality ban chuka hota hai.
