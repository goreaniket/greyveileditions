toh woh sirf ek connection nahi rehta.

Woh ek internal dependency banne lagta hai.

Aur mind—jo already sab kuch amplify karta hai—

woh chhoti cheezon ko bhi meaning dena start kar deta hai.

Conversation khatam ho jaati hai…

par analysis nahi.

Aap tone revisit karte ho.

Word choice revisit karte ho.

Silence revisit karte ho.

Aur har baar ek naya interpretation nikalta hai.

Aur yahin se cheezein quietly apni direction change karti hain.

Attachment problem nahi hota shuruaat mein.

Problem tab start hota hai jab emotional balance kisi aur ke reactions ke around slowly revolve karne lagta hai—without awareness, without control.

Aur tab insaan ko ya toh aur deeply feel hota hai…

ya pehli baar khud ko feel hota hai.

Aur shayad yahi attachment ka real beginning hota hai—
