PHASE VI - THE LAST SHIFT

What Remains When Everything Quiets Down

Kabhi kabhi life mein aisa phase aata hai…

jab sab kuch pehle jaisa nahi rehta.

Na log.

Na conversations.

Na priorities.

Na tum khud.

Aur pehle yeh change ajeeb lagta hai…

jaise kuch toot raha ho.

Par dheere dheere samajh aata hai…

shayad kuch toot nahi raha…

shayad kuch complete ho raha hai.

Kyuki life sirf jodne ka naam nahi hai…
