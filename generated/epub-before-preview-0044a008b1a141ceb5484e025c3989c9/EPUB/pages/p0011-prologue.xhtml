Prologue

Kabhi kabhi insaan suddenly change nahi hota.

Woh dheere dheere internally shift hota hai.

Pehle sab normal lagta hai.

Routine same hoti hai.

Log same hote hain.

Conversations bhi.

Zindagi ek fixed flow jaisi lagti hai jisme kuch bhi unusual nahi hota.

Par phir ek point ke baad ek subtle change start hota hai…

insaan sirf cheezein experience nahi karta,

woh unhe notice karna start kar deta hai.

Kis message ka wait reh gaya hai.

Kis silence ka impact mood par pad raha hai.
