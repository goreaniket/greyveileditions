Ki har cheez ko hold karna zaroori nahi tha.

Ki har connection permanent nahi hota.

Ki har feeling ka last hona bhi normal hai.

Kuch log aate hain.

Kuch log jaate hain.

Aur kuch sirf isliye aate hain ki woh tumhe yeh samjha sakein ki kuch cheezein rehne ke liye nahi hoti.

Aur jo reh jaata hai…

woh log nahi hote.

Na conversations.

Na moments.

Jo reh jaata hai…

woh tum hote ho —

thoda quieter, thoda heavier, aur thoda clearer than before.

Aur shayad yahi The Last Shift hai.

Jahan tum life ko control karna band kar dete ho…

aur usse samajhne ki koshish bhi dheere dheere chhod dete ho.

Na answers ki urgency rehti hai.

Na questions ka pressure.
