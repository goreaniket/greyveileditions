End Realization

Kuch cheezein life mein samajh aane ke liye nahi hoti… balki jee jaane ke liye hoti hain.

Hum aksar poori zindagi ek cheez chase karte rehte hain — clarity.

Har situation ka meaning, har insaan ka intention, har feeling ka reason.

Aur is process mein hum itna zyada sochne lagte hain ki jeena peeche chhoot jata hai.

Shuru mein lagta hai ki agar hum sab kuch samajh lenge toh shayad dard kam ho jayega.

Shayad agar har confusion ka answer mil gaya toh andar ka shor band ho jayega.

Par waqt dheere dheere ek alag hi sach dikha deta hai —
