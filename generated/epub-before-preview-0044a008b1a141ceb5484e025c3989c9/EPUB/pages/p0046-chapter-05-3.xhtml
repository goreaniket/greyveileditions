Bas neutrality.

Aur yeh neutrality peaceful nahi hoti.

Yeh ek blankness hoti hai jo dheere dheere familiar lagne lagti hai.

Pehle tum har silence ko meaning dete the.

Har delay ko story banate the.

Har tone ko decode karte the.

Ab silence silence lagti hai.

Delay bas delay.

Tone bas tone.

Kuch interpret karne ki zarurat hi nahi lagti.

Aur shayad yahi sabse confusing shift hota hai.

Kyuki tum lose nahi karte…

tum stop reacting kar dete ho.

Kabhi kabhi tum ek purani chat open karte ho.

Do-teen messages padhte ho.

Phir close kar dete ho.

Koi emotion trigger nahi hota.

Bas ek recognition hota hai — “yeh kabhi important tha.”

Aur tumhe khud par thoda ajeeb lagta hai.
