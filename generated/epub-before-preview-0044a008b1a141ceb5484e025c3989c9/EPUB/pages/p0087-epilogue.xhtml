Epilogue

Kabhi kabhi life ka end kisi loud moment se nahi hota.

Na koi final scene.

Na koi dramatic closure.

Bas ek slow silence hota hai…

jo pehle ajeeb lagta hai, phir normal ho jaata hai, aur phir aadat ban jaata hai.

Woh cheezein jo kabhi life ka center lagti thi…

dheere dheere apna weight kho deti hain.

Log jo kabhi har din ke thoughts ka part the…

woh sirf memory ban jaate hain.

Aur kab hua, kaise hua — yeh bhi clearly yaad nahi rehta.

Aur insaan ek strange state mein aa jaata hai…

na fully connected, na fully detached.
