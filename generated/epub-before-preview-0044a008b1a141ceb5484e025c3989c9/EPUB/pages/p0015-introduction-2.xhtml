Aur shayad isi liye hum kisi ke paas hote hue bhi thoda distance maintain karte hain.

Not because we don’t care…

but because care itself starts feeling risky.

Kuch log life se chale jaate hain loudly—arguments, endings, closure ke saath.

Aur kuch log physically present hote hue bhi dheere dheere emotionally disappear ho jaate hain.

Aur honestly…

second wala zyada hurt karta hai.

Kyuki usme ending visible nahi hoti.

Attachment interesting cheez hai.

Shuruaat mein yeh sirf comfort lagta hai.

Ek person, ek habit, ek presence… jo normal ban jaata hai.

Aur jab kuch normal ban jaata hai,

toh hum usse permanent maan lete hain—without realizing it.

Phir dheere dheere woh attachment ek routine ban jaata hai.

Routine expectation ban jaata hai.
