Phir dheere dheere ek aur change hota hai.

Tum observe karte ho, participate nahi karte.

Conversation ho rahi hoti hai…

aur tum uska part kam, spectator zyada hote ho.

Log kuch bolte hain.

Tum sunte ho.

Phir respond karte ho.

Par response ke peeche presence nahi hoti.

Bas reaction hota hai.

Aur reaction aur feeling mein farq hota hai.

Feeling andar se aati hai.

Reaction sirf kaam complete karta hai.

Self-distance yahin se grow karta hai:

Jab tum jeena kam, aur operate zyada karne lagte ho.

Phir ek aur strange cheez hoti hai:

Tumhare interests bhi door hone lagte hain.

Wohi cheez jo pehle tumhe alive feel karati thi…

ab effort lagti hai.

Music. Movies. Conversations. Plans.

Sab thoda heavy lagne lagta hai.

Aur tum sochte ho:
