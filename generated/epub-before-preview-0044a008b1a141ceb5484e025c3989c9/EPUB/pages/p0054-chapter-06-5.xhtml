Aur tum khud ko dekhte ho aur confuse hote ho.

“Main cold ho gaya hoon?”

“Ya main bas exhausted ho gaya hoon?”

Par answer dono lagte hain.

Emotional shutdown ka core yahi hai:

Insaan feel karna band nahi karta…

bas feel karne ki intensity reduce ho jaati hai

taaki system survive kar sake.

Jaise overloaded circuit khud ko dim kar deta hai…

taaki fuse na udd jaye.

Pehle silence uncomfortable hoti thi.

Ab silence normal lagti hai.

Aur kabhi kabhi silence safe lagti hai… kyuki usme demand nahi hoti.

Aur yahi shift tumhe confuse karta rehta hai:

“Main heal ho raha hoon?”

“Ya main numb ho raha hoon?”

Par dono ka boundary blur ho jaata hai.

Kyuki healing bhi kabhi kabhi numbness jaisi lagti hai…

aur numbness kabhi kabhi healing jaisi.
