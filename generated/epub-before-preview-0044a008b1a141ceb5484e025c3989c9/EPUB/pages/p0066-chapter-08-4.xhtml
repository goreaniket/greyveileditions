bas mujhe pata nahi tha.

Aur yeh realization thoda late aata hai.

Jab already energy lag chuki hoti hai.

Jab already expectations build ho chuki hoti hain.

Jab already internal world shift ho chuka hota hai.

Kabhi kabhi tum khud se puchte ho:

“Kya main zyada feel kar raha hoon?”

“Kya main sab kuch overcomplicate kar raha hoon?”

Par answer immediately nahi aata.

Kyuki mind dono sides argue kar raha hota hai.

Ek side kehti hai:

“Sab normal hai.”

Doosri side kehti hai:

“Kuch toh change hua hai.”

Aur insaan beech mein atak jaata hai.

Aur shayad yahi awareness ka real beginning hota hai.

Jab tumhe answers nahi milte…

sirf better questions milte hain.

Phir ek strange shift hota hai.

Tum blame karna kam kar dete ho.

Logon ko bhi.
