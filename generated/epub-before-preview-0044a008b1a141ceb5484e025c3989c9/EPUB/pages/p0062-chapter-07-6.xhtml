Koi explosion nahi.

Bas ek realization hota hai:

“Main apne hi saath present nahi hoon.”

Aur yahi moment dangerous bhi hota hai…

aur healing ka start bhi.

Kyuki pehli baar insaan notice karta hai:

Problem duniya nahi thi.

Problem disappearance andar se ho rahi thi.

Aur phir ek chhota sa shift aata hai.

Tum bhaagna kam kar dete ho.

Khud se.

Bas thoda rukte ho.

Thoda notice karte ho.

Thoda feel karne dete ho… bina label diye.

Aur shayad wahi se wapas aana start hota hai.

Not instantly.

Not perfectly.

Bas quietly.
