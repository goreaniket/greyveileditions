woh todne aur naye tarike se banne ka process bhi hai.

Aur jab purane layers girte hain…

toh pehle khali pan aata hai.

Ek aisa khali pan jahan noise kam hota hai…

par thoughts zyada loud ho jaate hain.

Aur insaan us silence se bachne ki koshish karta hai…

kyuki woh usko uske khud ke saamne le aata hai.

Na distraction.

Na validation.

Na escape.

Bas tum… aur tumhari soch.

Aur wahi pe woh cheezein surface karti hain jinhe tumne ignore kiya tha…

unanswered emotions.

unsaid words.

unfinished moments.

Par yeh sab ek saath nahi aata…

yeh layers mein aata hai.

Aur har layer ke saath ek realization aur clear hota hai:

73
