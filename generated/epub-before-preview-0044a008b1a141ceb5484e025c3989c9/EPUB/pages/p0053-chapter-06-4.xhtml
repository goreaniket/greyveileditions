Baat-cheet.

Hassi.

Sab kuch externally same rehta hai.

Par internally ek gap create ho chuka hota hai.

Tum experience kar rahe hote ho…

par absorb nahi kar rahe hote.

Aur yeh gap dheere dheere grow karta hai.

Pehle tum cheezein feel karte the aur sochte the.

Phir tum sirf sochte the.

Aur phir tum sirf observe karte ho.

Aur ek point ke baad observation bhi passive ho jaati hai.

Jaise life screen par chal rahi ho…

aur tum bas dekh rahe ho.

Kabhi kabhi tum kisi purani chat ko open karte ho.

Thodi der scroll karte ho.

Aur bina emotion ke band kar dete ho.

Koi nostalgia nahi.

Koi pain nahi.

Bas recognition:

“Ye ek phase tha.”
