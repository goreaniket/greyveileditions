Phir tum overcare karne lagte ho.

Zyada patience.

Zyada effort.

Zyada understanding.

Sirf is hope mein ki cheezein stable rahengi.

Lekin irony yahi hoti hai—

jahan care comfort banni chahiye thi…

wahan care pressure banne lagti hai.

Aur pressure distance create karta hai.

Phir ek din realization aata hai:

tum kisi ko lose nahi kar rahe…

tum khud ko dheere dheere lose kar chuke ho.

Aur wahi cycle repeat hota hai:

Fear.

Adjustment.

Overcare.

Confusion.

Distance.

Aur phir ek stage aati hai jahan sadness bhi nahi rehti—

sirf exhaustion reh jaati hai.
