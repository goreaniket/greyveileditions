Expectation.

Overthinking.

Fear.

Exhaustion.

Distance.

Aur insaan samajhta rehta hai ki woh life jee raha hai…

par woh bas layers accumulate kar raha hota hai.

Aur phir ek point aata hai jahan sab kuch reverse hota hai.

Ab tum situations ko feel nahi karte…

tum unhe analyze karte ho.

Har cheez ek question ban jaati hai.

“Iska matlab kya tha?”

“Usne aisa kyun bola?”

“Main overthink kar raha hoon ya kuch sach mein change hua hai?”

Aur sabse dangerous part yeh hota hai:

Dono answers valid lagte hain.

Overthinking bhi sahi lagti hai.

Intuition bhi sahi lagti hai.
