situations ko, khud ko, feelings ko.

Aur jab kuch bhi stable nahi hota…

tab tum fix karne lagte ho.

Aur yahin se exhaustion start hota hai.

Ek din nahi.

Ek moment nahi.

Bas ek slow collapse.

Strange baat yeh hai ki emotional exhaustion kabhi “breakdown” jaisa feel nahi hota.

Koi loud moment nahi hota.

Koi obvious ending nahi hoti.

Bas kuch chhoti chhoti cheezein change hone lagti hain:

tum reply jaldi nahi karte

tum explain karna avoid karte ho

tum cheezein repeat nahi karte

tum effort ko measure karne lagte ho

Aur sabse confusing part yeh hota hai:

Tumhe dard nahi hota…

par tum theek bhi nahi hote.

Kabhi kabhi tum kisi ka message dekhte ho.
