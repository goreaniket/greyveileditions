Dono thoughts equally real lagte hain.

Koi bhi completely false nahi lagta.

Aur yahi woh point hota hai jahan clarity slowly dissolve hone lagti hai.

Bas questions reh jaate hain.

Aur sabse frustrating baat yeh hoti hai ki tum kisi ko explain bhi nahi kar sakte—

kyuki technically kuch hua hi nahi hota.

Na argument.

Na ending.

Na distance ka proof.

Bas ek shift.

Ek invisible change.

Aur wahi se fear ka real form start hota hai—loss ka nahi, control ke slip hone ka.

Kyuki tumhe samajh aane lagta hai ki jo cheez tum casually apni life ka part samajh rahe the…

woh kabhi officially tumhari thi hi nahi.

Aur phir fear ek emotion nahi rehta.

Woh behavior ban jaata hai.

Tum zyada available ho jaate ho.
