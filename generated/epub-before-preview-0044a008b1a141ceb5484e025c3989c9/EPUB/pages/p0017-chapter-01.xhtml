PHASE I - ATTACHMENT

The Beginning of Attachment

Aajkal attachment itni naturally ho jaati hai ki insaan ko pata hi nahi chalta ki kab koi person uski life mein “thoda important” se “bahut important” ban gaya.

Aur yahi problem hai—koi announcement nahi hoti.

Koi signal nahi aata.

Koi moment aisa nahi hota jahan life ruk kar bata de ki ab kuch change ho raha hai.

Bas cheezein chalte rehti hain…

aur chalte chalte hi badal jaati hain.

Pehle conversations kabhi kabhi hoti hain…

phir woh regular ho jaati hain…
