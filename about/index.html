About Greyveil Editions

Greyveil Editions is an independent publishing house founded by Lalit Rakesh Mishra, built around a long-term vision of exploring the deeper realities of human existence.

Through psychological, philosophical, and introspective writing, Greyveil examines human thought, emotion, identity, relationships, perception, and the contradictions that quietly shape the way we live.

Rather than offering easy answers or telling people how to live, its work is rooted in observation — questioning what feels familiar and uncovering what often remains unnoticed beneath it.

A growing body of work devoted to understanding what it means to be human.

Publishing Hierarchy

Greyveil Editions brings together multiple collections and literary series..

Greyveil Editions serves as the publishing house and the foundation upon which every collection is built.

Each collection explores a different dimension of the human experience while remaining connected to the same philosophy of observation, reflection, and understanding.

Volume I brings together The Human Mind, The Human Paradox, and The Human Fiction. Volume II, The Human Experience, expands the catalogue through The Inner Experience, The Lived Experience, and The Expanded Experience. Together, the two volumes contain 24 books across six series.
