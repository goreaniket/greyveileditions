About Greyveil Editions

What is Greyveil?

Greyveil Editions is an independent publishing and research initiative devoted to exploring the visible and invisible dimensions of human existence. Through books, essays, reflections, and long-form explorations, Greyveil examines identity, perception, attachment, ambition, loneliness, memory, love, fear, hope, contradiction, meaning, and the countless patterns that shape the course of human life.

Founded by Lalit Rakesh Mishra.

Greyveil is interested not only in the moments that define us, but also in the moments we ignore; not only in comfort, but also in discomfort; not only in certainty, but also in doubt.

Every collection exists as a different perspective on the same question:

What does it truly mean to be human?

Greyveil is built on a simple belief:

Every life contains a story.
Every story contains a pattern.
Every pattern reveals a truth.
