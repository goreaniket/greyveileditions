01 / THE HOUSE

Not simply a catalogue.
A point of view.

Greyveil Editions is an independent literary and psychology publishing house built around one enduring subject: the human experience.

We publish work that looks beneath the surface of identity, perception, relationships, memory, attachment and the contradictions that shape ordinary life. The aim is not to hand readers quick answers. It is to make difficult questions more approachable, and to create books that stay in thought after the page is closed.

THE GREYVEIL POSITION

Accessible without being shallow. Thoughtful without being distant.

Greyveil is founded and led by Lalit Rakesh Mishra. Its catalogue brings together independent writing, editorial thinking and a growing reader experience designed around discovery, reflection and human response.
