01 / RIGHTS & PERMISSIONS

Respect the work.
Ask before reuse.

Greyveil Editions content, books, manuscripts, covers, brand assets and original website material are protected by applicable copyright and other intellectual-property laws.

Public availability does not mean free reuse. Do not reproduce, redistribute, upload, sell, translate, adapt, bulk-download or create derivative editions of protected material without written permission.

What readers may do

Readers may access and use purchased or otherwise authorised editions for personal reading, subject to the terms supplied with the edition. Brief quotations for genuine review, criticism or discussion should be limited, properly attributed and must not substitute for the original work.

What requires permission

Commercial use, republication, translations, adaptations, classroom packs, audiobook or video readings, cover reuse, bulk extraction, dataset building, model training and redistribution of book files require written permission unless a specific licence says otherwise.

AI, scraping and automated use

Do not scrape, crawl, bulk-download, reconstruct or reproduce complete books, manuscript text, reader data, covers or source files for model training, dataset creation, synthetic media or competing services without written permission. Ordinary search-engine indexing of public pages is distinct from bulk extraction or reuse.

Rights enquiries and concerns

For permissions, licensing, adaptations, translations, media use or to report a rights concern, email greyveileditions@gmail.com. Include the work or asset, the intended use and relevant details. Permission exists only when confirmed in writing.
