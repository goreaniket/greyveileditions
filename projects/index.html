The Human Experience — 5 Book Collection

What if the hardest parts of being human were never the obvious ones?

What if strength could become exhaustion? What if reality changed depending on the person looking at it? What if love carried fear, attachment, jealousy and expectation beneath it? What if helping someone could quietly become part of your own identity?

And what if, after all of that, the biggest question was simply this: What actually happens to a human being between birth and death?

Written by Lalit R. Mishra·Greyveil Editions™·Stories That Stay Within.

The Human Experience

The Human Experience is a five-book psychological and philosophical collection exploring the parts of life we usually feel before we understand them.

Book I — The Person Who Was Tired of Being Strong
The invisible cost of becoming the person everyone depends on.

Book II — The World We Thought We Saw
How memory, perception, assumptions and identity quietly shape the reality we believe we are seeing.

Book III — An Unknown Part of Life
Love beyond the beautiful explanation — attachment, proof, sacrifice, jealousy, betrayal, forgiveness and the truths that can exist beside love.

Book IV — The People We Couldn’t Save
What happens when caring for someone becomes the place where you feel most necessary — and where care, purpose, identity and ownership begin to overlap.

Book V — Between Birth and Death
The entire human journey — from dependence and childhood to love, ambition, responsibility, ageing, loss, mortality and acceptance.

Five books. Five different doors into the same question: What does it actually mean to be human?
